Notes from
the corridor.

Market reports town by town, and plain explanations of the things that decide a purchase out here. Written for people buying and selling between Carbondale and De Beque.

01 What goes here

Three things, and nothing that reads like a press release

Monthly market reports for one town at a time. Explanations of the local mechanics, wells, water rights, metro districts and septic. And what is actually changing, when something actually changes. The standing explanations live in the buying guide and the selling guide.

National headlines describe a market that does not exist here, and even Colorado figures are dominated by the Front Range. If you want the same material on video, the market reports are filmed monthly, and the communities pages carry the standing detail for each town.

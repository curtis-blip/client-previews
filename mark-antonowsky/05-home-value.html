VALUATION FACTORS

What Determines Your Phoenix Home Value

Recent Comparable Sales

Your home's value is benchmarked against similar properties that sold recently in your area. Size, condition, and features are compared.

Property Condition & Upgrades

Updated kitchens, bathrooms, roofing, and systems increase perceived value. Deferred maintenance can reduce it.

Location & Neighborhood

Proximity to schools, shopping, employment centers, and parks directly affects what buyers will pay.

Current Market Demand

The number of active buyers versus available inventory determines how competitive your pricing can be.

PRICING STRATEGY

Why Accurate Pricing Matters When Selling

Overpricing Risks

Homes priced above market value sit longer, attract fewer showings, and often sell for less after price reductions. Extended time on market signals problems to buyers.

Underpricing Risks

Pricing too low can leave money on the table. While it may generate fast offers, those offers may not reflect the property's true market value.

Mark's pricing strategy uses comparable sales data, current demand, and property-specific factors to find the sweet spot.

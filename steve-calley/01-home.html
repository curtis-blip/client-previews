Meet Steve

He explains everything, then negotiates hard

Buying or selling a home is one of the largest transactions most people ever make. Steve believes you deserve the best service and experience while you do it.

A Colorado native, born and raised in Arvada, Steve now lives in Broomfield with his wife and their four children. He works full time, answers texts and email quickly, and keeps a network of agents across the country for clients moving in or out of Colorado.

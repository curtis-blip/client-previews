Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to reach Donna?
The fastest way to reach Donna is by phone at (310) 801-0265. You can also email info@solsticeir.com or use the contact form on this page. Donna is known for responding quickly, and most inquiries receive a response the same business day.
How quickly does Donna respond to inquiries?
Donna prides herself on responsiveness. Clients consistently highlight her availability in reviews, noting that she responds immediately and is always accessible. You can expect a response within a few hours during business days, and often much sooner.
What does an initial consultation look like?
An initial consultation with Donna is complimentary and typically lasts 30 to 45 minutes. You will discuss your real estate goals, timeline, budget, and any specific neighborhoods or property types you are interested in. Donna will share current market insights and outline a tailored plan to help you achieve your goals.
What are the office hours for Solstice International Realty?
The Malibu office is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday by appointment. Donna and her team are often available outside of standard hours to accommodate client schedules, including evenings and weekends for showings and consultations.
Are virtual consultations available?
Absolutely. Donna works with clients across the country and internationally. Video consultations via Zoom, FaceTime, or phone are available for buyers and sellers who are not local to the area. Virtual property tours can also be arranged for out-of-town buyers.
Does Donna work with both buyers and sellers?
Yes. Donna and the Solstice team represent both buyers and sellers across Malibu, Pacific Palisades, Westlake Village, Calabasas, and beyond. She also handles luxury leasing and corporate relocation for major companies. Learn more about Donna's experience.

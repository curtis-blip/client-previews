Probate Expertise

Guiding executors through estate property sales

Probate real estate is not a standard sale. There are court filings, multiple stakeholders, properties that may need clearing or repair, and timelines set by the surrogate. Mary is a certified probate real estate specialist who works alongside estate attorneys, coordinates with the court when needed, and handles the property side so the executor can focus on the family.

  • Court-approved valuations and documentation
  • Coordination with estate attorneys and the surrogate
  • Property preparation including clearing, cleaning, and repair management
  • Marketing that attracts qualified buyers comfortable with probate timelines

01 The broker

The part of the job
nobody writes down

I grew up in the Roaring Fork Valley and I work the stretch of it people can still afford, from Carbondale down through Rifle and out to De Beque.

Most of what I do does not show up on a listing sheet. I have mowed a lawn twice before a property went live because it needed doing. I have walked one family through houses in Rifle, New Castle and Parachute until the right one turned up. I explain a well share or a metro district before you sign, not after.

Anthem Highlands at a glance

A master-planned neighborhood in north Broomfield

Anthem Highlands is the all-ages village of Anthem, the community Pulte and Del Webb planned on Broomfield's north side.

Its filings run from State Highway 7 south across West 160th Avenue and the Northwest Parkway, with Sheridan Parkway and Lowell Boulevard as the main streets. Anthem Reserve, with custom homes, and Anthem Ranch, a 55+ community, are its neighbors.

Questions

Anthem Highlands FAQ

How are Anthem Highlands, Anthem Reserve and Anthem Ranch different?

They are the three villages of Anthem. Anthem Highlands is the all-ages neighborhood, Anthem Reserve has custom homes, and Anthem Ranch is a 55+ community. The Anthem Highlands Community Association also covers Anthem Reserve.

Which schools serve Anthem Highlands?

Anthem is in Adams 12 Five Star Schools. The district's school finder returned Thunder Vista P-8 and Legacy High School for the addresses checked; confirm any specific address with the district.

Is there an HOA?

Yes. The Anthem Highlands Community Association runs the Parkside Community Center, with its pool and fitness center. Ask Steve for the current dues and documents on any home you're considering.

How do you get to Denver or Boulder from Anthem?

The Northwest Parkway connects Anthem to I-25 and to US-36, the main routes to Denver and Boulder. The Northwest Parkway and E-470 are toll roads.

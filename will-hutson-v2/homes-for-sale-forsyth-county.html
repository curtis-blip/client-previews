Forsyth County Real Estate Along GA-400

Homes for sale in Forsyth County center on Cumming and the fast-building south end, where GA-400 turns a Lake Lanier morning into a Perimeter commute. The county pulls families for its schools and its shoreline in equal measure.

New construction and resale trade blocks apart along Peachtree Parkway. Forsyth is one county on the North Georgia communities map.

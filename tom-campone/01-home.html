Meet Tom

A neighbor first, a broker second

Tom bought his first home in the South Loop in 2003, anxious and unsure of the process, and never forgot how that felt. Today he makes sure his clients never have to.

Before real estate, Tom spent a long, successful career in finance, closing some of the most complex multi-million dollar programs in North America. He brings that negotiating discipline to every condo, investment property, and single-family home he handles, paired with the patient, personal service his clients describe again and again.

"I want my clients to feel comfortable, well informed, and to actually enjoy the process."

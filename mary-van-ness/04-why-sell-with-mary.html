A Listing That Works

Your home deserves more than a sign in the yard

In Central New Jersey, the difference between a home that sells in ten days at the right price and one that sits for sixty at a discount often comes down to three things: how it is priced, how it is presented, and how it is marketed. I take all three seriously.

Every listing gets a pricing strategy built on comparable sales, professional photography, targeted marketing, and a real human on the phone. No auto-responders. No listing and walking away.

My Process

Simple, not easy

01

Home Walkthrough & Strategy

We walk the property together. I point out what Central NJ buyers look for, what to fix, and what to leave alone. You leave with a clear list and a preliminary pricing range.

02

Prep & Professional Photography

I coordinate any recommended updates, staging consultations, and professional photography. Your listing goes to market looking its best.

03

Pricing & MLS Launch

We set a price based on the last 90 days of real comparable sales, not wishful thinking. Your home hits the MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com, and my direct buyer network the same day.

04

Negotiation & Closing

When offers arrive, we review them together. I negotiate for the best price and terms, manage the inspection period, and coordinate with title and attorney through closing.

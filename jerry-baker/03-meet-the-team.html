The team

Two people, one office

Jerry runs the transactions. Sonia keeps everything else moving. That is the whole firm, and it is deliberate.

Broker and owner

Gerald "Jerry" Baker

I chose real estate for goal driven results, and for the part where a client's objective actually gets completed. The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development recruited me in 1988 to sell and manage its single family and multifamily housing portfolios, and I spent two decades there as a sales specialist, project manager and financial analyst.

I founded Black Hawk Real Estate in 2010 to do that work for local homes, businesses and community. The result has been very satisfied clients, over forty five star reviews, and referrals back to my own company. I am a proud veteran owned business, and I answer my own phone.

Real estate administrative professional

Sonia Ruiz

Raised in Colorado and local to the Gilpin County area her whole life. She holds a bachelor's degree in anthropology from the University of Colorado Denver, and after college went looking for a career that involved people and helping communities.

Outside the profession her passion is teaching pilates. Her other hobbies include nature walks in places like Golden Gate Canyon Park, art shows in RiNo and on Santa Fe Drive, and working through Denver's food, from mom and pop kitchens to the downtown rooms.

Her love for real estate comes from wanting to help people find a place to call home, or land they feel connected to.

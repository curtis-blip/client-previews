Meet Brandi

The driving force behind Faircloth Home

Brandi Faircloth has guided buyers and sellers across Middle Georgia for nearly thirty years. A Middle Georgia native and the owner of Robins Realty Group, she built the Faircloth Home Team on a simple principle, treat others the way you would want to be treated, and back it with the market knowledge that only comes from decades on the same streets.

An Accredited Buyer's Representative and Seller Representative Specialist, Brandi leads a team of eight, including veteran agents and dedicated VA and military relocation specialists who know Robins Air Force Base inside and out. Whether you are buying your first home or selling the one you raised a family in, the answer is the same.

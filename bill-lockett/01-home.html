About

The #1 agent at Coldwell Banker Collegiate Peaks Realty.

Bill has worked the Collegiate Peaks corridor since 1999. Owner and managing broker of two offices, Buena Vista and Salida. Most clients come back, and most send people. The longest-running ones are now into their second or third transaction with him.

Career credentials run deep: A-REO (Accredited REO Agent), e-PRO (Internet Professional), GRI (Graduate REALTOR® Institute), CRS (Certified Residential Specialist), MRE (Master of Real Estate). Past Board President and Director. Retired SWAT Master Operator for the local Sheriff's Department.

The work is the same whether the property is a $200,000 cabin or a $1.7M ranch: read the contract, answer the phone, show up.

The way Bill works Detail-obsessed. Long-term loyal. Two decades in the same corridor.

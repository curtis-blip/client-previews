Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

The Orr Group at Coldwell Banker D'Ann Harper, REALTORS respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on this website, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

We collect information you give us directly when you complete a contact form, request a home valuation, or create a property search account. That typically includes your name, email address, phone number, and whatever you tell us about your property or your search.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, including your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and how you arrived. This is standard web analytics.

Property search accounts

If you create an account to save searches or favourites, our IDX provider stores your saved criteria and the listings you view. We use that to send you better matches. We do not use it to cold call you.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and provide the service you asked for
  • To send listings, valuations or market information you requested
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations as licensed Texas real estate agents
  • To improve how this website works

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, such as our IDX provider, email platform and brokerage, and only so they can perform that work.

Communication and opting out

By submitting a form you agree we may contact you by phone, email or text about your enquiry. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text, using the unsubscribe link in any email, or telling us directly. Message and data rates may apply and message frequency varies.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for basic functionality and analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features, including saved property searches, will not work if you do.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Some records must be retained under Texas real estate law and brokerage policy. Email melissa@theorrgrp.com and we will respond.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Meet Dave

Straight answers from the first walk-through

Before a home goes on the market, Dave walks it with the owners and says plainly which repairs will pay for themselves and which will not. Then he builds the plan: how to prepare the house, what to ask for it, and how to present it to the buyers most likely to pay.

He came to real estate from pharmaceutical sales, which shaped how he approaches strategy and negotiation. His clients notice it in the short videos he records to answer their questions before they ask.

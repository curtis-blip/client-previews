Design Rules

The complete visual + typographic system for Team Shellien · Gem State Realty. Built for handoff to Jeff and Kane, the AgenticPress build team.

Brand Brief

Sports-Team Bold, Magic Valley Roots.

Shellien Gilliland's brand is bold, energetic, and unmistakably Idaho. Per her own designer note: "Think sports team with bold lettering, gold and black styling." The visual system is the brand. Brand voice is direct, professional, warm, never coach-speak.

1. Brand Is Palette, Not Background

Sports-team styling means gold + black ACCENTS over a normally-paced light page. Dark backgrounds are PUNCTUATION (hero, closing CTA, footer), not the dominant treatment.

2. Copy Voice = Professional Direct

Match Shellien's actual bio: warm-professional, plain-spoken, specific. NEVER coach-speak (no "deliver results", "make a move", "every step", "dream home", three-beat motivational cadences).

3. Evergreen Only

No stats that need ongoing maintenance. Banned: median price, population, DOM, list-to-sale, "125+ reviews" growing counters. Use designations (Idaho's Best 2x), drive times, county names, founding years.

Color

One Accent, Two Neutrals.

Gold (#cfb958) is the single accent. Black + warm off-white carry everything else. No secondary brand color. No pastels. No gradients beyond hero overlays.

Typography

Anton (Google Font) handles display H1s and bold accent moments. Inter (Google Font) handles every other typographic job from H2 through body. No italic-serif accent. UPPERCASE default on H1 / H2 / eyebrows.

Layout · Pacing

Light Dominant, Dark Punctuates.

Pages alternate light → light → light with dark sections used only as opening (hero) and closing (CTA + footer) punctuation. Never stack 3+ dark sections in a row. The brand is the type and accent treatment, not the background fill.

Hero · Dark

Full-bleed photo + dark overlay + gold credentials strip. Opens the page with brand presence.

Middle · Light

Cream + white alternating sections. Photos breathe. Gold accents on eyebrows + CTAs only.

Closing · Dark

Dark band (or parallax over landscape). Gold-outlined CTAs. Footer continues the dark.

Universal Rules

Apply To Every Page.

Nav

Logo links to home. "Home" never appears as a nav item. Dropdowns: Properties, Buy, Sell, Communities, About. Contact = right-side CTA button.

Footer

Identical 4-column dark footer on every page. EHO + REALTOR® + Idaho RE License + IMLS + AgenticPress credit + state compliance text.

Home Page External Links

ZERO external links on home page body. Footer state-compliance link is the only exception. Inner pages get Resources Strip.

No Stale Stats

Banned: median price, population, "125+ reviews," DOM, list-to-sale. Evergreen only: designations, drive times, founding years, identity nicknames.

Em Dashes

Banned everywhere: body copy, HTML comments, JS strings, alt text. Replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot (·).

Images

Real photography only. Phase 7 curation human-in-the-loop. No auto-inject top Unsplash result. Corner-badge placeholders until real photos curated.

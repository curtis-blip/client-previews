A Vacant House

A new job, a next house under contract, a date that cannot slide. If a listing's timeline does not fit yours, cash may.

Questions

Selling Your House Fast In Colorado Springs, Answered

Who is actually buying my house?

Colorado Guys Buy Homes, the real estate investment business run by Jose Medina. Jose is a licensed Colorado real estate broker and the founder of the Vantegic Home Team at HomeSmart.

How fast can a cash sale close?

As fast as the title work allows, or later if you need more time. There is no buyer's loan to wait on, which removes one of the biggest sources of delay. Tell Jose your date on the first call.

Do I have to fix or clean anything first?

No. Jose buys houses as-is, so there are no repairs to make and nothing to stage. Take what you want and tell him what you plan to leave behind.

Will a cash offer be lower than listing?

Usually, yes. A cash buyer pays for speed and certainty and takes on the repairs and the risk of resale. If your house is in good shape and you have time, a listing will often net you more. Get your home value so you have a number to compare.

Do I pay a commission?

No agent commission on a direct sale to Colorado Guys Buy Homes, because no agent is involved on your side. Your contract and closing statement will show every cost before you sign.

Is Jose my agent in a cash sale?

No. In a direct cash sale the buyer is on the other side of the deal from you and does not represent you. You are welcome to have your own attorney or real estate agent review any contract before you sign.

Can I still list if I do not take the offer?

Yes. Nothing commits you until you sign a contract. If a listing suits you better, the Seller's Guide walks through how the Vantegic Home Team runs one.

Which areas does Jose buy in?

Colorado Springs and the surrounding communities, including Briargate, Broadmoor, Northgate, the Powers Corridor, Monument, Fountain, Peyton and Black Forest. If your house is nearby but not on that list, ask.

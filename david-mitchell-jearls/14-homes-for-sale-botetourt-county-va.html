About Botetourt County

What it is like to live in Botetourt County

Botetourt County was created in 1769 and named for Governor Lord Botetourt. Fincastle has been the county seat since 1772, and the county counted 33,596 people in the 2020 Census.

The county is mostly open ground and small communities: the town of Fincastle, the James River town of Buchanan, and the town of Troutville along US 11. Daleville and Troutville each have their own page on this site, so this one covers the rest of the county.

Mitch renovated and resold houses before he sold them for other people, and he looks at county homes the same way. Many sit on acreage, so ask early about the well, the septic system and the road the driveway meets.

Highlights

What sets Botetourt County apart

  • Fincastle Historic District

    Founded in 1772 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1969, with a courthouse built in 1845 to 1848 and rebuilt after a fire in 1971. Virginia DHR

  • Blue Ridge Parkway at the James River

    The Parkway's James River area sits at milepost 63.7, with a visitor center on the canal history and short Canal Lock Trails. National Park Service

  • Parks and Recreation

    The county runs youth sports, adult basketball and an annual outdoor expo called BOCO WILD. Botetourt County

  • The I-81 curve near Arcadia

    The county announced a $270 million investment recommendation for the I-81 S-curve between mile markers 167 and 169. Botetourt County

Mitch's take

In Botetourt County the lot matters as much as the house. Ask about the well, the septic system and who maintains the road before you fall for a view, and have an older farmhouse's roof, wiring and foundation looked at before an offer.

Questions

Questions buyers ask about Botetourt County

Where is the county seat?+

Fincastle. It was established as the seat in 1772, and the courthouse there was built in 1845 to 1848 and rebuilt after a fire in 1971.

Which school division serves a Botetourt County address?+

Botetourt County Public Schools serves the county. Confirm the campus for any address with the division before you buy.

Do Daleville and Troutville have their own pages?+

Yes. Daleville is a community and Troutville is an incorporated town, and each has a page on this site. Mitch can search the whole county for you.

How do I look up what a home pays in real estate tax?+

The county publishes a Real Estate Taxes page on its site. Mitch can pull the assessment for any address you are looking at.

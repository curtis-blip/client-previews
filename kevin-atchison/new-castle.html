Living in New Castle, CO

About twelve minutes from Glenwood Springs at a fraction of the price, which is the whole argument for it. Lakota Canyon sits above the town and the older grid sits along the river.

Meet Kevin

00 Orientation

New Castle is the Glenwood commute without the Glenwood price

Twelve minutes separates New Castle from Glenwood Springs, and a great deal of money separates what the same house costs in each. That gap is the entire reason this town exists as a market, and it has held through every cycle I have sold in. The town splits cleanly: the original grid down by the Colorado, where the trees are mature and the houses are older, and the bench above it where most of the newer building happened. Neither is better. They suit different people, and I would rather show you both than assume.

01

The old grid by the river

The original town along the Colorado. Older houses, mature trees, short walks, and the part of New Castle that feels like it has been here a while, because it has.

02

Up in Castle Valley and Lakota

The bench above town, where most of the newer building happened. Newer housing stock, a golf course at the top of Lakota Canyon, and views back down the valley. It is a climb off the highway, so drive it once in weather before you decide the view is worth the grade.

01 Before you offer

What I would check for you

New Castle is the shortest commute to Glenwood at a real discount, which is the whole argument for it. Here is what I check before you offer.

Which side of town you are on

The old grid and the bench are two different markets with different ages of construction, different utilities and different winter access.

The association paperwork

Several of the newer developments have property owners associations. I get you the dues and the governing documents while you can still act on them.

Water and sewer

Town service in most of New Castle, but not universally at the edges. Worth confirming rather than assuming from the address.

What the commute is actually like

Twelve minutes to Glenwood in good conditions. Drive it at the hour you would really be driving it before you decide the trade is worth it.

Mineral rights

Often severed in this part of Garfield County. Check it in the title work rather than at closing.

Wildfire insurance

Anything up against the hillside should be priced for insurance while you are still under contract. On some properties it is now the deciding number.

Getting around

I-70 at the edge of town. This is the closest town to Glenwood Springs that still prices like the lower valley, and that gap is the reason people move here.

Outdoors

Elk Creek runs down through town to the Colorado, and the White River National Forest starts a short way up the drainage behind it.

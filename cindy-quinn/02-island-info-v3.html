About Anna Maria Island

Anna Maria Island is a seven-mile barrier island off Florida's Gulf coast, just west of Bradenton, made up of three distinct municipalities, each with its own zoning, rental rules, and character.

The City of Anna Maria sits on the quiet northern end with Pine Avenue's shops and restaurants. Holmes Beach runs through the middle, home to Key Royale and a mix of single-family homes, canals, and condos. Bradenton Beach caps the southern tip with its historic pier and walkable Bridge Street.

Whether you're moving full-time, buying a second home, or exploring investment property, knowing the character of each town is where a good decision starts.

Where to Shop Near Anna Maria Island

Boutiques, galleries, and a surf shop that's been operating on Gulf Drive since 1963. From Lilly Pulitzer's signature store on Pine Avenue to Shiny Fish's hand-picked treasures, the island has more shopping character than its size suggests.

For a bigger shopping day, St. Armands Circle and the Prime Outlets in Ellenton are both within a short drive.

Schools for Anna Maria Island Families

Anna Maria Island itself is small enough that its public schools sit on the mainland in Bradenton. Students from the island attend schools in the School District of Manatee County, with short commutes for elementary, middle, and high school.

The Anna Maria Island Community Center on Magnolia Avenue serves as the year-round hub for youth programs, sports leagues, and after-school activities.

Several well-regarded private schools sit within a 20-minute drive of the island, including Saint Stephen's Episcopal School and The Out-of-Door Academy. IMG Academy, the internationally known sports boarding school, is also nearby in Bradenton.

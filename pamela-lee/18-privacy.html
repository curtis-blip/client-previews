Privacy Policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we will (and will not) do with it.

Effective date: [REVIEW DATE] Last updated: [REVIEW DATE]

Pamela Lee Realty ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect on the Pamela Lee Realty website (the "Site"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly

When you contact us, request a home valuation, save a search, sign up for listing alerts, or fill out any form on the Site, we collect the information you provide. That typically includes:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Property address (for home valuations) and search preferences (for saved searches)
  • Approximate budget, timeline, and other notes you choose to share
  • Anything you write in a message or comment field

Information we collect automatically

When you visit the Site, our servers and analytics tools automatically log standard technical data:

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time on page, click paths, referring site
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 4)

Information from third parties

If you submit a saved-search form through our IDX provider (Showcase IDX or IDX Broker), or interact with embedded tools (mortgage calculators, valuation widgets, social plugins), those services may pass us information they collect from you. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and follow up on home searches, valuations, and contact requests
  • Send saved-search alerts, market updates, or newsletters you have opted into
  • Improve the Site, troubleshoot issues, and understand which pages are most useful
  • Comply with legal obligations (record-keeping, fair-housing reporting, tax)
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site (email delivery, analytics, CRM, IDX provider, hosting). They use your information only on our behalf.
  • Trusted referral partners (lenders, inspectors, attorneys, title companies) only when you specifically ask for a referral.
  • Legal compliance when required by law, court order, or to protect rights, property, or safety.
  • Business transfers if Pamela Lee Realty merges, is acquired, or sells assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies for essential functions (keeping you logged into saved searches, remembering preferences) and for analytics (understanding traffic patterns). You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some Site features may not work properly without them. We may use third-party analytics services such as Google Analytics, which set their own cookies under their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Choices

  • Email opt-out: every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us at pamelalee.realtor@gmail.com to be removed from any list.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to any text from us to opt out.
  • Access and correction: you can ask us what information we have about you and request corrections by contacting us at the address below.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal record-keeping requirements (real estate transactions and client communications are typically retained for several years per Georgia law and brokerage best practice).

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee that information transmitted over the internet will always remain confidential. Do not send sensitive financial details (bank account, social security number) through unencrypted email.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Sites

The Site may link to third-party websites (lenders, MLS portals, regulatory sites, social media profiles). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

9. State Privacy Rights

If you are a Georgia resident or live in a state with specific consumer privacy rights, you may have additional rights under state law (such as the right to know, the right to delete, or the right to opt out of certain data uses). Contact us using the information below to exercise any rights available to you.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced on this page.

Buckeye at a glance

Verrado and the White Tank Foothills

Verrado, a master-planned community in the White Tank Mountain foothills, centers on a Main Street district around its Village Green.

South of town, Buckeye Hills Regional Park looks out over the Gila River.

Questions

Buckeye FAQ

What is Verrado?

A master-planned community in Buckeye in the White Tank Mountain foothills, with a Main Street district around its Village Green.

What is Skyline Regional Park?

A City of Buckeye park with miles of trails for hikers, mountain bikers and riders.

Which school districts serve Buckeye?

Buckeye Elementary School District and Buckeye Union High School District, with Litchfield Elementary School District in parts of the city. Confirm the schools for any address with the district.

Which roads serve Buckeye?

Interstate 10, which ADOT has widened through Buckeye, and State Route 85.

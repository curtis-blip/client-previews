Get Your Bearings in Firestone

Firestone began in 1908 as a place to house the families of local coal miners, and it carries the name of Ohio investor Jacob H. Firestone. The last of the town’s five coal mines closed in 1947, and the old rail bed is now the 12 mile Firestone Trail, which strings the parks together from Settler’s Park to Coronado Park.

Firestone’s Parks and the State Park

Pick a stop and the map follows along: Noah’s video from the spot where he has filmed it, and the homes for sale around it.

Map data: U.S. Census Bureau. Choose a numbered pin or a stop above. The dotted loop is an order to see them in, not a road.

Stop 1

Central Park and Aisik’s Meadow

Central Park is at 6670 Sable Avenue. It holds a disc golf course and a dog park, and the town lists them this way:

  • Disc golf course
  • Aisik’s Meadow Dog Park
  • 6670 Sable Avenue

Stop 2

Firestone Regional Sports Complex

The sports complex is at 6850 Tilbury Avenue. The town’s list of what is on the grounds:

  • Baseball and softball fields
  • Picnic shelters
  • Restrooms and trails

Stop 3

Mountain Shadows Bike Park

The bike park sits south of Mountain Shadows Park. The town prints no street address for it, so the pin on the map is approximate. What the town says about it:

  • Opened in September 2023
  • Asphalt and dirt pathways
  • Built for bikers, skateboarders and scooter riders

Stop 4

St. Vrain State Park

Colorado Parks and Wildlife lists St. Vrain State Park in Firestone, at 3785 Weld County Road 24 1/2. It used to be called Barbour Ponds, and the water is the draw:

  • A state park run by Colorado Parks and Wildlife
  • Formerly named Barbour Ponds
  • Several ponds

Before you buy

What to Check on Any Firestone Address

Four things that change from one side of town to the other. Each takes a few minutes to look up and is worth doing before you write an offer.

Five Hidden Costs Before Moving to Northern Colorado

12:37 · Costs

  1. Which county is it in?

    The whole town is in Weld County. A Firestone mailing address can still sit outside the town limits, so look the parcel up on the Weld County Property Portal before you count on town services.

  2. Which schools go with the address?

    About nine in ten Firestone homes are in St. Vrain Valley School District, and the rest are in Weld County School District RE-1. The address decides, and St. Vrain Valley runs a school boundary lookup. Confirm the campus before you write an offer.

  3. Is it in a floodplain?

    Firestone has no floodplain page of its own that we found. FEMA’s Map Service Center shows the effective flood map for any address, so look up each home you are serious about.

  4. Who supplies the water, and is there a special district?

    The town sits inside the Northern Colorado Water Conservancy District, and Central Weld County Water District serves domestic water in the Tri-Town area. Which one serves a given address is not spelled out, so ask the Town of Firestone. It also explains special districts, and the state maps them.

Quick answers

Firestone Questions Buyers Ask

Which county is Firestone in?

Weld County. Confirm a specific parcel on the Weld County Property Portal, because a Firestone mailing address can sit outside town limits.

Which school district serves Firestone?

Most of the town, about 90 percent by Census area, is in St. Vrain Valley School District, and about 10 percent is in Weld County School District RE-1. Use each district’s lookup with the street address.

How did Firestone get its name?

It is named for Ohio investor Jacob H. Firestone. The town was established in 1908 to support local coal miners and their families.

Is there a trail through town?

Yes. The 12 mile Firestone Trail follows the old coal rail bed from Settler’s Park to Coronado Park, and it connects to the St. Vrain Legacy Trail and the Colorado Front Range Trail.

Houston County

Warner Robins

The hub of Middle Georgia and home to Robins Air Force Base, Warner Robins is a diverse, fast-growing city built around military and civilian families alike. Its central location just off Interstate 75 makes commuting simple, whether you are heading to the base, north to Macon, or south to Perry. Families are drawn here for the strong schools, steady job market, and the everyday convenience of shopping, dining, and recreation close to home. As VA loan specialists, the Faircloth Home Team is especially well suited to guide service members and veterans relocating to the area.

Houston County

Kathleen

Just south of Warner Robins, Kathleen offers a quieter, more residential pace while keeping you minutes from the base and the city's amenities. It is a favorite among families thanks to its highly regarded schools and newer neighborhoods with a little more room to breathe. The setting feels suburban and settled, yet you are still an easy drive from everything Houston County has to offer. For buyers who want top-rated schools and calm streets without giving up convenience, Kathleen is hard to beat.

Perry

Perry pairs genuine small-town charm with a calendar that never sits still, anchored by the Georgia National Fairgrounds and Agricenter that draw visitors and events year-round. There is always something going on, from festivals and rodeos to concerts and livestock shows. Add in well-rated schools, a walkable historic feel, and a lineup of great local restaurants, and the appeal is easy to see. Sitting right along Interstate 75, Perry keeps you connected to the rest of Middle Georgia while holding onto its welcoming, community-first character.

Houston County

Bonaire

Bonaire is a sought-after Houston County community known for its quiet streets, custom-built homes, and proximity to Robins Air Force Base. Golfers appreciate the nearby courses, and families are drawn to the strong local schools and the relaxed, residential feel. It strikes a comfortable balance, offering newer construction and a peaceful setting while remaining a short drive from Warner Robins shopping and the base. For buyers who want a little more polish and privacy without leaving the heart of the county, Bonaire is a standout.

Houston County

Centerville

Centerville is a tidy, convenient Houston County city known for gated neighborhoods like Eagle Springs, where residents enjoy a community pool and clubhouse. Shopping and everyday essentials are close at hand, and quick access to Interstate 75 makes commuting throughout Middle Georgia simple. The amenity-rich neighborhoods and central location make it a comfortable fit for families and professionals who want a low-maintenance lifestyle. With its blend of community perks and easy access, Centerville offers an appealing balance of comfort and connection.

Peach County

Byron

Just over the line in Peach County, Byron offers small-town quiet and a noticeably affordable cost of living while keeping you close to Interstate 75. It is an easy choice for buyers who want a calmer setting yet still need a quick commute to Warner Robins or Macon. The pace is relaxed and the community is friendly, with the conveniences of the interstate corridor only minutes away. For first-time buyers and anyone stretching their budget further, Byron delivers real value in a welcoming setting.

Bibb County

Macon

Macon brings downtown energy and genuine city life to Middle Georgia, famous for its music heritage and the beloved Cherry Blossom Festival each spring. Residents enjoy a lively calendar of festivals, a deep restaurant and nightlife scene, and a cost of living that stretches your dollar further than most cities its size. The historic architecture and walkable districts give it real character, while job and entertainment options keep things interesting. For buyers who want culture, dining, and a true downtown within reach, Macon is the cultural anchor of the region.

Houston County

Elko

Elko is a rural pocket on the outskirts of Perry, prized by buyers looking for farms, acreage, and room to spread out. If your idea of home includes open land, a slower rhythm, and real distance between neighbors, this is the kind of place that delivers. You trade the bustle of the city for wide skies and quiet, while staying a manageable drive from Perry and Interstate 75. For those drawn to country living and the freedom that comes with acreage, Elko is worth a close look.

Pulaski County

Hawkinsville

Hawkinsville is a small, quaint city built around a classic town square, with a rural character all its own. It is well known for its harness-racing heritage and horse-training tradition, and a golf course adds to the easygoing pace of life. The setting is genuinely small-town and unhurried, ideal for buyers who want peace, space, and a tight-knit community feel. For anyone seeking an authentic rural Georgia lifestyle a short drive south of the Warner Robins area, Hawkinsville offers exactly that.

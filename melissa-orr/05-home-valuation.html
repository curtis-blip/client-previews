Unlocking Your Home's Value

What is your home worth today

Get a starting number in seconds. Then we will tell you what it is actually worth in this market, and what would move that number before you list.

Step One

Start with the instant number

Enter your address and you will get an automated estimate built from public records and recent sales nearby. It takes about ten seconds and it is genuinely useful as a starting point.

Then read the next section, because the automated number is not the number.

IDX_ZONE: the Showcase IDX valuation widget replaces this form in WordPress.

Step Two

Why the automated number is usually wrong

An algorithm sees square footage, bedroom count and the last sale price. It does not see any of this.

Condition

Two identical floorplans on the same street can be forty thousand dollars apart on roof, HVAC and kitchen alone.

Lot and position

Corner, cul-de-sac, backing a road, backing a field. Buyers price these very differently and models mostly do not.

Insurance and flood

On the coast, windstorm and flood exposure change what a buyer can afford monthly, which changes what they will offer.

Documented repairs

An engineer-signed foundation repair with a transferable warranty is worth real money. An undocumented one costs you money.

Land and outbuildings

Acreage, shops, barns and water rights are priced by a different logic entirely, and automated models handle them badly.

Right now, not last quarter

Public data lags. We know what went under contract last week and at what price, because we were in those negotiations.

Step Three

Ask for the real one

We will walk your house, pull true comparables, and give you three numbers: what it would list at today, what it would likely close at, and what it could be worth if you did the two or three things worth doing first.

No cost, no obligation, and no follow-up campaign if you tell us you are not ready.

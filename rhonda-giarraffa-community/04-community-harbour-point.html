ABOUT HARBOUR POINT

What it is like to live here

On Lake Lanier, buyers weigh the water before the floor plan: how deep it is at the slip, how the boat is kept, what the view looks like in winter and how the lot falls to the shore.

Harbour Point answers those questions with one of the lake's bigger community marinas, about 250 covered slips by the count in recent listings, and a yacht club at the center of the neighborhood. Homes on the market here range from estate homes to open homesites.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE BUYING

Two ways to own on Lake Lanier

No two lake properties are the same. The first question is how you reach the water.

In a community like Harbour Point

A neighborhood with a shared marina. Your boat lives in a slip, and the community's rules decide how slips are owned, rented or assigned.

  • We confirm in writing which slip, if any, comes with the home.
  • We read the marina rules, the dues and any wait list with you.
  • We find out what the yacht club costs on top of the HOA dues.

A lakefront home of its own

A home on its own lot, often with a private dock on Corps shoreline. The dock permit ends at the sale, so the new owner applies to the Corps in their own name.

  • We check the permit, the dock's condition and the shoreline rules.
  • We look at depth at the dock against the lake's lows.
  • We walk the lot: the slope to the water matters every day.

QUESTIONS

Questions buyers ask about Harbour Point

Does a boat slip come with every Harbour Point home?

No. Slips are in the community marina, and whether one conveys differs from home to home. We confirm in writing which slip, if any, comes with the house, and what the marina's rules and fees are.

Does a dock permit transfer when I buy a lake home?

Not on Lake Lanier. A private dock's Corps permit ends when the home sells, and the new owner applies in their own name.

How deep is the water at a Harbour Point slip?

It depends on the slip and the lake level, which the Corps manages and posts daily. We check depth at the slip against the lake's recent lows before you commit.

What do the Harbour Point dues cover?

It changes with the community's budget. We pull the current HOA and yacht club costs, and what each includes, before you make an offer.

Get Your Bearings in Fort Collins

Fort Collins began in 1862 as Camp Collins, a military post on the Cache la Poudre River. The Agricultural Colony platted the town in 1872, it was incorporated in 1873, and Colorado Agricultural College, now Colorado State University, opened there in 1879. Today the whole city sits in Larimer County.

Stop 1

Old Town and the Museum of Discovery

Start where downtown lives. Old Town is a local historic district, and the Fort Collins Museum of Discovery is on Mason Court, at Cherry and Mason Streets. A few facts from the city and the museum:

  • Old Town designated a local historic district in 1979
  • A somewhat larger Old Town district entered in the National Register of Historic Places in 1978
  • The museum opened in 2012, built on the city’s museum (1941) and the Discovery Science Center (1989)
  • Parking at the museum is free

Stop 2

City Park

City Park is in northwest Fort Collins at 1500 W. Mulberry Street. It has been a community hub for more than 100 years, and the city holds its Independence Day Celebration there. What the city lists:

  • A public pool and a nine-hole golf course
  • The Recreation Department’s pottery studio
  • Sheldon Lake, a fishing spot

Stop 3

Lee Martinez Park

Lee Martinez Park is at 600 N. Sherwood Street, steps from downtown. From the city’s page:

  • A direct connection to the Poudre River Trail
  • The Farm at Lee Martinez Park, a working farm owned and run by the city, on the west side of the park

Before you buy

What to Check on Any Address in Town

Four things that change from one side of town to the other. Each takes a few minutes to look up and is worth doing before you write an offer.

Five Hidden Costs Before Moving to Northern Colorado

12:37 · Costs

  1. Which county is it in?

    All of Fort Collins is in Larimer County. Land outside the city limits can still carry a Fort Collins mailing address and fall under county rules, so look the parcel up in the Larimer County property search.

  2. Which schools go with the address?

    Two districts serve the city: Poudre School District, about 91 percent of it by area, and Thompson School District, about 9 percent. Poudre’s neighborhood school page leads to a tool that works from an address, and Thompson points to its own School Locator. Confirm the campus with the district before you write an offer.

  3. Is it in the floodplain?

    The City of Fort Collins asks you to look at both the FEMA floodplain map and the City’s own floodplain maps, and to call Floodplain Administration at 970-416-2632 about a specific property. Outside the city limits, the county’s engineering office handles flood questions.

  4. Who provides the water?

    Fort Collins Utilities is one of several water providers in the city, and the Fort Collins-Loveland Water District serves part of the area. Confirm the provider with the address.

Quick answers

What Buyers Ask First

What county is Fort Collins in?

All of it is in Larimer County. A property just outside the city limits can still have a Fort Collins mailing address and fall under county rules, so the county’s property search is the way to confirm.

Which school district serves Fort Collins?

Two do. Poudre School District covers about 91 percent of the city by area and Thompson School District about 9 percent. The district’s tool for the address settles it.

Is there a historic district in Fort Collins?

Yes. The Old Town Historic District was designated locally in 1979, and since 1968 the city has recognized hundreds of landmarks and several landmark districts. Owners in a designated landmark or district go through design review for exterior changes.

Who supplies water in Fort Collins?

Fort Collins Utilities is one provider among several, and the Fort Collins-Loveland Water District serves some addresses, so the provider depends on the address.

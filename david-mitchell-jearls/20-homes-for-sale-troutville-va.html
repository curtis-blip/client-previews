About Troutville

What it is like to live in Troutville

Troutville is a small incorporated town in Botetourt County, with 458 people in the 2020 Census. The town was established in 1956, and its name goes back to 1881, when a railroad station was built and the place became Troutsville for the Trout family.

US 11, the Lee Highway, runs through town, and the Town Office is on it. Interstate 81 is close by. The Town Park has a quarter-mile walking path and a memorial garden, and Troutville is an Appalachian Trail Community.

Lots here can be small town lots or larger county-style parcels just outside the limits, so ask which one a house sits on. Mitch will confirm town limits, water and sewer, and any zoning questions before you write an offer.

Highlights

What sets Troutville apart

  • Troutville Town Park

    A quarter-mile walking path, picnic space and room for children to play, with a memorial garden. Town of Troutville

  • Blue Star Memorial garden

    The garden holds a Blue Star Memorial Highway marker and hosts the town's Veterans Day service each year. Town of Troutville

  • How the town got its name

    A railroad station built in 1881 led to the name Troutsville, for the Trout family. Town of Troutville

  • Troutville Town Hall

    The hall seats about 100 people and the town rents it out for $180. Town of Troutville

Questions

Questions buyers ask about Troutville

Is Troutville a town or part of the county?+

Both. Troutville is an incorporated town, established in 1956, and it sits inside Botetourt County. Some addresses nearby are in the county, not the town, so confirm the locality for any home you like.

Which school division serves a Troutville address?+

Botetourt County Public Schools serves the town. Confirm the campus for any address with the division before you buy.

Does the town have water bills?+

Yes, the town's site offers electronic payment of water bills. Ask whether a house is on town water and what serves it for sewer.

Where is the Town Office?+

At 5419 Lee Highway, the road that runs through town. The town's site lists zoning rules and the Town Hall rental.

Buyer's Guide

Buying in the South Loop

Whether it's a first South Loop condo, an investment property, or a single-family home, you deserve a broker who treats the search like the major decision it is. Here is how Tom helps you buy with clarity and confidence.

Why buy with Tom

Confidence in every decision

Buying real estate can feel stressful and confusing. Tom's job is to make it simple: detailed analysis of the home, the market, and the competing inventory, so you always understand exactly what you are deciding and why.

It is the same disciplined approach he honed over a long finance career, now focused entirely on getting you the right home on the right terms.

The process

Your path to the keys

Step 01

Get to know your goals

We start with a real conversation about what you want, your timeline, and your budget, before we ever look at a single listing.

Step 02

Get pre-approved

Tom connects you with trusted Chicago lenders so you shop with a clear budget and a competitive edge when you find the one.

Step 03

Search the right way

We focus the search on the neighborhoods and buildings that fit your life, with live MLS data and Tom's on-the-ground South Loop knowledge.

Step 04

Tour with a critical eye

Tom points out what matters: layout, light, assessments, building health, and resale, not just the staging.

Step 05

Make a smart offer

Backed by real analysis of comparable sales and competing inventory, we craft an offer that wins without overpaying.

Step 06

Inspect and verify

We line up inspection and review the condo documents and assessments closely, so there are no surprises after closing.

Step 07

Appraisal and financing

Tom coordinates with your lender through appraisal and underwriting, keeping the timeline on track and on terms.

Step 08

Close and get the keys

We review every figure before the table, handle the details, and hand you the keys to your new Chicago home.

Meet Stephanie

She Lives Where She Works

Stephanie Sills has called the Coastside home since 2006. She and her husband, Dan, raised their twin daughters in Half Moon Bay, and today they live in Moss Beach. When she tells you what a street, a school or a commute is like, she is speaking from experience.

Before real estate, Stephanie spent 20 years in internet software, data management and consulting, leading client relationship and customer success teams. She brings that discipline to pricing, marketing and negotiation, and she was a top producer from her first year.

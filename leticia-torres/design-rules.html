Typography Hard Rules
  • Headings are Outfit in uppercase at weight 500 (the stats heading is weight 200). Body is Inter. Load both from Google Fonts on every page.
  • H1: caps at 38px minimum, 64px maximum, and 32px at 544px and under. H2 caps at 28px and H3 at 22px at 544px and under. Body drops to 16px at 760px and under.
  • Every eyebrow carries a short green rule under it (36px by 2px, 12px gap): green_deep text on light, green_light on navy, white over photos.
  • Home H1 two lines ideally at desktop; every hero is centred, home included.
  • Never substitute a system font as the primary face. System fonts only as the fallback in the stack.
  • Never introduce a third font family. Heading and body only.
  • No italic-serif accent spans inside H1s ("real estate, reimagined" is banned).
  • No em dashes anywhere in copy: replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.

Color Hard Rules
  • Every colour is a token from the client stylesheet (mockups/styles.css :root). Take values from there, never from memory.
  • Body text color contrast must meet WCAG AA against the background (this palette is verified).

  • Primary button: square, 1px green_deep outline, green_deep caps text, fills green on hover. Photo and navy grounds use a white outline; the palm ground uses an ink outline.

Button Hard Rules
  • Border-radius: 0. Square buttons only.
  • Never use 3+ buttons in a single hero. One primary, optionally one secondary, never three.
  • Transition duration 400ms with easing cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .1, 1) on hover.
  • Always include `:focus-visible` indicator (browser default OK, or styled with accent outline).

Motion

Slow, subtle motion. The stylesheet uses one easing, --ease. Never bouncy, never fast.

Motion Hard Rules
  • No bounce easing. No springs. No spinning loaders inside content.
  • Photo hover zoom maxes at 1.03 (3%). The stylesheet runs the town tile and path card image zoom at 900ms.
  • Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: disable photo-zoom and section fade-in when set.

Community Pages

The six town pages (Carlsbad, Oceanside, San Marcos, Vista, Fallbrook, Escondido) share one renderer and one layout, ported as drawn from the approved Claude Design board. Carlsbad is the reference.

Shape
One long page. A sticky section bar sits under the sticky site header (together they stay under 20 percent of the screen; 120px plus 48px at 1440). A small script highlights the section being read, and the bar links scroll to each section.
Section order
Quick facts, neighborhoods by quarter of the town, living here, shopping and dining, schools, the commute, before you buy, ask Leticia, helpful links, nearby towns.
Left out on purpose
No IDX anywhere on the site: no listings, no search, no Home Search page. No live market data (no median prices, days on market, HOA dues or drive minutes). Leticia's Picks stay blank until she supplies her own words.
Heroes and eyebrows
Every hero is centred. The H1 runs two lines at desktop. Every eyebrow has the short green rule under it.
Heading breaks
Theme headings.pair_max is 17: the mockups join the word pairs of up to 17 letters with a non-breaking space, and always the last two words of every heading (h1 to h4). Carry this into the build so no heading ends on a single word. 17 is deliberate: "Property Management" (19 letters) clipped at 375px.
Neighborhoods
Grouped by quarter of the town. No ZIP codes are printed (town-facts rule). Source links are checked; some city sites refuse scripts and are marked in each town's JSON.

Voice and Content Reminders

Content writers and designers both need to remember these voice rules. Even if it's the writer's job, a designer who notices an em dash or "your trusted partner" should flag it before publish.

Copy Hard Rules
  • Never use em dashes (the long dash or a double hyphen): replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.
  • Never use generic AI phrases: "dream home", "seamless experience", "every step of the way", "your trusted partner", "in today's market", "with confidence".
  • Never use generic city descriptors: "thriving", "vibrant", "nestled", "perfect blend", "world-class", "rich history", "something for everyone".
  • Never use parallel triplets ("Clear, calm, confident"), hyphenated compound metaphors ("courtroom-trained eye"), or conditional buildups ("Whether you're A, B, or C...").
  • Every page H1 references at least ONE specific item from the client's brief (years, credentials, niche, personal story).

Full doctrine: references/copy-voice.md in the Optimus Prime plugin.

Anti-Patterns Summary

The shortlist of "do not do this." Every rule below was learned the hard way on past builds.

Never Do These
  • Em dashes anywhere in copy
  • Emojis as icons (inline SVG only)
  • Rounded corners on cards, buttons, images
  • Carousel sliders in heroes
  • Italic-serif "register lift" spans inside H1
  • 3+ CTAs in a hero (1 default, 2 max)
  • Split heroes anywhere except a clean home-page exception
  • Two dark-background sections in a row
  • Aggressive form or large CTA inside the hero (exception: Home Valuation page widget)
  • Gradient placeholders past first draft
  • Raw shortcode embeds without styled container
  • Flat text-only service boxes (must have SVG icons)
  • Zigzag timeline layouts for process steps (use alternating image+text rows)
  • "Recently Sold Nearby" on Home Valuation page
  • 4-step process blocks shipped as production (always default to 8)
  • FAQ mode: faq on About page (use mode: expertise: Areas of Expertise)
  • Communities home-page treatment on inner pages (V2 home-only)
  • About teaser block on /about (always use full Agent Bio)

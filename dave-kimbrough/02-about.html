About Dave

About Dave Kimbrough

A Grand Junction broker since 2003, managing partner of The Agency Grand Junction, and the agent behind The Kimbrough Team.

His story

It started with selling his own house

Before real estate, Dave built his foundation in pharmaceutical sales, where he learned a disciplined approach to strategy, negotiation and client advocacy. When he was selling his own home, his agent encouraged him to go into the business. He was licensed in Colorado in 2003, and in his words, "within two or three years, my business had kind of boomed."

He built The Kimbrough Team through more than twenty years with RE/MAX. In February 2026 he and his sister, Jan Kimbrough Miller, opened The Agency Grand Junction on Main Street as its managing partners, bringing a global brand's marketing to an office run by people who have worked this valley for two decades.

He is relentlessly passionate, borderline obsessed, about buying and selling real estate. He studies market trends, technology and how buyers shop, so his clients get a smarter, faster and more profitable result.

How he works

The plan comes before the sign goes up

Most of the work on a sale happens before anyone sees the listing. Dave walks the house with the owners and says plainly which repairs will pay for themselves and which will not.

Then comes the plan: how to prepare the home, what to ask for it, and how to present it, with professional photography, drone, video and a virtual tour from the team's own marketing staff. Once an offer is in, he runs the contract, the inspection, the appraisal and every deadline, and he tells you where things stand before you have to ask.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Boschma Farms in Ridgefield WA?

It is a large former dairy farm parcel being developed for commercial, employment and education uses. Located in Ridgefield's expansion corridor along I-5, the property is positioned to become a dynamic hub for job creation and regional growth.

Who owns the Boschma Farms property?

The land was donated and sold by the Boschma family and is now offered for development via Jim West Commercial Real Estate.

What size is the Boschma Farms site and where is it located?

The initial parcel is 34.84 acres in the northern portfolio, plus additional acreage in other parcels in Ridgefield, WA. Sizes range from approximately 3 to 20+ acres depending on the parcel portfolio.

How can I inquire about acquiring a parcel in Boschma Farms?

Contact Jim West Commercial Real Estate to discuss availability, parcel sizes, zoning and pricing. You can use the inquiry form on this page, call (360) 223-3048, or email jim@jimwestcommercialre.com.

What zoning applies to Boschma Farms land?

It is zoned Commercial Regional Business (CRB) and Employment (E) districts designed for job-creating uses. The zoning prohibits retail enterprises for the general public, preserving the land for employment and industrial uses.

What are typical parcel sizes at Boschma Farms?

Sizes range from approximately 3 to 20+ acres depending on the parcel portfolio (e.g. 12 acres, 20 acres, 4.94 acres). Parcels are offered in portfolios such as "North Parcels" and "East/South Parcels" with detailed acreages and zoning identified.

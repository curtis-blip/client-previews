Questions

Selling Your House Fast In Colorado Springs, Answered

Who is actually buying my house?

Colorado Guys Buy Homes, the real estate investment business run by Jose Medina. Jose is a licensed Colorado real estate broker and the founder of the Vantegic Home Team at HomeSmart.

What happens after I fill out the form?

The form on this page is our intake form, so we already have the basics about the house. We reach out right away by call, voicemail, text and email until we connect, then set up a time to talk. Nothing is decided on that first call.

How do you decide what to offer?

We research the property, run the numbers and find out what matters most to you. On the offer call we walk you through how we got to our number and answer every question before you decide anything.

How fast can a cash sale close?

As fast as the title work allows, or later if you need more time. There is no buyer's loan to wait on, which removes one of the biggest sources of delay. Tell us your date on the first call.

Do I have to fix or clean anything first?

No. We buy houses as-is, so there are no repairs to make and nothing to stage. Take what you want and tell us what you plan to leave behind.

Will a cash offer be lower than listing?

Usually, yes. A cash buyer pays for speed and certainty and takes on the repairs and the risk of resale. If your house is in good shape and you have time, a listing will often net you more, and we will tell you so. Get your home value so you have a number to compare.

Do I pay a commission?

No agent commission on a direct sale to Colorado Guys Buy Homes, because no agent is involved on your side. Your contract and closing statement will show every cost before you sign.

Is Jose my agent in a cash sale?

No. In a direct cash sale the buyer is on the other side of the deal from you and does not represent you. You are welcome to have your own attorney or real estate agent review any contract before you sign.

What if I am not ready to decide?

Then do not decide. Nothing commits you until you sign a contract. If the timing is not right, we check in over the following weeks and months with no pressure. If a listing suits you better, the Seller's Guide walks through how the Vantegic Home Team runs one.

Which areas do you buy in?

Colorado Springs and the surrounding communities, including Briargate, Broadmoor, Northgate, the Powers Corridor, Monument, Fountain, Peyton and Black Forest. If your house is nearby but not on that list, ask.

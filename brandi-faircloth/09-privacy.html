Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Faircloth Home Team, brokered by Robins Realty Group, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website. By using the site you agree to the practices described below. This policy is provided for general informational purposes and is not legal advice.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to share with us and limited information that is gathered automatically as you browse.

  • Contact forms. When you submit a contact, valuation, or showing request, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message or property preferences you include.
  • Property and IDX search. When you search listings through our IDX home search tool, we may receive the search criteria you enter, the properties you view or save, and saved-search or alert preferences you create.
  • Automatic data. Like most websites, we and our service providers may collect technical details such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and how you navigate the site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate the site and to serve you as a buyer or seller. Typical uses include:

  • Responding to your inquiries and following up about properties, valuations, and appointments.
  • Delivering saved searches, listing alerts, and other real estate services you request.
  • Improving the performance, content, and usability of the website.
  • Meeting our professional, brokerage, and legal obligations.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep the site functioning, and understand how visitors use our pages. We may use analytics tools that report aggregated usage data. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings, though some site features may not work as intended if you do.

Third Parties

We work with trusted providers to deliver this website and our services. We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • Our IDX provider, which powers the home search and listing data on this site.
  • Our brokerage, Robins Realty Group, and members of the Faircloth Home Team who assist with your request.
  • Service providers such as hosting, email, customer relationship, and analytics platforms that support our operations.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law or to protect our rights.

Your Choices

You control how you interact with us. You may decline to provide information, although some requests cannot be completed without it. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link in those messages, adjust your browser cookie settings, or contact us to ask about the information we hold and to request a correction or deletion where applicable.

Why Some Anthem Highlands Homes Are Selling, and Others Aren't

Anthem Highlands market report · October 2025

Steve CalleyREALTOR®, The Calley Group of Your Castle Real Estate

Let's be honest: the headlines aren't telling the whole story. You've heard people say the market is slowing down, or that buyers have vanished. Here in Anthem Highlands, that's not the case.

The market isn't crashing. It's correcting. The fast, frenzied pace of 2021 is gone, and in its place is something more strategic, more balanced and more revealing. Right now, the difference between listed and sold has never been clearer.

October 2025: what really happened

This fall, 19 market events were recorded in Anthem Highlands.

4Homes closed
4Price reductions
6Went pending
5Listings withdrawn

Well-priced, well-presented homes are still selling. Sellers are adjusting to buyers who are choosier, and some are pressing pause until spring. This isn't a downturn. It's a reset, and presentation, pricing and patience matter again.

Inside the sales numbers

Among the homes that closed in October:

  • Average sale price: $1,512,000
  • Price range: $1,075,000 to $2,322,866
  • Average finished area: about 3,073 square feet
  • Typical home: 4 to 5 bedrooms, 4 to 5 baths, 3-car garage

Homes that were updated, move-in ready and priced realistically found buyers. Homes that weren't lingered, or left the market entirely.

What's driving the shift

  • Mortgage rates: still in the mid-6 percent range, trimming affordability and pace.
  • Inventory: more homes means more options and slower decisions.
  • The season: fall always cools activity as buyers focus on the holidays and school schedules.

What it means for you

This isn't 2021 anymore, and that's a good thing. The chaos has cleared, which leaves room for strategy. If you price your home precisely, prepare it well and market it with intention, it will still sell, and sell well. If you rely on outdated expectations, you risk becoming another withdrawn listing.

If you're even thinking about selling in the next 6 to 12 months, now is the time for an Anthem Highlands market analysis built on today's conditions, not an online guess. I track this neighborhood every day. Let's talk about where your home stands.

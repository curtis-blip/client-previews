About Steve

Meet Steve Calley

Steve grew up in Arvada and now sells homes across Broomfield and the north Denver metro, where he and his wife are raising their four kids.

The Calley Group of Your Castle Real Estate

A Colorado native who does what he says he'll do

Steve is a Colorado native, born and raised in Arvada. He now lives in Broomfield with his wife and their four children.

Steve believes buyers and sellers deserve the best service and experience when they buy or sell a home. It is one of the largest transactions people will be a part of, after all. He knows the Denver metro closely and enjoys helping people reach their real estate goals. As a full-time agent who keeps up with the latest market information, you can trust him to work with integrity and give you recommendations on listings and property values that are accurate and empowering.

He treats everyone he serves with respect and care. That means teaching you the ins and outs of buying and selling real estate, responding quickly to your questions throughout the process, and negotiating fervently on your behalf.

Call Steve if you or someone you know is thinking about buying, selling or investing in real estate in the Denver metro or anywhere in the country; he has a strong network of agents across the United States. His commitment is to provide the highest quality of service, do what he says he is going to do, and maximize the value you receive as a buyer or seller.

Where Steve spends his days

Buying in Broomfield

Steve lives in Broomfield and knows its neighborhoods, its school district lines and which streets back onto open space. Start with the Buyer's Guide or homes for sale in Broomfield.

Selling your home

Staging advice, professional photos and video, and an open house just for your neighbors. The Seller's Guide lays out how he prices and markets a home.

First-time buyers

About Roanoke

What it is like to live in Roanoke

Roanoke is the Roanoke Valley's city: an independent city of about 100,000 people, with its own government and its own school division, separate from Roanoke County around it. Downtown sits at the foot of Mill Mountain, where the Roanoke Star was first lit in 1949.

Around downtown you will find older neighborhoods with porches and mature trees, condos and lofts near the City Market, and quieter streets farther out. Mitch works from the MKB, REALTORS® office on Electric Road, in the 24018 ZIP code.

Many homes here are decades old. Before you write an offer, have the roof, the wiring and the foundation looked at, and check the flood map for any home near the Roanoke River or its creeks.

Highlights

What sets Roanoke apart

  • The Roanoke Star

    Built on Mill Mountain in 1949 and first lit that November, it stands 88.5 feet tall above downtown. Visit Roanoke

  • Roanoke City Market

    Trading since 1882, in a market building from 1922, and open seven days a week all year. Downtown Roanoke

  • Roanoke River Greenway

    A paved greenway across Roanoke, Salem and Roanoke County, with nine trailheads. Roanoke Valley Greenways

  • Virginia Museum of Transportation

    Steam, diesel and electric locomotives in a freight station on Norfolk Avenue, founded in 1963. VMT

  • Mill Mountain Zoo

    A non-profit zoo on Mill Mountain, open seven days a week. Mill Mountain Zoo

Questions

Questions buyers ask about Roanoke

Is Roanoke part of Roanoke County?+

No. Roanoke is an independent city with its own government, taxes and schools. Some homes with a Roanoke mailing address are in Roanoke County, so check the locality for any address you like.

Which school division serves a Roanoke address?+

Roanoke City Public Schools serves addresses inside the city, and Roanoke County Public Schools serves the county around it. Confirm the campus with the division before you buy.

How do I check whether a home is in a flood zone?+

Look the address up on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center and read the seller's disclosure. Ask early on any home near the Roanoke River or its creeks.

How do I look up a home's property assessment?+

The City of Roanoke publishes assessments by address on its real estate valuation pages. Mitch can pull one for any home you are looking at.

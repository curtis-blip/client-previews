Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on buying, selling, and renting in the South Loop and across Chicago. If you don't see your question here, just ask Tom.

Buying

It depends on your loan and your goals. Many buyers put down anywhere from 3 to 20 percent, and some loan programs go lower. Tom connects you with local lenders early so you know your real numbers, including monthly assessments and closing costs, before you ever start touring.

The building matters as much as the unit. Tom helps you read the association's reserves, assessment history, owner-occupancy, and any special assessments or pending litigation. A well-run board with healthy reserves protects both your monthly cost and your resale value down the road.

Well-priced listings move quickly, especially in spring. Tom watches comparable sales and competing inventory closely, so when the right place comes up you can move fast with a strong, well-supported offer instead of guessing or overpaying.

With a conversation, not a listing. Tom walks you through pre-approval, budgeting for assessments and taxes, and what to expect at each step through to closing. He bought his own first South Loop home in 2003 and remembers exactly how that felt, so nothing gets glossed over.

Selling

With analysis, not a guess. Tom studies recent comparable sales, current competing listings, and what is actually under contract in your building or block, then walks you through the reasoning. You get a price grounded in the market and a clear strategy, rather than an automated estimate.

It depends on the property, the price, and the season, so Tom sets expectations from real local data instead of a generic promise. The right preparation and pricing up front are what shorten time on market. Tom will give you a candid read on your specific home and neighborhood.

Professional photography, accurate and compelling listing copy, syndication across the MLS and major search portals, and direct outreach to active buyers and agents. Tom also draws on his South Loop network and keeps you in steady communication so you always know where things stand.

Tom explains exactly how compensation works in plain terms before you list, including what is negotiable and how buyer-side arrangements are handled under current rules. You will see everything in writing, with no surprises. Specific rates are set per listing. [NEEDS_CLIENT]

Renting & landlords

Tom handles the whole placement for owners: pricing the unit, marketing it, showing it, screening applicants, and managing the lease paperwork. As one South Loop owner put it, he took care of everything, found a quality tenant, and handled the documents so they could rest easy.

Carefully and in line with fair-housing law. Tom reviews credit, income and employment, rental history, and references so you can make a confident, well-documented decision. The goal is a reliable, long-term tenant, not just a signed lease.

The same neighborhoods he knows as a resident and broker: the South Loop first, plus Pilsen, Bridgeport, and the greater Chicago market. That local knowledge helps price your unit right and reach the renters most likely to stay.

St. Marlo in four chapters

01 / 04

What the Association Runs

The St Marlo Association, Inc. runs a pool complex with a Junior Olympic pool and a zero-entry leisure pool with a slide, a summer swim team, a racquet club with six hard courts, two Har-Tru clay courts and four pickleball courts, a cabana with a club room for groups of up to 35, a grill arbor, a playground, and a sports field on St Marlo Fairway Drive with a soccer field and a basketball court.

Its website also lists a front gate, a back gate and a visitor entry page for residents.

02 / 04

Golf That Is Not Only for Residents

The 18-hole, par 72 course at St. Marlo Country Club was designed by Denis Griffiths. The club calls itself semi-private: it takes tee times from the public and now also offers golf memberships. It has a clubhouse with a restaurant and a bar and grille, locker rooms and a large practice area.

The club has its own address, 7755 St Marlo Country Club Parkway, and its own website. Ask it about membership and tee times directly.

03 / 04

Reaching St. Marlo From the Parkways

St Marlo Country Club Parkway meets Old Atlanta Road, and the golf club describes itself as just off McGinnis Ferry Road. Laurel Springs and Olde Atlanta Club also have their main parkways on Old Atlanta Road; see Laurel Springs and Olde Atlanta Club.

For the whole county, go to homes for sale in Forsyth County.

04 / 04

A Duluth Address in Forsyth County

St. Marlo uses a Duluth mailing address, but its association places it in the southern part of Forsyth County, and the City of Duluth is in Gwinnett County. Use Forsyth County's pages for property records and taxes, starting with the property tax guide, and Forsyth County Schools for schools.

County records show most homes were built from 1993 to 2011. The Georgia buying guide walks through the rest.

Questions

St. Marlo FAQ

St. Marlo has a Duluth address. Is it in Gwinnett County?

No. The association places St. Marlo in the southern part of Forsyth County, and Duluth is only the mailing address. Check any specific address with Forsyth County before you rely on a Gwinnett page.

Which school district should I check for St. Marlo?

Forsyth County Schools. Start there rather than with a Gwinnett district, and confirm the campus for any address with the district.

Can anyone play the St. Marlo golf course?

The club calls itself semi-private, takes tee times from the public and also offers memberships. Check current policies with the club on its own website.

How do the gates work at St. Marlo?

The association's site lists 24-hour gated access, a front gate, a back gate and a visitor entry page for residents. Ask the association how guests, contractors and deliveries are handled.

Gilpin County real estate and the high country around it

Eight communities between the Front Range foothills and the Continental Divide, ranging from 5,675 feet to 8,510 feet.

These eight towns are not variations on one another. Golden sits at 5,675 feet with a university and a brewery. Central City sits at 8,510 feet with an opera house and a hundred and sixty years of mining claims above it. The same budget buys very different things depending which one you pick.

Click any community for elevation, what actually trades there, and Jerry's own read on the market. Each one links through to the current listings.

8,042 ft · Gilpin County

Black Hawk

The county's economic engine, and the reason Gilpin has a tax base.

8,042 ftElevation
GilpinCounty
35 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

Black Hawk is the county's major economic hub known for its casinos, resorts and gambling industry, offering more modern residential enclaves while still being close to nature.

What actually trades here

Vacant land, mining claims, cabins, manufactured homes, and the occasional single family house on the ridges above the gulch.

Highlights

  • Casino and resort employment base
  • Historic gulch townsite
  • Ridge lots above the town centre
  • Highway 119 access
  • Gilpin County seat services nearby
  • Peak to Peak Highway at the top of town
Most of what I sell in Black Hawk is above the casinos, not in them. People see the gaming district from Highway 119 and assume that is the whole town. The residential ground is up on the ridges and it is quiet.
Jerry Baker

8,510 ft · Gilpin County

Central City

Founded on an 1859 gold strike, and the gulches still hold the claims.

8,510 ftElevation
GilpinCounty
40 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

Central City is the county's historic gold mining town, established in 1859. The town offers a mix of residential properties featuring Victorian architecture, historic landmarks like the Central City Opera House, and smaller scale casinos. Both towns sit about 45 minutes outside Denver.

What actually trades here

Victorian houses in town, gulch acreage above it, and patented mining claims. This is where most of the claim transactions happen.

Highlights

  • Central City Opera House
  • Victorian era housing stock
  • Russell Gulch and the surrounding claims
  • Central City Parkway to I-70
  • National Historic Landmark District
  • Smaller scale casinos
Russell Gulch is where I sold three and a third acres that the owner thought was unsellable because the only way in was an unmaintained jeep road. It went at the number, on the timeline. Central City ground rewards patience.
Jerry Baker

7,526 ft · Clear Creek County

Idaho Springs

Thirty miles up Clear Creek Canyon, with hot springs in the middle of town.

7,526 ftElevation
Clear CreekCounty
30 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

Located in Clear Creek Canyon, upstream from Golden, about 30 miles west of Denver. This former mining town offers a blend of adventure and relaxation, with picturesque Colorado scenery, Loveland ski area nearby and Indian Hot Springs in town.

What actually trades here

Canyon homes, in town Victorians, and hillside lots. Lower elevation than Gilpin, which means an easier winter.

Highlights

  • Indian Hot Springs
  • I-70 corridor access
  • Loveland ski area half an hour west
  • Clear Creek whitewater
  • Mount Blue Sky scenic byway
  • Historic mining district
Idaho Springs is the easiest winter of anywhere I sell. It is a thousand feet lower than Central City and it sits right on I-70, which matters if somebody is commuting to Denver rather than retiring up here.
Jerry Baker

8,228 ft · Boulder County

Nederland

A trading post in 1874, and the gateway to the Indian Peaks.

8,228 ftElevation
BoulderCounty
45 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

In the foothills of southwest Boulder County by Barker Meadow Reservoir, Nederland started as a trading post in 1874 and has become one of the best places in the area for outdoor recreation. Close to Indian Peaks Wilderness, Rocky Mountain National Park and Roosevelt National Forest.

What actually trades here

Forested acreage, cabins, and building lots with genuine Divide views. Boulder County land use rules apply here, not Gilpin's.

Highlights

  • Barker Meadow Reservoir
  • Indian Peaks Wilderness access
  • Eldora ski area
  • Roosevelt National Forest
  • Boulder Canyon to Boulder
  • Peak to Peak Highway
Nederland buyers are usually coming from Boulder rather than Denver, and they are buying the wilderness access. The photograph on this page is a real parcel, and that cleared pad in the middle of the trees is what a building site up here actually looks like.
Jerry Baker

8,422 ft · Gilpin County

Rollinsville

Established 1871, and still a cluster of buildings and a rail line.

8,422 ftElevation
GilpinCounty
50 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

Established in 1871, this historic mining settlement is a small cluster of residences and several businesses on State Highway 119 between Nederland and Black Hawk, on the road up to Rollins Pass at the summit of the Front Range.

What actually trades here

Meadow and forest acreage, a handful of homes, and land running up toward the pass. Small inventory, so listings move when the right buyer appears.

Highlights

  • Rollins Pass road
  • Moffat Tunnel east portal
  • South Boulder Creek
  • Highway 119 between Ned and Black Hawk
  • Roosevelt National Forest
  • Tolland and the rail corridor
Rollinsville has maybe a dozen things for sale in a good year. The aerial on this page is the whole town. If you want ground here you need to be ready when something lists, because there is no second option down the street.
Jerry Baker

8,314 ft · Gilpin County

Pinecliffe

A post office, a creek, a railroad, and national forest on both sides.

8,314 ftElevation
GilpinCounty
55 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

Located on Highway 72 near Golden Gate Canyon, an unincorporated community of a post office and residences. Roosevelt National Forest lies to the north and Arapaho National Forest to the south, with South Boulder Creek and a railroad running through.

What actually trades here

Creek lots, steep forested parcels, and cabins. This is the smallest market of the eight and the most likely to have an access question.

Highlights

  • South Boulder Creek frontage
  • Roosevelt National Forest to the north
  • Arapaho National Forest to the south
  • Highway 72 access
  • Rail corridor through the canyon
  • Fishing on the creek
I sold a small lot on South Boulder Creek that had very limited building capability because of the terrain. It needed one particular buyer who wanted the peace and the fishing more than a house site. That is Pinecliffe in one sentence.
Jerry Baker

5,675 ft · Jefferson County

Golden

Where the plains stop and the Front Range starts.

5,675 ftElevation
JeffersonCounty
20 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

Along the base of the Front Range about 15 miles west of Denver. This former 1859 gold rush town is now home to the Colorado School of Mines and the Coors brewery, with a range of housing and small business. Foothills views, dining, shopping, lodging and outdoor recreation.

What actually trades here

A full residential market, unlike the high country towns. Jerry works the foothills edges and the land above town rather than competing on tract housing.

Highlights

  • Colorado School of Mines
  • Coors brewery
  • Clear Creek through downtown
  • North and South Table Mountain
  • Lookout Mountain
  • Golden Gate Canyon road to Gilpin County
Golden is a real market with plenty of agents, and I am not going to pretend I am the busiest one in it. Where I am useful is the foothills ground on the way up Golden Gate Canyon, which is really Gilpin County property with a Golden address.
Jerry Baker

7,040 ft · Jefferson County

Evergreen

Named for the state tree, and busier than the towns north of it.

7,040 ftElevation
JeffersonCounty
35 minDrive to Denver
MLSLive listings below

What it is

Located in Jefferson County, the community is known for the surrounding evergreen trees, Colorado's state tree. About 30 miles west of Denver, this mountain community hosts music festivals, art festivals, food events and sporting events, blending outdoor adventure with small town character.

What actually trades here

Mountain homes on acreage, and land parcels in the surrounding hills. A larger and more active market than Gilpin County.

Highlights

  • Evergreen Lake
  • Alderfer Three Sisters open space
  • Year round events calendar
  • Highway 74 to I-70
  • Mount Blue Sky access
  • Elk herd on the meadow
Evergreen has more going on than the rest of the list put together, and it prices accordingly. Buyers who look at Evergreen and then look at Gilpin County are usually surprised by what the same money buys forty minutes further up.
Jerry Baker

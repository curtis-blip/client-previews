Before you book flights

Plan a Scouting Trip That Settles It

A weekend here goes fast. Four steps make it count, and three of them you can do from your couch.

Plan It With Noah

  1. Short-list three towns

    Use the map and the comparison videos above. Three towns is about what fits in one weekend without rushing any of them.

  2. Take each town’s tour first

    Every town page has a tour: the stops worth seeing, Noah’s video from each and the homes for sale around it. Start with Longmont.

  3. Talk to Noah before you book

    Tell him where you are coming from, your budget and your timeline. He can suggest which towns to see and in what order.

  4. Drive the stops, then the homes

    See the places you watched in person, then tour homes near the ones you liked.

Quick answers

Questions About Moving to Colorado

Where do I start if I have never been to Colorado?

Start with Noah’s pros and cons video at the top of this page, then use the map to see where the 19 towns sit along the Front Range.

How do I choose between the towns?

Watch the head-to-head videos for the pair you are weighing, then take each town’s tour on its own page. Cut the list to three before you visit.

How far are these towns from Denver and the airport?

It depends on the town. From Longmont’s Main Street, without traffic, Noah covers the drive to Denver, the airport and the mountains in his video “How Far Is Everything in Northern Colorado, Really?”

Can I start working with Noah before I move?

Yes. Call him or send a note from the contact page with where you are coming from and when you hope to move. He will help you plan the scouting trip around the towns that fit.

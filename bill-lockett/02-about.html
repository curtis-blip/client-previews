About Bill

Twenty-five years as a Buena Vista realtor.

Owner and managing broker of Coldwell Banker Collegiate Peaks Realty. Offices in Buena Vista and Salida.

REALTOR® · Owner / Managing Broker · Coldwell Banker Collegiate Peaks Realty

Bill has worked the Collegiate Peaks corridor since 1999. Today he's the owner and managing broker of Coldwell Banker Collegiate Peaks Realty, with offices in Buena Vista and Salida, and consistently the #1 producing agent at the brokerage.

Most of his work comes from returning clients. Some have worked with him through three and four transactions over twenty years. The shorthand is simple: read the contract, answer the phone, show up. Whether the property is a $200,000 cabin off a forest service road or a $1.7M ranch on the Arkansas, the work is the same.

Bill earned his real estate license in 1999 and has since accumulated five advanced designations: A-REO (Accredited REO Agent), e-PRO (Internet Professional), GRI (Graduate REALTOR® Institute), CRS (Certified Residential Specialist), and MRE (Master of Real Estate). He's a past Board President and Director of the local association.

Community service

"Retired SWAT Master Operator for the Sheriff's Department. The patience and preparation that work demanded show up in the real estate work, too."

Bill's life in the Collegiate Peaks runs deeper than the brokerage. He raised his family here, his wife is also in real estate, and he's served the community in roles ranging from board leadership to sheriff's department deployment. The community-rooted angle isn't marketing; it's the operating thesis.

If you're thinking about buying or selling in Buena Vista, Salida, Nathrop, Park County, Westcliffe, or Howard, the conversation starts the same way it has for two and a half decades: a phone call, no script, no rush.

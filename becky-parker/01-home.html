The Promise

Georgia real estate, without the stress. Two things make that real: a calm process, and honest pricing.

A Calm Process

One agent, one phone number, one inbox. You will hear from me directly at every step, from the first showing to the closing table. No handoffs, no junior staff fielding your questions on a Tuesday afternoon.

Honest Pricing

I walk your home before I price it. I pull recent comps in your neighborhood, not your zip. If a number does not match the market, I will say so the first time we meet, not three weeks into a stale listing.

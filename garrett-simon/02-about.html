About Garrett & Jessica

Meet Garrett and Jessica Simon

Locals since the late 1990s, selling Telluride real estate since 2004, and working every sale as a team.

The team

Two locals, one Telluride practice

Garrett leads the market work and the negotiation; Jessica keeps every transaction organized and personal. Most of their clients live somewhere else, and the split is built for that.

Garrett Simon

Broker associate · Telluride Properties

Garrett has called Telluride home since 1998 and has been helping buyers and sellers here since 2004. He started in the valley as a rental manager and a real estate assistant, and decided that working directly with buyers and sellers was the job he wanted.

He sells luxury homes, ski-in/ski-out properties, mountain estates, condos and land throughout Telluride, Mountain Village and the mesas around them. His relationships with local brokers, lenders, appraisers and inspectors are the part of the work that goes well beyond the MLS.

Before the box canyon, he played NCAA Division I soccer at the University of South Alabama. He lives with Jessica, their son and two dogs on the sunny side of town.

Jessica Simon

Broker associate · Telluride Properties

After graduating from the University of Alabama, Jessica moved to Telluride in 1997 for what was meant to be a couple of years. Summers led to winters, and she stayed.

Licensed in Colorado since 2013, with a background in vacation rental management, she brings the personal attention, organization and follow-through that keep a transaction moving when you are not here to see it.

Their family includes their son Taj and two dogs, Iggy and Milo, a rescue.

How they work

Two agents on every transaction

You deal with both of them: Garrett on pricing, strategy and the negotiation, Jessica on the details, the paperwork and the people who need to be in the house on the right day.

They value an honest, direct approach to negotiating. Having lived in Telluride since the late 1990s, they know the local brokers, mortgage bankers, appraisers and inspectors who get a deal closed, and which of them to call first.

Areas of expertise

What Garrett is asked to do most

Luxury homes and mountain estates

From the historic grid in town to the larger homesites on Deep Creek Mesa and Turkey Creek Mesa. Garrett has written about more than thirty named places around Telluride.

Ski-in/ski-out

Slope-side homes and condos, most of them in Mountain Village. What "ski-in/ski-out" means for a specific property is something he checks on the ground, not in the listing.

Condos in town and the Village

Condos in the Town of Telluride and Mountain Village, including the HOA documents, rental rules and parking questions that decide whether one fits you.

Land and building sites

One client asked him to find a vacant parcel on the sunny east side of town. He knocked on doors and called owners until he found one that was not on the market. Start with the Buyer's Guide.

Buying from out of state

Tours, inspections and closings for buyers who live somewhere else, plus a rental to stay in and help with the trip while you look.

Selling a property that is hard to sell

No parking and no dogs allowed made one Mountain Village condo hard to sell. He sold it on its rental history. See how he markets a listing.

About Donna

Meet Donna Bohana

Broker/Owner, Solstice International Realty

Born and raised in Los Angeles, Donna left for boarding school at TASIS England before returning to earn her degree in International Studies from Pepperdine University, right in the heart of Malibu. After 14 years as a Coldwell Banker Top Producer and member of the top 2% of agents worldwide, she founded Solstice International Realty in 2007 to deliver a more personal, boutique approach to luxury real estate.

Today, Donna and her team serve Malibu, Pacific Palisades, Westlake Village, Calabasas, and LA's most sought-after coastal communities. Her client roster includes celebrities, entertainment executives, corporate relocations, and international buyers who trust her for discretion, professionalism, and above-and-beyond service.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Malibu Real Estate

What is the average home price in Malibu?
The median home price in Malibu typically ranges from $3.3 million to $3.7 million, though luxury and oceanfront properties regularly close well above $10 million. Pricing depends heavily on location within Malibu, whether the home is beachfront, bluff-top, or canyon-side, and the current inventory levels. For an accurate valuation of your property or a specific neighborhood analysis, request a free home valuation from Donna.
What neighborhoods are in Malibu?
Malibu stretches across 27 miles of coastline and includes distinct neighborhoods such as Point Dume, Carbon Beach (Billionaire's Beach), Broad Beach, Malibu Colony, La Costa, Big Rock, Malibu Park, and the Palisades Highlands. Each area has its own character, price range, and lifestyle appeal. Donna also serves Pacific Palisades, Westlake Village, Calabasas, Brentwood, and Hollywood Hills. Explore all communities here.
How do I find a luxury real estate agent in Malibu?
Look for an agent with deep local knowledge, a proven track record of luxury transactions, and strong relationships across the Malibu community. Donna Bohana has been a top-producing Malibu agent for over 20 years, served as President of the Malibu Association of Realtors, and has represented high-profile clients from entertainment, sports, and corporate backgrounds. Learn more about Donna's experience.
Is Malibu a good place to buy a home?
Malibu has consistently been one of the most sought-after residential markets in the United States. With 27 miles of coastline, world-class dining, top-rated schools in surrounding communities, and a level of privacy that attracts high-net-worth buyers from around the world, Malibu offers both a premier lifestyle and long-term investment potential. Donna can walk you through current market conditions, inventory trends, and what to expect as a buyer. Read the Buyer's Guide.
How long does it take to sell a home in Malibu?
Average days on market in Malibu vary depending on the price point, property type, and current conditions. Correctly priced luxury homes with professional marketing can sell within 30 to 90 days, while unique or ultra-luxury properties may take longer to find the right buyer. Donna's approach combines global marketing reach, celebrity-level presentation, and strategic pricing to move homes efficiently. See how Donna sells homes.

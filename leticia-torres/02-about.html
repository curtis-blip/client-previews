About Leticia Torres

About Leticia Torres, North County San Diego Real Estate Agent

Helping North County San Diego buyers and sellers since 2008.

Meet Leticia

A Licensed California Agent Since 2008

Leticia Torres has been a licensed California real estate agent since 2008. She works with buyers and sellers across North County San Diego, from Carlsbad and Oceanside on the coast to San Marcos, Vista, Fallbrook and Escondido inland.

She works in English and Spanish, and she keeps every client up to date in whichever language they prefer, from the first conversation to closing day.

Leticia is an agent with Andrew Arroyo Real Estate, a San Diego brokerage.

How She Works

How Leticia Works With You

Every move starts with a conversation about what you want, then a plan you can follow.

  1. 01

    Listen and Learn

    Leticia starts with your goals, your timeline and your concerns, whether you are buying, selling or holding a rental.

  2. 02

    Make a Plan

    She lays out the steps ahead: pre-approval and the towns to look in for a buyer, preparation and pricing for a seller.

  3. 03

    Carry It Out

    Showings, marketing, offers and negotiation, with clear updates at each step so you always know where things stand.

  4. 04

    Close and Stay in Touch

    Inspections, the appraisal and the paperwork through to closing day, and a call whenever you have a question after.

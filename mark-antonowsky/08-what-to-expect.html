WHAT TO EXPECT

What to Expect When Buying a Home in Phoenix

Buying a home in Phoenix involves more than choosing a property. Pricing, financing, inspections, and contract terms all affect how the purchase moves forward. Knowing how each part works helps you make informed decisions along the way.

THE PROCESS

How Buying a Home in Phoenix Works

The CFPB outlines a similar framework nationally. Mark applies it to the specifics of the Phoenix market.

01

Consult

Understand your financial position and what you can comfortably afford. Loan options, down payment, and price range are reviewed so expectations match what lenders will approve.

02

Search

Homes are evaluated based on price, condition, location, and recent comparable sales. This helps you understand whether a property is priced appropriately for the current market.

03

Offer

An offer is built around more than just price. Terms like contingencies, timelines, and seller concessions are adjusted based on competition and property value.

04

Review

After acceptance, inspections, appraisal, and lender requirements are completed. This stage confirms the home's condition and whether agreed terms still make sense.

05

Finalize

Complete loan approval, review documents, and confirm title details. Everything is checked to ensure the purchase is accurate and ready for transfer.

Westminster at a glance

Between Denver and Boulder on US-36

Westminster is a city of about 116,000 people split between Adams and Jefferson counties.

Its west side runs out to Standley Lake, its north end to the Orchard Town Center at I-25, and Downtown Westminster is growing on the 105-acre former Westminster Mall site at 88th Avenue and Sheridan Boulevard.

The lake, the pavilion and the castle

Standley Lake Regional Park has trails and boating; there's no swimming because the lake is a drinking-water supply. The Butterfly Pavilion is on 104th Avenue, and the red-stone 1908 Westminster University building, the “Westminster Castle”, is on the National Register of Historic Places.

What to check before you offer

  • Which countyWestminster sits in Adams and Jefferson counties, and the county sets the tax bill.
  • Which school districtJeffco, Adams 12 and Westminster Public Schools all serve parts of the city. Confirm the district for the address.
  • Metro district and HOA costsNewer Colorado neighborhoods often sit in a metropolitan district that adds to the property tax bill, and many carry HOA dues. Get both figures in writing before your contract deadlines.

Questions

Westminster FAQ

Which school district serves Westminster?

Three: Jeffco Public Schools in the west, Adams 12 in the north and east, and Westminster Public Schools in the south-central area. Confirm a specific address with the district.

Can you swim in Standley Lake?

No. Standley Lake is a drinking-water supply, so there's no swimming, but the regional park has trails and boating.

Is there a train from Westminster to Denver?

Yes. The RTD B Line runs from Westminster Station at 6995 Grove Street, opened in 2016, to Union Station. Flatiron Flyer buses run on US-36.

What is the Westminster Castle?

The red-stone 1908 Westminster University building, listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Independent Film

Bringing Powerful Stories to Life on Screen.

By day I sell homes across Southside Virginia. The rest of the time, I produce independent film. Both jobs come down to the same thing: telling a story people can step into.

The Producer

Storytelling Has Always Been the Heart of Everything I Do.

Most people around Martinsville know me as a dedicated Realtor. What some may not know is that I am also a passionate independent film producer.

The two lives are not as different as they sound. In real estate, I help families write new chapters in their lives. In film, I help bring powerful stories to life on screen. Different sets, same heartbeat.

My storytelling has already reached a national audience through hosting and producing American Dream TV episodes across Southside Virginia. Independent film is where that experience goes next.

Boulder at a glance

Beneath the Flatirons, 25 miles up US-36

In 1967 Boulder voters approved the country's first sales tax dedicated to buying open space.

The city now holds more than 46,000 acres of open space with about 155 miles of trails, and about 106,000 people live here, 25 miles up US-36 from Denver.

01

Pearl Street and Chautauqua

The Pearl Street Mall, dedicated in 1977, is the heart of downtown. Chautauqua Park is the gateway to the Flatirons, and the University of Colorado Boulder opened in 1877.

02

The open space

More than 46,000 acres of city open space and about 155 miles of trails surround Boulder. The 5.5-mile Boulder Creek Path runs through town, and Boulder Reservoir sits to the northeast.

03

The homes

Homes of every era, from older neighborhoods near downtown to later ones farther out, with the greenbelt all around. Ask Steve how different parts of the city compare.

04

What to check before you offer

  • Older homesAdd a sewer line scope to the inspection and ask about the age of the roof and systems.
  • The school, by addressBoulder Valley covers about 98 percent of the city; a small northern edge is in St. Vrain. Confirm the address.
  • Metro district and HOA costsNewer Colorado neighborhoods often sit in a metropolitan district that adds to the property tax bill, and many carry HOA dues. Get both figures in writing before your contract deadlines.

Questions

Boulder FAQ

How far is Boulder from Denver?

About 25 miles up US-36, with Flatiron Flyer bus rapid transit to Union Station.

Which school district serves Boulder?

Boulder Valley School District covers about 98 percent of the city; a small, mostly undeveloped northern edge is in St. Vrain. Confirm a specific address with the district.

How much open space does Boulder have?

More than 46,000 acres, with about 155 miles of trails. In 1967 Boulder voters approved the country's first sales tax dedicated to buying open space.

When did the Pearl Street Mall open?

It was dedicated on August 6, 1977.

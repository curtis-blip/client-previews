About Jim

Meet Jim Holbrook

Broker and founder of Top Tier Realty, selling homes in Southwest Riverside County since 2005.

Broker and founder

More than twenty years of selling homes here

Jim Holbrook has sold homes in Southwest Riverside County since he was licensed in 2005, and he has run his own team as a broker since 2008. Before real estate, he worked for a Fortune 500 company.

The way he works fits in one line: when you hire Jim, you get Jim. An experienced staff handles the details behind the scenes, and Jim stays in the strategy, the communication, the offers and the negotiation himself, from the first conversation to the close of escrow.

Most of his work is in Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Winchester and Canyon Lake, and the team covers San Diego County as well. The business runs on repeat clients and referrals, and his rule is to treat every client's sale as if it were his own.

How he works

Pricing, presentation and the negotiation

For a seller, Jim puts together professional presentation, marketing online and off, pricing against the homes buyers will compare, and negotiation. The goal is the highest price on the strongest terms, not simply a sale.

Buyers get the same hands-on approach: his knowledge of these neighborhoods and two decades of negotiating, used to find the right house and to protect you through the inspections, the appraisal and escrow.

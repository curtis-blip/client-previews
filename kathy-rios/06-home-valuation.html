Two numbers, not one

The estimate, then the real one

An instant estimate takes about a minute and uses public record and market data. It is genuinely useful for orientation, and it is not a listing price. No algorithm has been inside your house.

When you are ready for the number you would actually list at, Kathy walks the property, looks at what is competing with it right now, and gives you a full market analysis with the reasoning attached. That is the step where condition, finish, position and timing get priced in.

The step after the estimate

Nobody prices a house from a spreadsheet

Condition, finish, where it sits on the street and what is competing with it this month. Those are the things that move the number, and all four need someone standing in the room.

Life on Lanier

The Lake Lanier Resource Guide

Your local map to the best of Lake Lanier: the waterfront restaurants, marinas, parks, boat ramps, and campgrounds that make this lake worth living on every single day.

Waterfront Restaurants Marinas Parks & Boat Ramps Campgrounds & RV

Living on Lanier is not just about the home, it is about everything around it: the dock you tie up to, the deck where you watch the sun drop, and the cove you call your own. We put this guide together from years of lake days so you can spend less time searching and more time on the water. When you are ready to make it your daily address, the communities and active lake listings are a click away.

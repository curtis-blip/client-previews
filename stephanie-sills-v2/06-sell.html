Selling Here

Why Selling on the Coastside Takes a Plan

Selling a home on the San Mateo County Coastside is different from selling in many other parts of the Bay Area. Coastal properties often come with factors of their own, from blufftop locations and marine environments to rural zoning and specialized disclosures.

Buyers come from across the Bay Area and beyond for ocean views, open space and a slower pace within reach of San Francisco and Silicon Valley, so a Coastside listing has to reach well past local buyers. Pricing, presentation and exposure decide the result.

Two programs carry most of the preparation: Compass Concierge and the three-phase launch.

Questions

Selling a Home on the Coastside: Questions

Should I make improvements before I list?

Usually some, chosen for return: paint, floors, staging and repairs that buyers notice. Compass Concierge can pay for them up front, with nothing due until closing.

What is a private exclusive?

Phase one of the three-phase launch: your home is shared with Compass agents and their buyers before the public sees it. How it works, and what it gives up.

How long does it take to sell?

It depends on the home, the price and the season. The first seven to fourteen days on the open market tell you the most; Stephanie reviews that evidence with you and recommends what to do next.

What does it cost to sell?

Commission is negotiable and set out in your listing agreement, and there are escrow, title and county transfer tax costs. Stephanie prepares a net sheet so you see the figures before you list.

What is my home worth?

Start with the instant estimate. For a real pricing plan, Stephanie walks the house and prepares a full market analysis.

Home/Seller's Guide

For Sellers

Selling a home in Victoria County

Priced for the market you are actually in, marketed properly, and negotiated by people who do this every week.

Pricing

The first two weeks decide everything

A listing gets the most attention it will ever get in its first fortnight. Price it above the market and you spend that attention teaching buyers to wait for a reduction. By the time the price is right the listing looks tired, and tired listings sell for less than correctly priced ones ever would have.

We would rather have the uncomfortable pricing conversation in your kitchen than watch you chase the market down for four months.

The Process

Six steps to sold

01

Walk the house together

We look at it the way a buyer and an inspector will, and tell you what is worth fixing and what is not.

  • Anything an inspector will find is better handled on your terms
  • Roof and HVAC affect appraisal and insurability, so they rarely wait
  • Gather any foundation paperwork and transferable warranties
02

Price on evidence

Real comparables, adjusted for condition, lot and location. You get a written net sheet at the same time.

  • Closed sales carry the argument, active listings are only asking
  • Acreage, water access and outbuildings need their own valuation logic
  • Start with an instant valuation, then we refine it in person
03

Prepare and photograph

Declutter, repair, stage where it helps. Professional photography and aerials, because most buyers meet your house on a phone.

  • Clear surfaces and pack away roughly a third of each room
  • Take down anything with a name on it
  • Aerials for acreage and waterfront, where the lot is the story
04

Launch properly

MLS, syndication, social and our own database of buyers already looking in your price band.

  • The first fourteen days draw more attention than the next two months
  • Views without showings usually means the photos or the price
  • Showings without offers usually means condition or something on site
05

Negotiate the whole offer

Price, financing type, option period, closing date, repairs. The highest number is not always the best deal.

  • Compare net proceeds to you, not the headline price
  • A shorter option period is worth real money in certainty
  • Leaseback after closing is negotiable and can solve your timing
06

Manage to closing

Appraisal, inspection response, title and survey. Most deals fall apart here, so this is where we work hardest.

  • Decide in advance what you will and will not repair
  • A low appraisal has more answers than just cutting the price
  • Keep utilities on so the final walkthrough goes smoothly

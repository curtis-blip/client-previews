Buying new

What to Know Before You Sign With a Builder

A new home comes with paperwork a resale does not: the builder's contract, the builder's rules for your agent and, in many communities, a special district's assessment.

  • The builder's contract

    The builder writes the contract, so its terms on deposits, deadlines and changes are the builder's. Florida requires most builders to tell you that your deposit, up to 10 percent of the price, goes into escrow unless you waive that right in writing (section 501.1375, Florida Statutes). Read that clause before you sign it.

  • Your own agent

    The people in the sales center work for the builder. Builders set their own rules for when your agent has to register you, and some want your agent with you on your first visit, so call Ryan or Katie before you walk into a model home. Buyer's agent compensation is negotiable and set in your written buyer agreement. Sometimes the seller agrees to pay part or all of it.

  • Inspections on a new home

    Permit inspections are the county's: in Manatee County, inspectors check the trades a permit requires, structural, gas, plumbing, mechanical and electrical, and you can look up a home’s permits by address. A home inspector you hire works only for you. Ask the builder when one may come in: before the drywall goes up, while the framing and wiring still show, and again before the final walk-through.

  • CDD fees and special districts

    Lakewood Ranch has a stewardship district that collects a bond assessment and an operations assessment each year, and Wellen Park's improvement district, the West Villages Improvement District, is an independent special taxing district (Wellen Park). In a community development district, the first sale contract must say in bold type that the district may levy taxes or assessments on top of county taxes (section 190.048), and a district can collect them on the county property tax bill (section 197.3632). Ask for the yearly amount before you sign.

Questions

New Home Questions

What is a CDD?

A community development district is a special district under chapter 190 of the Florida Statutes. Its taxes and assessments pay to build, run and maintain certain public facilities and services, its board sets them each year, and they come on top of county taxes (section 190.048).

Can Ryan and Katie represent me with a builder?

Yes, as long as the builder's rules for registering your agent are met. That is why it pays to call them before your first visit to a sales center.

Should I get a home inspection on a brand-new home?

It is your choice on any purchase. On a new home, the builder may set when an inspector can come in, so ask before you sign.

Is Wellen Park in Venice or North Port?

Depending on where a home sits, Wellen Park is in the City of North Port or in unincorporated Sarasota County, and its own welcome center gives a Venice address (Wellen Park FAQ). Check the parcel with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser.

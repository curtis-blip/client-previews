Who We Are

One team, not a directory of solo agents

The Hutson Group is a real estate team in Woodstock, GA that Will Hutson built around a single idea: clients first equals clients for life. Instead of handing you off, we put specialists where they do the most good, a buyer's agent on showings, a listing lead on your sale, a marketing desk producing your photos and video, and a coordinator keeping the file on track to closing.

That structure is why one team covers Cherokee, Cobb, Forsyth, and nine more counties without dropping the ball. You get the reach of a top 1% North Georgia operation and the feel of working with the same people start to finish.

Inside the Team

How a Hutson Group transaction actually runs

Four roles, working in parallel, so the right person handles each part of your move.

Buyer Specialists

Agents who tour homes with you across Woodstock, Canton, Marietta and beyond, write the offer, and run due diligence to closing.

Listing & Marketing

A pricing lead plus an in-house marketing desk producing pro photos, video, and the Zillow Showcase placement most agents cannot offer.

Client Care & LiveEasy

A complimentary move concierge for every client, from utility setup to trusted local vendors, available long after closing day.

Transaction Coordination

A coordinator tracks every deadline, inspection, and signature so your contract stays on schedule from binding agreement to keys.

Good Questions

Working with the team, explained

What clients ask before they hire the best realtor in Woodstock, GA for the job.

You get the whole team, not a single overloaded agent. Will stays involved in strategy and pricing, while a buyer specialist or listing lead handles your day-to-day. It is the reason we can give every client real attention across twelve counties instead of stretching one person thin.

Around seventeen people, counting agents, marketing, and client-care staff, all working from our Woodstock office at 6424 East Bells Ferry Road. We are a top 1% North Georgia team by sales volume, with more than 750 closed transactions behind that number.

Twelve counties across North Atlanta: Cherokee, Cobb, Forsyth, Fulton, Bartow, Paulding, Pickens, Dawson, Hall, Gwinnett, Douglas, and DeKalb. Woodstock, Canton, Marietta, Kennesaw, Roswell, Alpharetta, and Cumming are where we list and sell most often. Browse our communities to start by county.

Yes, and they go to different specialists. Buyers work with our buyer's agents through the six-step buying process; sellers get a pricing lead and the in-house marketing desk. Plenty of clients do both with us in the same move.

Every agent is licensed in Georgia. You can confirm any license at the Georgia Real Estate Commission, the state body that regulates real estate licensees. We are happy to point you to a specific license number on request.

We bring on agents who fit the clients-first standard. If you are licensed or working toward it in Georgia, look at careers with The Hutson Group to see what working here looks like and how to start a conversation.

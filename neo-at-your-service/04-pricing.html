The honest breakdown

What does an answering service cost?

Here's how the answering service cost actually works with Neo, in plain English, so there's nothing to decode on your bill.

How the bill adds up

A base fee plus a small per-minute charge

Your monthly cost is two simple parts. First, a flat monthly fee, $59 on the founder plan or $99 standard. Second, a small per-minute charge, $0.30 or $0.35 a minute, that only applies when a real customer is actually on the line.

Spam, robocalls, and silent hang-ups are blocked before they ever count, so you are never charged for noise. That keeps the AI receptionist cost predictable: you pay for conversations that can turn into jobs, not for junk.

  • Flat monthly fee you always know in advance
  • Per-minute charge on real calls only
  • No setup fee and no minimum commitment

A typical month, framed simply

Between the base fee and the minutes from real calls, most shops land around $130 a month all in. That is far less than a traditional answering service and a fraction of a full-time receptionist. Your own number will depend on how many real calls you take, which is exactly how it should work for a virtual receptionist cost.

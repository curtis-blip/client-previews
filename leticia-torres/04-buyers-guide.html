Buyer's Guide

Buying a House in San Diego, Step by Step

From pre-approval to the keys, here is how a purchase in North County works and where Leticia comes in.

Before You Start

Start With Your Finances and Your Needs

Before touring any home, it helps to know what you can borrow and what you want from a home. A lender reviews your credit, income and debts and explains the loan options. Then you decide what matters most: the neighborhood, the lifestyle, the budget.

Leticia Torres works with buyers across North County San Diego, in English and Spanish. The eight steps below are the order most purchases follow, and she is with you at each one.

01

Step 1 of 8

Get Pre-Approved

A pre-approval letter from a lender shows sellers you are a serious, qualified buyer and tells you what you can borrow. Banks and credit unions can review your credit, income and debt and explain the loan products.

What Leticia does: She connects you with lenders she knows, so you can compare options before you tour.

02

Step 2 of 8

Define Your Needs

Once you are pre-approved, write down what matters most: the way you want to live, the neighborhoods you like and the budget you are comfortable with.

What Leticia does: She talks it through with you and turns it into a clear list for the search.

03

Step 3 of 8

Choose Your Towns

North County has a lot of range, from coastal Carlsbad and Oceanside to San Marcos, Vista, Escondido and Fallbrook inland. Each community has its own feel, and it helps to see them in person.

What Leticia does: She shares what she knows about each area and points you to the ones that fit your list.

04

Step 4 of 8

Tour Homes

Walk through the homes you shortlist, in person or by video, and look closely at the layout, the condition and the surroundings.

What Leticia does: She arranges the showings, points out what to look at and answers your questions on the spot.

05

Step 5 of 8

Make an Offer

When you find the right home, you write an offer with a price and terms. The seller can accept, decline or counter.

What Leticia does: She reviews comparable sales with you, proposes terms and negotiates for you.

06

Step 6 of 8

Inspections and Appraisal

After an accepted offer, inspectors look at the structure, systems and safety of the home, and the lender orders an appraisal.

What Leticia does: She organizes the inspections and helps you read the reports and decide whether to ask for repairs or credits.

07

Step 7 of 8

Loan and Insurance

Your lender finishes underwriting the loan, and you arrange homeowners insurance before closing. Keep your finances steady during this stretch.

What Leticia does: She keeps in touch with your lender and the other parties so nothing is missed.

08

Step 8 of 8

Close and Get the Keys

At closing you sign the final paperwork, the loan funds and ownership transfers to you. Then you get the keys.

What Leticia does: She coordinates with the lender, escrow and title, and stays in touch after closing with questions and local contacts.

Financing

Loan Options for Buyers

Leticia is not a lender. Your lender can tell you which of these you may qualify for, and some can be combined, such as a conventional loan with down payment help.

  • Conventional Loans

    Loans not backed by a government agency. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explains how to compare loan options.

  • FHA Loans

    Loans insured by the Federal Housing Administration. HUD has a guide to FHA loans.

  • VA Loans

    For eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses. The Department of Veterans Affairs explains VA home loans.

  • First-Time Buyer Programs

    The California Housing Finance Agency offers CalHFA programs for eligible first-time buyers. Ask your lender about eligibility.

Buyer's agent compensation is negotiable and set in your written buyer agreement. Sometimes the seller agrees to pay part or all of it.

Questions

Questions Buyers Ask Leticia

Where do I start?

Start with a conversation about your goals and a pre-approval from a lender. Leticia can introduce you to lenders and then help you shape your list of needs and towns.

Do I need a pre-approval before I tour homes?

It is the usual first step. A pre-approval shows you what you can borrow and shows sellers you are ready to make an offer.

Which towns does Leticia work in?

All six, from Carlsbad to Fallbrook.

Are there programs for first-time buyers?

Some programs, such as those from CalHFA, are meant for first-time buyers. Your lender can tell you which ones you may qualify for.

Does Leticia work in Spanish?

Yes. Leticia explains each step of a purchase in English or Spanish.

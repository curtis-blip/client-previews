ABOUT MICHELLE

Buckeye AZ Homes for Sale, Guided by Local Expertise

I'm Michelle Seymour, an eXp Realty ICON agent and one of the top-producing solo agents in the West Valley. Before real estate, I spent nearly 30 years as a paralegal, which taught me to protect my clients' interests with the same attention to detail I bring to every transaction.

Whether you're relocating from out of state, buying your first home, or selling to move up, you'll work directly with me from start to finish. No hand-offs, no assistants. Just honest guidance backed by local knowledge and over 291 community tour videos on my YouTube channel.

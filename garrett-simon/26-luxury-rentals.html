Luxury vacation rentals

Try a Telluride luxury rental before you buy

Garrett finds vacation rentals for clients in the Town of Telluride and Mountain Village, and on the mesas when that is where you are looking. He sorts through what is available, negotiates the rate and handles the back and forth with the rental company, so you deal with one person.

He started in the valley as a rental manager, and Jessica has a background in vacation rental management, so they know the rental companies in town and in Mountain Village.

He will also make the case for staying where you might buy: a week in a neighborhood answers questions a day of showings cannot.

Things to Do

Search Telluride properties

01 · The Town of Telluride

A week in town

You walk to dinner, to the festivals in Town Park and to the gondola and the Oak Street Lift, and the free Galloping Goose bus covers the rest.

A stay in the historic district shows you what living there is like day to day: where you park, the walk home after dark, and what a festival weekend sounds like from the porch.

Homes for sale in Telluride

02 · Mountain Village

A week in Mountain Village

Ski-in/ski-out homes and condos, the golf course and the free gondola down to town.

Staying there shows you how far a given address really is from a lift, and whether the village core or a home farther out suits you better.

Homes for sale in Mountain Village

03 · The mesas

A few nights on the mesas

Deep Creek, Turkey Creek, Wilson and the other mesas trade the walk to town for space, privacy and long views.

If the mesas are on your list, ask Garrett what is available for your dates. A few nights out there show you the drive to town and the quiet after dark better than a tour can.

Renting it out yourself

If you plan to rent the home you buy

Each town licenses short-term rentals (the Town of Telluride and the Town of Mountain Village), and San Miguel County runs its own permits for unincorporated land. The rules are theirs and they change, so read them for the exact address, along with any HOA rules, before you write an offer.

The Buyer's Guide covers what to check, and Garrett can tell you what he knows about a building or a street before you get that far.

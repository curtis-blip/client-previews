What to tell me

It helps to start with the situation

You do not need to have it worked out. But these are the things I will ask anyway, so if you have them to hand it saves a round trip.

  • Buying · price range, area, and whether it is somewhere to live or something that has to pay for itself
  • Selling · the address, roughly what condition it is in, and when you want to move
  • Property management · where it is, whether it is tenanted now, and what it rents for

If none of those fit, say so in your own words. Most first conversations are twenty minutes and end with me telling you whether I am the right person for it.

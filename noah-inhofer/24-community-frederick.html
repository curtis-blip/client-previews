Get Your Bearings in Frederick

Three young women incorporated Frederick in 1907 and named it for their father, Frederick A. Clark, who owned the land. Coal mining built the town, and every year it still holds a Miners Day. Interstate 25 runs through Frederick today, with State Highway 52 along its south side.

Frederick’s Museum, Parks and Lake

Stop 1

Miners’ Memorial Museum at Crist Park

The old Frederick Town Hall was moved into Crist Park downtown, and it now holds the museum, a Colorado Historic Landmark since 2012. The town’s notes, and a warning that the hours are limited:

  • 105 5th Street
  • Open on request, the first Friday of each month and during some town events
  • A diorama of the original town, mining tools and the original mayor’s chair

Stop 2

Centennial Park

Centennial Park is the open space along Eighth Street, at 630 8th Street. The town lists:

  • Playground
  • Large open fields
  • Five-station fitness loop
  • Views of the Rocky Mountains

Stop 3

Frederick Recreation Area and Milavec Lake

The recreation area is at 8201 Colorado Boulevard. The town’s history page says townspeople banded together to buy what is now Milavec Lake as a dependable water source. Today the town lists:

  • Non-motorized boating when the lake is clear of ice, no permit needed
  • Fishing with a Colorado license for anyone over 16, no ice fishing
  • A 1.26 mile crusher fine trail around the reservoir
  • 1.7 more miles of paved trail connected to it

Stop 4

Carbon Valley Recreation Center

The recreation center at 701 5th Street belongs to the Carbon Valley Parks and Recreation District, which serves Frederick, Firestone and Dacono. It lists:

  • Lap pool, activity pool and lazy river
  • Gymnasium
  • Elevated indoor track

Before you buy

What to Check on Any Frederick Address

Four things that change from one side of town to the other. Each takes a few minutes to look up and is worth doing before you write an offer.

Five Hidden Costs Before Moving to Northern Colorado

12:37 · Costs

  1. Which county is it in?

    Frederick is in Weld County, on both sides of I-25. Unincorporated Evanston is a separate area the town also serves, so check how a parcel is treated on the Weld County Property Portal.

  2. Which schools go with the address?

    Nearly all of Frederick, about 97 percent, is in St. Vrain Valley School District, and a small piece is in Weld County School District RE-8. Start with the district’s boundary lookup, and try RE-8 for an address it does not match.

  3. Is it in a floodplain?

    The Town of Frederick has floodplain rules and requires a permit before work in a flood hazard area. FEMA’s Map Service Center shows the effective map for an address.

  4. Who supplies the water?

    Three suppliers serve areas inside town limits: the Town of Frederick, Central Weld County Water District and Left Hand Water District. Everyone east of I-25 is a Town water customer, and a home west of I-25 is a Left Hand Water District customer. Ask for the utility billing page of the address you are considering.

Quick answers

Frederick Questions Buyers Ask

Which county is Frederick in?

Weld County, on both sides of I-25.

Who provides water in Frederick?

Three suppliers: the Town of Frederick, Central Weld County Water District and Left Hand Water District. The town says everyone east of I-25 is a Town water customer, and residents west of I-25 are Left Hand Water District customers.

Which school district is Frederick in?

About 97 percent of the town is in St. Vrain Valley School District and about 3 percent in Weld County School District RE-8. The address decides, so use the district lookup.

What is the history of Frederick?

Three young women incorporated the town in 1907 and named it for their father, Frederick A. Clark. Coal mining shaped it, and its original town hall is now the Miners’ Memorial Museum, a Colorado Historic Landmark.

Living in Rocky Mount.

The Town

The Harvester Made Rocky Mount a Music Destination.

Rocky Mount is located in Franklin County and sits in the foothills of the Blue Ridge Mountains. You're surrounded by rolling farmland, scenic drives, and fall foliage that is truly stunning.

One of the town's biggest draws is the nationally known Harvester Performance Center, bringing live music and entertainment year-round. National acts, intimate venue. It's the kind of place where you can walk to a show.

Franklin County is also home to Smith Mountain Lake and is known for its excellent school system. Rocky Mount gives you mountain views, real community, slower pace, and just enough activity to keep life interesting.

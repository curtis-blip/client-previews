Explore Space Coast Communities and Neighborhoods

From waterfront living to family-friendly neighborhoods, find your perfect community across Brevard County.

Florida's Space Coast is one of the state's most desirable regions for homebuyers looking for a blend of coastal beauty, strong schools, and growing local economies. Spanning from Titusville in the north to Melbourne Beach along the southern Atlantic coastline, these space coast neighborhoods offer something for every lifestyle. Whether you are drawn to the excitement of living near Kennedy Space Center, the tranquility of a barrier island, or the modern amenities of a master-planned community near Orlando, Brevard County and its surrounding areas deliver exceptional value and quality of life.

Canaveral Groves

Canaveral Groves is the Space Coast's answer to country living without the long commute. This unincorporated Brevard County community is tucked between Titusville and Cocoa, offering sprawling lots, quiet roads, and the kind of open space that is increasingly rare in Central Florida. If you have ever wanted a property where your nearest neighbor is an acre away rather than a wall away, Canaveral Groves is worth a serious look.

What to Expect

  • Homesites typically range from 1 to 5+ acres, with many parcels offering room for workshops, barns, and hobby farming
  • Most properties sit outside HOA jurisdictions, giving homeowners full freedom to customize their land
  • Custom-built and newer construction homes dominate the market, with ranch-style layouts and modern farmhouse designs trending
  • Equestrian properties are common throughout the area, with established riding trails nearby
  • Direct access to I-95 keeps commute times manageable for those working in Titusville, Cocoa, or along the coast

Schools and Education

Families in Canaveral Groves are served by Brevard Public Schools, one of Florida's top-performing districts. Coquina Elementary and Mila Elementary anchor the area's younger learners, while Space Coast Jr/Sr High provides strong academic and athletic programs for middle and high school students. Several private and charter school options are available within a short drive as well.

Lifestyle and Amenities

Life in Canaveral Groves revolves around the outdoors. Residents enjoy horseback riding on private trails, fishing along the St. Johns River, and exploring the Tosohatchee Wildlife Management Area just to the west. Weekend trips to Playalinda Beach and the Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge are quick drives. For everyday shopping and dining, Titusville and Cocoa Village are both within 15 minutes, and Orlando's attractions sit about 45 minutes down I-95.

Merritt Island

Merritt Island sits between the Indian River Lagoon and the Banana River, creating one of the most unique geographic settings on Florida's east coast. This barrier island community blends waterfront luxury with laid-back coastal charm, offering everything from established canal-front neighborhoods to brand-new waterfront construction. Its proximity to Kennedy Space Center and Port Canaveral makes it a favorite among aerospace professionals and boating enthusiasts alike.

What to Expect

  • Waterfront and canal-front properties dominate the housing market, many with private docks and direct river access
  • A healthy mix of established homes from the 1970s-90s alongside modern new construction
  • Kennedy Space Center and Port Canaveral are minutes away, making launch viewing a regular neighborhood event
  • Strong sense of community with local farmers markets, seafood festivals, and waterfront parks
  • Some of the best fishing and kayaking access on the entire Space Coast

Schools and Education

Merritt Island is home to some of Brevard County's most sought-after schools. Audubon Elementary consistently earns top ratings from parents and education ranking sites, while Edgewood Jr/Sr High offers excellent academics, STEM programs, and competitive athletics. Lewis Carroll Elementary and Tropical Elementary round out the area's strong public school options. Families also have access to several well-regarded private academies on the island.

Lifestyle and Amenities

Water defines daily life on Merritt Island. Residents launch boats from neighborhood docks, paddle through mangrove tunnels in the wildlife refuge, and watch rocket launches from their backyards. Kelly Park and Rotary Park provide family-friendly recreation, while the Merritt Square Mall area offers everyday shopping and dining. The Banana River Aquatic Preserve is a paddleboarding and kayaking paradise, and Cocoa Beach is just a short bridge crossing away for surf and sand.

Titusville

Titusville is the northern gateway to the Space Coast and one of Brevard County's best-kept secrets for homebuyers seeking value, character, and convenience. With a revitalizing downtown corridor, proximity to Kennedy Space Center, and some of the most affordable home prices on the coast, Titusville has attracted a surge of new residents and small businesses in recent years. The city pairs small-town authenticity with a front-row seat to some of the world's most exciting rocket launches.

What to Expect

  • Significantly more affordable than communities further south on the Space Coast, with strong value across both existing and new construction
  • The downtown Historic District features independent shops, restaurants, breweries, and a growing collection of public murals
  • US-1 and I-95 corridor access makes commuting to Orlando, Cocoa, or Melbourne straightforward
  • Playalinda Beach, one of the most beautiful and uncrowded beaches in Florida, sits within the Canaveral National Seashore
  • Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge is accessible directly from town, offering birding, hiking, and wildlife photography

Schools and Education

Titusville is served by Brevard Public Schools and offers a range of quality options for families. Apollo Elementary, Riverview School, and Oak Park Elementary serve younger students, while Astronaut High School is the area's flagship public high school known for its engineering and space science programs. Brevard Virtual School and several charter options provide additional flexibility for families with specific academic needs.

Lifestyle and Amenities

Living in Titusville means waking up close to some of Florida's most iconic natural and cultural landmarks. The Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex is five minutes away, the Brevard Zoo is a short drive south, and the Indian River Lagoon provides world-class fishing and boating. Chain of Lakes Park, Sand Point Park, and the Parrish Medical Center Trail offer green space and waterfront recreation within city limits. The historic downtown continues to grow with new restaurants, art galleries, and community events throughout the year.

Laureate Park

Laureate Park is one of the most talked-about neighborhoods within the Lake Nona master-planned community in southeast Orlando. Designed for modern living, this walkable neighborhood features resort-style amenities, diverse architecture, and some of the highest-rated schools in Orange County. For buyers who want a connected, active lifestyle with a strong sense of neighborhood identity, Laureate Park consistently ranks among Central Florida's top choices.

What to Expect

  • A true walkable neighborhood with sidewalks, bike paths, pocket parks, and a community Town Center within reach of most homes
  • Resort-style pool, fitness center, dog park, event lawn, and Art Walk featuring rotating local installations
  • Architecture ranges from townhomes and courtyard homes to larger single-family residences, with builders including Ashton Woods, Craft Homes, and Taylor Morrison
  • Located within Lake Nona, meaning residents have access to the broader community's trails, golf courses, and Town Center
  • 20 minutes to downtown Orlando and 25 minutes to Orlando International Airport

Schools and Education

Education is a major draw for families moving to Laureate Park. Laureate Park Elementary, located right within the community, is one of the top-rated elementary schools in Orange County. Nearby options include Innovation Middle School and Lake Nona High School, both known for their STEM and Advanced Placement programs. The area is also home to several highly regarded private schools and the UCF Lake Nona Medical Campus, which brings a steady stream of academic and research opportunities to the region.

Lifestyle and Amenities

Daily life in Laureate Park centers around community connection. Morning jogs on the 44-mile trail network, afternoon swims at the aquatic center, evening strolls through the Art Walk, and weekend meals at Lake Nona Town Center form the rhythm of the neighborhood. The Nona Adventure Park offers zip lines, climbing, and water sports for families looking for weekend thrills. Orlando's theme parks, restaurants, and cultural attractions sit within easy reach, while the Orlando International Airport makes travel convenient for frequent flyers.

Lake Nona Region

The Lake Nona region in southeast Orlando has emerged as one of the fastest-growing areas in all of Central Florida. Anchored by Medical City, a world-class health and life sciences hub, the area attracts healthcare professionals, technology workers, and families drawn to its modern infrastructure, top-tier schools, and resort-caliber amenities. New neighborhoods, retail centers, and employment corridors continue to develop at a rapid pace, making Lake Nona a smart long-term investment for homebuyers.

What to Expect

  • Medical City is the centerpiece, housing the VA Medical Center, UCF College of Medicine, Nemours Children's Hospital, and multiple research facilities
  • Lake Nona Town Center provides walkable shopping, dining, and entertainment options that continue to expand each year
  • Golf enthusiasts enjoy access to premier courses including the Lake Nona Golf and Country Club
  • A mix of new construction communities at various price points, from starter townhomes to luxury lakefront estates
  • The Brightline high-speed rail station is planned for the area, which will connect Lake Nona to Tampa, Miami, and beyond

Schools and Education

Lake Nona's school system is one of the strongest draws for relocating families. Moss Park Elementary, Northlake Park Community School, and Sun Blaze Elementary all earn high marks from parents. Innovation Middle School and Lake Nona High School round out the public options with advanced academic tracks, athletic programs, and performing arts. The concentration of medical and research institutions in the area also feeds a culture of education and innovation that extends well beyond the K-12 level.

Lifestyle and Amenities

The Lake Nona lifestyle balances health, recreation, and convenience in ways few Florida communities can match. Over 44 miles of trails connect neighborhoods to parks, lakes, and town centers. The USTA National Campus puts world-class tennis within reach of every resident. Boxi Park offers a family-friendly food truck and entertainment village, and the growing Town Center hosts concerts, seasonal markets, and community events year-round. For healthcare professionals, the short commute to Medical City is a significant quality-of-life advantage that draws buyers from across the state.

Melbourne Beach

Melbourne Beach is the quiet, charming jewel at the southern end of the Space Coast. This small oceanfront community trades the bustle of larger beach towns for genuine coastal tranquility, attracting retirees, second-home buyers, and full-time residents who prioritize a slower pace and direct beach access. With its old-Florida character and tight-knit community feel, Melbourne Beach offers the kind of authentic seaside living that is becoming increasingly rare along the Atlantic coast.

What to Expect

  • A compact, walkable community where most homes sit within a short distance of the ocean or the Indian River Lagoon
  • Housing options include oceanfront single-family homes, elevated beach cottages, riverfront estates, and low-rise condos
  • No high-rise development, keeping the skyline low and the beach uncrowded
  • Sebastian Inlet State Park, one of Florida's premier surfing, fishing, and wildlife destinations, anchors the southern boundary
  • A strong second-home and vacation rental market for buyers interested in investment properties

Schools and Education

Melbourne Beach students attend Brevard Public Schools, with Gemini Elementary serving as the local elementary option. Ocean Breeze Elementary and Hoover Middle School are nearby in the greater Melbourne area, and Melbourne High School and Eau Gallie High School offer strong academic and extracurricular programs. Several respected private schools in Melbourne proper provide additional choices for families who want smaller class sizes or specialized curricula.

Lifestyle and Amenities

Life in Melbourne Beach revolves around the ocean. Residents start mornings with sunrise beach walks, spend weekends surfing at Sebastian Inlet, and gather for community fish fries and holiday events at Ryckman Park. The Melbourne Beach Pier is a local landmark for fishing and sunset viewing. For dining and shopping beyond the immediate area, Historic Downtown Melbourne is a short drive across the causeway, offering an eclectic mix of boutiques, galleries, and waterfront restaurants along the Eau Gallie Arts District.

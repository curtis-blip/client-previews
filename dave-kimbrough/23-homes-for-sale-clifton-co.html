Living in Clifton

More than 20,000 people, and no city government

Clifton sits on Interstate 70 between Grand Junction and Palisade, about 12 minutes from each by road. It is a census-designated place, not a city, so Mesa County is its local government.

The 2020 Census counted 20,413 people in Clifton. The Clifton Water District, a special district, serves the area, and the headquarters of James M. Robb Colorado River State Park is here, at Corn Lake on 32 Road.

01

A census place that Mesa County governs

Clifton has no city hall and no city website. It is a census-designated place in unincorporated Mesa County, so the county is its local government, for all 20,413 people the 2020 Census counted here.

For a buyer, that means the county's rules and services apply. If a home sits near the edge of Grand Junction, ask Dave to confirm whether it is inside the city or out in the county.

02

Water and sewer from Clifton's own districts

The Clifton Water District is a special district serving the Clifton area, and its website also carries the customer pages for the Clifton Sanitation District.

Confirm the water and sewer providers for an address before you buy.

03

Corn Lake and the Colorado River

Corn Lake, at 361 32 Road, is the headquarters of James M. Robb Colorado River State Park. It is a day-use section with a boat ramp to the Colorado River, picnic sites, fishing and a 0.9-mile trail along the lakeshore, and Colorado Parks and Wildlife stocks the lake in spring and fall.

04

A state park in five sections

The park runs along the river in five sections, west to east: Fruita, the Colorado River Wildlife Area and Pear Park Trails, Connected Lakes, Corn Lake and Island Acres. It took its current name in 2005; before that it was Colorado River State Park. CPW lists 130 campsites, 13 miles of trails and 35 miles of river.

Mesa County and the City of Grand Junction set up the Colorado Riverfront Commission in 1987 to protect this river corridor from Loma to Palisade.

05

On the interstate at Exit 37

Interstate 70's Exit 37 is the Clifton exit, onto Business 70. The National Park Service routes westbound visitors that way through Grand Junction to Monument Road and the Colorado National Monument.

By road, downtown Grand Junction and Palisade are each about 12 minutes from the center of Clifton without traffic, and the airport about 14.

Questions

Clifton FAQ

Is Clifton part of Grand Junction?

No. Clifton is a census-designated place in unincorporated Mesa County, so the county is its local government. For a home near the city's edge, confirm the jurisdiction before you rely on city services.

Which schools serve Clifton?

Mesa County Valley School District 51. Chatfield Elementary School and Rocky Mountain Elementary School are both inside the Census area. Schools are assigned by address, so confirm a specific home with the district's boundary information.

Who provides water in Clifton?

The Clifton Water District, a special district, serves the Clifton area, and its website also carries the Clifton Sanitation District's customer pages. Confirm the providers for a specific address.

How far is Clifton from downtown Grand Junction?

About 6.6 miles by road from the center of Clifton, roughly 12 minutes without traffic by OpenStreetMap routing. Palisade is about 12 minutes east.

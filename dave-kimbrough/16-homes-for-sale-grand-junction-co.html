Living in Grand Junction

A city named for where two rivers meet

Grand Junction was laid out in the fall of 1881 at the junction of the two largest rivers in the state, the Grand, now called the Colorado, and the Gunnison. It voted to incorporate in 1882.

Today about 65,000 people live here (2020 Census), with Colorado Mesa University, a regional airport and Interstate 70 in town and the Colorado National Monument on the western edge. The Riverfront Trail follows both rivers through the city.

01

Downtown and Main Street

Main Street is the center of downtown, with shops, galleries, restaurants and the street festivals the downtown partnership holds through the year. Since 1984 Art on the Corner has set rotating sculptures along its sidewalks, one of the first exhibits of its kind on city sidewalks in the country.

City Hall is at 250 North 5th Street. Dave's office, The Agency Grand Junction, is at 601 Main Street.

02

North Grand Junction

North Avenue was the northern edge of the original townsite, and the side of town beyond it is what this page calls North Grand Junction. It takes in the Horizon Drive business district between G Road and H Road, Canyon View Park on 24 Road, and the airport on Walker Field Drive.

The city has no neighborhood by that name, so a search for it is drawn by area, and the school for any address is confirmed with District 51.

03

Along the two rivers

The Colorado and Gunnison rivers meet just downstream of Las Colonias Park, the confluence the city is named for. The park adds a river park, ponds, a dog park and an amphitheater beside downtown.

Mesa County and the city set up the Colorado Riverfront Commission in 1987, and the Riverfront Trail it built now links Fruita, Grand Junction and Palisade along the water.

04

The monument next door

The Colorado National Monument, set aside in 1911, rises on the city's western edge. Its east entrance on Monument Road is about ten minutes from City Hall, reached from I-70 at Exit 31 or Exit 37, and the visitor center is 19 miles in.

The Redlands neighborhoods sit at its foot.

05

A college town and a regional hub

Colorado Mesa University began here in 1925 as a junior college with 39 students and took its current name in 2011. Grand Junction Regional Airport opened as the municipal airport in 1930 and has had commercial service since 1946.

St. Mary's Regional Hospital on North 7th Street and Community Hospital on G Road are both in the city.

Questions

Grand Junction FAQ

Where does the name Grand Junction come from?

From the junction of the two largest rivers in the state: the Grand River, now the Colorado, and the Gunnison. The name was agreed in November 1881, the fall the townsite was laid out.

Is North Grand Junction an official neighborhood?

No. It describes the side of town north of North Avenue, the original townsite's northern edge. The city's own plans name parts of it, such as the Horizon Drive district. Ask Dave which subdivisions a search should include.

Which school district serves Grand Junction?

Mesa County Valley School District 51 serves the whole city. Schools are assigned by address, so confirm a specific home with the district's boundary information.

How far is the airport from downtown?

Grand Junction Regional Airport is about 5 miles from City Hall, roughly 13 minutes by road without traffic.

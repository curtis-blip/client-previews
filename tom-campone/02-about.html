Meet Tom

A neighbor first, a broker second

I bought my first South Loop home in 2003. I was anxious and a little confused, unsure of what each step really meant or what I was signing. I never forgot how that felt, and it is exactly why I do this work the way I do. Every client I represent gets the calm, patient guidance I wish I had back then.

Before real estate, I spent a long and successful career in finance and insurance, closing some of the most complex multi-million dollar programs in North America for the largest brokers on the continent. It was award-winning work, and I was a consistent top performer year after year. That experience taught me how to read a deal, hold a position, and get to the right outcome under real pressure.

I bring that same negotiating discipline to residential real estate, paired with tailored, personal service. Detailed analysis of the home, the market, and the competing inventory comes standard, but so does picking up the phone, explaining the trade-offs in plain language, and treating your decision like the major one it is. The corporate rigor is in service of you, never the other way around.

My education is rooted in Chicago: I earned both a Bachelor's degree and a Master's degree from DePaul University. I have lived in this city for more than 15 years, and I make my home in the South Loop with my family. Before settling here, I lived in Lincoln Park, Lakeview, and the West Loop, so I know firsthand how different each Chicago neighborhood feels and how to help you find the one that fits.

Today I work across the full range of the city's housing: condos and high-rises, investment properties, single-family homes, and rentals with tenant placement. Whether you are buying your first place, building a portfolio, selling a home you have loved for years, or leasing out a unit, you get a broker who has done the homework and genuinely cares how the process feels.

How Tom works

Comfortable, well informed, in control

My job is to take the stress out of a high-stakes decision. That starts with listening: your goals, your timeline, your budget, and what a home really needs to do for your life. From there, every recommendation I make is backed by real analysis of the property and the market, never a rushed guess or a hard sell.

I keep you in constant communication and explain each step in plain terms, so you always know what is happening and why. You stay in control of the decision, with a steady hand beside you. That is how a client should feel at the table, and it is the standard I hold myself to on every deal.

Where Tom can help

The South Loop has been my home since 2003, and high-rise living is where I am most fluent. I know the buildings, the assessments, the reserves, and the views, and I read the financial health of an association the way I once read corporate deals. That firsthand knowledge helps buyers choose well and sellers price with confidence.

I work with investors on condos, multi-unit buildings, and single-family homes across Chicago, and I can speak directly to rental demand and tenant placement on the near south side. My finance background means cash flow, comparable sales, and downside risk are part of the conversation from day one, not an afterthought.

I remember exactly how it felt to buy my own first South Loop home, anxious and unsure of the process. First-time buyers get patient, step-by-step guidance from pre-approval to keys, with every figure and trade-off explained in plain language so the decision never feels overwhelming.

Pricing, preparation, marketing, and negotiation handled with a calm, reassuring hand. I market your home to the right buyers, bring hard-deal negotiating discipline to the table, and keep you in constant communication from listing to close.

For owners who would rather lease than sell, I handle rentals and tenant placement end to end: showing the unit, screening for a quality tenant, and managing the paperwork. The goal is simple, that you can rest easy knowing the right person is in your property.

I have lived in Lincoln Park, Lakeview, the West Loop, and now the South Loop, so I know how different each Chicago neighborhood really feels. Whether you are moving across town or across the country, I help you match the right neighborhood to the life you want here.

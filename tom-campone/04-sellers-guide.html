The process

From listing to closing

Step 01

Pricing strategy

We start with a detailed analysis of your home, recent comparable sales, and the competing inventory, so we set a price grounded in the market rather than a guess.

Step 02

Prep and staging

Tom walks the home with you and recommends the repairs, decluttering, and light staging that earn the strongest return, never more work than the sale calls for.

Step 03

Professional photography

Your home is photographed to show its light, space, and best angles, because the first showing almost always happens on a screen.

Step 04

Launch and market

We position the listing for the right buyers and syndicate it across the MLS and the major search portals, with Tom's South Loop network working behind it.

Step 05

Showings and feedback

Tom coordinates showings around your life and brings you honest feedback from the market, so we can adjust quickly if we need to.

Step 06

Offers and negotiation

When offers come in, Tom reviews every term with you and negotiates hard on price, contingencies, and timing to protect your bottom line.

Step 07

Under contract and escrow

Tom manages the inspection, appraisal, and condo document review, keeping every party on schedule so the deal holds together to the close.

Step 08

Close

We review every figure before the table, handle the final details, and you walk away with the sale done and your next chapter ready to begin.

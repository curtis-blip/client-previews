Allied Gardens and Del Cerro at a glance

Two neighborhoods below Cowles Mountain

Allied Gardens and Del Cerro are neighborhoods of the City of San Diego, north of Interstate 8 on the City's east side. Both are in the Navajo community, with Grantville and San Carlos, and in Council District 7.

Most of the land came into the City between 1948 and 1954, and both neighborhoods were built on it. The Navajo community takes in Lake Murray and Cowles Mountain, and Mission Gorge marks its northern edge.

01

A tract annexed in 1951

Allied Gardens was built on the Southern Title and Trust Company tract, 1,152 acres annexed to the City in 1951. The City's community plan describes the west side of the Navajo community as detached and attached homes in Allied Gardens, with businesses along Mission Gorge Road in Grantville.

The Allied Gardens/Benjamin Library on Zion Avenue opened in 1965, paid for by a bequest from Edwin A. Benjamin. Allied Gardens Community Park, a 13.5-acre City park, has a recreation center and a pool.

02

The river and the trolley

On the community's west side is Grantville, where the Junipero Land and Water Company laid out a town in 1887. Its post office was registered as Orchard, but the town became known as Grantville, after President Ulysses S. Grant.

The San Diego River runs along the community's western edge, and the City's plan notes that it is subject to flooding. The Grantville trolley station, on the Green Line, runs to downtown and San Diego State University, and a 2015 amendment to the community plan steers new housing toward it.

03

Del Cerro, above the freeway

Del Cerro went up within Waring Tract No. 2, almost 5,000 acres annexed in 1954 and one of the largest annexations in the City's history. The City describes the center and east of the community, Del Cerro and San Carlos, as mainly residential, on hillsides cut by steep canyons.

The Navajo Community Plan asks that houses on steep ground follow the terrain and keep grading down. It also notes soils in parts of the community that can seep groundwater and slide, and calls for a geologic report before new building there. The San Carlos Library on Jackson Drive serves Del Cerro.

04

Lake Murray and Mission Trails

Lake Murray, a City reservoir whose dam was built in 1918, sits in the southeast of the community: 171 acres of water when full, with 3.2 miles of shoreline open for fishing and boating. Cowles Mountain rises behind it, and the City's plan gives its peak, 1,591 feet, as the highest point in the City.

Mission Trails Regional Park, which the City started in 1974, spans more than 8,000 acres, with about 60 miles of trails. In Mission Gorge stands the Old Mission Dam, built by Native American laborers between 1812 and 1816 to store water for the Mission San Diego de Alcala.

05

What Jim would check here

  • Which plan, which district Both neighborhoods are in the City's Navajo Community Plan area and Council District 7. The plan's hillside and canyon guidelines shape what a sloped lot can become.
  • Canyons and fire If the lot backs onto a canyon or open space, look it up on CAL FIRE's fire hazard maps. In a high or very high zone, state law requires a defensible space inspection when the house is sold, and the City's brush management rules apply.
  • Slopes and soils The community plan flags soils in parts of the area that can seep groundwater and slide. On a hillside or canyon-edge house, ask for the geotechnical history.
  • Older houses and flood maps The City screens houses 45 years or older for historic value before most exterior permits. Near the San Diego River, look the address up on FEMA's flood maps.

Questions

Allied Gardens and Del Cerro FAQ

Are Allied Gardens and Del Cerro part of the City of San Diego?

Yes. Both are City of San Diego neighborhoods in the Navajo community planning area, with Grantville and San Carlos, and both are in Council District 7.

Which school district serves Allied Gardens and Del Cerro?

San Diego Unified School District. Its School Finder lists the schools for a street address, so confirm a specific address there.

How big is Mission Trails Regional Park?

The City puts it at more than 8,000 acres, with about 60 miles of trails. It started the park in 1974, and Lake Murray's boating and Kumeyaay Lake's campground are part of it.

Does the trolley serve Allied Gardens and Del Cerro?

The Grantville station, on the Green Line, sits on the community's west side and runs to downtown and San Diego State University. In 2015 the City amended the community plan to steer new housing toward the station.

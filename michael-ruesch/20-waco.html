Before you offer

What we would check for you

None of this is special to Waco. It just comes up here more than it does in a city.

Several districts, one city

Waco ISD, Midway, China Spring, Robinson and others all serve parts of the metropolitan area. The district can change street to street, so verify the address.

Older housing stock near the centre

The historic neighbourhoods are the appeal and the reason to inspect properly. Foundation, wiring and plumbing are where the money hides.

Well and septic

Outside the city limits, expect a well or rural water and a septic system. We have both checked on their own, never folded into the home inspection.

Who maintains the road

County road, private easement or association street. Three different answers, and three different costs across the years you own the place.

Flood plain

Elevation decides the insurance premium, sometimes by thousands a year. We pull the map before you are attached to the house.

Good to know

Schools

Several districts serve parts of the city, including Waco ISD and Midway ISD. Verify by address rather than by neighbourhood name.

Everyday

Everything the region needs: full retail, hospitals, the airport, and the restaurants that came with the last decade of growth.

Getting around

I-35 runs through the middle. Austin is roughly ninety minutes south, the Metroplex ninety minutes north.

What is here

Baylor University, Cameron Park on the bluffs above the Brazos, and the Silos district that changed the city's visitor economy.

Common questions

Answered

Which school district will I be in?

It depends on the exact address, not the neighbourhood. Waco, Midway, China Spring, Robinson and others all serve parts of the area.

Are the historic homes a good buy?

They can be excellent, and they can be expensive to hold. Michael walks these carefully, because what is cosmetic and what is structural is not obvious from photos.

Is it a rental market?

Yes, particularly near the university, and short term rental rules vary by area. Check before you buy for that purpose.

How far is Lake Whitney?

About thirty miles north, roughly forty minutes. A lot of our buyers work in Waco and keep a place at the lake.

Is there new construction?

Yes, mostly toward the edges of the city and the suburbs south and west.

Do you work the whole city?

Yes, along with the surrounding towns. We are based north of here on the lake, which is where most of our listings sit.

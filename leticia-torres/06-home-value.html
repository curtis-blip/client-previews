What You Get

What the Estimator Shows

Enter an address and the estimator answers in three parts.

  1. 01

    An Estimated Value

    One figure for what the home may be worth today, a place to start the conversation.

  2. 02

    A Range

    A low and a high end around that figure, because homes rarely sell at one exact number.

  3. 03

    A Confidence Score

    A plain sign of how firmly the estimate stands, so you know how much weight to give it.

The Next Step

Questions

Questions About Your Home's Value

How does the home value estimator work?

Type in your address and the estimator returns an estimated value, a range and a confidence score for the home.

Is an online estimate the same as an appraisal?

No. An estimate is a starting point. An appraisal is done by a licensed appraiser, usually for a lender.

What is a full market analysis?

It is a report Leticia builds after walking through your home. It looks at how your home compares with others nearby and what that means for pricing.

Do I have to list my home to ask for a market analysis?

No. You can ask for one while you are still deciding, and nothing about it commits you to selling.

Which towns does Leticia cover?

All six, from Carlsbad to Fallbrook.

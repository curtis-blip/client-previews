San Marcos at a glance

A valley town with two campuses

San Marcos sits on State Route 78 in northern San Diego County, between Vista and Escondido. It became a city in 1963 and counted 94,833 people at the 2020 Census.

Two public campuses, California State University San Marcos and Palomar College, are in the city, and the Sprinter rail line stops at both. The General Plan divides San Marcos into eight neighborhoods and counts 48 specific plan areas, each with rules of its own. San Marcos is one of the six cities on Jim's North County San Diego page.

01

Named for the little valleys

According to a legend the City's General Plan retells, Spanish soldiers came on a fertile valley in 1797 and named it Los Vallecitos de San Marcos, the Little Valleys of Saint Mark, for the day they found it: April 25, St. Mark's Day. The valley became a land grant in 1840.

The first town stood near what is now Grand Avenue and Rancho Santa Fe Road. When the Santa Fe Railroad laid its tracks a mile away, the town followed, moving in 1903 to what are now Mission Road and Pico Avenue. Farming was the main business until dairies and poultry joined it in the mid-1900s, and growth took off in 1956, when the first Colorado River water arrived.

02

Two campuses on the Sprinter

California State University San Marcos was founded on September 1, 1989, as the 20th campus of the California State University. It grew out of a San Diego State center in the city, broke ground on the former Prohoroff Ranch in 1990 and held its first classes that fall. Palomar College, a public two-year community college, has its campus on West Mission Road.

The Sprinter, a 22-mile rail line between Oceanside and Escondido, stops at Palomar College, the San Marcos Civic Center and Cal State San Marcos. State Route 78 runs the same corridor, linking Interstate 5 with Interstate 15.

03

City streets and county land

Richland, in the northeast, was one of the first parts of San Marcos to develop. Twin Oaks Valley keeps a rural look, with greenhouses and estate homes on large lots, and more than half of it lies outside the city limits. San Elijo Hills was built under its own specific plan in the city's southernmost neighborhood, which also holds the Double Peak ridgeline.

Lake San Marcos, in the central southwest, is not part of the city at all: it is an unincorporated community of San Diego County, counted separately at the 2020 Census. Ask Jim which side of the line a house sits on, because county zoning and permits apply around the lake.

04

Parks and 72 miles of trail

The City counts 44 parks on more than 300 acres and a growing trail network of about 72 miles, open to hikers, cyclists, runners and riders. Double Peak Park, on the Cerro de las Posas ridge, looks out over much of North County to the Pacific.

The City also runs Jack's Pond Nature Center, with field trips and free admission days, and a ranger program that leads group hikes. In the south, the former 200-acre San Marcos Landfill has been replanted with coastal sage scrub and chaparral and will stay open space.

What Jim would check in San Marcos

  • City or county Lake San Marcos and more than half of Twin Oaks Valley are unincorporated, so county zoning and permits apply there, not the City's.
  • Mello-Roos The City runs several Community Facilities Districts, including CFD 99-01 for San Elijo Hills. Look for any line marked CFD under Fixed Charge Assmts on the county tax bill. Service districts for police, fire, lighting and landscaping have no end date.
  • Which water district Vallecitos Water District serves San Marcos and Lake San Marcos, and Vista Irrigation District serves portions of the city. Confirm by address.
  • Fire and flood maps Look the address up on CAL FIRE's fire hazard maps, especially on the hillsides, and on FEMA's flood maps near the creeks.

Questions

San Marcos FAQ

Is Lake San Marcos part of the city?

No. Lake San Marcos is an unincorporated community of San Diego County, so county zoning and permits apply there. Vallecitos Water District serves it.

Which school district serves San Marcos?

San Marcos Unified School District, which is based in the city. Its MySchool Locator lists the home schools for a street address, so confirm a specific address there.

What is Mello-Roos in San Marcos?

A Community Facilities District adds a special tax to the county property tax bill for parcels inside it. San Marcos administers several, including CFD 99-01 for San Elijo Hills.

How far is San Marcos from San Diego and the coast?

By road from the Civic Center, downtown San Diego and San Diego International are each about 35 miles away, Carlsbad Village about 15 miles and John Wayne Airport about 63 miles.

How to sell my house in Corpus Christi, the steady-hand version

Eight steps to a clean close on the Coastal Bend, the right buyer, and a price that holds.

Selling a home in Texas shouldn't feel like a leap of faith. With a real CMA, coastal-aware prep, and a sharp marketing rollout, your listing wins on its first weekend.

Dodee starts with price discipline grounded in a real Coastal Bend comparative market analysis, not a Zestimate. From there, she builds a marketing package around professional photography, drone work for waterfront and island lots, a 3D walkthrough, and AARE's syndication network, not a flat MLS dump that drowns in the feed. Every showing, every offer, every negotiation, you get plain-spoken updates on what buyers are saying, what feedback to act on, and what to ignore.

The home selling process, eight steps from listed to closed

The full sequence Dodee runs on every Corpus Christi and Coastal Bend listing.

01 Step 01

Comparative market analysis and price strategy

Most "what's my home worth" tools pull comps that don't actually compare. Different square footage, different finishes, different streets, different windstorm tier. Dodee builds your CMA off Coastal Bend properties that share your floorplan, your finish level, your school zone, and your insurance picture, then layers in active inventory and current days on market so you know what you're competing against today, not last quarter.

Selling a home in Texas means pricing against a market that's hyper-local. A Southside home isn't priced like Flour Bluff, and Padre Island isn't priced like Portland. Start with the valuation form on this site, ninety seconds, and Dodee follows up with a real comp set, not an automated guess.

spreadsheet-pricing-analysis
02 Step 02

Coastal prep and pre-list staging

You don't need to renovate. You need to prep, the coastal way. Fresh interior paint in a neutral palette, a deep declutter, light staging in the rooms that drive showings (living, primary, kitchen), and a focused repair triage on items inspectors and insurers flag.

The Coastal Bend adds a layer most sellers don't think about: storm-prep documentation. Pull together your most recent roof and AC service records, your windstorm certificate of compliance (WPI-8) if the home has one, your flood-zone status, and any hurricane impact glass or shutter receipts. Buyers and their insurance agents will ask, and having it ready de-risks their offer. Dodee walks the home with you and ranks every item by ROI, what's worth doing, what's not.

interior-painting-prep
03 Step 03

Photography, 3D tour, and drone

How your home shows up on Zillow, Realtor.com, and Instagram drives the calls. Phone photos and a quick MLS upload aren't enough at any price point in this market, and they're disqualifying on a waterfront, island, or Southside listing.

Every Dodee Hill listing gets a professional real-estate photographer, twilight shots on the right homes, a 3D Matterport tour, drone work for waterfront and island lots and anything where the lot, dock, or water access tells the story, a full walkthrough video, and short vertical cuts edited for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. The package is built into the listing, not an upsell.

real-estate-photographer
04 Step 04

Marketing rollout across the right channels

MLS is the floor, not the ceiling. A listing that lives only on the MLS is a passive game. Dodee runs an active rollout on top: Showcase IDX feature placement on this site, syndication to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, and Trulia, geo-fenced social ads aimed at active home shoppers in Corpus Christi, Portland, Rockport, Aransas Pass, and Kingsville, and an agent-network preview through AARE.

Every channel has a job. Showcase IDX captures the local-search buyer. Social and syndication catch the relocator. The AARE network catches the agent who has a buyer in their back pocket. Every channel is measurable, and Dodee reports on what's actually working.

social-media-marketing
05 Step 05

Showings and open houses, peak Coastal Bend season

The Coastal Bend has a buying rhythm. Peak buyer activity runs January through April, when out-of-state buyers, retirees, and military families on PCS orders are deciding. Coordinated to that calendar, your launch weekend lands in a much bigger pool than a slow July listing in the same year.

Dodee coordinates a launch-weekend open house, a broker preview when the price point calls for it, and a tightly managed showing schedule that bunches appointments rather than scattering them. Bunched showings create urgency, scattered showings create ghosts. Lock-box self-showings work for some price points, guided tours are better for others. Dodee recommends the right format for your home and follows up on every showing for real feedback.

open-house-living-room
06 Step 06

Offer review and negotiation

Price is one variable, and not always the most important one. Dodee reviews every offer on six dimensions: price, financing strength, lender quality, contingencies, close date, and earnest money. A higher price with a weak out-of-state lender and a long close can be worth less than a lower offer with cash reserves and a fourteen-day close.

In multiple-offer scenarios you'll see every offer side by side, with Dodee's read on which terms are negotiable, which are deal-breakers, and which buyers are likely to actually close on a Coastal Bend property once windstorm and flood quotes land.

contract-negotiation-table
07 Step 07

Inspection response and appraisal management

Once you're under contract, the option period and inspection are the next pressure points. Coastal Bend buyers and their inspectors will pay extra attention to roof age and storm history, AC condition, foundation movement, salt-air corrosion, windstorm certificate status, and flood-zone disclosures. Dodee tells you which findings warrant a credit, which warrant a repair, which are simply normal for a Gulf-coast home, and which are the buyer trying to renegotiate.

Appraisal management is the second piece. If a low appraisal lands, Dodee works with the buyer's lender on a reconsideration, finds the comp set the appraiser missed, or negotiates a price-and-gap solution that keeps the deal alive without giving back what doesn't need to be given back.

08 Step 08

Closing and title transfer at a Texas title company

In Texas, closings happen at a title company, not at an attorney's office. The final walkthrough, the settlement statement review, the wire confirmation, the signing appointment, the keys handed off. Dodee coordinates with the title company, the buyer's agent, and the lender so nothing slips on closing day. If issues come up at the walkthrough, she handles the negotiation, not you.

After close, Dodee stays in touch. Most of her sellers are future buyers, future referrers, or future neighbors. The relationship is the business, the transaction is one chapter of it.

Pricing in the Coastal Bend is the most important decision you'll make

Overpricing feels like leaving room to negotiate. In practice, it costs Coastal Bend sellers real money. Listings that sit accumulate days on market, buyers and their agents start treating the home as stale, and the inevitable price reduction signals weakness, not flexibility. By the time the home sells, it usually closes for less than it would have at the right price from day one. The home selling process rewards discipline at the start, not optimism.

  • 01
    The first 14 days matter most

    Homes that sell well in Corpus Christi and Nueces County typically draw their best offers in the first two weeks on market, when buyer attention is highest and the listing is fresh.

  • 02
    Coastal Bend peak season is January through April

    Out-of-state buyers, retirees, and military relocators concentrate their search in the first quarter of the year. Properly timed listings get the most eyes during this window.

  • 03
    Every price reduction signals room to negotiate

    Once you cut the price, buyers expect you to cut it again. The stale-listing tax is real, and it usually outweighs whatever upside the original aspirational number was supposed to capture.

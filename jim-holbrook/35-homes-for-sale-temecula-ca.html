Temecula at a glance

A stagecoach town inside a planned city

Temecula sits in the southwestern corner of Riverside County, with Interstate 15 running through it. It became a city on December 1, 1989, and counted 110,003 people at the 2020 Census.

The city takes in two very different places. Old Town grew up around a stop on the Butterfield Overland Stage and the railroad that followed it. Most of the rest was laid out under the Rancho California plan, drawn up after Kaiser Aluminum and Chemical bought the Vail Ranch in 1964. East of the city limits, the vineyards of the Temecula Valley wine country are county land, with county rules.

01

Old Town grew up around a stage stop

The Butterfield Overland Stage ran through Temecula from 1858 to 1861, and the California Southern Railroad line opened here in 1882. In 1905 the railroad brought Walter Vail, who began ranching the 87,000-acre Vail Ranch; it stayed a ranch until 1964.

Old Town has a Specific Plan of its own today, which governs what is built there. The City runs the Temecula Valley Museum on Mercedes Street, and the Old Town Temecula Community Theater is on Main Street.

02

Neighborhoods built to a plan

Kaiser Aluminum and Chemical prepared the Rancho California Development Plan after it bought the Vail Ranch, and the city's land use and roads still largely follow it. The City now has 14 active Specific Plans, each governing what is built in its area. Paloma Del Sol, Redhawk, Vail Ranch, Wolf Creek, Harveston, Roripaugh Ranch and Altair are among them.

Many of the newer neighborhoods also sit inside a Community Facilities District, known as Mello-Roos: a special property tax on top of the regular one, paid by the owners inside the district. The City lists ten, Harveston, Wolf Creek, Crowne Hill and Roripaugh Ranch among them.

03

Wine country is county land

The Temecula Valley American Viticultural Area was established in 1984 and took its current name in 2004. The federal record puts it at about 33,000 acres of southwestern Riverside County, where marine air reaches the valley through gaps in the Santa Ana Mountains.

The wineries are outside the city limits, so Riverside County's rules apply. Its Temecula Valley Wine Country Community Plan, adopted in 2014, splits the area into three districts: the Winery District for wineries and the businesses that serve visitors, the Equestrian District to protect horse properties, and the Residential District for estate lots. Before you fall for a parcel out here, ask Jim which district it is in (the county's design guidelines).

04

Two water districts and a river

Temecula gets its water from two districts. Rancho California Water District serves the city, parts of Murrieta and the unincorporated land around them, wine country included. Eastern Municipal Water District serves parts of the city too. Which one serves a house depends on the address.

Murrieta Creek and Temecula Creek meet east of Interstate 15, and the Santa Margarita River begins where they join. From there it cuts through Temecula Gorge on its way to Camp Pendleton and the ocean.

05

What Jim would check in Temecula

  • Mello-Roos Ask whether the house sits in one of the City's Community Facilities Districts. The special tax is on the county property tax bill, and it can make two similar houses cost different amounts each month.
  • City or county Wine country, De Luz and French Valley are unincorporated Riverside County, so county zoning and permits apply there, not the City's.
  • Which water district Rancho California or Eastern Municipal, by address. Out in wine country, ask about the well and septic as well.
  • Flood and fire maps Look the address up on FEMA's flood maps, especially near the creeks, and on CAL FIRE's fire hazard maps, which can affect insurance and building rules.

Questions

Temecula FAQ

Is Temecula wine country inside the city?

No. The wineries are in unincorporated Riverside County, east of the city limits, under the county's Temecula Valley Wine Country Community Plan. County zoning and permits apply there.

Which school district serves Temecula?

Temecula Valley Unified School District. Its online locator lists the elementary, middle and high schools for a street address, so confirm a specific address there.

What is Mello-Roos in Temecula?

A Community Facilities District, known as Mello-Roos, adds a special property tax on top of the regular one for owners inside its boundaries. The City of Temecula lists ten, including Harveston, Wolf Creek, Crowne Hill and Roripaugh Ranch.

How far is Temecula from San Diego and the airports?

By road from Old Town, downtown San Diego and San Diego International are each about 58 miles away, Ontario International about 51 miles and John Wayne Airport about 67 miles.

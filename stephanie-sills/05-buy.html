Coastside Living

Why Buy on the San Mateo County Coastside

The Coastside offers something rare in the Bay Area: a genuine small-town lifestyle just 30 minutes from San Francisco and Silicon Valley. Miles of beaches, world-class hiking trails, local farms, and a tight-knit community make this one of the most desirable places to live in Northern California.

Buyers are drawn to Half Moon Bay, El Granada, Montara, Moss Beach, and Pacifica for the quality of life, the natural beauty, and the slower pace. Properties here hold their value well, and limited housing inventory means long-term appreciation has historically been strong across the Coastside market.

Whether you are looking for a primary residence, a weekend retreat, or an investment property, the Coastside delivers a lifestyle that few other Bay Area locations can match.

Financial Overview

Understanding Buyer Costs on the Coastside

Earnest Money Deposit

Typically 1-3% of purchase price, held in escrow to show the seller you are committed to the transaction.

Home Inspection

$400-$800 depending on property size, type, and any specialized inspections required for Coastside properties.

Appraisal Fee

$500-$1,000, required by most lenders to confirm the property value supports the loan amount.

Closing Costs

Title insurance, escrow fees, and lender fees typically run 2-3% of the purchase price at closing.

Homeowner's Insurance

Required before closing. Rates vary by property location, construction type, and proximity to fire zones on the Coastside.

Property Taxes

San Mateo County tax rates apply. Taxes are prorated from your closing date, with supplemental assessments in the first year.

Stephanie walks every buyer through expected costs upfront so there are no surprises at closing.

What About Agent Commission?

Following recent industry changes, buyer agent compensation is negotiated as part of each transaction. Stephanie will explain your options clearly and in advance so you know exactly how representation works and what to expect before you start your home search.

Questions About Buying on the Coastside

The Half Moon Bay median listing price is approximately $1.69M as of early 2026. Prices vary significantly by neighborhood, with options ranging from Pacifica condos starting in the $600Ks to oceanfront estates in Montara and El Granada that exceed $3M. Stephanie can help you understand pricing in each Coastside community and find properties that fit your budget.
The Coastside market has limited inventory and strong demand from Bay Area buyers looking for a lifestyle change. Pre-approval, a knowledgeable local agent, and flexibility on timing are key advantages. Understanding local factors like microclimates, coastal regulations, and commute options also helps you make a confident decision. Talk to Stephanie about your specific situation.
The typical timeline is 30-60 days from accepted offer to closing, though competitive situations with pre-inspected or contingency-free offers can move faster. The search itself varies depending on inventory and how specific your criteria are. Stephanie helps buyers stay prepared so they can act quickly when the right property comes up.

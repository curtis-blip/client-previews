Home/Buyer's Guide

Buying a Home in Whitney, Texas

Eight steps, written out plainly, so you know what happens next and nothing arrives as a surprise.

Before you start

Buying here is not the same as buying in a city

Around Lake Whitney you are usually looking at three different transactions wearing the same word. A house in town, a place on the water, and land. They run on different timelines, they carry different risks, and the questions worth asking are not the same.

We have been doing this here for fifteen years, and between us we have built houses and designed them. That is most of what you are paying us for: knowing which problems are cosmetic and which ones should change your offer.

The process

What happens, in order

No step here is a surprise to us. It should not be one to you either.

01

The first conversation

Before anything else we want to know what you are actually trying to do. Move up, move back, buy the weekend place you have been circling for years. That conversation shapes every recommendation that follows, and it costs you nothing.

02

Getting your financing straight

Talk to a lender before you fall for a house. In this market a seller will take a slightly lower offer from a buyer who is genuinely ready over a higher one that might not fund. We will point you at lenders who actually close in Hill and Bosque counties.

03

Seeing what is out there

You will get everything that matches, not just our listings. We will also tell you which ones are not worth the drive, which is the part most people find more useful.

04

Walking the house properly

This is where Michael's construction background earns its keep. Foundation, roof age, what the crawlspace is telling you, whether that crack matters. Joanna reads the other half: what the space could be, and which changes pay you back when you sell.

05

Making the offer

Price is one term out of many. Closing timeline, what conveys, who pays for what, how the option period is set. We build the offer around what the seller actually wants, which is usually not simply the biggest number.

06

Option period and inspections

You have a short window to learn everything you can and walk away if you need to. We line up the inspector, read the report with you, and separate the items worth negotiating from the ones that are just an old house being an old house.

07

Appraisal, survey and title

The quiet stretch where deals actually die. Lake and acreage properties bring their own questions here: survey lines, easements, septic, what the corps line means for what you can build.

08

Closing and keys

We sit at the table with you, read the numbers before you sign them, and hand you the keys. Then we stay in touch, because you will want a plumber eventually and we know one.

Common questions

Buying here, answered

How much do I need for a down payment around here?

It depends entirely on the loan. Conventional can start near three percent, FHA around three and a half, and VA and USDA can reach zero, and a good part of Hill and Bosque counties is USDA eligible. Talk to a lender before you assume you need twenty percent.

Do I pay you as a buyer?

We will walk you through exactly how our compensation works, in writing, before you tour anything. Since the 2024 rule changes that conversation happens up front rather than at closing, which we think is an improvement.

How long does buying take?

From an accepted offer to keys, usually thirty to forty five days with financing. Cash can close in under two weeks. Finding the right house is the part with no schedule, and we would rather you take longer there than hurry into the wrong one.

What is different about buying on the lake?

Lake Whitney is a Corps of Engineers lake, so what you can build, how a dock is permitted, and where your usable land actually ends are all questions a normal inspection will not answer. We check them before you are emotionally committed.

What about buying land or acreage?

Different transaction entirely. Water rights, mineral rights, fencing, legal access and septic all matter more than square footage. Our Farm and Ranch page covers it properly.

Can you help if I am moving from out of the area?

Most of our buyers are. We will video walk anything you cannot see in person and give you the honest read, including the things a listing photo is carefully not showing you.

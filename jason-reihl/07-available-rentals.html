Ready to Apply?

How to Apply

1
Find a rental property you're interested in through our search or by contacting our office directly.
2
Submit a completed rental application online. We review applications in the order received.
3
The application fee is $30 per adult. This covers credit, background, and rental history screening.
4
All adults 18 and older who will occupy the property must submit a separate application.

What You'll Need

  • Two most recent pay stubs or proof of income
  • Government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport)
  • Completed online rental application
  • $30 application fee per adult applicant
  • Current landlord or property manager contact info
  • Employment verification details

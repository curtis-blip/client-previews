Services

Buyer representation, listing strategy, relocation, military PCS, investment, second-home and beach. Full-service work, with one steady hand from first call to closing.

What I do

Six ways I work with Coastal Bend clients

Buyer representation

From the first home tour to the closing table, I work for you, not the listing. Pre-approval prep, neighborhood fit, offer strategy, inspection response, and coastal-specific items like windstorm certificates and salt-air maintenance. Especially helpful for first-time buyers and Texas relocators.

Read the buyer's guide

Listing & selling

Comparative market analysis, prep and staging guidance for Coastal Bend homes, professional photography, drone for waterfront and island, MLS plus Showcase IDX syndication, and tight negotiation through inspections, appraisals, and closing.

Read the seller's guide

Relocation

I moved my own family from Wyoming to South Dakota to Corpus Christi. I know what relocators need: video tours, school comparisons, neighborhood walkthroughs, and an honest read on commute, lifestyle, and resale. If you're moving to the Coastal Bend, I can be your first call.

See the communities

Military relocation

NAS Corpus Christi and NAS Kingsville bring families in and out of the Coastal Bend year after year. PCS timelines, VA loans, base-proximity neighborhoods (Flour Bluff, Padre Island, Kingsville), windstorm and flood considerations, and rental options on tight orders. I work this niche on a regular basis.

Talk military relocation

Coastal & island

Beach homes on North Padre Island and Mustang Island, Port Aransas vacation properties, Rockport waterfront, jetty-side and bayfront. I cover insurance basics (windstorm WPI-8, flood zones), HOA short-term-rental rules, and what to actually expect from a beach-house ownership cost stack.

Search coastal homes

Investment & second home

Buy-and-hold rentals in Corpus and Kingsville, short-term rental analysis on Padre and Port A, fix-and-flip comps in Southside and Flour Bluff, and second-home strategy for out-of-state owners. Honest pencil-out, not pressure.

How I work

Plain-spoken, prepared, on your side

01

Listening first

The first conversation is mostly questions, not a pitch. The plan only works if I understand the buyer or seller in front of me. That's how I learned hard work and word-is-bond, growing up in Wyoming.

02

Local at the street level

Southside Specialist is not a tagline. Kings Crossing, Cimmaron and S Staples corridor, Flour Bluff schools, Padre Island stretches, Rockport and Port A. I work these markets every week.

Steady through the rough patches

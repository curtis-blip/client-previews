Meet Mitch

Mitch Jearls leads The Jearls Home Team at MKB, REALTORS® in Roanoke. He came to real estate from running small businesses and leading a sales team, and he has bought, renovated and resold homes across the valley on his own account.

That is what he brings to a showing or a listing appointment: what a repair will really cost, what buyers here will pay for, and what can wait. You get the plain version, early, so every decision is yours to make with the facts in hand.

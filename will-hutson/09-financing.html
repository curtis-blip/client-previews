Why It Matters

Get pre-approved before you fall for a house

A mortgage pre approval in Georgia is a lender's written confirmation of how much you can borrow, based on your income, credit and debts. It is the single most useful thing a first-time buyer can do, and it changes how sellers see you the moment your offer lands.

Listings around Cherokee and Cobb counties can move fast. When a seller stacks two offers side by side, the pre-approved buyer almost always wins. Here is what pre-approval does for you.

It strengthens your offer.A pre-approval letter tells a Woodstock seller you are serious and already qualified, which carries real weight against buyers who are not.
It removes the uncertainty.You learn your real budget early, so you stop guessing and never waste a Saturday touring homes you cannot finance.
It lets you act fast.When the right home appears, you can write a clean, competitive offer the same day instead of scrambling to start a loan.

The Process

How a mortgage pre approval works

Four steps, usually wrapped up in a few days. We hand you off to a lender we trust and stay in the loop the whole time.

01

Gather your numbers

Recent pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, bank statements, and a rough picture of your monthly debts. A self-employed buyer in Forsyth County brings a couple of years of returns instead.

02

The lender reviews you

A trusted local lender checks your income, credit and employment, then confirms the loan amount and program you qualify for. This is the part most first-timers dread, and it is easier than it sounds.

03

You get your letter

You receive a pre-approval letter stating your borrowing power. That letter is what we attach to an offer on a Canton or Kennesaw listing so a seller takes you seriously.

04

You shop on solid footing

Now your home search is anchored to a real number. We line up tours across the counties that fit your budget, and you make offers knowing the financing is already lined up.

Good to Know

Financing questions, answered

Pre-qualification is a quick estimate based on numbers you tell the lender, with nothing verified. Pre-approval is the real thing: the lender checks your credit, income and employment, then issues a letter stating what you can borrow. North Georgia sellers take a pre-approval seriously. They rarely react to a pre-qualification.

There is no single cutoff. Many conventional loans start in the mid-600s, while FHA loans can go lower with a larger down payment. A higher score usually earns a better rate. The fastest way to know where you stand is a pre-approval conversation, which also shows you exactly what to nudge upward if anything.

Less than most first-timers assume. Some conventional programs allow 3 to 5 percent down, FHA sits around 3.5 percent, and VA and USDA loans can require nothing down for buyers who qualify. On a $400,000 home near Canton, 5 percent is $20,000, not the $80,000 many people picture. Closing costs are separate, and some can be negotiated or covered by a seller credit.

Yes. The Georgia Dream Homeownership Program, run by the state housing agency, offers down payment help to eligible first-time buyers who meet income and purchase-price limits, and some counties and cities add their own programs. Eligibility and funding change, so our lender partners will check what you currently qualify for and fold it into your pre-approval.

An FHA loan is government-backed and built for buyers with smaller down payments or thinner credit, which makes it popular with first-timers. A VA loan is for eligible veterans, active-duty service members and certain spouses, and it often requires zero down with no monthly mortgage insurance. Whether either fits depends on your situation, and a lender can compare them against a conventional loan for your North Georgia purchase.

A pre-approval letter is typically valid for 60 to 90 days, then the lender refreshes it. The credit pull is a hard inquiry that may dip your score a few points temporarily. Mortgage-shopping inquiries within a short window are generally treated as one event, so comparing a couple of lenders will not stack up against you.

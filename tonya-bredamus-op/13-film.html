Tonya Bredamus, Producer

Beyond Real Estate: Film Producer & Creative Visionary.

In real estate, I help families write new chapters. In film, I help bring stories to life on screen. Same instinct, different medium.

The Dual Identity

Storytelling Has Always Been at the Heart of What I Do.

While many people know me as a dedicated Realtor committed to serving my community, what some may not know is that I am also a passionate independent film producer.

In real estate, I help families write new chapters in their lives. In film, I help bring powerful stories to life on screen.

As a producer of independent films, my mission goes far beyond entertainment. I am committed to creating opportunities for new, undiscovered talent. Actors, writers, directors, crew members, and creatives who deserve a chance to practice their craft, grow their confidence, and get noticed. Everyone deserves a platform. Everyone deserves a first opportunity.

Core Principles

Three Things Every Project Stands On.

Opportunity

Real-world experience for emerging talent. First roles, first credits, first chances to do the work.

Excellence

Meaningful, high-quality independent films. Not vanity projects. Real craft.

Impact

Stories that resonate with audiences around the world. Stories that move people, that linger.

The Bigger Goal

Building Momentum for Careers, Not Just Movies.

Independent film allows creativity to thrive without limits. It allows fresh voices to be heard. It allows bold ideas to become reality. Through each project, I aim to build something bigger than a movie. I aim to build momentum for careers and create entertainment that leaves a lasting impression.

Whether I'm helping someone find the perfect home or helping a new actor land their first role, my goal is the same: open doors.

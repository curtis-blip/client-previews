Dawson County

Dawson Pointe

A Day in the Life. Mornings are unhurried here. You walk a few minutes to the 48-slip community dock, drop the boat in deep water, and have the quiet north end of Lanier mostly to yourself. By afternoon it is swim, tennis, or pickleball at the clubhouse, then a five-minute hop to GA-400 for dinner or the North Georgia outlets.

What You'll Find. A gated neighborhood of spacious homesites and mature trees, with a laid-back vibe and real room to breathe. Amenities include swim, tennis, pickleball, and a community clubhouse, all wrapped around easy deep-water access.

Why Buyers Love It

Dawson Pointe is the answer for buyers who want true deep-water boating, gated privacy, and a quieter stretch of Lanier without giving up GA-400 convenience. It tends to draw second-home owners and full-timers who value the unhurried north-end pace.

Forsyth County

Long Hollow Landing

A Day in the Life. This is lake living that fits real life. Sidewalks and a family-friendly layout make for easy evenings, weekends mix swim and tennis at the clubhouse with time on the water, and shopping and GA-400 are right around the corner.

What You'll Find. One of the better lifestyle-and-value communities on Lanier, where you get lake access without full waterfront pricing. Highlights include community dock access with some deeded slips, swim and tennis, a clubhouse, and strong Forsyth schools.

Hall County

Marina Bay

A Day in the Life. Marina Bay is built for easy, elevated lake days. Step out to resort-style amenities, take a community boat slip out onto the wide north end of Lanier, and come home to a private, polished neighborhood that still feels like a getaway.

What You'll Find. Luxury and privacy in a sought-after north-lake location, with world-class resort-style amenities and community boat slips available. It is a refined setting for buyers who want the lake lifestyle without giving up comfort.

Forsyth County

Hickory Lakeside

A Day in the Life. Hickory Lakeside was designed around lake living, not just near it. Take the golf cart down to the community docks, spend the day on the water, then gather at the lakeside firepit as the sun drops. Big porches and outdoor entertaining spaces make home feel like part of the lifestyle.

What You'll Find. A gated, boutique-luxury lake community of newer custom and semi-custom homes in a modern farmhouse and craftsman style. Much of the neighborhood is built around community boat slips, with some assigned deep-water slips, plus a clubhouse, pool, tennis, pickleball, and cart paths to the docks, all in a top Forsyth school district.

Dawson County

Chestatee

A Day in the Life. Chestatee blends golf, lake, and mountains into one neighborhood. Play a round on the dramatic Denis Griffiths course, take the boat out from the private marina, then walk the trails or meet neighbors at the lakeside pavilion. Many buyers describe it as a North Georgia lake resort that people happen to live in.

What You'll Find. A long-established lifestyle community on the north end, anchored by championship golf and a large private marina with covered and uncovered slips, some deeded or leased by section. Amenities run deep: a Junior Olympic pool, tennis, pickleball, trails, and a craftsman, mountain-lake feel in nearby Dawsonville.

Hall County

Harbour Point

A Day in the Life. Harbour Point is a true yacht-club community. The marina and clubhouse are the heart of it, so days center on the water, with a full-time harbormaster, golf-cart access to the slips, and a clubhouse that functions more like a private resort than an HOA. It is built for people who want a vacation-every-day feel.

What You'll Find. A fully gated luxury community in Gainesville with estate-style homes, long-range lake and mountain views, and one of the largest private residential marinas on Lanier, with more than 250 covered slips. The 8,000-plus square foot clubhouse adds a Junior Olympic pool, tennis, pickleball, a fitness center, and trails, near Port Royale and easy open-water access.

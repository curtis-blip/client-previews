Wilson Mesa, Colorado

Homes for Sale on Wilson Mesa, CO

Ranch lots and large parcels below Wilson Peak, and the water and winter roads to check before you buy one.

Wilson Mesa at a glance

Ranch lots at the wilderness edge

Wilson Mesa rises west of Ilium and south of the San Miguel River, about 16 road miles from downtown Telluride. The county maps two subdivisions on it: Wilson Mesa Ranches, where the median lot is 8.7 acres, and Elk Creek Meadows Ranch, six parcels of about 42 to 237 acres.

It is farther out than Deep Creek and Turkey Creek mesas: about 11 miles from downtown in a straight line, against 4 or 5 for those two. Silver Pick Road climbs to it from CO-145 near the old Vanadium mill site and is unpaved; Fall Creek Road also leaves the highway, paved at the bottom and unpaved higher up. To the south, the Lizard Head Wilderness holds three fourteeners: Wilson Peak, Mount Wilson and El Diente.

Wilson Mesa Ranches, the main subdivision

Wilson Mesa Ranches covers about 1,328 acres on the county map, about 11 miles west of downtown Telluride in a straight line and 16 by road. County records list 106 parcels under the name, from 1.4 to 415 acres, with a median of 8.7; a few of those records may be open space or common tracts.

The county plows Wilson Mesa Ranch Circle (County Road 57J) and County Road 56L. The subdivision borders Colorado State Land Board land, National Forest lies less than a mile away, and four Nature Conservancy easements and a Rocky Mountain Elk Foundation easement recorded at 2,384 acres touch its boundary. At the county's sample point the zoning is Forestry/Agriculture.

02

Elk Creek Meadows Ranch, the big parcels

West of Wilson Mesa Ranches, Elk Creek Meadows Ranch is six parcels in county records, from about 42 to 237 acres, on about 819 acres in all. It borders public land managed by the Bureau of Land Management, and State Land Board land and National Forest lie less than a mile away.

Its sample point sits lower than the main subdivision's, at about 9,160 feet, in the same Forestry/Agriculture zone. By road it is about 17 miles to downtown Telluride and 16 to the airport.

03

Woods Lake and the Lizard Head Wilderness

South of the mesa, the 41,496-acre Lizard Head Wilderness holds Wilson Peak (14,017 feet), Mount Wilson (14,246) and El Diente (14,159). The Forest Service lists the Wilson Mesa Trailhead (East) as a start of the Lizard Head Trail, and the Woods Lake and Elk Creek trails enter from the north. No bikes or motors are allowed inside.

Fall Creek Road runs on past its plowed miles toward Woods Lake, where Colorado Parks and Wildlife manages the 162-acre Woods Lake State Wildlife Area, about 4 miles south-southwest of Wilson Mesa Ranches. The standard route up Wilson Peak, the Rock of Ages Trail, starts at the end of Elk Creek Road off Silver Pick Road; Colorado Mountain Club volunteers and the Forest Service rerouted it in 2009 around private land to restore public access.

What Garrett would check on Wilson Mesa

  • WaterEPA lists an active community water system named Wilson Mesa, on surface water, but not which parcels it serves. Ask whether a lot is on it. If it is not, a new or replacement well needs a state permit, and the permit file says what the well may be used for.
  • SepticSewer service on the mesa is not documented. Septic systems under 2,000 gallons a day are permitted by the county's on-site wastewater program.
  • Who plows the roadThe county plows about 4 miles of Silver Pick Road, County Road 56L, Wilson Mesa Ranch Circle and the first 3.8 miles of Fall Creek Road. Other segments are plowed privately or not at all, so confirm it for the road to any lot.
  • Zoning and fireThe county's sample points show Forestry/Agriculture zoning and the Telluride Fire Protection District. Check a parcel's zone in the county's Zone District Finder before you plan a build.
  • PowerSan Miguel Power Association serves the county, but whether power reaches a given lot line is not documented publicly. Ask before you plan around it.

Questions

Wilson Mesa FAQ

How far is Wilson Mesa from Telluride?

About 16 miles by road from Wilson Mesa Ranches to downtown Telluride, and about 11 in a straight line. Deep Creek and Turkey Creek mesas are closer to town. Telluride Regional Airport is about 15 road miles away, and Montrose Regional about 62.

How big are the lots on Wilson Mesa?

County records list 106 parcels in Wilson Mesa Ranches, from 1.4 to 415 acres, with a median of 8.7 acres. Elk Creek Meadows Ranch has six, from about 42 to 237 acres.

Is there a water system on Wilson Mesa?

EPA lists an active community water system named Wilson Mesa, which draws on surface water. It does not say which parcels it serves, so confirm it for the lot you are looking at. A lot that is not on it needs another source, such as a permitted well.

Are the roads to Wilson Mesa plowed in winter?

Partly. The county plows about 4 miles of Silver Pick Road (County Road 60M), County Road 56L, Wilson Mesa Ranch Circle and about 3.8 miles of Fall Creek Road. Other segments are plowed privately or not at all.

Meet Jerre

Two decades, one market, both sides of the table.

Jerre Walker is a long-time North County resident and has maintained her strong ties to the community by staying in touch with friends, former co-workers and fostering community relationships for many years. Before becoming a successful REALTOR® and Property Manager, Jerre had a career in the legal field, and her experience working in the courtroom environment is evident in her dedication and attention to detail.

Jerre began her real estate career at Judson Real Estate in Escondido, then joined Dave Stubbs Real Estate in Solana Beach. There she helped to develop a loyal following of property management and sales clients. In 2006 she helped form Fisher Real Estate Advisors in Encinitas and continued to build her property management business there until 2019. In April 2019, she brought her experience and growth back to Judson Real Estate, where she efficiently manages an extensive portfolio of rental properties while also excelling in sales with numerous multi-transaction clients.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-22

This Privacy Policy explains how Christian Kelly, operating as High Rocky Living with Worth Clark Realty (collectively "we," "us," and "our"), collects, uses, and protects information you provide through www.highrockyliving.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically through your visit, and information from third-party services.

  • Information you provide: name, email address, phone number, property address, mailing address, search preferences, message content, and any other details you submit through contact forms, home-valuation requests, saved searches, or email subscriptions.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, device type, browser type and version, operating system, pages viewed, time spent on pages, referring URL, and general geographic location, gathered through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information from third-party services: if you sign in or interact with us through a third party (for example, Showcase IDX saved searches or social media), we may receive limited account and interaction information from that service in accordance with its own privacy practices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the real estate services you request.
  • Send property listings, home valuations, market updates, and other communications you have asked for.
  • Maintain saved searches, listing alerts, and account preferences on the Site.
  • Improve the Site's content, layout, and performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations, including real estate licensure requirements in Colorado.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the Site and our business operations.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure how visitors interact with content, and support saved searches in the IDX. Most browsers allow you to control cookies through settings. Disabling cookies may limit certain Site features, including IDX functionality and saved searches.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the Site, including:

  • Showcase IDX for MLS listing display, search, saved searches, and lead capture.
  • Google Analytics for aggregated visitor analytics. Google may use cookies and similar technologies as described in its own privacy policy.
  • Email and CRM platforms for sending communications, managing client relationships, and following up on inquiries.
  • Hosting and security providers for delivering the Site reliably and protecting against threats.

Each third-party service maintains its own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies for details on how they handle data.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to deliver the services you have requested, including:

  • With our brokerage, Worth Clark Realty, for transaction support and compliance.
  • With lenders, inspectors, title companies, and other vendors you ask us to connect you with.
  • With service providers who help us operate the Site and our business, under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of any person.

6. Your Choices

You can ask us to update or correct information you have provided, unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of each message, or delete your saved searches and account preferences at any time by emailing highrockyliving@gmail.com. We will respond to all reasonable requests promptly.

7. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is fully secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. 8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act give you certain rights, including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion of personal information, the right to correct inaccurate personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at highrockyliving@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. International Visitors (GDPR)

If you visit the Site from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, which may have data protection laws different from those in your country. If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and the right to object to processing. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when the policy was most recently revised. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or our handling of your information can be sent to highrockyliving@gmail.com or mailed to High Rocky Living, 429 E Main Street, Buena Vista, Colorado 81211.

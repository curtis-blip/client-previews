Buy Before You Sell

Compass Bridge Loan: Buy Before You Sell

In the Bay Area, timing is one of the hardest parts of moving: the equity you need for the next home is still tied up in the one you live in.

Compass Bridge Loan Services help qualified buyers use the equity in their current home to buy the next property before selling. It takes the pressure off coordinating two transactions at once, and it lets you write an offer without a sale contingency.

  • Buy your next home before selling your current property
  • Access equity from your existing home
  • Reduce the pressure of coordinating two transactions
  • More flexibility when making an offer

Stephanie can explain how it works and connect you with lending partners. Terms depend on the lender and on you.

Questions

Services, Answered

Do I have to use Stephanie's vendors?

No. The list is a starting point: people her clients have hired and would hire again. Use as many or as few as you like.

Who qualifies for Compass Concierge?

Qualifying sellers who list with a Compass agent. The Concierge page covers what it pays for and how repayment works.

How does a bridge loan work?

A lender advances part of the equity in your current home so you can buy the next one; you repay it when the first home sells. Rates and terms come from the lender, and Stephanie can introduce you.

Can Stephanie help with a move off the Coastside?

Yes. She and the Palermo Properties Team work across the Peninsula, San Francisco, Silicon Valley and the East Bay, and Compass agents nationwide.

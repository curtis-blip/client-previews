The Selling Process

Six steps from listed to closed.

The full sequence we run on every Lake Lanier and North Georgia listing.

01 Home Evaluation

Walk the property, read the market

We start at your home, on the water, looking at the details that set value on Lanier: water depth, dock type and permits, lot, views, and finishes. Then we pull real local sales to see exactly where your home sits in today's market, not where an online estimate guesses it does.

Want to see how the surrounding market is moving first? Browse active listings and the communities around the lake before we set your strategy.

02 Prep & Stage

Make every room and every view count

You do not need to renovate. You need to prep. We give you a clear, prioritized punch list: what to fix, what to skip, and where a small investment returns the most. From decluttering and neutral paint to staging the dock and outdoor living space, we make sure buyers see the lifestyle the moment they arrive.

The goal is a clean, current, photograph-ready home, not a flip. We rank every item by return so your money lands where it actually moves the price.

03 Data-Backed Pricing

Price it right from day one

This is where our edge shows. Rhonda's certification in Data Science in Real Estate from MIT turns comparable sales, days on market, and absorption into a pricing strategy built to sell, not sit. Lake and luxury homes rarely have a tidy stack of identical comps, so we layer in the lake-specific factors that move value: dock type, water depth, cove protection, and view.

The first two weeks set the tone for the whole sale. Overpricing feels like leaving room to negotiate, but in practice it costs sellers real money, every reduction signals weakness, and the home usually closes for less than it would have at the right price. We get the number right before we go live. See the full methodology on the home value page.

04 Marketing Launch

Take it to market, loud

A lakefront home sells on lifestyle as much as square footage, so the marketing has to show the water. We launch with cinematic photo and video, aerial coverage that captures the shoreline and the dock, and a story-driven listing that sells the life lived loud on the lake. Generic listing photos leave value on the table on Lanier.

Then we put it everywhere buyers look, across the MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com, and the major portals, plus our own channels, and target the lake and luxury audiences most likely to write an offer.

05 Showings & Offers

Qualify buyers, negotiate hard

We manage showings, gather real feedback, and keep you informed in real time. When offers come in, we read the full picture, not just the top-line price: terms, financing strength, lender quality, contingencies, close date, and earnest money. A higher price with a weak lender and a long close can be worth less than a cleaner offer that actually closes.

You see every offer side by side, with our read on which terms are negotiable, which are deal-breakers, and which buyers are most likely to make it to the table.

06 Close

Inspection to keys, handled

We guide you through inspection, appraisal, and any repair negotiations, then keep the contract on track to the closing table. Lake homes have extra moving parts, from dock permits to survey questions, and we know how to keep them from slowing your sale.

After close, we stay in touch. Most of our sellers are also future buyers, future referrers, or current neighbors. The relationship is the business, the transaction is one chapter of it.

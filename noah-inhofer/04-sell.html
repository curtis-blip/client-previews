How Noah sells a home

How Noah Prices, Photographs and Shows Your Home

Noah’s own words for it: “As a real estate professional, I want to take your stress and put it on my shoulders.” Here is what that means for a seller.

  1. A market analysis to set the price

    He starts with a detailed market analysis of your home, free, so the asking price comes from comparable homes and not a guess. You can start with a quick estimate of what your home is worth.

  2. Professional photography, everywhere

    Your home is professionally photographed and marketed across all major online platforms, social media and Noah’s exclusive network of buyers.

  3. Showings and open houses

    Noah runs the showings and open houses, so you are not choosing between your day and a buyer’s tour.

  4. Negotiation and closing

    He negotiates the offers and carries the details through to closing day.

How selling works

Eight Steps From Valuation to Closing

Each step lists what you do, so you are never guessing at the next one.

  1. Step 1

    Learn what your home is worth

    Start with an estimate for your address, then Noah builds a detailed market analysis around your home.

    What it needsYour address and what you have done to the home.

  2. Step 2

    Prepare the home

    Noah tells you what to tidy, fix or leave alone before buyers walk through. He has flipped houses, so he knows homes from the inside.

    What it needsA few days of decluttering and any repairs you agree on.

  3. Step 3

    Set the price

    Price is the one choice that decides how a listing goes. Noah walks you through the comparable homes and his post on the one thing sellers need to know about their asking price in Colorado.

    What it needsYour goals, your timeline and the number you need.

  4. Step 4

    Photograph the home

    Professional photography, so the first look online is the best look.

    What it needsThe home ready on the day, and a few hours of access.

  5. Step 5

    List and market

    Your home goes across the major home sites and social media, and to Noah’s exclusive network of buyers.

    What it needsYour sign-off on the photos and the description.

  6. Step 6

    Showings and open houses

    Noah schedules and runs the showings and open houses.

    What it needsFlexibility on showing times, and a plan for pets.

  7. Step 7

    Negotiate the offers

    Noah reads every offer with you, price and terms together, and negotiates for the best result.

    What it needsYour decisions on each offer and any repair requests after the inspection.

  8. Step 8

    Close and move on

    Appraisal, title and closing paperwork come together, and Noah keeps the dates straight to the day you hand over the keys.

    What it needsYour signatures, and your moving date.

Selling a Home, Answered

How does Noah decide on the price?

He builds a detailed market analysis from comparable homes, and you set the price together. Start with an estimate for your address.

How will my home be marketed?

Professional photography, listing across the major home sites and social media, and Noah’s network of buyers.

Who runs the showings?

Noah does, including open houses.

Is the home value estimate the same as the market analysis?

No. The estimate is a fast number from public data. The market analysis is Noah looking at your home and the homes around it.

Can I see homes Noah has sold?

Yes. See his sold listings and the homes he has for sale now.

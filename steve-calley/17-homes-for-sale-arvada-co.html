Arvada at a glance

Olde Town, the G Line and the foothills

Steve was born and raised in Arvada, a Jefferson County city of about 123,000 people between Denver and the foothills.

Gold Strike Park marks Lewis Ralston's 1850 gold find at the mouth of Ralston Creek, and Olde Town grew from the town plat of 1870. Today the G Line runs from three Arvada stations to Denver Union Station.

Olde Town and the G Line

Olde Town Arvada is the center of it: the historic district around the G Line station, with the Arvada Center for the Arts and Humanities up Wadsworth Boulevard. The G Line opened in 2019 and runs from Arvada Gold Strike, Olde Town Arvada and Arvada Ridge stations to Union Station.

02

The neighborhoods

Established neighborhoods close to Olde Town, and newer ones toward the foothills and the Apex Center. Steve has sold homes across Arvada, from a condo near Olde Town to newer homes on the west side.

Toward the foothills

Majestic View Nature Center, the Two Ponds National Wildlife Refuge and the Apex Center cover the outdoors and the gym. I-70 and I-76 run to the south and east, and State Highways 72, 93 and 95 reach north and west.

04

What to check before you offer

  • Which countyMost of Arvada is in Jefferson County, but a small portion east of Sheridan Boulevard is in Adams County and Westminster Public Schools.
  • Metro district and HOA costsNewer Colorado neighborhoods often sit in a metropolitan district that adds to the property tax bill, and many carry HOA dues. Get both figures in writing before your contract deadlines.
  • Older homes near Olde TownAdd a sewer line scope to the inspection and ask about the age of the roof and furnace.
  • Near the rail lineIf you're close to the G Line, visit at commute time.

Questions

Arvada FAQ

Which school district serves Arvada?

Jeffco Public Schools covers about 99 percent of the city. The small Adams County portion east of Sheridan Boulevard is in Westminster Public Schools. Confirm any address with the district.

Is there a train from Arvada to Denver?

Yes. The RTD G Line opened in 2019 and runs from Arvada Gold Strike, Olde Town Arvada and Arvada Ridge stations to Denver Union Station.

What is Olde Town Arvada?

The city's historic district, grown from the 1870 town plat, with shops and restaurants around Olde Town Square and the G Line station.

Is Arvada in Jefferson County?

Mostly. About 120,000 residents live in Jefferson County and about 2,800 in a small Adams County portion east of Sheridan Boulevard.

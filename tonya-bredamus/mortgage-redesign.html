The financing process

From "is this even possible?" to keys in hand

Five steps. I'll be beside you for all of them, coordinating with your lender so nothing falls through the cracks.

Loan types

The right loan depends on you

There's no single "best" mortgage. Here are the programs most of my Southside Virginia buyers use — and who each one tends to fit.

Conventional

The most common option for buyers with solid credit. As little as 3–5% down, and you can drop mortgage insurance once you reach 20% equity.

Strong credit · Flexible terms

FHA

Government-backed and forgiving — as little as 3.5% down with lower credit-score requirements. A great fit for first-time and credit-rebuilding buyers.

Low down payment · First-time friendly

VA

For eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses: zero down payment, no monthly mortgage insurance, and competitive rates.

$0 down · No monthly MI
Rural — Henry / Patrick / Franklin

USDA

Zero-down financing for homes in eligible rural areas — and much of Henry, Patrick, and Franklin County qualifies. Income limits apply.

$0 down · Rural eligibility
First-time + down-payment help

Virginia Housing

State programs built for first-time buyers — down-payment assistance, grants, and tax credits that can make a Virginia home reachable sooner.

Assistance · Grants · Tax credits

What I do on the financing side

Your guide, not your loan officer

I don't originate loans — and that's the point. My job is to make sure the financing piece moves smoothly while you stay informed and unpressured.

  • Connect you to trusted lenders

    I'll introduce you to local lenders I've worked with for years — people who pick up the phone and explain things, not call centers.

  • Coordinate inspection & appraisal timing

    I keep the calendar tight so deadlines are met, your earnest money stays protected, and closing doesn't slip.

  • Explain every step — no pressure

    You'll always know what's happening next and why. Ask me anything, as many times as you need. Decisions stay yours.

Less than most people think. Conventional loans can start around 3–5% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA and USDA loans (common in Henry, Patrick, and Franklin counties) can be zero down for eligible buyers. Virginia Housing also offers down-payment assistance for first-timers. We'll match a program to what you actually have saved — you don't need 20% to buy.
It depends on the program. Many conventional loans look for around 620+, FHA can go lower (often 580, sometimes less with a larger down payment), and VA lenders set their own thresholds. If your score isn't where you want it yet, a good lender will give you a short, specific list of moves to lift it — and I'll connect you with one who does that for free, no obligation.
Closing costs are the one-time fees to finalize your loan and transfer the home — lender fees, appraisal, title work, taxes, and prepaid items like insurance and escrow. In Virginia they typically run about 2–5% of the purchase price. The good news: they're often negotiable, and sellers can sometimes contribute toward them. We'll plan for them up front so there are no surprises at the table.

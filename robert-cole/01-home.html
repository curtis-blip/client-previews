The Cole Home Team

The Cole Home Team sold 88 homes in the last 12 months, roughly $25.5M in volume, across a $75,000 to $1.6M price range with an average sales price of $351,000. With the median agent nationally closing 10 to 12 transactions a year, that is a different kind of practice.

Mandy Cole, known as The Redhead REALTOR®, has worked Kaufman and Henderson counties since 2021, after eight years in commercial construction sales. Robert Cole spent two decades as a logistics executive and Director of Operations before real estate, and it shows in how the team runs a transaction.

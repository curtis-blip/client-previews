Land, Acreage & Investment

Acreage doesn't price
like a subdivision.

Kaufman County and the land around Cedar Creek Lake move differently than a subdivision. Acreage, water access, road frontage, ag exemptions, MUD and PID districts, and what a builder will and will not take on all change the number.

We represent buyers and sellers on raw land, acreage homesites, and investment property, and we will tell you plainly when a parcel is not worth what it is listed for.

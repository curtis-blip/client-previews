Malibu

The Quintessential Coastal Lifestyle

Stretching 27 miles along the Pacific Coast Highway, Malibu is one of the most iconic stretches of real estate in the world. From Carbon Beach (known as Billionaire's Beach) to the dramatic bluffs of Point Dume, every neighborhood offers a distinct character shaped by ocean views, mountain backdrops, and California's most exclusive addresses.

Donna has sold and leased property throughout Malibu for over two decades, setting records in the Palisades Highlands and representing some of the most discerning buyers and sellers in the market. Whether you are seeking a beachfront compound on Broad Beach or a canyon retreat with equestrian acreage, Donna's local expertise is unmatched.

Pacific Palisades

Family-Friendly Luxury on the Westside

Nestled between Malibu and Santa Monica, Pacific Palisades offers the rare combination of beachfront proximity, top-rated schools, and a walkable village center. The Riviera neighborhood features hillside estates averaging 5,000+ square feet on winding streets named after Italian and French coastal cities.

Donna set the records for both the highest sale and highest rental in the Palisades Highlands, a testament to her deep knowledge of this market. The post-fire rebuilding cycle has created new opportunities for buyers looking to invest in one of LA's most resilient and desirable communities.

Calabasas

Gated Luxury in the Santa Monica Mountains

Calabasas is home to more than 20 gated communities, with over 10 featuring 24-hour guard gates. The Oaks of Calabasas is the crown jewel, a celebrity enclave with resort-style amenities and custom mansions exceeding 10,000 square feet. Hidden Hills, an entirely residential incorporated city within Calabasas, offers average home values of $7 million with top properties reaching $20 million.

Privacy, security, award-winning schools, and mountain scenery define the Calabasas lifestyle. Donna has represented buyers and sellers across multiple Calabasas gated communities.

Brentwood

Architectural Elegance on the Westside

One of LA's most affluent neighborhoods, Brentwood sits between Westwood, Santa Monica, Pacific Palisades, and Bel Air. With a median real estate price of $5.4 million, Brentwood is more expensive than 99% of California neighborhoods. The architectural diversity is remarkable, from Tuscan villas and Frank Lloyd Wright designs to contemporary masterpieces featured in Architectural Digest.

Celebrity residents have included Tom Brady, LeBron James, and many entertainment industry leaders. Donna has sold and leased property throughout Brentwood's tree-lined streets for decades.

Further east, lots increase in size while maintaining proximity to the water.

Known for professional beach volleyball, top-ranked schools, and exceptional dining, Manhattan Beach is a South Bay gem that Donna has served throughout her career.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Pacific Palisades Luxury Homes & Communities

What are the most popular neighborhoods in Malibu?
Malibu is made up of distinct enclaves, each with its own personality. Carbon Beach (known as Billionaire's Beach) and Broad Beach attract ultra-luxury buyers seeking beachfront living. Point Dume offers a quieter, family-oriented atmosphere with dramatic cliffside views. Malibu Colony and La Costa Beach are favorites among entertainment industry professionals who value both privacy and proximity to the city. Donna has sold and leased properties across all of these neighborhoods and can help you find the right fit. Learn more about Malibu.
What is the average home price in these communities?
Prices vary significantly by community. Malibu's median home price sits in the $3.3 to $3.7 million range, with beachfront properties often exceeding $10 million. Pacific Palisades ranges from $2.5 million to $15 million depending on location. Calabasas gated communities like The Oaks typically start around $2 million. Westlake Village and Lake Sherwood offer lakefront estates from $1.5 million to $8 million. Manhattan Beach and Newport Beach both command premium pricing for coastal proximity. Contact Donna for a current market analysis specific to the community you are interested in. Get a free home valuation.
Which communities have the best schools?
Families consistently rank Pacific Palisades and Calabasas among the top choices for schools. Pacific Palisades is served by the Palisades Charter School Complex, one of the highest-performing public school systems on the Westside. Calabasas benefits from the Las Virgenes Unified School District, known for its strong academic programs and small class sizes. Manhattan Beach Unified is regularly ranked among the best districts in Los Angeles County. Malibu families also have access to excellent private options including Malibu High School and several independent academies. Ask Donna about family-friendly communities.
How do the Malibu and Pacific Palisades lifestyles compare?
Malibu offers a more remote, beachfront-focused lifestyle with dramatic coastline views and a quieter pace. It appeals to buyers who want to feel removed from the city while staying within reach. Pacific Palisades, by contrast, delivers a walkable Village center with restaurants, boutiques, and coffee shops alongside top-rated public schools and quick access to both the beach and the 405 corridor. Many families choose the Palisades for its balance of luxury and convenience, while Malibu attracts those seeking privacy and wide-open ocean views. Explore Pacific Palisades.
Which areas are best for families with children?
Pacific Palisades, Calabasas, and Westlake Village consistently rank as the top family-friendly choices. Pacific Palisades offers a tight-knit community feel with Palisades Recreation Center, youth sports leagues, and walkable streets. Calabasas provides gated-community safety with The Oaks and Hidden Hills offering large lots and private amenities. Westlake Village blends lakefront recreation with excellent Las Virgenes schools and a quieter suburban feel just 20 minutes from the beach. Manhattan Beach is also popular among families who want a beach lifestyle with top-tier public schools. Read the Buyer's Guide.
Where can I find beachfront homes in the communities Donna serves?
True beachfront properties are available in Malibu (Carbon Beach, Broad Beach, La Costa, Malibu Colony), Manhattan Beach (The Strand), and Newport Beach (Balboa Peninsula and Corona del Mar). Malibu offers the widest selection of oceanfront homes, ranging from classic beach cottages to architectural compounds exceeding $20 million. Manhattan Beach features a competitive Strand market where homes rarely stay listed for long. Newport Beach combines harbor-front and ocean-front options with a yacht-club lifestyle. Donna has represented buyers and sellers across all three coastal markets. Search luxury listings.

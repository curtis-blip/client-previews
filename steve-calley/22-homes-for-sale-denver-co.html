Denver at a glance

A city and county at the center of the metro

Denver has been a consolidated city and county since 1902, and about 741,000 people live here.

Union Station, reopened in 2014 as the transit hub, connects the A Line to the airport and the B, G and N lines north to Westminster, Arvada and Thornton.

03

Parks and the rail lines

City Park holds the Denver Zoo and the Denver Museum of Nature & Science, and Washington Park has Smith and Grasmere lakes. The A Line reaches Denver International Airport from Union Station in about 37 minutes.

04

What to check before you offer

  • Older homesMuch of Denver's housing is decades old. Add a sewer line scope to the inspection and ask about the roof, windows and electrical.
  • The school, by addressDenver Public Schools serves the whole city; confirm the assigned schools for the address.
  • Metro district and HOA costsNewer Colorado neighborhoods often sit in a metropolitan district that adds to the property tax bill, and many carry HOA dues. Get both figures in writing before your contract deadlines.

Questions

Denver FAQ

How long is the train to the airport?

The RTD A Line runs from Union Station to Denver International Airport in about 37 minutes. It opened in 2016.

Which school district serves Denver?

Denver Public Schools serves the whole city. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district.

Is Denver a city or a county?

Both. Denver has been a consolidated city and county since 1902.

Which rail lines connect Denver to Steve's towns?

From Union Station the B Line runs to Westminster, the G Line to Arvada and the N Line to Thornton.

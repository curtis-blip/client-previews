Privacy policy

Last updated 10 August 2026 · Black Hawk Real Estate, 1495 Lump Gulch Highway, Black Hawk, CO 80422 · (303) 881-3953

What this covers

This policy explains what Black Hawk Real Estate collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what choices you have. It applies to this site only, not to third party services we link to.

Information you give us

When you submit a contact form, request a valuation or save a property search, we collect what you enter: typically your name, phone number, email address, and any details you choose to share about a property.

We use it to answer you, to provide the service you asked for, and to keep working with you if you become a client. We do not sell it.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this one records basic technical information such as pages visited, approximate location, browser type and referring site. This is used to understand how the site is used and to keep it working.

  • Analytics, to see which pages are useful
  • Cookies set by our listing search provider, to remember your saved searches
  • Security and error logs kept by our hosting provider

Listing search and third parties

Property search on this site is provided through a third party IDX service. When you use it, that provider processes your search activity under its own privacy terms. Any account you create with it is separate from this site.

How long we keep things

Enquiries are kept while the conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. Client transaction records are kept for the period Colorado real estate rules require.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any record keeping we are legally required to maintain. You can unsubscribe from any email we send at any time. Write to blackhawkcorealestate@gmail.com or call (303) 881-3953.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Equal housing

Black Hawk Real Estate supports the Fair Housing Act and the Equal Opportunity Act. We do business in accordance with federal, state and local fair housing laws.

Central New Jersey

Communities across five counties

From Bridgewater's family neighborhoods to Bernardsville's estate homes, from Princeton's walkable downtown to Westfield's commuter lines, Mary K. Van Ness knows Central New Jersey community by community.

One broker, every town I list in

Central New Jersey is not one market. Somerset County feels different from Mercer. Bernardsville is not Bridgewater. Before we write an offer or list a home, I make sure you understand the town you are buying into or selling out of. Taxes, schools, commute, character, and the real feel of a Saturday morning.

Meet Jennifer

Most of what she knows about these towns did not come from the MLS

Jennifer earned her license in 2020 and has worked the same stretch of Central Indiana every year since. She came up through Leadership Shelby County and sits with the Chamber, Mainstreet Shelbyville, and the MHP Women's Alliance. That is the part of the job that never shows up on a listing sheet, and it is the reason she can tell you what is happening on a street before you write an offer on it.

She is solo by choice. The person who answers the phone is the person who negotiates your contract, which puts a deliberate limit on how many clients she takes at once. The buyer's guide and the seller's guide both set out how she works.

Her Story

01  ·  Buying

First house, or the next one

A lot of her buyers are buying their first. You get the offer, the inspection, the appraisal and what actually happens at closing explained in plain words before you need to know any of it, not while you are signing.

Buyer's Guide

02  ·  Selling

Priced from the comps, not from hope

Overpricing the first two weeks costs you the buyers who were ready to move. In a small market there are fewer comps to hide behind, so she shows you the sales the price came from and tells you if she thinks your number is wrong.

Seller's Guide
03  ·  The signature practice

She buys and renovates here too, so the numbers are not theoretical

Shelby County and the towns along I-65 still have entry prices that make a rental or a flip work. That is why she built this side of the practice, and it is why clients bring her split parcels and problem properties other agents pass on.

Before you make an offer on an investment, three numbers decide whether it works. Everything else is detail.

01 / Where the office is

Shelbyville

The county seat, and home. Blue River runs through it, the courthouse square still anchors downtown, and the office sits on North Riley Highway. Most of her closings happen inside twenty minutes of this street.

View Shelbyville
Directional photo

02 / Decatur County

Greensburg

Half an hour southeast, and a different market entirely. Lower entry prices, a working downtown, and the tower with a tree growing out of it. Buyers priced out of Johnson County often find their number here.

View Greensburg
Directional photo

03 / Johnson County

Franklin

The I-65 side, and the fastest-moving of the three. Franklin College, a restored downtown, and Camp Atterbury close enough that military relocation is a regular part of the work here rather than an occasional one.

View Franklin

Six more sit alongside them: Rushville, Greenfield, Whiteland, Columbus, Edinburgh and Greenwood. Each prices differently, and the communities page takes them one at a time.

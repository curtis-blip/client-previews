North Phoenix

Tatum Highlands

Where founders still know the neighbors.

SettingDesert on three sides
BuildersSeven original builders
CenterpieceTeets Park
From Mark

I've been a Tatum Highlands homeowner since 1998 and I'm proud to be one of the original founding families. I've loved living here ever since.

Overview

Tatum Highlands sits in North Phoenix, surrounded by natural desert on three sides, with stunning scenery and easy access to hiking and mountain biking right out the door.

The community centerpiece is Teets Park, a five-acre grassy hub with a tot lot, lighted basketball and volleyball courts, and fitness stations. Developed by seven original builders (TW Lewis, US Home, Richmond American, KB Homes, Presley, Graystone, and Coventry by Del Webb), Tatum Highlands offers a wide mix of home styles and price points.

A day in the life

Mornings start with a hike on one of the desert trails ringing the community, then a quick stop at Teets Park where the lighted courts come alive in the afternoon. Weekends are easy here, with neighbors who have known each other for decades and a community history that newer master plans simply can't replicate.

Verdin by Taylor Morrison

Brand-new desert living, built around the preserve.

BuilderTaylor Morrison
Acreage470+ acres
Home sizes2,000 to 4,800 sq ft
PricingMid $600K to $1.5M+
Overview

Verdin is a brand-new master-planned community by Taylor Morrison along Sonoran Desert Drive, with direct access to Apache Wash Trailhead and the Sonoran Preserve. Spanning 470+ acres, it pairs modern living with natural desert surroundings.

Five home collections (Discovery, Passage, Odyssey, Summit, and Capstone) range from 2,000 to 4,800 sq ft, with options like RV garages and NextGen-style casitas. Over 100 acres are preserved as open space. Model homes debut Summer 2025, with move-ins expected by late 2026.

A day in the life

Step out the back of a Verdin home and you're at the Apache Wash Trailhead in minutes. Mornings here lean toward fitness center sessions and pickleball, and the open Sonoran Preserve beyond the community line keeps the sightlines wide. Late afternoons mean the resort pool and casual gatherings on the trail.

TSMC Corridor

Halo Vista

A city within a city, rising next to the chip campus.

Investment$7B mixed-use plan
AnchorTSMC campus
MixResidential, retail, office, industrial
TimelineVertical builds early 2026
Overview

Halo Vista is a planned $7 billion mixed-use development centered around the TSMC campus in north Phoenix. Designed as a self-sustaining city within a city, the project will combine residential neighborhoods with retail, office, and industrial space.

The vision is to attract talent and create jobs near the chip manufacturing complex. Infrastructure work is set to begin in summer 2025, with vertical construction starting in early 2026.

A day in the life

Halo Vista is being built for the people who will work at TSMC and the businesses that follow them. Living here means having retail, office, and industry in the same footprint as your home, with the kind of self-contained convenience that's rare in metro Phoenix.

Desert Ridge

The benchmark master plan for North Phoenix.

Acreage5,700 acres
BuildersToll Brothers, D.R. Horton, Del Webb, Pulte, and more
MixLuxury, townhomes, condos, retail
HubDesert Ridge Marketplace
Overview

Desert Ridge is one of North Phoenix's premier master-planned communities, spanning 5,700 acres with a full mix of residential and commercial. Its convenient position near Loop 101 and SR-51, paired with upscale amenities, has made it one of the most established corridors in the metro.

Sub-communities include Aviano (Toll Brothers luxury), Fireside (D.R. Horton), Talinn (gated townhome and single-family), and Toscana (upscale condominiums). The area is anchored by Desert Ridge Marketplace and the Mayo Clinic Phoenix campus nearby.

A day in the life

Most residents structure the day around the Marketplace and Loop 101. Morning errands or appointments at JW Marriott or Mayo Clinic, lunch at one of the dozens of restaurants nearby, and the High Street walking district for evenings out. Sub-communities like Aviano, Fireside, Talinn, and Toscana let buyers pick their pace.

WHY PHOENIX

Why Phoenix Continues to Attract Buyers

Phoenix has evolved from a retirement destination into one of the most dynamic metros in the U.S. A booming job market powered by tech, healthcare, and finance companies continues to draw professionals and families from across the country. Combined with year-round sunshine, an affordable cost of living compared to coastal cities, and an outdoor lifestyle that few metros can match, the Phoenix area offers a quality of life that keeps demand strong across every price point. The City of Phoenix covers more than 500 square miles, giving buyers a wide range of neighborhoods and price points to consider.

Job Growth & Economy

Major employers in tech, healthcare, and financial services continue to expand operations in the Phoenix metro, creating sustained demand for housing and driving long-term appreciation.

Outdoor Lifestyle

With 300+ days of sunshine, world-class hiking, championship golf courses, and water sports at nearby lakes, Phoenix delivers year-round recreation that coastal cities simply cannot match.

Affordable Compared to Coastal Cities

Buyers relocating from Los Angeles, San Francisco, or Seattle gain significantly more space and value per dollar, making Phoenix one of the strongest value propositions in the western U.S.

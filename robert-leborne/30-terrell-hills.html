Homes for Sale in Terrell Hills, TX

A quiet residential city of large lots and established homes, next door to Alamo Heights and Fort Sam Houston.

Quiet by design

A small city made almost entirely of homes

Terrell Hills is a small, almost entirely residential city east of Alamo Heights, with its own city government and police.

There is little commercial land inside the city, so streets stay quiet and shopping is a short drive away on Broadway. Lots tend to be larger than in central San Antonio, with mature trees.

01

The homes

Terrell Hills is known for established houses on generous lots: traditional two-story homes, mid-century ranch houses and newer custom builds where an older house came down.

02

What Robert would check

  • Foundation and drainage. San Antonio's clay soils move, so foundation repairs and drainage are part of every older-home inspection.
  • Pools and outbuildings. Older pools, guest houses and garages get their own inspection.
  • Trees and drainage. Mature trees are part of the appeal; check their roots against the foundation and the yard's drainage.
  • Remodeling rules. Terrell Hills handles its own permits; plans for an addition start with the city.

03

Close to everything

Broadway's shops in Alamo Heights, JBSA-Fort Sam Houston to the southeast and downtown San Antonio are all short drives.

Questions

Terrell Hills FAQ

Is Terrell Hills separate from San Antonio?

Yes. It is an independent city with its own government and police, surrounded by San Antonio.

Which school district serves Terrell Hills?

Alamo Heights ISD for most addresses. Confirm any address with the district.

Is it close to Fort Sam Houston?

Yes. JBSA-Fort Sam Houston sits just southeast of the city.

How big are the lots?

Larger than most in central San Antonio, though they vary street by street.

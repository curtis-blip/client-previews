About Stephanie Sills

Principal Agent with the Palermo Properties Team at Compass, living on the San Mateo County Coastside since 2006.

Her Story

On the Coastside Since 2006

Stephanie Sills is a top-producing real estate advisor serving Half Moon Bay and the greater San Mateo County Coastside, including Moss Beach, Montara, and El Granada. As a Principal Agent with Palermo Properties Team at Compass, she represents buyers and sellers of distinctive coastal homes, view properties, and lifestyle-driven residences near the beach.

A Moss Beach homeowner with over 25 years in the Bay Area, Stephanie pairs strategic pricing and market analytics with thoughtful, client-centered guidance, helping clients move forward with clarity and confidence. She and her husband, Dan, raised their twin daughters in Half Moon Bay.

Before Real Estate

Twenty Years in Software, Then Real Estate

Before real estate, Stephanie spent 20 years in internet software, data management and consulting, leading client relationship and customer success teams.

She brings the same habits to a sale: read the evidence, make a plan, then watch what buyers actually do and adjust. In her words, the asking price is a strategic tool, not the scorecard.

How She Works

Four Habits Her Clients Notice

  • Prepare First

    Paint, floors, staging and repairs before the photos, often paid through Compass Concierge and done by vendors she has vetted.

  • Price From Evidence

    Comparable sales, then what buyers do once the home is out: who tours, who asks for disclosures, who comes back.

  • Launch in Phases

    A private exclusive, then Coming Soon, then the open market, so the price is tested before the public sees it.

  • Stay Reachable

    Clients describe answers within minutes, weekly calls from another time zone and vendor visits she attends in person.

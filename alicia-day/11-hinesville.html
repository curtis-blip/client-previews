01 / Liberty County

About Hinesville

Hinesville sits in Liberty County, one of the ten counties The Day Group covers. The largest market in the ten counties, and the fastest moving. Fort Stewart sets the rhythm.

Everything below describes the town and its market. If you want to know what your budget buys here specifically, that is a five minute conversation rather than a web page.

02 / Known for

What Hinesville is known for

Fort Stewart

The installation sits alongside the city and drives much of the housing demand. Rotations mean the market turns over faster here than anywhere else we work.

Newer housing stock

More recent construction than the smaller towns, and more of it. Subdivisions rather than scattered rural lots.

Coastal access

Closest of the ten to Savannah and the coast, which matters for commuting and for weekends.

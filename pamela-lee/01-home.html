Common Questions

What buyers and sellers often ask.

How well do you actually know Dublin and Laurens County?
I was born and raised in Dublin. I know the neighborhoods, the schools, the back roads, and the families. When you ask about an area, you are getting answers from someone who has lived in it, not someone who just sells in it.
Do you only work with buyers in Dublin, or do you cover the whole county?
I serve more than 40 ZIP codes across Laurens County and surrounding GA. From Dublin and Dudley to Dexter and the smaller communities, if it's in or near Laurens County, I can help.
I'm relocating from out of state. Can you help me get a feel for the area?
Absolutely. A lot of my clients are moving in from elsewhere. We'll talk through what you're looking for, what your day-to-day is like, and I'll match you to the parts of the county that fit.
How do you decide what my home is worth?
I run a real comparative market analysis using recent local sales, current inventory, and condition. Zillow's estimate is a starting point, not an answer. My valuation considers what's actually moving in your specific neighborhood right now.
I've never bought a home before. Where do I start?
With a conversation. We'll talk through budget, lender preferences, the areas you want, and the practical timeline. Then I'll set up a search that filters down to homes worth your time.
How quickly do you respond?
Same day in almost every case. Real estate moves fast in Laurens County, and a slow agent costs you opportunities. Call (478) 342-5200 or email me directly.

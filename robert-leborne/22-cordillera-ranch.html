Homes for Sale in Cordillera Ranch, TX

A gated Hill Country community of large homesites and a private golf club, east of Boerne.

Ranch land, gated

Large homesites in the Hill Country

Cordillera Ranch is a gated community in Kendall County east of Boerne, known for large homesites, Hill Country views and a private golf and social club.

Many buyers purchase a homesite and build, so the community mixes finished custom homes with land. The Guadalupe River and Guadalupe River State Park are nearby.

01

Build or buy

A good share of Cordillera Ranch sales are homesites rather than houses. Buying land to build means choosing a builder, working through architectural review and planning for a longer timeline.

02

What Robert would check

  • Architectural review. Plans go through the community's review before you build; ask for the guidelines early.
  • Water and septic. Many homesites rely on wells or community water and septic systems; confirm what serves the lot.
  • Club membership. The golf and social club is separate from the property owners association.
  • The school boundary. Boerne ISD serves most addresses; confirm with the district.

03

The river and the hills

Guadalupe River State Park is a short drive away, and Boerne's Main Street and the Cibolo Nature Center are about 15 minutes west.

Questions

Cordillera Ranch FAQ

Is Cordillera Ranch gated?

Yes. It is a gated community in Kendall County.

Can I buy land and build?

Yes. Homesites are a large part of the market, and plans go through architectural review.

Which school district serves Cordillera Ranch?

Boerne ISD for most addresses. Confirm any address with the district.

Is the golf club part of the HOA?

No. The club is separate from the property owners association; ask about membership.

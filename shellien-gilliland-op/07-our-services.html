Buyer, Seller, And Behind-The-Scenes Support

How Team Shellien Works For You.

Having the right real estate team makes all the difference. My name is on the line, and I take full accountability throughout the transaction.

What We Do

Four Client-Facing Services.

Buyer Representation

Full buyer-side guidance from pre-approval to closing keys. Strategy, tour management, negotiation, due diligence, and closing coordination. First-time buyers and out-of-state buyers welcome.

Seller Representation

Pricing strategy, prep and staging, professional marketing, offer negotiation, and a transaction coordinator on every file. The 8-step process is in writing and shared with you.

Home Valuation

A real comparable-sales analysis, not an online estimate. Includes positioning recommendations and a no-obligation conversation about your options.

Out-Of-State Client Support

Virtual tours, video walk-throughs, neighborhood drive-arounds, and coordinated repairs for sellers managing properties from a distance. Standard service, not an upcharge.

Our Operating Model

The Crew That Makes It Work.

Beyond Shellien, a small group of trusted professionals handles the work behind every successful transaction. Most prefer to stay out of the spotlight, but their fingerprints are on every closing.

Garrett Gilliland · Assistant Realtor

Shellien's son, licensed Realtor. First-time buyer specialty, signage and staging support, day-to-day team operations.

Transaction Coordinator

Keeps every deadline, document, and disclosure on track from offer to closing day.

Listing Assistant

Manages signage, lockboxes, MLS data, and the daily logistics behind every active listing.

In-House Handyman

Small repairs, staging touch-ups, last-minute fixes.

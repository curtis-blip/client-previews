The Florida side of owning a home

What Christina checks before you make an offer

Most of what decides the real cost of a Florida home never shows in the listing photos.

Christina asks for homeowners insurance quotes before you write the offer, not after it, and looks up the home's flood zone and elevation so you know what flood coverage could mean for your budget. On an older home she explains what an insurer looks for in a 4-point inspection, and why the age of the roof matters as much as its condition.

On the water, she asks who last inspected the seawall and what shape the dock is in. In a condominium or a deed-restricted neighborhood, she reads the association's rules on rentals, pets and repairs with you while there is still time to change your mind.

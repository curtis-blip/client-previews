STORY
The Idea

Will built the team around one sentence: clients first equals clients for life.

Will Hutson · Founder

It started with one agent who got tired of watching people get rushed through the most expensive decision of their year. So Will did the opposite. He answered the phone. He told sellers the truth about price, even when the truth cost him the listing. He stayed in touch after closing, when most agents disappear.

Word travels in towns like Woodstock and Canton. One happy seller becomes a referral, that referral becomes a repeat buyer, and a few years in you have a team. Every agent on it is a REALTOR, held to the National Association of REALTORS Code of Ethics.

Working with The Hutson Group

Our office is on East Bells Ferry Road in Woodstock, GA. From there we cover twelve counties across North Atlanta, including Cherokee, Cobb, Forsyth, Fulton, Hall, and Paulding, and cities like Canton, Marietta, Kennesaw, Roswell, Alpharetta, and Cumming. If your home is in North Georgia, odds are we know the street.

Both, depending on what you need. Will stays close to every client, and you also get a specialist matched to your situation, whether that is a buyer's agent who knows Cherokee County inside out or a listing agent who handles your marketing. You are never handed off and forgotten.

Will has been licensed since 2017 and has closed more than 750 transactions totaling over $200 million in career sales. You can confirm any Georgia agent's license at the Georgia Real Estate Commission.

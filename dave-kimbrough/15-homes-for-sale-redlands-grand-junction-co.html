Living in the Redlands

Below the monument, across the river from downtown

The Redlands is the country south and west of the Colorado River. The county's joint plan runs it from the Highway 340 bridge at Fruita to the Gunnison River, with Colorado National Monument along its southern edge.

About 9,061 people live in the Census-defined Redlands (2020). Part of it is inside Grand Junction and part is unincorporated county, and its subdivisions run from The Ridges and Redlands Mesa to Redlands 360, which the city expects to build out over about 25 years.

01

The Ridges and Redlands Mesa

The Ridges is a platted development inside Grand Junction's city limits, recorded in numbered filings, with addresses on West Ridges Boulevard. City records speak of the Ridges/Redlands Mesa development as one area, and The Enclave at Redlands Mesa is a planned development inside The Ridges.

Redlands Mesa is also the name of the golf course, 18 holes designed by Jim Engh. Searching by either name? The saved search for this area takes in both.

02

Redlands 360, planned for the next 25 years

Redlands 360 is a 600-acre planned development in the City of Grand Junction, set out under Ordinance 5051 in 2022 with an outline plan for the whole site. It lies south of the Redlands Parkway's junction with Highway 340, between South Camp Road and Highway 340, and north of The Ridges.

The ordinance anticipates building out over about 25 years. Ask Dave which phases have homes for sale now.

03

Spyglass Ridge, and which side of the city line

Spyglass Ridge is a platted subdivision inside Grand Junction's city limits, recorded with Mesa County in numbered filings. Its saved search is drawn by the subdivision's name, so every listing filed under it shows there.

Not all of the Redlands is in the city. The Census counts it as a place with no government of its own: part is inside Grand Junction and part is unincorporated Mesa County, and a 2002 plan shared by the county and the cities of Grand Junction and Fruita covers the whole area. Confirm which side of the line an address is on before you buy.

04

Water came before the houses

The Redlands stayed desert rangeland until 1905, when the Redlands Irrigation Company brought water from the Gunnison River, and the first peach orchards were growing by 1907. In 1925 ownership passed to the shareholders, and the company took the name it has today, Redlands Water and Power Company.

It still supplies irrigation water south and west of the confluence of the Gunnison and the Colorado, through a hydroelectric plant, four pump stations and four lift ditches. If a listing mentions irrigation water, ask whether shares come with the home.

05

At the foot of the monument

The Redlands takes its name from its red soils and from the monument's red cliffs and canyons, which rise as much as 2,000 feet above the valley floor. Colorado National Monument was set aside in 1911. Civilian Conservation Corps crews did most of the work on Rim Rock Drive from 1933, and the 22-mile road was finished in 1950.

Monument Road climbs to the east entrance, and Highway 340 runs west to the other one, about three miles from Fruita. Tiara Rado, a City of Grand Junction golf course, sits with the monument to its west, and the Connected Lakes section of James M. Robb Colorado River State Park has trails and fishing piers by the river.

Questions

Redlands FAQ

Is the Redlands part of Grand Junction?

Partly. The Redlands is a Census place with no government of its own. Parts of it, including The Ridges and the Redlands 360 site, are inside Grand Junction's city limits, and the rest is unincorporated Mesa County. Confirm a specific address before you buy.

What is Redlands 360?

A 600-acre planned development in the City of Grand Junction, south of the Redlands Parkway's junction with Highway 340 and north of The Ridges. The city's Ordinance 5051 set its outline plan in 2022 and anticipates build-out over about 25 years.

Are The Ridges and Redlands Mesa the same place?

City records treat them as one area, the Ridges/Redlands Mesa development. The Ridges is the platted development, in numbered filings; Redlands Mesa is also the name of the golf course and of The Enclave at Redlands Mesa, a planned development inside The Ridges.

Which schools serve the Redlands?

Mesa County Valley School District 51. Broadway Elementary and Redlands Middle School are inside the Census Redlands, and the county's 2002 plan also names Wingate Elementary among the area's schools. Schools are assigned by address, so check the district's boundary information for a specific home.

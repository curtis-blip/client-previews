Where We Work

Ten towns, ten different markets

Victoria County and the towns around it each have their own price per acre, their own insurance picture and their own quirks. Here is what we know about each one.

County Seat

Victoria County

The region's hub. Established neighborhoods, newer builds out north, and the deepest inventory in the area.

On The Bay

Port Lavaca

Waterfront and near-water living. Wind insurance and a WPI-8 certificate change the math here.

Acreage

Inez

Land, shops and room to spread out, a short drive east of Victoria. Well and septic country.

Historic

Goliad

One of the oldest towns in Texas. Ranch land, historic homes and a genuinely walkable square.

Lake Texana

Edna

Jackson County seat, minutes from Lake Texana. Steady values and a strong small-town school draw.

DeWitt County

Cuero

Turkey Fest, restored Victorians and some of the best value per square foot in the region.

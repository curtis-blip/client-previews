Common Questions

Concierge Questions, Answered

Are there services Concierge does not cover?

Concierge works with a wide range of vendors for your convenience and covers over 100 improvement types. Stephanie helps determine which services qualify for your Coastside property during your consultation.

When do I pay for Concierge services?

Zero due until closing. Repayment is triggered when your home sells, you terminate your listing agreement with Compass, or 12 months pass from your Concierge start date, whichever comes first. Depending on your state of residence, fees or interest may apply.

Who coordinates the vendors?

Stephanie handles the coordination using her trusted Coastside vendor network. You approve the work and budget. She manages the timeline, the contractors, and quality control so you focus on your move.

How do I participate in the program?

Contact Stephanie for a walkthrough of your property. She will assess which improvements make sense for your home and outline the Concierge process from start to finish. Call (650) 678-5707 or use the form above.

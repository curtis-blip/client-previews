Troon North

Far north Scottsdale. Giant saguaros, granite boulder outcrops, and two desert courses with the valley below them.

Home/ Communities/Troon North

The character

Desert first, golf second

Troon North sits at the far northern edge of Scottsdale, near Pinnacle Peak Park, where the High Sonoran Desert is at its most dramatic. Granite boulder formations, mature saguaros and long sightlines across the valley are the reason people buy here.

The community is organized around Troon North Golf Club and its two desert courses, but the golf is set into the landscape rather than laid over it. Lots run large and density stays low, so houses sit apart from one another rather than shoulder to shoulder. That is the practical difference between Troon North and the master-planned communities further south: you are buying space and quiet as much as amenity.

Architecture leans southwestern and contemporary desert, much of it custom. Trailheads for hiking and mountain biking are minutes from most front doors, and the McDowell Sonoran Preserve is the back yard.

Living here

The practical detail

Housing

Large custom lots dominate, with southwestern and contemporary desert architecture. Lower density than the master-planned communities to the south, and a good deal of the inventory is custom rather than production built.

Golf

Troon North Golf Club runs two courses through the boulder fields. Unlike Desert Highlands, membership is not tied to property ownership here.

Outdoors

Direct access to hiking and mountain biking, with Pinnacle Peak Park and the wider City of Scottsdale parks system close by. The preserve trail network runs to over two hundred miles.

Schools

The area falls within Paradise Valley Unified School District. Boundaries are set by address, so check the district’s own map for any specific property.

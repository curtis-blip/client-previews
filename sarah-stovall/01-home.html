Meet Sarah

Born and raised in Atlanta, Sarah Stovall leads Stovall Properties Group with CENTURY 21 Results in Cumming.

Her clients buy first homes, sell long-held family properties, add second homes and build investment portfolios. She served as 2020 president of the Northeast Atlanta Metro Association of REALTORS® and as a Georgia REALTORS® director in 2022, and earned the CENTURY 21 CENTURION® award from 2023 to 2025.

“I understand that real estate is often a person’s largest asset, so I handle each detail with the care and professionalism it deserves.”

Sarah Stovall

Off the clock, you will find her in her Jeep exploring new neighborhoods, at an outdoor concert, or at home with Cooper and Boomer, the dogs, and Charlie and Paprika, the cats.

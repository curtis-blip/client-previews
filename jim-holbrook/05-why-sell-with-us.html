Why sell with Jim

Sell Your House in Murrieta and Temecula

Presentation, pricing, exposure and negotiation, with Jim on every offer himself.

When you hire Jim, you get Jim

The broker does the selling

On every listing, an experienced staff handles the details, and Jim handles the parts that decide the price: the pricing, the offers and the negotiation.

Simply putting a home on the MLS isn't a marketing strategy. Competition is what drives a price up, and competition comes from exposure: more qualified buyers seeing your home, sooner, at its best.

Questions

Selling with Jim

How does Jim price a home?

From the recent sales of comparable homes and the homes on the market now, then adjusted on evidence once buyers start responding.

What does the marketing include?

Staging advice, professional photography, the MLS and the home search sites it feeds, and targeted advertising beyond them.

Can I sell if I have already moved away?

Yes. Several of Jim's reviews are from owners who sold from out of state, with the team handling the house and Jim in touch by phone and email.

Do you handle trust and estate sales?

Yes. Trustees describe Jim explaining what the sale needed and running it for them from a distance. Your attorney or tax advisor handles the legal and tax side.

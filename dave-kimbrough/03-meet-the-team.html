The Kimbrough Team

Meet The Kimbrough Team

Dave Kimbrough and the brokers and marketing staff who work each sale with him at The Agency Grand Junction on Main Street.

Dave Kimbrough · Managing partner

Dave leads every sale himself

Dave has been licensed in Colorado since 2003. He leads The Kimbrough Team at The Agency Grand Junction, the Main Street office he and his sister, Jan Kimbrough Miller, opened as its managing partners in February 2026.

He walks each house with the owners before it is listed and says plainly which repairs will pay for themselves and which will not. Then he sets the plan and the price, negotiates the offer, and runs the inspection, the appraisal and every deadline with the team behind him.

He came to real estate from pharmaceutical sales. Colorado Associate Level Real Estate Broker, license FA.040033615.

About Dave

The team

Who you will hear from during your sale

Around Dave are the brokers who work alongside him and the marketing and listing staff behind the photography, drone, video and the listing itself. When you call with a question, it reaches someone who already knows your file.

Every broker on the team is licensed in Colorado and works under The Agency Grand Junction, where Jan Kimbrough Miller is the responsible broker.

With Dave

The brokers on your file

  • Responsible broker

    Jan Kimbrough Miller

    Dave's sister and his fellow managing partner at The Agency Grand Junction. She works new construction and commercial listings, and came to real estate after 25 years in the medical field as a clinician and business owner, running three physical therapy clinics.

    In her words, the two jobs are the same: “listening carefully to what people need and doing everything I can to get them to where they want to be.”

    Colorado Employing Level Real Estate Broker, license ER.100013124

  • Broker associate

    Carolee Hawkins

    Carolee has called the Western Slope home since 2003 and has lived in both Grand Junction and Montrose. Before real estate she worked in marketing, community outreach and residential property insurance adjusting, which gave her a solid construction background.

    For buyers moving from out of the area, she sends video of the homes, so they can shop the valley from anywhere.

    Colorado Associate Level Real Estate Broker, licensed since 2021, license FA.100093000

  • Broker associate

    Paula Kochevar

    A Grand Junction native, Paula works with first-time buyers, investors, relocating families, and owners moving up or downsizing, across Grand Junction, Fruita, Palisade, the Redlands, Whitewater, Loma and Mack.

    Before real estate she built a foundation in human resources, management and business operations.

    Colorado Associate Level Real Estate Broker, licensed since 2020, license FA.100090599

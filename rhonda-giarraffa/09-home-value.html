Know the Number That Matters

What a home value really is, and why it matters

There are three numbers people throw around, and they rarely agree. The assessed value is what the county uses to calculate property taxes, and it often trails the real market by years. An online estimate, like a Zestimate, is an algorithm guessing from public data it has never seen in person. Neither one knows your dock, your water depth, or the view from your back porch.

Market value is the only number that counts when it's time to sell. It's what a real, qualified buyer will actually pay for your home in today's market, on today's lake. For lakefront and luxury homes, that number swings hard on details a national algorithm can't see, which is exactly why a strategic, local valuation is worth more than any instant estimate.

Get it right and your home sells with momentum. Get it wrong and it sits, collects price cuts, and trains buyers to wonder what's wrong with it. We help you start at the right number, backed by data, so your home is positioned to sell. When you're ready, the seller's guide walks through what comes next.

Our Method

How we find your value

A defensible number comes from real sales and real analysis, not an algorithm reading tax records from a server. Here is what goes into yours.

Real Lanier Comps

We price against homes that actually sold on the lake, not a national average. Recent waterfront and luxury sales in your cove and county set the baseline, so the number reflects this market, today. See what's moving now in featured listings.

Lake-Specific Factors

Dock type and permit, water depth, slip access, frontage, and the quality of the view can move a lakefront home's value by a wide margin. We weigh the details an automated estimate simply cannot see, and that vary by community.

MIT-Trained Analysis

Rhonda's certification in Data Science in Real Estate from MIT brings real analytical rigor to the number. We read the trends behind the comps, not just the headline price, so your valuation holds up to scrutiny when an offer comes in.

Priced to Sell, Not Sit

The goal is a number that attracts qualified buyers and protects your equity. We position your home to sell with momentum from day one, not to languish through round after round of price cuts.

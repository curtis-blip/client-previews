Downtown and the river

Old Main Street and the Riverwalk meet at the same waterfront

Bradenton is a city on the Manatee River and the seat of Manatee County, a status an 1888 election gave it. Old Main Street runs from the old downtown to the Twin Dolphin Marina, and the Riverwalk follows the river between the Green and DeSoto bridges.

The Village of the Arts, from 9th Avenue West to 17th Avenue West, fills a few blocks of restored historic homes with galleries and studios. State Road 64 (Manatee Avenue) crosses downtown, U.S. 301 runs along First Street, and State Road 684 (Cortez Road) leads to Anna Maria Island.

Questions

Bradenton: the seat, the ferry and the ballpark

Is Bradenton the county seat?

Yes. An 1888 election made the town the Manatee County seat, and the present courthouse at 1115 Manatee Avenue West was built from 1913 to 1914.

How do I get to Anna Maria Island?

By road, State Road 684 (Cortez Road) crosses to the island. By water, the Gulf Islands Ferry leaves the Riverwalk Pier for the Historic Bridge Street Pier and Coquina Beach, about 50 minutes each way; the operator sets the days it runs.

Who plays at LECOM Park?

The Pittsburgh Pirates have trained there since 1969. The ballpark was originally built in 1923.

How do I get around without a car?

Freebee runs a ride service in downtown Bradenton through its app, and Manatee County Area Transit serves the wider area. MCAT Route 99 connects Bradenton with downtown Sarasota.

Which airport serves Bradenton?

Sarasota Bradenton International Airport (SRQ). Tampa International is about a 45-minute drive.

Should I check the flood zone before I buy in Bradenton?

Near the Manatee River or a marina, yes, and look up the house, not the street. The flood zone guide shows how to find the zone and the elevation.

Applicants

Rental Application Process for San Diego Properties

Red House Property Management makes applying for a rental straightforward. Here is everything you need to know about our application requirements, screening process, and what to expect after approval.

Getting Started

How to Apply

Follow these five steps to submit your rental application for any Red House property in San Diego.

1
Browse available listings on our rentals page to find properties that match your needs, budget, and preferred San Diego neighborhood.
2
View the property in person. An in-person viewing is required before we process any rental application.
3
Submit an online application through our portal. There is a $30 non-refundable application fee per applicant.
4
All adults age 18 and older who will reside in the property must submit individual applications.
5
Provide all required documentation within 48 hours of submitting your application. Incomplete applications may delay processing.

Application Requirements

What We Need from Applicants

Required Documents

  • Pay stubs from the most recent 2 to 3 months
  • Self-employed applicants: first 2 pages of tax returns plus 4 to 6 months of bank statements
  • Verification of other income sources (child support, Social Security, retirement, etc.)
  • Copy of valid government-issued ID, driver's license, or passport
  • Completed online application with electronic signature and $30 payment

Qualification Criteria

  • Good credit history and FICO score
  • Combined household income approximately 2.5 to 3 times the monthly rent
  • No eviction history
  • Proper notice given to current or previous landlord
  • No outstanding funds owed to previous landlords
  • Three years of verifiable residency history
  • Minimum renter's or tenant liability insurance required
Screening

Approval Process

Applications are evaluated based on overall qualification, not on a first-come, first-served basis. Our screening includes a thorough review of credit history, background checks, eviction records, income verification, and rental references from previous landlords. We look at the full picture to find responsible tenants who will take care of the property and meet their lease obligations. You will be notified of the decision as soon as the review is complete.

After Approval

Move-In Timeline & Funds

Once your rental application in San Diego is approved, here is what happens next.

Security Deposit

The security deposit is due within 2 business days of approval. Certified funds only (cashier's check or money order). Personal checks are not accepted for deposits.

Electronic Lease

Your lease agreement will be emailed for electronic signature within 24 to 48 hours after we receive your deposit. Review all terms carefully before signing.

72-Hour Signing Window

The lease must be signed and returned within 72 hours of receipt. If the lease is not signed within this window, the approval offer may be rescinded.

Raised Here. Still Here.

Michael and Joanna Ruesch. A husband and wife team, born and raised in the Lake Whitney area.

Michael + Joanna Ruesch

One of us builds. One of us designs.

We were both born and raised in the Lake Whitney area, and we are raising our own family here. We believe we have been called to serve people well, and we try to show that in how we handle a transaction: straight answers, clear communication, and the same care we would want on the other side of the table.

Between us that is fifteen years in this market and two trades. It means you are not getting one opinion twice. You are getting a read on the structure and a read on the space, at the same showing.

Michael

Construction

He came into real estate from the building side, so he reads a house structurally. What is cosmetic, what is expensive, and what should change the number you offer.

Joanna

Design

She came in from design. She sees what a room could be, and she knows which changes actually return money at resale and which ones simply cost it.

How we work

What you can expect

Honest representation

We will tell you when a house is wrong for you, when the timing is wrong, and when the number you have in mind is not the number the market has in mind.

Two trades, one showing

Michael reads the structure and Joanna reads the space, at the same visit. You get a build opinion and a design opinion without paying for two appointments.

Questions

Home value questions

How accurate is an instant estimate?

It is a starting point. It cannot see inside the house, so condition and updates are guesses. For a price you can list at, ask Robert for a written valuation.

What is the difference between market, appraised and assessed value?

Market value is what a buyer will pay. An appraisal is a lender's independent opinion of that value for a loan. The appraisal district's value is for property taxes and often lags the market.

Does a valuation cost anything?

No. Robert's valuations are free and carry no obligation to list with him.

What does Robert need from me?

The address, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the approximate square footage, the year built if you know it, and any notable improvements.

Can I protest my property tax value?

Yes. If the appraisal district's value looks too high, you can protest it by the deadline on your notice. The Texas Comptroller explains how.

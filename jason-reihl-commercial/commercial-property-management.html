Commercial Property

Commercial Property Management for Coastal San Diego

Two levels of management. One accountable local partner.

Tell Us About Your Property
Our Focus

Attentive Management, Clear Communication

Your commercial property deserves attentive management, clear communication, and a team that understands its day-to-day needs. Red House Property Management provides personalized service for owners of small to modest-sized office, retail, and mixed-use properties throughout coastal San Diego.

These properties are our focus. We understand the importance of keeping tenants satisfied, maintaining inviting common areas, controlling operating expenses, and giving owners a clear picture of their investment. Our boutique approach combines hands-on oversight with practical systems that keep property operations organized and ownership informed.

Two Service Options

Choose the Level of Support That Fits Your Building

Whether you want comprehensive management or support with the physical operation of your building, we offer two flexible service options.

Option 1

Full-Service Property Management

Our full-service management brings financial oversight and daily property operations together under one local team. We handle the details that demand your time while helping you make informed decisions about your property.

Tenant communication is an essential part of our role. We serve as a consistent point of contact, respond to operational concerns, and help maintain productive relationships between ownership and tenants. Through regular updates and practical recommendations, we keep you connected to your property’s performance and priorities.

Services include

Rent collection and payment tracking
Annual operating budgets
Financial reporting
CAM/NNN accounting and annual reconciliations
Routine maintenance coordination
Vendor scheduling and work-order follow-up
Monthly property walks to review conditions and identify maintenance needs
Option 2

Facilities Management

For owners who prefer to retain financial and administrative responsibilities, our facilities management service provides dependable oversight of the physical property.

Our attention extends to common areas, building systems, and the everyday details that shape how tenants and visitors experience your property. You receive maintenance updates and recommendations to help plan repairs and improvements.

Services include

Monthly property walks
Routine maintenance coordination
Responses to tenant maintenance requests
Vendor scheduling and follow-up

This service focuses on property operations; rent collection, accounting, budgeting, CAM/NNN reconciliations, and lease administration remain with ownership or its designated team.

Tailored to Your Building

The Right Fit for Your Property

From a neighborhood retail building to a professional office property or a mixed-use investment, our services are tailored to the building, its tenants, and your preferred level of involvement. Leasing, renewals, and major project management can be addressed through separately agreed services.

Responsive Service. Consistent Follow-Through.

With Red House, you gain a local management partner focused on responsive service, consistent follow-through, and the long-term care of your property.

Common Questions

What Commercial Owners Ask Us

What types of commercial property does Red House manage?

Small to modest-sized office, retail, and mixed-use properties throughout coastal San Diego, from a neighborhood retail building to a professional office property or a mixed-use investment.

What is the difference between full-service and facilities management?

Full-service management brings financial oversight and daily property operations together under one local team, including rent collection, operating budgets, financial reporting and CAM/NNN reconciliations. Facilities management covers the physical property: monthly property walks, routine maintenance, tenant maintenance requests and vendor follow-up. With facilities management, rent collection, accounting, budgeting, CAM/NNN reconciliations and lease administration remain with ownership or its designated team.

Do you handle leasing, renewals and major projects?

Leasing, renewals, and major project management can be addressed through separately agreed services. Tell us what your property needs and we will talk through the options.

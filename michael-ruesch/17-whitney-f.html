Homes for Sale in Whitney, Texas

A small town three miles from a very large lake

Whitney sits in Hill County, about three miles east of the water it is named after. That gap splits the market in two, and most people call us about one side of it and buy on the other. Everything below is what we would tell you before you made an offer on either.

What to expect

Three markets that share a name and almost nothing else. They price differently, they inspect differently, and they suit completely different people.

01

In town

The 1876 grid around Main Street. Square lots, the schools a few minutes away, everyday shopping on Main Street. If being able to walk somewhere matters to you, this is the part of Whitney where you can.

Homes in town
02

On the water

Three miles west, about 23,500 acres of the Brazos held back by a Corps of Engineers dam since 1951, with White Bluff behind its gate on the shoreline. The Corps still controls that shoreline, and it decides what you can build and what you can put in the water.

Lake and White Bluff
03

Out on land

Every other direction is open country. Acreage, pasture and long driveways, with neighbors further apart than you are used to. Out here the questions change from square footage to water, septic and access.

Farm and ranch

The lifestyle

What the area is actually built around, and what day to day looks like.

The lake

About 23,500 acres on the Brazos. Lake Whitney State Park sits on the east shore with camping, trails and public water access, no shoreline ownership required.

State park

Everyday

Groceries and a handful of longtime restaurants are in town. For a bigger shop it is Hillsboro, twelve miles east at the interstate, or Waco, about thirty miles south.

Schools

Whitney ISD runs four campuses, all in town. Boundaries change and an address outside the city limits is not automatically included, so check a specific property with the district.

Whitney ISD

Getting around

State Highway 22 meets I-35 at Hillsboro. No bus route, though the Heart of Texas Rural Transit District runs a weekday dial-a-ride across the county.

Rural transport

What we would check

None of this is special to Whitney. It just comes up here more than it does in a city.

The shoreline is federal

Lake Whitney belongs to the Corps of Engineers. Docks and retaining walls are permitted through them, not the county. We find out what actually transfers to you.

The water line moves

The lake is run for flood control first, so the level shifts through the year. We will tell you what the shoreline looks like at both ends of that.

Weekend place or year-round

A lot of what went up near the water started life as a weekend place. Some have been made into full-time homes and some have not. Ask us which is which.

Well and septic

Outside the city limits, expect a well or rural water and a septic system. We have both checked on their own, never folded into the home inspection.

Who maintains the road

County road, private easement or association street. Three different answers, and three different costs across the years you own the place.

The association paperwork

White Bluff and several lake developments have property owners associations. We get you the dues and the governing documents while you can still act on them.

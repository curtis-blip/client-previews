Our Story

The lake and luxury team that lives the lifestyle

Team Giarraffa specializes in Lake Lanier and North Georgia luxury real estate, serving buyers and sellers who want more than a transaction. They want trusted guidance, strong strategy, and a clear understanding of the lifestyle they're buying or selling. Led by Rhonda and Louis Giarraffa, our team focuses on lakefront homes, lake-access properties, and luxury homes on and around Lake Lanier, helping clients make confident decisions in one of Georgia's most unique and desirable markets.

Lake and luxury real estate require a deeper level of expertise. From dock permits, water depth, protected coves, lake views, and marina access to pricing strategy, market positioning, and elevated home marketing, we understand the details that directly impact value and buyer appeal. Rhonda's certification in Data Science in Real Estate from MIT brings an added layer of insight to pricing and market analysis, allowing our clients to approach each move with clarity, confidence, and strategy. It is the same thinking that shapes our home valuations and the strategy in our seller's guide.

Our mission is simple: educate, guide, and protect our clients' best interests while helping them move toward the lifestyle they truly want. Whether you're selling a luxury lake home, searching for the perfect waterfront retreat through our buyer's guide, or exploring upscale living near Lake Lanier, Team Giarraffa brings the local knowledge, marketing expertise, and personal commitment needed to help you move well and Live Life Loud.

What we know cold

The corners of the Lanier market where deep, specific knowledge separates a great move from an expensive mistake.

Buying or selling on the water is a different game. We work through dock permits, water depth, protected coves, and shoreline access so you understand exactly what you're buying or what your home is worth. Luxury lake properties demand sharper pricing and elevated marketing. We position high-end homes on and around Lake Lanier to reach the right buyers, with presentation and strategy that match the caliber of the home. From estate-style waterfront to gated lake communities, we know how this tier moves, and our seller's guide shows the marketing plan behind it.

Rhonda's certification in Data Science in Real Estate from MIT brings real market analysis to every decision. We price homes to sell rather than sit, and we help buyers make confident, informed offers grounded in actual Lanier sales data, not a generic online estimate. Request a strategic read on the home value page.

About Corey O’Kelley

A finance discipline most brokers don’t bring.

Corey O’Kelley brings more than 20 years of experience in specialty finance to commercial real estate, a background built on structuring complex transactions and negotiating optimal outcomes. Raised by Realtors, he developed an early understanding of market dynamics, and over the past eight years in the Pensacola area he has built a deep network of professional and personal relationships.

He specializes in retail and office assets, with a focus on sales, leasing, and investment strategy, and is currently pursuing the CCIM designation. Corey combines financial discipline with local market insight to deliver results that maximize value and reduce risk.

Results you can quantify, not just a deal that closes.

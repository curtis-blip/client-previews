Meet the Founder

Will Hutson, real estate agent in Woodstock, GA

Will Hutson is a real estate agent in Woodstock, GA and the founder of The Hutson Group at Century 21 Results. He works with buyers and sellers across Woodstock, Canton, Marietta, Kennesaw, Roswell, Alpharetta, Cumming and the counties around North Atlanta. Licensed since 2017, he has closed more than 750 transactions and over $200 million in career sales, roughly 75 homes a year.

The Hutson Group ranks in the top 1% of teams serving North Georgia, and was named the #2 Century 21 team in the state of Georgia for homes sold (2025). Clients have left more than 100 five-star reviews. Numbers are nice. What they actually mean is that a lot of North Georgia families trusted one team with the biggest purchase of their lives, and would do it again.

STORY
The Idea

Will built the team around one sentence: clients first equals clients for life.

Will Hutson · Founder

It started the way a lot of good things do, with one agent who got tired of watching people get rushed through the most expensive decision of their year. So Will did the opposite. He answered the phone. He told sellers the truth about price, even when the truth cost him the listing. He stayed in touch after closing, when most agents disappear.

Word travels in towns like Woodstock and Canton. One happy seller becomes a referral, that referral becomes a repeat buyer, and a few years in you have a team. Today The Hutson Group is a full bench of agents, marketers, and client advocates, but the rule has not changed. Take care of the person in front of you and the business takes care of itself. Every agent on the team is a REALTOR, held to the National Association of REALTORS Code of Ethics.

That is why so much of our work comes from people we have already helped. Read what those clients say about working with The Hutson Group, or see how we sell homes across North Georgia. The standard is the same whether you are buying your first place in Holly Springs or selling a lake house on Lanier.

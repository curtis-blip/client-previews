How tenant placement works

Step 01

Consultation

We start with a conversation about your property, your goals, and your timeline, so Tom understands exactly what you want from the lease before anything goes to market.

Step 02

Price the rental

Tom analyzes comparable rentals and current demand across the South Loop and near south side, so your unit is priced to attract strong applicants without leaving money on the table.

Step 03

Market & list

Your rental is photographed and listed where renters actually look, with the same care Tom brings to a sale, reaching qualified prospects quickly.

Step 04

Screen applicants

Every applicant is screened carefully: credit, income, background, and references, so the person who signs is someone you can rest easy about.

Step 05

Showings

Tom coordinates and hosts showings, fielding questions and presenting your property well, so you do not have to juggle calls or meet strangers at the door.

Step 06

Review, recommend & lease

Tom reviews the qualified applicants with you, recommends the strongest fit, and handles all the lease paperwork from signing to move-in, exactly the experience Jerry and Trish describe below.

Renters & landlords

Rentals and tenant placement, answered

Tom prices your rental, markets and lists it, screens every applicant, hosts the showings, and then reviews the qualified prospects with you before handling the lease paperwork through move-in. You stay informed at each step while Tom does the legwork.

Every applicant is reviewed for credit, income, background, and rental references. The goal is simple: place a reliable tenant you can rest easy about, not just fill the unit quickly.

Tom's tenant placement fee is one month of rent, earned when a qualified tenant signs the lease. He will walk through exactly what is included during your first conversation, with no surprises.

It depends on the unit, the price, and the season, but a well-priced South Loop rental marketed correctly tends to move quickly. Tom keeps you posted on interest and showings so you always know where things stand.

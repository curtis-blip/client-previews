Where we work

White Bluff, ranch land, and the water

Whitney is not one market. There is the gated side, there is the land behind it, and there is the shoreline. We work all three.

01

White Bluff

The gated community on Lake Whitney, and the part of this market people most often ask us about first. A mix of weekend places and full time homes, with amenities that hold their value when the wider market moves.

Explore White Bluff
02

Farm & Ranch

Acreage, pasture, and working land across Hill and Bosque counties. Buying land is a different transaction from buying a house, with its own questions about water, fencing, minerals, and access, and it deserves someone who has walked the ground.

See land and ranch property
03

Lake Homes

Waterfront and water access homes on Lake Whitney. What looks like a small difference on a listing sheet, corps line, slope to the water, how a dock is permitted, is usually the difference that matters most.

Seller's Guide

How to sell your home in North County, with strategy not guesswork.

Eight steps to a clean close, the right buyer, and a price that holds.

Selling shouldn't feel like a leap of faith. With the right pricing, prep, and marketing, your listing wins on its first weekend.

Jerre brings courtroom precision to every contract and two decades of North County pricing memory to every comp. Her marketing isn't an MLS dump and a lockbox. It's photography that flatters the home, copy that tells the story, and showing-feedback she actually shares with you, the same week, in plain language. That mix is how listings sell quickly, cleanly, and at the number they were meant to hit.

The eight-step listing process

From valuation through closing, every step has a purpose and Jerre runs them all herself.

01 Valuation

Get an honest valuation

The starting line is a real number, not a number you wish were true. Jerre pulls comps that actually compare, similar block, similar lot, similar finishes, recent enough to matter, and reads them against active inventory and average days on market in your specific pocket of North County.

You'll get her read on what your home would do today, what it would do with two weekends of prep, and where the real ceiling lives. The home valuation form on this site is the easiest way to start that conversation.

02 Pricing

Strategic pricing, not aspirational pricing

Pricing too high feels like leaving room to negotiate. In practice, it leaves the home sitting. North County buyers and their agents notice when a listing sits past its first weekend, and the conversation quietly shifts from "do we offer over asking" to "how much room are they going to give us."

The first-week offer almost always beats the third-week offer. Jerre prices the home where the market is, not where you wish it were, and the data backs it up listing after listing.

03 Pre-list prep

What's worth doing, what's not

Coastal homes don't need a renovation to show well. They need a clean palette, decluttered surfaces, light staging in the rooms buyers fixate on, and a triage list of small repairs that would otherwise show up on inspection.

Jerre walks the home with you and tells you, plainly, what the return is on each line item. Sometimes paint and a power-wash is the entire list. Sometimes it's worth a weekend of light staging. She'll tell you which one this house actually needs.

04 Photography + Video

Professional photography and walkthrough video

How the listing shows up online is what drives the calls. The hero shot, the twilight exterior, the drone pull-back that puts the home in its coastal context, the walkthrough video buyers send to their out-of-area spouse, all of it earns its keep before anyone schedules a showing.

Every Jerre Walker listing gets professional photography, twilight when it serves the home, drone where the lot deserves it, plus a walkthrough video and social-ready vertical cuts for Instagram and Facebook reels.

05 Marketing

Marketing across the right channels

The MLS is the floor of the marketing plan, not the ceiling. Once your home is listed, it syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, and the Showcase IDX feeds across every San Diego brokerage, that part is automatic.

Above that floor is the work that actually moves homes: targeted social ads geo-fenced to the right zip codes, an agent-network preview to the AARE roster and Jerre's two-decade contact list, and a launch-week promotion plan that gets the home in front of the right buyer pool before the first open house.

06 Showings

Showings that convert

A home tour is a sales conversation, even when no one is selling out loud. Jerre coordinates the showing schedule, runs broker preview the week of launch, and sets the open-house cadence around how coastal North County buyers actually shop, Saturday morning for the serious ones, Sunday afternoon for the casual scroll.

You'll get feedback after every showing, the same week, in plain language. No mystery. No "the agent didn't say much." If something's not landing, you'll hear about it while there's still time to adjust.

07 Offers + Negotiation

Reviewing offers and negotiating terms

Price is one variable on the offer sheet. Contingencies, close date, lender quality, escrow size, the way the buyer asks for credits, all of it shapes whether the deal actually closes at the number on page one.

Jerre's read on contracts comes from her years in the legal field before real estate. She'll walk you through every offer side by side, flag the soft spots, and negotiate the terms that actually change the outcome, not just the headline price.

08 Closing

Closing and what comes after

Final walkthrough, settlement statement, the buyer's lender clearing-to-close, and the moment keys actually change hands, every step is something Jerre coordinates personally so nothing falls between escrow, the lender, and you.

After close, she stays in your corner. Vendor referrals, tax-document questions in April, future buying or renting plans, a quick check-in next year when your neighbor wonders what their place is worth. Most of her business comes from past clients and their referrals, that doesn't happen by accident.

Pricing is the most important decision you'll make.

Overpricing rarely costs you in week one. It costs you in week three, week five, and the eventual price reduction that signals to every agent watching that there's room. Stale listings get stale-listing offers. The home that sells at the right number on the first weekend almost always nets more than the home that chases the market downward for two months. The math is consistent, and it's why pricing gets more of Jerre's attention than any other decision in the listing process.

  • 01 Most homes that sell quickly are priced right from week one.
  • 02 Properly priced listings see most activity in the first weekend.
  • 03 Every price reduction signals to buyers that there's more room.

Typography Hard Rules
  • Two families only: Montserrat for headings, Roboto for everything else. Never add a third.
  • No italic, serif or coloured words inside headings. Headings are one colour (ink, or white on a photo).
  • Keep pairs together on a line break (non-breaking spaces, as in "Roanoke&nbsp;VA"); no lone last word.
  • No text under 12px anywhere, including tags, labels and the footer.
  • No em or en dashes in any copy.

Voice and Content Reminders

Copy Hard Rules
  • No em or en dashes anywhere. Use commas, periods, colons or the middle dot.
  • Write REALTOR® with the mark, and "MKB, REALTORS®" as the brokerage.
  • No generic AI phrases ("dream home", "seamless experience", "your trusted partner") or filler city words ("thriving", "vibrant", "nestled").
  • Every H1 reads like a person and names something specific from the brief.
  • Stats on the home page are placeholders until DPOR and Zillow figures settle them; do not publish them as final.

Anti-Patterns

Never Do These
  • Rounded corners on anything but the quick-search pills and social icons
  • Gold anywhere but the hero search button and the call bar's Call half
  • Italic, serif or coloured words in headings
  • Full-bleed reviews, home value, financing or closing blocks (they float)
  • Buttons inside a section head beside the H2
  • Text below 12px
  • The stock IMPress search form, or a listing card without the firm line

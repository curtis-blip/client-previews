About

Twelve years in one valley

Licensed as a Broker-Salesperson and working as a property manager across Las Vegas, Henderson and North Las Vegas. In English, Nepali, Hindi and Urdu.

Somnath Rana · Broker-Salesperson · AARE

Sales and management, in four languages, across the Las Vegas Valley.

I have spent twelve years in Las Vegas residential real estate, licensed as a Broker-Salesperson and working as a property manager. Many of the people I work with are investors, and a lot of them own from out of state.

So the business runs in two halves. One finds the property and checks whether the numbers hold up. The other looks after it afterwards: tenants, leases, rent, repairs and inspections. I have never wanted to run only one of those, because the half you skip is the half where an owner loses money.

I work in English, Nepali, Hindi and Urdu. In a city with a large Nepali community that turns out to matter more than I expected when I started, because the biggest purchase of someone's life is easier to think through in the language they grew up in.

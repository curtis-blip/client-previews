Corpus Christi homes for sale

Corpus Christi is the metro hub of the Coastal Bend, a bayfront city built around its downtown waterfront, the seawall, and the Sparkling City by the Sea identity. The market spans historic in-town neighborhoods, the booming Southside, military housing tied to NAS-CC, and barrier-island access just minutes from downtown. It is the largest, most liquid market on this list, and it is where most of my buyers and sellers start.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: coastal-city pace with a real downtown, the bayfront seawall, marinas, the USS Lexington, and the Texas State Aquarium.
  • Geography: Nueces Bay to the north, Corpus Christi Bay to the east, Padre Island a short causeway away.
  • Housing stock: entry-level bungalows in older neighborhoods through new-build Southside subdivisions and waterfront luxury along Ocean Drive.
  • Employers: NAS Corpus Christi, the Port of Corpus Christi, Driscoll Children's Hospital, CHRISTUS Spohn, refining and energy.
  • Notable amenities: bayfront walk, downtown marina, Mustang Island access, year-round Gulf weather.

Kings Crossing Corpus Christi

Kings Crossing is my signature neighborhood. It is a master-planned, prestigious subdivision on the Southside, set between Cimmaron and S Staples just past Yorktown Boulevard. Custom-built homes, larger lots, wider streets, mature trees, and a level of pride-of-ownership that you feel the moment you turn into the neighborhood. If you want Southside Corpus done right, this is it.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: quiet, established, family-oriented streets with the kind of mature landscaping the rest of the Southside is still growing into.
  • Housing stock: custom-built single-family homes on larger lots, established luxury, no two homes alike.
  • Streetscape: wider streets, mature trees, considered planning, walks and bike rides feel like a different city.
  • Location: Cimmaron to S Staples corridor, past Yorktown Boulevard, easy reach to Southside shopping, dining, and SPID.
  • Schools: Corpus Christi ISD Southside cluster, multiple Southside school options nearby.

Flour Bluff homes for sale

Flour Bluff is the peninsula community sitting between Oso Bay and the Laguna Madre, just south of the Southside and right at the doorstep of Padre Island and NAS Corpus Christi. It is anchored by the #1-ranked Flour Bluff Independent School District, and that single fact drives more buyer relocations into this corner of Corpus than almost any other school story in the Coastal Bend.

What it's like

  • Schools: #1-ranked Flour Bluff ISD, the headline reason families target this community.
  • Military: immediate proximity to NAS Corpus Christi, a top pick for active-duty Navy and contractor families.
  • Lifestyle: peninsula living with bay water on both sides, easy boat-ramp access, Padre Island a quick drive over the JFK Causeway.
  • Housing stock: entry to mid-tier single-family, mix of established and newer construction, strong rental demand from NAS-CC.
  • Notable amenities: Padre Island access, Oso Bay Wetlands Preserve, fishing piers and boat ramps within minutes.

North Padre Island homes for sale

North Padre Island is barrier-island living inside the Corpus Christi city limits. You drive over the JFK Causeway and you are home, with the beach literally at your back door, the Laguna Madre on the bay side, and Packery Channel cutting through the middle. Buyers come here for fishing, boating, and the lifestyle of living somewhere people travel to vacation.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: beach plus fishing from your back door, sunrise on the Gulf side, sunset on the bay side.
  • Geography: Mustang and Padre stretch, Bob Hall Pier area, Packery Channel jetties, Padre Island National Seashore right next door.
  • Housing stock: mix of canal-front homes with private docks, beachside condos, and single-family on stilts. Entry to high-tier.
  • Notable amenities: Packery Channel surf, Bob Hall Pier, Padre Balli Park, marinas and bait stands in every direction.
  • Schools: Corpus Christi ISD Island schools, served by Flour Bluff and Corpus Christi feeder patterns depending on address.

Port Aransas homes for sale

Port Aransas, Port A to anyone who has been here twice, sits at the north tip of Mustang Island. It is reached by ferry from Aransas Pass or by causeway from North Padre, and the town keeps a deliberately small-town beach feel. Vacation-rental income, second-home buyers, and full-time residents who refuse to live anywhere else all share these streets.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: beach town, golf-cart culture, jetty fishing, ferry rides at sunset, no traffic-light obsession.
  • Geography: north Mustang Island, Mustang Island State Park to the south, the Port A jetties and ship channel to the north.
  • Housing stock: condos for vacation-rental buyers, canal homes for boat owners, single-family for full-timers. Mid to high-tier with strong second-home demand.
  • Notable amenities: Mustang Island State Park, the jetty, Roberts Point Park, ferries to Aransas Pass, working harbor.
  • Schools: Port Aransas ISD, small-district feel, well-regarded.

Rockport TX homes for sale

Rockport is the artsy, fishing, birding-and-bay-shrimp coastal town a short drive up the coast from Corpus. It draws second-home buyers and retirees who want a slower coastal pace, a working harbor, an active arts scene, and proximity to wildlife refuges. Hurricane Harvey put Rockport through a hard rebuild, and the town has come back with new energy and updated housing stock.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: small coastal town, boutiques and galleries, Texas Maritime Museum, harbor culture, slower pace than Corpus.
  • Birding and nature: Aransas National Wildlife Refuge nearby, whooping crane country, the Bay Education Center.
  • Housing stock: mix of waterfront, canal homes, second-home cottages, and single-family inland. Entry to mid-tier with waterfront premium.
  • Notable amenities: Rockport Beach, the Texas Maritime Museum, Bay Education Center, working harbor.
  • Buyer profile: retirees, second-home buyers, fishing and birding enthusiasts.

Portland TX homes for sale

Portland sits across the Harbor Bridge from Corpus, on the north side of Nueces Bay. It is the family-friendly bedroom community for buyers who work in Corpus, in the Port, or up the coast at the refineries, but want newer construction, quieter streets, and Gregory-Portland ISD for the kids. It has been one of the steadier-growing communities in the Coastal Bend.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: quiet, family-friendly, suburban pace with bay views and short commutes back into Corpus.
  • Schools: Gregory-Portland ISD, well-regarded, the headline draw for relocating families.
  • Housing stock: newer construction in active subdivisions, entry to mid-tier, strong starter and move-up inventory.
  • Commute: Harbor Bridge into downtown Corpus, easy access to the Port and energy corridor.
  • Notable amenities: Indian Point Park, Sunset Lake Park, bay-side trails, growing dining scene.

Aransas Pass real estate

Aransas Pass is the working-coastal town that serves as the gateway to Port Aransas. Catch the ferry here, or stay and put in at the Lighthouse Lakes Paddling Trails. It keeps a blue-collar, honest-coastal-town character that the more polished tourist towns do not, and the price point reflects that. A solid pick for buyers who want coastal access without the second-home premium.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: working-coastal, fishing-first, no-pretense town with the ferry, the shrimp boats, and the harbor at the center of life.
  • Recreation: Lighthouse Lakes Paddling Trails, jetty fishing, Conn Brown Harbor, easy access to Port A by ferry.
  • Housing stock: entry-level single-family, older established neighborhoods, some new build, lower entry point than Port A or Rockport.
  • Buyer profile: first-time buyers, fishing and paddling enthusiasts, second-home buyers priced out of Port A.
  • Schools: Aransas Pass ISD, small-town feel.

Odem TX homes for sale

Odem is a small-town, agricultural-roots community north of Corpus Christi, just off US-77. Buyers who want a lower price point, larger lots, more breathing room, and a slower pace, with Corpus a manageable commute, find their home here. It is the un-suburb of the Coastal Bend.

What it's like

  • Lifestyle: small-town Texas, agricultural surroundings, friendly streets, no big-city pace.
  • Housing stock: entry-level single-family on larger lots, older homes, occasional new build, room to add a shop or a barn.
  • Commute: roughly 25 minutes north of Corpus on US-77, manageable for buyers working in town.
  • Buyer profile: first-time buyers, families wanting acreage on a budget, anyone trading square footage on the lot for square footage in the house.
  • Schools: Odem-Edroy ISD, small district, community-focused.

Kingsville TX homes for sale

Kingsville sits south of Corpus in Kleberg County, anchored by Texas A&M University-Kingsville and surrounded by the legendary King Ranch. NAS Kingsville keeps a steady flow of military families in the market, the university adds a college-town energy, and the price point lets buyers stretch their budget further than they can in Corpus proper.

What it's like

  • University: Texas A&M University-Kingsville sets the cultural pace, with college sports, events, and a steady rental market.
  • Military: NAS Kingsville drives consistent demand from active-duty Navy aviation and contractor families.
  • Heritage: King Ranch country, one of the most storied working ranches in the world, sits at the city's doorstep.
  • Housing stock: affordable single-family, mix of older established homes and newer subdivisions, entry to mid-tier.
  • Schools: Kingsville ISD plus the A&M-Kingsville footprint.

Overview

Living in Plano

Plano is a mature market, and the housing reflects three distinct eras: the original town centre and the 1970s and 1980s neighbourhoods around it, the large 1990s and 2000s build-out to the west, and the newer high-density development around Legacy. Each one prices differently and attracts a different buyer.

The corporate presence is the engine. Toyota Motor North America, JPMorgan Chase's regional campus and the wider Legacy West development brought a sustained relocation pipeline, and that demand tends to concentrate in specific pockets rather than spreading evenly across the city. Knowing which pocket a buyer actually wants is most of the work.

The Basics

Collin County. 20 miles north of dallas. Served by Plano ISD.

Who Buys Here

Corporate relocation, move-up buyers inside Plano ISD, and downsizers staying in the area.

Around Town

What is nearby

Legacy West

The mixed-use district that anchors the corporate corridor, with retail, dining and residential together.

Downtown Plano Arts District

The original town centre, on the DART line, with local restaurants and a theatre.

Arbor Hills and the trail system

A large nature preserve and a connected network of trails running through the city.

DART rail access

Light rail into Dallas, which matters more to relocation buyers than to long-time residents.

Questions

Questions Buyers and Sellers Ask Leticia

How long has Leticia been a real estate agent?

Leticia has been licensed in California since 2008 and has worked with North County San Diego buyers and sellers ever since.

Which areas does Leticia serve?

Carlsbad, Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos, Fallbrook and Escondido, in North County San Diego.

Does Leticia speak Spanish?

Yes. Leticia works in English and Spanish and can explain each part of a purchase or sale in either language.

What is the best way to start buying a home?

Start with a short consultation. Leticia will go over pre-approval, the towns you are considering and what fits your budget.

Do I need to be pre-approved before I look at homes?

Pre-approval shows what you can spend and makes your offer stronger. Leticia can introduce you to local lenders if you do not have one.

How does Leticia help sell a home?

With a pricing strategy based on recent local sales, professional marketing and negotiation of the offers that come in.

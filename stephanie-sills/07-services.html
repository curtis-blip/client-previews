Bridge Loan Services

Here's the scenario: you found a home in El Granada with the ocean views you've been waiting for, but your place in Montara hasn't sold yet. Without bridge financing, you watch that listing go to someone else. Compass Bridge Loan Services let you tap the equity in your current home to make an offer now, before your sale closes.

This matters on the Coastside because inventory is limited and desirable properties move fast. Coordinating two transactions at once is stressful. Bridge financing removes that pressure, giving you the flexibility to buy on your timeline and sell your current home without the rush.

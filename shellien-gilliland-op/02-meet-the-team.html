Shellien Gilliland · Founder

An Idaho-Native Realtor Who Gets The Details Right.

Shellien Gilliland, founder of Team Shellien, is a trusted real estate professional known for delivering an exceptional client experience rooted in expertise, dedication, and genuine care. A Twin Falls native with deep family roots in Buhl, Shellien has built a reputation as the agent who not only gets results, but makes the journey smooth, informed, and even enjoyable.

Clients consistently describe Shellien as responsive, detail-oriented, and fiercely committed. Whether working with first-time buyers, relocating families, or seasoned sellers, she takes the time to truly listen, then goes to work crafting a strategy tailored to their goals. From staging advice and market insights to navigating negotiations and closing complexities, she is known for handling every detail with precision and professionalism.

What truly sets Shellien apart is her willingness to go above and beyond. She is frequently praised for solving unexpected challenges, accommodating out-of-state clients with virtual tours and hands-on support, and being available whenever her clients need her. Many clients note that working with her feels less like a transaction and more like having a trusted advocate and friend by their side.

With deep knowledge of the Magic Valley market, strong local connections, and a passion for helping people succeed, Shellien leads a team that operates with efficiency, integrity, and heart. The result: homes sold quickly, buyers confidently guided, and clients who become lifelong referrals.

If you're looking for a real estate partner who's as invested in your outcome as you are, Team Shellien delivers, every step of the way. Shellien is a local advocate of the American Red Cross, the great Idaho outdoors, College of Southern Idaho Basketball, Small Business Development, and Financial Peace University.

Areas Of Expertise

How I Help.

The work I do most often, told plainly.

Most first-time buyers don't know what they don't know. I walk you through pre-approval, the Magic Valley map, what's negotiable, what's not, and where the real costs are. No assumptions, no industry jargon, no rushing the decision.
Virtual tours, video walk-throughs, neighborhood drive-arounds on your schedule. I've moved buyers from out of state and helped sellers manage homes from across the country. The distance never becomes your problem.
Pricing strategy, staging recommendations, professional photography, and a marketing mix tailored to the home and the buyer pool. I tell you what's actually moving the needle, not what generic checklists say.
Twin Falls, Jerome, Kimberly, Buhl all read differently. I'll ask about your work, your commute, your kids' schools, your budget, then tell you honestly which Magic Valley town fits and which doesn't. Saves months of looking at the wrong houses.
Parents downsizing while kids buy their first home, families consolidating across two properties, or estate transitions that need both a buyer and a seller side handled together. I coordinate the moving pieces so you don't have to.

Homes for Sale on Turkey Creek Mesa, CO

Homes and land from the Telluride Ski Ranches to The Preserve, and what to check before you buy on the mesa.

Turkey Creek Mesa at a glance

Five subdivisions south of Mountain Village

Turkey Creek Mesa lies south of Mountain Village along Highway 145, in unincorporated San Miguel County. The county groups the Telluride Ski Ranches, Elk Run, Raspberry Patch and West Meadows here, and The Preserve sits beside Raspberry Patch.

The Ski Ranches, Elk Run and Raspberry Patch sit on the uphill, east side of the highway; West Meadows and The Preserve lie to the west. Parcels run from about an acre in the Ski Ranches to more than 60 acres in Raspberry Patch, on ground at roughly 9,300 to 9,830 feet. Downtown Telluride is about 6 to 8 miles away by road.

01

Telluride Ski Ranches

The county calls it simply Ski Ranches, and its outline touches the Mountain Village town line. The county's parcel records show 208 parcels, 199 of them under 3 acres, with a median of about 1.6 acres. The entrance is off Highway 145 south of Mountain Village, about 6.6 miles from downtown Telluride by road and 5.7 from the airport.

Inside run about 6.7 miles of county roads, Fox Farm Road, Skunk Creek Road and Mount Wilson Way among them, and the county plows every segment; Fox Farm and Skunk Creek are paved. The Town of Mountain Village supplies the water but not sewer (the Town's service area). County zoning at its center is medium density, and it borders National Forest land.

02

Elk Run and Raspberry Patch

South of the Ski Ranches, two smaller subdivisions climb the uphill side of Highway 145.

  • Elk RunJust south of the Ski Ranches. The homeowners association describes a gated community of 30 lots with its own water system, tennis courts, private hiking trails, a maintained road system and a design review board, and EPA lists the Elk Run Homeowners Corporation as a community water system on groundwater. Its main road is paved, and the subdivision borders National Forest.
  • Raspberry PatchJust south of Elk Run: seven parcels of about 17 to 65 acres, the county records show, at about 9,830 feet. National Forest land lies within a kilometer, and Trout Lake and Ophir are farther south.

03

West Meadows and The Preserve

Across Highway 145, both western subdivisions border National Forest and a 229-acre conservation easement held by The Nature Conservancy.

  • West MeadowsWest of the Ski Ranches: 16 parcels of 35.0 to 35.5 acres in the county's records, zoned for forestry and agriculture. Colorado Parks and Wildlife maps elk winter range within it. By road it is about 6.3 miles to downtown and 5.4 to the airport.
  • The PreserveAcross the highway from Raspberry Patch: 15 parcels with a median of about 13.5 acres, at about 9,300 feet. Preserve Drive, the road in, is unpaved, and downtown is about 8 miles away by road.

04

What Garrett would check on the mesa

  • Water and sewerThe Ski Ranches get water from the Town of Mountain Village but no sewer, so ask how a house there handles wastewater; Elk Run runs its own water system. In The Preserve, Raspberry Patch and West Meadows, ask where the water comes from. A new or replacement well needs a state permit, and a septic system under 2,000 gallons a day needs a county on-site wastewater permit.
  • Roads in winterThe county plows every county road inside the Ski Ranches, and Elk Run's association maintains its own roads. Preserve Drive is unpaved, and Alta Lakes Road, which the county maintains, is not plowed in winter.
  • Lot countsCounty parcel records do not always match older lot counts: the Elk Run association gives 30 lots where the county shows 37 parcel records, which can include common tracts. Read the plat and the covenants for the lot you want.
  • ZoningCounty zoning differs by subdivision: medium density in the Ski Ranches, low density at Elk Run, Raspberry Patch and The Preserve, forestry and agriculture in West Meadows. Check a parcel in the county's Zone District Finder.

Questions

Turkey Creek Mesa FAQ

Where is Turkey Creek Mesa?

South of Mountain Village along Highway 145, in unincorporated San Miguel County. The county groups the Telluride Ski Ranches, Elk Run, Raspberry Patch and West Meadows here; The Preserve sits beside Raspberry Patch.

Do the Telluride Ski Ranches have Mountain Village water?

Yes. The Town of Mountain Village supplies water to the Ski Ranches, but not sewer service. The county plows every county road inside the subdivision.

How big are the lots in West Meadows and The Preserve?

County records show West Meadows as 16 parcels of about 35 acres each, and The Preserve as 15 parcels with a median of about 13.5 acres. Both border National Forest and a 229-acre Nature Conservancy easement.

Which school district serves Turkey Creek Mesa?

Telluride School District R-1, for all five subdivisions. Always confirm a specific address with the district.

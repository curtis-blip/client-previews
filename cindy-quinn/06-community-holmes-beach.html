The Middle of the Island

Holmes Beach

The largest and most varied of Anna Maria Island's three towns. From Key Royale's bridge-accessed luxury enclave to canal homes, Gulf-front condos, and classic coastal cottages, Holmes Beach has the most inventory and the widest price range.

About the City

The heart of Anna Maria Island.

Holmes Beach occupies the middle three miles of Anna Maria Island. It's the largest of the three municipalities, the most varied in inventory, and the most active real estate market on the island.

Key Royale sits on its own bridge-accessed peninsula along the bay, home to the island's highest-end waterfront estates. The rest of Holmes Beach mixes interior neighborhoods, deep-water canals, and a long stretch of Gulf-front condos.

Rental rules vary by zoning district. Weekly and short-term rentals are allowed in some areas, prohibited in others. If investment or rental income matters to you, verify eligibility early.

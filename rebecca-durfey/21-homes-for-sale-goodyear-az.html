Spring Training and the Estrella Mountains

Questions

Goodyear FAQ

What spring training teams play in Goodyear?

The Cleveland Guardians and the Cincinnati Reds, at Goodyear Ballpark.

How big is Estrella Mountain Regional Park?

19,840 acres, near where the Gila and Agua Fria Rivers meet.

Which school districts serve Goodyear?

Litchfield Elementary School District and, for high school, Agua Fria Union High School District serve parts of Goodyear. Confirm the schools for any address with the district.

Which roads serve Goodyear?

Interstate 10 and Loop 303, which meet at an interchange in Goodyear (ADOT).

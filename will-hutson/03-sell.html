The Process

Eight steps from list to keys

A clear path from the first walkthrough to handing over the keys and collecting your check.

01
Step One

Get It Ready

Small fixes return big money. We tell you which ones are worth it before you spend a dollar.

  • Repairs first: the leaky tap, the sticking door, the cracked plaster a buyer's eye lands on.
  • Light updates: paint and fixtures that lift the room without a renovation budget.
  • A deep clean: top to bottom, so the home photographs and shows the way it should.
02
Step Two

Set the Price

We sit down with the comps together and land on a number you are comfortable with.

  • Real comparables: what actually sold near you, not what neighbors hoped to get.
  • Your competition: the homes a buyer will tour the same weekend as yours.
  • Your call: we bring the data, you set the price you are confident listing at.
03
Step Three

Stage It

Staged homes sell for more. We walk you through every room so it lands with buyers.

  • Furniture and flow: arrange each space so it reads open and easy to move through.
  • Depersonalize: help buyers picture their life here, not study yours.
  • The largest return: targeted staging where it moves the needle, skipped where it does not.
04
Step Four

Take Photos

Most buyers meet your home on a phone screen first. Ours is shot to win that scroll.

  • Professional photographer: on the team, not a phone snap from the listing agent.
  • Right light, right angles: rooms shown at their largest and brightest.
  • Drone and video on our Platinum listings, so the lot and setting read in full.
05
Step Five

Create the Materials

Our in-house graphic designer builds everything that markets your home.

  • Property brochures and feature sheets buyers take home and remember.
  • Neighborhood flyers aimed at the people most likely to make the move.
  • A listing built for the feed: ads ready for every online platform buyers use.
06
Step Six

Show It

The hardest stretch for any seller. We keep it organized so your life still runs.

  • Showings and open houses, coordinated around your schedule.
  • Show-ready, not stressed: a simple routine to keep the home presentable.
  • Events that draw buyers: neighborhood open houses and the occasional wine and cheese.
07
Step Seven

Review Offers

When offers land, we negotiate on your behalf and read every one with you.

  • The full picture: price is one line, terms and conditions decide the rest.
  • Hard negotiation: counters on price, closing date, and contingencies.
  • Your decision: accept, counter, or pass, with our read on each option.
08
Step Eight

Closing Day

We schedule the closing, you hand over the keys and collect the check.

  • Coordinated closing: we line up the date and keep every party on track.
  • The walkthrough: a final check before the paperwork is signed.
  • Keys and proceeds: the part you have been working toward.

Pricing Strategy

Price it right and the market answers fast

Two things set your list price: where the market sits today, and how your specific home stacks up against it.

Read the market first

Seller's market

Demand outruns supply. Fewer homes for sale tilts the leverage to you, and well-priced listings move quickly.

Buyer's market

Supply outruns demand and prices soften. Sharp pricing and strong marketing matter most here.

Balanced market

Supply and demand sit close to even. Neither side holds a clear edge, so positioning decides the sale.

Four things that set your number

  • 1
    Past salesWhat comparable homes near you actually closed at recently.
  • 2
    Active listingsYour direct competition, the homes buyers tour alongside yours.
  • 3
    ConditionUpdated kitchens and renovated rooms earn more than dated finishes.
  • 4
    The marketBuyer's versus seller's conditions set the strategy around all of the above.

The Offer Explained

An offer is more than its price

Seven things make up an offer. Knowing them is how you tell a strong bid from a shaky one before you sign.

01

Price

The dollar figure a buyer is offering for your home.

02

Deposit

The earnest money a buyer puts down to show they mean it.

03

Terms

How they intend to pay, from cash to a specific financing plan.

04

Conditions

What must happen first, such as inspection, appraisal, or financing.

05

Inclusions & exclusions

Which appliances, fixtures, and extras stay with the home.

06

Irrevocable

The window you have to accept before the offer expires.

07

Closing date

When the sale completes and possession changes hands.

Then

You decide

Accept, counter, or refuse. We read each option with you.

Good to Know

Seller questions, answered

It depends on price, condition, and the season, but well-priced North Georgia homes often go under contract in days to a few weeks. Zillow Showcase listings are about 10% more likely to go pending within 14 days. If speed is the priority over top dollar, ask us about a cash offer through our cash offer program.

The CMA weighs recent sales on your street, your home's condition, and the homes you are competing against right now, which a public estimate cannot see.

Real estate commissions are not set by law and are fully negotiable. Your costs typically cover marketing, brokerage fees and admin, and the agent fee, and we lay all of it out in the listing agreement before you sign. There are no surprise charges at closing.

Usually the high-value fixes, yes, and we tell you which ones earn their cost back before you spend. Staged homes tend to sell for 1 to 5% more, so even light staging often pays for itself. Our marketing day prep guide walks you room by room.

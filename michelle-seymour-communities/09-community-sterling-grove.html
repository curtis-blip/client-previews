Living in Sterling Grove

Golf, a clubhouse and new homes behind the gates

Sterling Grove is a 780-acre, staff-gated community by Toll Brothers in Surprise, near Cotton Lane and Cactus Road just west of Loop 303. It opened in 2020 on a former working farm and is planned for about 2,200 homes, with 55+ collections and all-age collections behind the same gates.

Toll Brothers builds every home here, from condos and lock-and-leave villas to estate homes, in one- and two-story designs. The 20,000-square-foot clubhouse sits on a 10-acre amenity center. Before you buy new, read my guide to buying new construction.

Questions

Sterling Grove questions I hear most

Is Sterling Grove a 55+ community?

Partly. Sterling Grove has 55+ collections and all-age collections behind the same gates. I can tell you which a home is in before you see it.

What are HOA fees in Sterling Grove?

Most homes run about $200 to $300 a month. I explain the full picture in my video on Sterling Grove HOA fees above, and I confirm the exact amount for any home you are considering.

Which schools serve Sterling Grove?

Sterling Grove is in the Dysart Unified School District. The east side is zoned to Rancho Gabriela Elementary and Sonoran Heights Middle School, the west side to Mountain View School, and all of it to Shadow Ridge High School.

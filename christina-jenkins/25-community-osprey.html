Who governs Osprey

Osprey has no city hall: Sarasota County runs it

Osprey is a census designated place, not a city, so Sarasota County is the government for every address in it. U.S. 41 runs through it. A house here is on the water, behind the gates of The Oaks Club, or in a neighborhood beside preserved land.

Historic Spanish Point, a campus of Selby Gardens, and Oscar Scherer State Park are both inside the community, and the Legacy Trail passes through it. Public schools are the Sarasota County School District's.

Inside the limits

Two preserves and a trail inside Osprey

  • Historic Spanish Point

    The second campus of Selby Gardens (Marie Selby Botanical Gardens), at 401 North Tamiami Trail. selby.org

  • Oscar Scherer State Park

    A Florida State Park inside Osprey, run by Florida State Parks. floridastateparks.org

  • The Legacy Trail

    A trail that passes through Osprey.

  • The Oaks Club

    Two golf courses named Eagle and Heron, tennis and pickleball, behind gates at 301 MacEwen Drive.

Gated community

The Oaks Club: three gated communities

The Preserve, Clubside and Bayside are the club's three gated communities. Its website says membership begins with home ownership in one of them, and lists a 7,500 square foot fitness and wellness center beside the golf and tennis.

Questions

Osprey, The Oaks Club and the county

Is Osprey a city?

No. Osprey is a census designated place in Sarasota County with no city government of its own.

Is The Oaks Club part of Osprey?

Yes. The club's website gives its address as 301 MacEwen Drive, Osprey, and describes a private country club with three gated residential communities, The Preserve, Clubside and Bayside.

Do I have to be a member to buy at The Oaks Club?

The club says membership begins with home ownership in one of its communities. Confirm the current terms with the club before you write an offer.

Is Historic Spanish Point in Osprey?

Yes. It is the second campus of Selby Gardens, at 401 North Tamiami Trail, Osprey.

Is Oscar Scherer State Park inside Osprey?

Yes. It sits inside Osprey's limits, and Florida State Parks runs it.

Should I check the flood zone before I buy in Osprey?

On a waterfront street, yes, and look up the house, not the street. Osprey has no city government, so the records for an address are Sarasota County's. The flood zone guide shows how to look up the zone and the elevation.

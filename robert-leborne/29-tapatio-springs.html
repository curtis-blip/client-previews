Homes sit along the fairways and on the hillsides around them, from smaller homes and condos to custom houses, ten minutes from Boerne's Main Street.

What Robert would check

  • The association. Dues and rules vary between sections of the community; read the HOA documents.
  • Resort and club. The resort and its golf are separate from the homeowners association.
  • Short-term rental rules. If you plan to rent, confirm what the HOA and the county allow.
  • The school boundary. Boerne ISD serves most addresses; confirm with the district.

Boerne, ten minutes away

Questions

Tapatio Springs FAQ

Is Tapatio Springs in Boerne?

It sits just outside the city of Boerne, in Kendall County, about ten minutes from Main Street.

Which school district serves Tapatio Springs?

Boerne ISD for most addresses. Confirm any address with the district.

Is the golf course part of the HOA?

No. The resort and its course are separate from the homeowners association.

Can I rent my home short term?

It depends on the HOA and county rules; confirm both before you buy.

Living in Sun City Festival

A Del Webb community under the White Tanks

Sun City Festival is a 55+ active adult community built by Del Webb, part of PulteGroup, against the White Tank Mountains in north Buckeye. It opened in 2006, and Del Webb is still selling new homes here, including a series where yard work and exterior landscaping are handled for owners.

Life centers on the Sage and Saguaro recreation centers, with resort-style pools and spas, fitness centers and an indoor walking track, plus the smaller Senita center for clubs. Residents run more than 40 clubs and interest groups, and Festival Learning holds classes at the Sage Center. If you are weighing new against resale, my new construction guide covers what to check.

Questions

Sun City Festival questions I hear most

Is Sun City Festival a 55+ community?

Yes. Sun City Festival is an age-restricted 55+ community. Ask me how the age rules apply to any home you are considering.

What are HOA fees in Sun City Festival?

About $180 a month, billed quarterly. I confirm the current amount for any home you are considering.

Can I buy a new home there?

Yes. Del Webb is still selling new homes in Sun City Festival, and resales come up across the community. The new construction search above shows what is listed now.

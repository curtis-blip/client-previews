How a listing works

A listing here has to reach buyers who are not here yet

Most of Garrett's clients live somewhere else; reviewers on his page write from New York, Florida, Arizona and San Francisco. A listing has to reach buyers like them where they are, give them a reason to fly in, and be easy to show when they arrive.

Garrett has been licensed here since 2004, and Jessica works every sale with him. Below is how they price a property, put it in front of buyers locally and through the network, and handle the sale while you are away.

What clients say

A seller's story

The condo that was hard to sell

No parking and no dogs allowed made Ann Siner's Mountain Village condo a hard sell. Garrett tried several ways to promote it, and he kept its strong rental history in front of buyers until it sold.

Every property has something buyers want and something they will hold against it. The work is putting the first in front and pricing honestly for the second.

Pricing

A Telluride listing agent walks it first

A list price needs more than an online estimate can see.

There are fewer sales here than in a city, and fewer still that match yours: the same building, the same side of town, the same mesa with the same view. Garrett walks the property, studies the closest recent sales and what is for sale now, and builds a full market analysis from them. Then he gives you his honest number and the sales behind it.

What moves this market

Local brokers, and a network beyond the valley

In the valley

Garrett and Jessica work through Telluride Properties, which has offices in Telluride and Mountain Village. Your listing goes into the local MLS, in front of the brokers who show property here, and Garrett knows the ones whose buyers it may suit.

Beyond it

Telluride Properties is the Telluride member of Forbes Global Properties, a membership network of brokerages, so your listing also reaches buyers through the network.

Two of us on your listing

Garrett on the price, Jessica on the details

You deal with both of them for the whole sale, which matters most when you are not here to see it.

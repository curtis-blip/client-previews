Get Your Bearings in Longmont

Longmont began in 1870 as the Chicago-Colorado Colony: one square mile, stores along Main Street and homes in a grid around it. Today the city runs from McIntosh Lake in the northwest to Union Reservoir in the east, across two counties.

Stop 1

Main Street

Longmont started here. The colony’s plan put its stores along Main Street, and by the summer of 1871 there was a small town named for Longs Peak. Roosevelt Park, a short walk north, is one of the colony’s original parks: an outdoor pool, a rose garden and a seasonal ice rink.

Stop 3

Golden Ponds and the St. Vrain Greenway

Golden Ponds opened in 1990 on land the V.V. Golden Foundation gave the city: four ponds, 2.6 miles of trails and a view of Longs Peak. It is also the west trailhead of the St. Vrain Greenway, which the city calls the crown jewel of its trail system. The trail runs about 8 miles east to Sandstone Ranch. Parts are still being rebuilt after the 2013 flood, so check the city’s trail status map before you go.

A Guide to Exploring the Great Outdoors in Longmont

Stop 4

McIntosh Lake

A reservoir on the northwest side of town that the city keeps as a nature area. The rules tell you what kind of lake it is:

  • Carry-on boats only, no motors
  • Fishing and paddleboarding
  • Gravel trails and picnic areas
  • No swimming

Stop 5

Union Reservoir

On the east side, across the county line in Weld County. The city says Union Reservoir was first called Calkins Lake and is one of only a few natural lakes in Colorado. Today it has wakeless boating, a swim beach, a dog beach and a fishing pier.

Before you buy

What to Check on Any Longmont Address

Four things that change from one side of town to the other. Each takes a few minutes to look up and is worth doing before you write an offer.

Is Longmont a Good Place to Live? Five Downsides Nobody Tells You

13:17 · The honest version

  1. Which county is it in?

    Longmont straddles two counties: roughly four fifths of the city’s area is in Boulder County and the rest is in Weld County. The county decides the assessor and the tax bill. Look an address up in the Boulder County property search or the Weld County property portal.

  2. Which schools go with the address?

    St. Vrain Valley Schools covers the whole city, and attendance areas are set by address. The district’s boundary lookup shows the neighborhood schools for any address. Confirm the campus with the district before you write an offer.

  3. Is it in the floodplain?

    The September 2013 flood was the worst in the city’s history, mostly along St. Vrain Creek. The city’s floodplain map shows the FEMA floodplain for any property inside city limits, the same map lenders use.

  4. Who provides the water?

    Inside city limits it is the City of Longmont. Around the edges, Left Hand Water District and Longs Peak Water District serve some addresses. Longmont Water Utilities can tell you which applies.

Quick answers

Longmont Questions Buyers Ask

What county is Longmont in?

Most of Longmont is in Boulder County, and the east side of the city is in Weld County. County Line Road marks the change. The county sets the assessor and the property tax bill, so check it for every address you consider.

Which school district serves Longmont?

St. Vrain Valley Schools serves the whole city. Which campus goes with a home depends on its address, and the district’s boundary lookup gives the answer for any address.

How far is Longmont from Boulder, Denver and the airport?

Without traffic, Main Street to downtown Boulder is about 27 minutes, Denver Union Station about 46 and Denver International Airport about 52. Noah covers the airport, Denver and the mountains in his video “How Far Is Everything in Northern Colorado, Really?”

Yes. Start with Noah’s neighborhood guide at the top of this page, take the five-stop tour, then watch his driving tour of the whole town. When you are ready to see homes in person, tell Noah which stops you liked and he can show you homes near them.

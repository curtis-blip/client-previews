Typography Hard Rules
  • Poppins, Barlow and Caveat load from Google Fonts at the top of every page.
  • Caveat is for the tagline and Noah's sign-off only, one short line, never a heading.
  • Never substitute a system font fallback as the primary face, only as the secondary in font-stack.
  • Never introduce a fourth family.
  • No italic-serif accent spans inside H1s ("real estate, reimagined" is banned).
  • No long dashes anywhere in copy, replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.

Button Hard Rules
  • Border-radius: 0. Sharp geometry only. No rounded buttons.
  • Never use 3+ buttons in a single hero. One primary, optionally one secondary, never three.
  • Transition duration 300ms with easing cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .1, 1) on hover.
  • Always include `:focus-visible` indicator (browser default OK, or styled with accent outline).

Client Rules

Curtis's standing rules for this client. Each applies to every page unless it says otherwise.

Rules From Curtis
  • No pills. No pill-shaped buttons, chips or tags anywhere. A choice of towns is the map itself plus one plain chooser, and labels are plain caps text (2026-11-24).
  • No coloured accent line on any box edge. Top, bottom or side, on any box, panel or card (2026-11-25).
  • Closing call to action is option G. No box. One row low on a tall parallax photograph: the question and a short line on the left, two buttons on the right, over a bottom-up fade. Its photo is never the page's hero photo (2026-11-25).
  • The Communities menu is a dropdown of all 19 towns, on every page: Arvada, Berthoud, Boulder, Broomfield, Commerce City, Dacono, Erie, Firestone, Fort Collins, Frederick, Johnstown, Lafayette, Longmont, Louisville, Loveland, Lyons, Thornton, Westminster, Windsor.
  • The area map is illustrated and alive, never a flat outline. Never a dark strip under a full-bleed row of photo tiles.
  • External links: 2 to 3 official sources per town page; any core page except the home page, only where relevant. Each checked live, rel="noopener".

Town Page Pattern

One template, one record per town. The 19 community pages are built by mockups/_src/town.py from _src/town/page.html (the whole page), _src/town/<block>.html (one item of each repeated block) and _src/towns/<slug>.json (that town's words, videos, photos and sample listings). To change a block on every town, edit its file in town/ and rerun; to change one town, edit its record. Never edit a built NN-community-<town>.html by hand.

Motion

Slow, subtle motion. Reveals fade up 28px over 900ms as a section scrolls in (never the hero). Photo tiles and video thumbnails zoom 5 to 6 percent on hover; cards lift 6px. Reduced motion resolves everything to its final state.

TokenValueUse
--motion-duration-fast300msLink underline color changes, small UI feedback
--motion-duration-default300msButton hover, accordion expand
--motion-duration-slow900msSection reveal on scroll
--motion-easing-defaultcubic-bezier(.2, .7, .1, 1)All hover transitions
Motion Hard Rules
  • No bounce easing. No springs. No spinning loaders inside content.
  • Photo hover zoom is 5 to 6 percent, the house value for this client.
  • Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, disable photo-zoom and section fade-in when set.

Voice and Content Reminders

Content writers and designers both need to remember these voice rules. Even if it's the writer's job, a designer who notices an em dash or "your trusted partner" should flag it before publish.

Copy Hard Rules
  • Never use long dashes (the long dash or a double hyphen), replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.
  • Never use generic AI phrases: "dream home", "seamless experience", "every step of the way", "your trusted partner", "in today's market", "with confidence".
  • Never use generic city descriptors: "thriving", "vibrant", "nestled", "perfect blend", "world-class", "rich history", "something for everyone".
  • Never use parallel triplets ("Clear, calm, confident"), hyphenated compound metaphors ("courtroom-trained eye"), or conditional buildups ("Whether you're A, B, or C...").
  • Every page H1 references at least ONE specific item from the client's brief (years, credentials, niche, personal story).

Full doctrine: references/copy-voice.md in the Optimus Prime plugin.

Anti-Patterns Summary

The shortlist of "do not do this." Every rule below was learned the hard way on past builds.

Never Do These
  • Long dashes anywhere in copy
  • Emojis as icons (inline SVG only)
  • Rounded corners on cards, buttons, images
  • Pills, chips or tags of any kind
  • A coloured accent line on the edge of any box, panel or card
  • Carousel sliders in heroes
  • Italic-serif "register lift" spans inside H1
  • 3+ CTAs in a hero (1 default, 2 max)
  • Split heroes anywhere except a clean home-page exception
  • Two dark-background sections in a row
  • Aggressive form or large CTA inside the hero (exception: Home Valuation page widget)
  • Gradient placeholders past first draft
  • Raw shortcode embeds without styled container
  • Flat text-only service boxes (must have SVG icons)
  • Zigzag timeline layouts for process steps (use alternating image+text rows)
  • "Recently Sold Nearby" on Home Valuation page
  • 4-step process blocks shipped as production (always default to 8)
  • FAQ mode: faq on About page (use mode: expertise, Areas of Expertise)
  • Communities home-page treatment on inner pages (V2 home-only)
  • About teaser block on /about (always use full Agent Bio)

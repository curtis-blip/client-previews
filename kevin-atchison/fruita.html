Living in Fruita, CO

Twelve miles west of Grand Junction, at the mouth of the Colorado National Monument. Mountain biking built the town's reputation and it has held its own character rather than becoming a suburb.

00 Orientation

Fruita kept being a town instead of becoming a suburb

Twelve miles west of Grand Junction, at the western edge of the Colorado National Monument, Fruita had every opportunity to turn into a commuter subdivision and did not. It still has its own main street, its own school district campuses and its own reason for existing, which is mostly the trail network north of town. Mountain biking built its reputation and still brings people through, and a good number of them end up buying. You pay a premium over Grand Junction for that, and the premium has been durable, so the question is whether the town is the thing you actually want rather than whether it is worth it.

01

The town centre

A distinct small town rather than a Grand Junction suburb, with its own main street and its own character. Smaller older homes near the centre, newer building on the edges.

02

Orchard and river ground

Out toward the river and the old agricultural parcels. Irrigation, outbuildings and land still in production, fifteen minutes from a city. Land in active agricultural use is assessed differently, so changing what you do with it can change the tax bill more than people expect.

01 Before you offer

What I would check for you

Fruita carries a premium over Grand Junction and holds it. These are the things that decide whether a particular property is worth that premium.

Irrigation shares

On the agricultural ground this is the value. What conveys, from which company, and what the annual assessment is.

What is still in production

Land in active agricultural use is assessed differently from land that is not. Changing the use changes the number, sometimes substantially.

Domestic water

Several providers serve different parts of the area. Confirm which one and what it costs rather than assuming from the address.

Outbuildings and what they are permitted for

A barn, a shop and a second dwelling are three different things to the county. I check what is actually permitted before you plan around it.

Mineral rights

Severed minerals are common in Mesa County and belong in the title review.

Why people move here

Most of them came for the riding and stayed for the town. If that is not you, be honest about it, because you are paying for it either way.

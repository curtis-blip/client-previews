Wellen Park at a glance

A Master-Planned Community Between Venice and North Port

Wellen Park is a master-planned community west of I-75, reached from U.S. 41 and River Road. It is not a town of its own: depending on the address, a home sits in the City of North Port or in unincorporated Sarasota County, and its mailing address says Venice.

The land was ranch land, known in turn as Berry Ranch, Taylor Ranch and Thomas Ranch, before the City of North Port annexed it and the Legislature approved the West Villages Improvement District to build its roads and stormwater systems. The community grew up as the West Villages, took the Wellen Park name in 2020, and opened the Great Lawn, Grand Lake and The Yard in Downtown Wellen in March 2023.

01

Downtown Wellen and Grand Lake

Downtown Wellen is the community’s social hub, with restaurants, shops, a hardware store, a spa and fitness businesses looking out on Grand Lake. Its Great Lawn, Grand Lake and The Yard, a food and entertainment park, opened in March 2023, and the 2.8-mile Grand Lake Trail loops through downtown along the water on paved and shell paths. Solis Hall, a waterfront event hall, has an outdoor stage on the Great Lawn, and the events calendar for October 2026 listed a Sunday farmers market and Friday night concerts.

02

CoolToday Park and Braves spring training

CoolToday Park, on West Villages Parkway, is the spring training home of the Atlanta Braves, who arrive each February. The Braves developed it with Sarasota County, the City of North Port and West Villages and Mattamy Homes, the ballpark books other events all year, and Visit Sarasota says its Tomahawk tiki bar stays open year-round.

03

Trails, dog parks and the water

Wellen Park counts more than 30 miles of trails across the community. Oakheart Trail runs more than three miles on natural ground, with shell paths, boardwalks and a lakeside fishing pier, and Buttonwood Trail follows Manasota Beach Road for 2.5 miles. Dogs have Paw Park at Fetch, fenced with agility equipment, and Bark Park at Blue Heron, with water fountains and trails. Play Park downtown is a nature-themed play area, and there is a fishing pier at The Landing and a kayak launch at Senator Bob Johnson’s Landing.

04

The neighborhoods and the newer homes

The West Villages, the community’s first name, is now one district within Wellen Park, and neighborhoods such as Sarasota National and Grand Palm, in unincorporated Sarasota County, kept their own names. The West Villages Improvement District maintains Grand Lake and the roads and pathways of Downtown Wellen. For buying a home from one of the builders, see New Construction.

05

North Port, the county and a Venice address

Wellen Park sits in two jurisdictions. Depending on the address, a home is in the City of North Port, where Wellen Park falls in commission District 5, or in unincorporated Sarasota County, and the West Villages Improvement District spans both. Mailing addresses say Venice even where the home is in North Port, so check any parcel with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser. Golf carts are allowed on district roads and sidewalks only when licensed, registered and insured by the state of Florida, and Breeze OnDemand rides serve the Venice and North Port areas.

Questions

Wellen Park FAQ

Is Wellen Park in Venice or North Port?

Depending on the address, Wellen Park is in the City of North Port or in unincorporated Sarasota County, not the City of Venice, even though mailing addresses say Venice. Check the parcel with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser.

Which school district serves Wellen Park?

Sarasota County Schools, which serves both North Port and unincorporated Sarasota County. Confirm the school for any address with the district.

What is the West Villages Improvement District?

A special district approved by the Florida Legislature to build roads and stormwater systems. It spans the North Port and county parts of Wellen Park, and it maintains Grand Lake and the roads and pathways of Downtown Wellen.

Can you drive a golf cart in Wellen Park?

Only one that is licensed, registered and insured by the state of Florida. Other golf carts are not allowed on the district's roads and sidewalks.

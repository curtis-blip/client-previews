The Selling Process

Five steps from list to sold.

01

Set the right price.

Pricing is the most important decision in the listing. We run a real comparative market analysis using actual recent sales in your specific neighborhood, not a Zillow number. The right list price brings the right buyers and protects your equity when you sell your home in Dublin GA.

02

Prep the home.

Before we list, we walk through what to fix, what to leave, and what to stage. I'm honest about what matters in the Dublin market and what doesn't. Most homes need less work than people think.

03

Market it well.

Professional photography, MLS listing written to attract serious buyers, exposure across the platforms where Dublin buyers actually look (Zillow, Realtor.com, social, my own network). Open houses if the property warrants it.

04

Negotiate the offer.

When offers come in, we evaluate them together. Price is one factor. Terms, contingencies, financing strength, and timeline matter just as much. I'll show you the trade-offs and recommend the path that gets you the cleanest close.

05

Close clean.

Inspection responses, appraisal coordination, repairs, title work, closing prep. I stay on top of the timeline so you don't have to chase anyone. By closing day, all the work is already done.

Seller Questions

Things sellers ask me most.

How do you decide what to list my home for?
I run a real comparative market analysis (CMA) using actual recent sales in your neighborhood, current active listings as competition, and the condition of your home compared to those comps. Zillow's number is a starting point, not an answer. The CMA accounts for what's actually selling.
How long does it take to sell your home in Dublin GA?
It depends on price, condition, and location. Some homes go under contract in days, others take months. The biggest predictor is whether the price matches the market when we list. Overpricing costs you time and ultimately money.
What do I need to fix before listing?
Less than most people think. We walk through together and I'm honest about what matters to Dublin buyers and what doesn't. Some repairs return their cost at sale. Others don't. I'll tell you the difference.
Do I have to be moved out before listing?
No. Most of my listings are still occupied during showings. We'll talk through how to keep the home showing-ready and how showings get scheduled so it stays manageable for your family.
How much does it cost to sell with you?
My commission is competitive for the Dublin market and is paid by you at closing from the sale proceeds, not out of pocket. We'll talk through the full breakdown of seller closing costs in our first conversation so there are no surprises.
What if my home doesn't sell?
We adjust. Pricing, marketing, presentation, or strategy. I won't keep doing the same thing if it isn't working. The goal is your home sold at a fair price, not just listed.

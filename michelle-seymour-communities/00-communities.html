Living in the West Valley

Why the West Valley?

The West Valley is one of the fastest-growing parts of the Phoenix area. With new communities, championship golf courses, miles of mountain trails and home prices that still offer real value, cities like Buckeye, Goodyear and Surprise are drawing buyers from across the country.

I have called this area home for over a decade and have helped hundreds of buyers find the right community for their goals. Each community below has its own page, with my video tours, live home searches and the shops, restaurants and schools nearby.

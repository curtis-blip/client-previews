What you will need

Four short stacks of paper.

You will not need all of this to start the application. You will need it before underwriting can issue a pre-approval letter. Pull together what you have, start the form, and we will tell you exactly what is missing.

Identification

  • Government-issued ID, driver's license or passport
  • Social Security number
  • Permanent address history, two years

Income

  • Last two W-2s
  • Recent pay stubs covering 30 days
  • Two years of tax returns if self-employed

Assets

  • Two months of bank statements, all pages
  • Retirement account statements
  • Investment account statements

Debt

  • List of any open obligations
  • Most show on your credit report automatically
  • Flag anything new we should know about

Don't have everything? Start anyway. The application saves as you go, and we will tell you exactly what is missing within one business day. Most clients submit the form first and chase down statements over the next few days.

Before you apply

Common questions, direct answers.

The four questions we hear most often the day before someone starts the application. If yours isn't here, the full FAQ lives on the contact page or call and ask.

Will applying hurt my credit score?

Yes, slightly. A mortgage application triggers a hard credit inquiry, which typically drops a score 3 to 7 points. Most scores recover fully within 30 to 60 days as long as you keep balances down and pay on time.

Multiple mortgage inquiries inside a 14 to 45 day window count as one for scoring purposes, so shopping around the same week does not stack the impact. The bigger move is what you do next, not whether you applied.

How long is the application?

About 15 minutes online. The form covers basic identification, employment and income, the property type you are buying or refinancing, and a few questions about the home and your goals. It saves as you go, so you can pause and come back to it.

Document upload is separate. Once the form is submitted, you will get a secure link to upload IDs, paystubs, and statements. Most clients spread that across a few days.

Can I apply if I haven't found a home yet?

Yes, and you should. The whole point of pre-approval is to know what you can buy before you start touring homes. The application asks for a property type and price range, not a specific address.

Once you find the right house, the file is already underwritten. We swap in the address, update the appraisal request, and your offer goes out with a letter listing agents recognize.

What if my situation is unusual?

Self-employed, recently relocated, buying across state lines, building from the ground up, refinancing investment property, or working with non-traditional credit. We have closed every one of those scenarios in the last twelve months.

Apply anyway, or call first. Three decades of files means we have seen the version of your situation before, and we know which loan program lines up with it.

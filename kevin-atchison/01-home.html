Meet Kevin

Born and Raised in the Roaring Fork Valley

I grew up here, and I work the stretch of the valley people can still afford, from Rifle out to Parachute and De Beque and up to Glenwood Springs. Most of my work is with investors and complex deals. When I am not showing a house, I am usually on the river with a fishing rod.

Real estate can be complicated, so I walk you through the process, the timeline and the paperwork one step at a time. I explain a well share or a metro district before you sign, not after.

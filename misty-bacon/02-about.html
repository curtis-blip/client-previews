Meet Your Broker

Texas Born. Bay Area Houston Rooted.

Misty Bacon grew up in Texas, earned dual Bachelor's degrees in Communications and Public Relations from Stephen F. Austin State University, then took the scenic route to real estate. She started as a radio DJ, worked the fast lanes of New York and Los Angeles in marketing, sales, HR, and recruiting, and brought all of that people-first experience back to Texas when she got her license in 2007.

Nineteen years later, she runs Bacon Realty Group, a boutique 3-agent brokerage with Spanish-speaking agents on staff. No call centers. No being handed off to a random showing agent. When you call Misty, you get Misty.

That approach works. 85% of her business comes from referrals, and her HAR rating sits at 4.96 out of 5 from 126 client surveys. She covers everything from Galveston to the Medical Center, Baytown to Sugar Land, with a core focus on condos and townhomes in the Clear Lake and NASA corridor.

Professional Designations

6 Certifications. One Purpose: Your Advantage.

Every designation translates to specialized training that directly benefits your transaction. Here's what Misty brings to the table.

ABR
Accredited Buyer's Representative

Advanced buyer-side negotiation and advocacy training, so your offer stands out in competitive Bay Area Houston markets.

GRI
Graduate REALTOR Institute

Deep-dive coursework in contracts, finance, and technology that most agents never complete. Fewer than 20% of REALTORS earn the GRI.

SRS
Seller Representative Specialist

Pricing strategy, staging guidance, and listing marketing built specifically for sellers in the Houston market.

SRES
Seniors Real Estate Specialist

Specialized knowledge for retirees and downsizers navigating Medicare communities, reverse mortgages, and estate considerations near Clear Lake.

CPRES
Certified Probate Real Estate Specialist

Trained to handle probate property sales with sensitivity and legal awareness, reducing stress during difficult transitions.

TRLP
Texas REALTORS Leadership Program

Selected for the 2016 leadership cohort, building connections across Texas that benefit clients through off-market opportunities and industry insight.

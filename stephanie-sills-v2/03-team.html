Our Team

One Team, Many Bay Area Markets

Palermo Properties Team includes agents with expertise across several Bay Area markets, including the Coastside communities of Half Moon Bay, El Granada, Montara, and Moss Beach, as well as San Mateo County, San Francisco, and Santa Clara County.

Each agent brings local knowledge and professional experience to the team, so clients get one agent's personal attention and the shared resources of the whole group. Stephanie leads on the Coastside; read her story or see the towns she covers.

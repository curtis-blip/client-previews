Way one

A Cash Offer, As‑Is

Independent investors buy the house in the condition it is in today. Mark brings it to several of them, so you are not stuck with one number, and checks each offer's terms in writing.

Who it fits: a house that needs work, a deadline such as a move or a foreclosure date, a rental with tenants, or a seller who does not want showings.

  • Usually no repairs, cleanout or showings
  • No buyer's loan to fall through
  • Closing date asked for to fit your plans

Way two

Cash to Win: Buy Before You Sell

Need the next home before this one sells? Through Wallick & Volk, Cash to Win lets you make a strong offer on your next home without a home-sale contingency, then sell the old one on the open market once you have moved.

Who it fits: a seller who needs the next home first and can get full mortgage pre-approval. Mark will tell you on the first call whether it is worth asking Wallick & Volk.

  • No home-sale contingency on your next offer
  • Move once, then sell
  • Needs full mortgage pre-approval through Wallick & Volk

Way three

List on the Open Market

Mark lists the house on the MLS with full marketing, so the most buyers compete for it. It usually brings the highest price; it also usually means repairs, showings and a buyer's loan.

Who it fits: a house ready to show, with time to wait for the right buyer. If the numbers say listing wins, Mark tells you plainly.

  • Usually the highest price
  • Most buyers competing for the house
  • Repairs and showings, negotiated as usual

Is selling for cash a smart move?

It can be, when speed, condition or certainty matter more than top price. A cash offer usually comes in below full market value; in exchange you usually skip repairs, showings and a buyer's loan falling through. Mark sets the cash number beside what a listing would likely net, so you can see the trade before you decide.

Why get several offers instead of one?

Investors price the same house differently, depending on what they plan to do with it. One offer tells you one buyer's number. Several offers, side by side, tell you what the house is worth to cash buyers.

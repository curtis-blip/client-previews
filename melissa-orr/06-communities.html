County Seat

Victoria County

The centre of gravity for the whole Crossroads region.

VictoriaCounty
The hubTo Victoria
Victoria ISDSchool district
Established and new buildCharacter

Overview

Victoria is where most of the region's inventory, employment and services sit. You get genuinely established neighborhoods near the older core, newer construction pushing north along Navarro, and acreage as soon as you clear the city limits. It is also the only place in the footprint with real depth of choice in most price bands.

Who it suits

Buyers who want options, a short commute and city services. Also the easiest market to sell into, because it has the most active buyers.

Highlights

Victoria College and UHV
Riverside Park and the Guadalupe
Regional medical centre
Deepest inventory in the region
Newer construction out north
Acreage just past the city limits
The Orr Group's take

If you are relocating and do not yet know the area, start here. Victoria gives you the most choice, and once you know what you actually want, the outlying towns start making sense.

On The Bay

Port Lavaca

Water changes the math on everything.

CalhounCounty
About 35 minutesTo Victoria
Calhoun County ISDSchool district
Coastal and waterfrontCharacter

Overview

Port Lavaca sits on Lavaca Bay, and that proximity is the whole story. Waterfront and near-water homes, a working fishing and industrial economy, and a very different insurance picture from anywhere inland. Windstorm coverage and a WPI-8 certificate are routine parts of a transaction here, not exceptions.

Who it suits

Buyers who want water access, weekenders, and anyone comfortable underwriting coastal risk properly rather than discovering it at closing.

Highlights

Lavaca Bay frontage
Lighthouse Beach and Bird Sanctuary
Fishing and boating access
Working industrial employment base
Windstorm and WPI-8 considerations
Weekend and second-home market
The Orr Group's take

Do not price a Port Lavaca house the way you would price an inland one. Insurance can move the monthly payment by hundreds, and that changes what a buyer will offer. Get quotes early.

Acreage

Inez

Room to spread out, fifteen minutes from town.

VictoriaCounty
About 15 minutesTo Victoria
Industrial ISDSchool district
Rural and acreageCharacter

Overview

Inez is unincorporated Victoria County out along US 59 east. This is well and septic country, with larger parcels, shops and outbuildings, and the kind of space that is simply not available inside the city. The trade is that you are responsible for your own water and wastewater, and both need proper inspection.

Who it suits

Buyers who want land without a long commute, people with trailers, livestock or equipment, and anyone tired of a subdivision lot line.

Highlights

Larger parcels and acreage
Shops and outbuildings common
Well and septic systems
Short drive to Victoria
Industrial ISD
Quiet, low-density setting
The Orr Group's take

Budget for the due diligence out here. A well test, a septic inspection and a survey are not optional, and finding a problem in week one is a negotiation. Finding it after closing is a bill.

Historic

Goliad

One of the oldest towns in Texas, and it looks it in the best way.

GoliadCounty
About 30 minutesTo Victoria
Goliad ISDSchool district
Historic and ranch landCharacter

Overview

Goliad is genuinely historic, with a preserved courthouse square, Presidio La Bahia and Mission Espiritu Santo just outside town. The housing stock runs from period homes near the square to ranch land in every direction. It is small, it is quiet, and people who move here tend to have chosen it deliberately.

Who it suits

Buyers drawn to history and land, ranch and recreational buyers, and people who want a real town square rather than a strip centre.

Highlights

Preserved courthouse square
Presidio La Bahia
Mission Espiritu Santo
Goliad State Park
Ranch and recreational land
Period homes near the square
The Orr Group's take

Goliad rewards patience. Inventory is thin, so the right property does not appear on demand. If you want to be here, get on a saved search and be ready to move when something lands.

Lake Texana

Edna

A working small town with a lake on its doorstep.

JacksonCounty
About 25 minutesTo Victoria
Edna ISDSchool district
Small town and lakeCharacter

Overview

Edna is the Jackson County seat, close to Lake Texana and Brackenridge Park. It offers steady, unglamorous value: solid housing stock at prices below Victoria, a functioning downtown, and genuine outdoor recreation minutes away.

Who it suits

Buyers priced out of Victoria who do not mind a commute, retirees, and anyone who wants lake access without paying waterfront prices.

Highlights

Lake Texana nearby
Brackenridge Park and campground
Jackson County seat
Below Victoria on price per foot
Edna ISD
Working downtown
The Orr Group's take

Edna is the value play in this footprint. You give up about twenty-five minutes each way and get meaningfully more house, plus a lake most people in Victoria forget is there.

DeWitt County

Cuero

Restored Victorians and some of the best value per square foot anywhere here.

DeWittCounty
About 30 minutesTo Victoria
Cuero ISDSchool district
Historic small townCharacter

Overview

Cuero has a genuinely handsome historic district, a lot of restored and restorable Victorian housing stock, and a strong local identity built around Turkeyfest. Prices per square foot are among the most reasonable in the region, which makes it attractive to buyers who want character rather than new construction.

Who it suits

Restoration-minded buyers, people who want architecture, and anyone whose budget will not stretch to the equivalent home in Victoria.

Highlights

Historic Victorian housing stock
Turkeyfest each October
DeWitt County seat
Strong value per square foot
Cuero ISD
Walkable historic core
The Orr Group's take

Older houses here are a real opportunity and a real project. Get a thorough inspection, and budget for electrical and plumbing on anything that has not been updated.

Yorktown

Quiet, affordable, and genuinely rural.

DeWittCounty
About 35 minutesTo Victoria
Yorktown ISDSchool district
Small town and countryCharacter

Overview

Yorktown is a small DeWitt County town with a tight community and country property in every direction. Housing is affordable relative to anywhere closer to Victoria, and the pace is slower by design.

Who it suits

Buyers who want country living at the lowest entry point in the footprint, and people with ties to the area.

Highlights

Western Days each year
Country and acreage property
Yorktown ISD
Lowest entry prices in the footprint
Tight-knit community
Easy access to Cuero and Victoria
The Orr Group's take

Inventory is genuinely limited, so a saved search matters more here than almost anywhere else we work. Things sell to people who were already watching.

Lavaca County

Yoakum

The leather capital, with a working main street to match.

Lavaca and DeWittCounty
About 45 minutesTo Victoria
Yoakum ISDSchool district
Small town, working economyCharacter

Overview

Yoakum straddles the Lavaca and DeWitt county line and has a long history in leather goods manufacturing, which still anchors part of the local economy. It has a real downtown, affordable housing stock, and an annual Tom Tom Festival that the whole town turns out for.

Who it suits

Buyers who want a genuine small town with its own economy rather than a bedroom community.

Highlights

Leather goods manufacturing heritage
Tom Tom Festival
Working downtown
Affordable housing stock
Yoakum ISD
Straddles two counties
The Orr Group's take

Yoakum is far enough out that it is a lifestyle choice rather than a commute compromise. Buyers who choose it are usually choosing the town, not just the price.

Lavaca County

Hallettsville

Kolaches, a courthouse square, and deep local roots.

LavacaCounty
About 50 minutesTo Victoria
Hallettsville ISDSchool district
Czech and German heritageCharacter

Overview

Hallettsville is the Lavaca County seat, with strong Czech and German heritage that still shows in the food, the festivals and the churches. The courthouse square is the centre of town, and the surrounding county is farm and ranch land.

Who it suits

Buyers who want heritage, land, and a town where the annual festival calendar is a real part of life.

Highlights

Lavaca County courthouse square
Kolache Fest and Czech heritage
Farm and ranch land
Hallettsville ISD
Halfway toward Houston and San Antonio
Strong local traditions
The Orr Group's take

This is the far edge of our footprint and we know it well enough to be useful. If you are buying land out here, the survey and the mineral rights question matter more than the house.

Gonzales County

Gonzales

Where the Texas Revolution started, and it has never let you forget it.

GonzalesCounty
About 55 minutesTo Victoria
Gonzales ISDSchool district
Historic and agriculturalCharacter

Overview

Gonzales is the Come and Take It town, and the history is genuinely central to its identity. Beyond that it is an agricultural county seat with a handsome historic district, significant poultry and cattle operations, and land in every direction.

Who it suits

History-minded buyers, agricultural and land buyers, and people looking between San Antonio and the coast.

Highlights

Come and Take It heritage
Historic district and museums
Agricultural economy
Gonzales ISD
Land and ranch inventory
Between San Antonio and the coast
The Orr Group's take

Gonzales pulls buyers from San Antonio as much as from Victoria, which means you are sometimes competing with a different budget. Know that going in.

How to use this

Search by town, then by county line

Listings in this market do not sort neatly by city name. A Greenwood address and a Whiteland address can sit two minutes apart in the same school corporation, and a Franklin listing may be closer to Edinburgh than to Franklin's own downtown. If you are searching by town alone you will miss things.

Jennifer can set up a saved search that runs on the criteria that actually matter to you, including county, tax rate band, lot size and whether a property has the kind of maintenance history worth inheriting. Those arrive by email as they list, which in the faster towns is the difference between seeing a house and hearing about it. Start on the communities page if you are still narrowing down.

ABOUT MARK

Mark Antonowsky, Phoenix Realtor Since 1986

Mark Antonowsky has been actively helping buyers and sellers in the Greater Phoenix area since 1986. Licensed with the Arizona Department of Real Estate since 1986, he brings deep local market knowledge, a background in mortgage lending, and a practical approach to every transaction. His experience spans residential resale, new construction, investment properties, luxury homes, and distressed sales including short sales and REO properties.

With designations including CRS, GRI, ABR, ePRO, and SFR, Mark combines formal expertise with real-world transaction experience. He focuses on accurate pricing, clear communication, and thorough coordination across every stage of the buying or selling process.

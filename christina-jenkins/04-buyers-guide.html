Questions buyers ask

Before you make an offer

Insurance, flood, 4-point and association questions come up before the offer, not after.

When should I get homeowners insurance quotes?

Before you write the offer. Ask for quotes while you are still choosing between homes, so the yearly cost of insuring the home is part of the decision, and Christina can go through them with you.

Do I have to buy flood insurance?

It depends on the flood zone and the loan. Flood insurance is required for a federally backed mortgage on a home in a high-risk zone, which are the zones that begin with A or V, and it is recommended in lower-risk zones. Christina can look up the zone and elevation with you; the Flood Zones guide shows how.

What is a 4-point inspection?

An inspector checks the electrical, plumbing, HVAC and roof. Citizens Property Insurance asks for one on homes more than 20 years old. The 4-Point and Roof guide goes through it.

Will the seller tell me if the home has flooded?

Florida law requires the seller to complete a flood disclosure and give it to you at or before the time the contract is signed (s. 689.302, F.S., read October 27, 2026). Read it with Christina before you sign.

What should I ask about a waterfront home?

Who last inspected the seawall, what shape the dock is in, and what the flood zone and elevation are.

What should I know about a condominium or a neighborhood with an association?

Read the association's rules before you make an offer, especially the limits on rentals and pets, and especially if you plan to rent the home out or use it only part of the year.

How does a VA loan change the process?

The VA appraisal and inspection requirements shape which homes work. Christina can explain them before you tour, so you know what to expect.

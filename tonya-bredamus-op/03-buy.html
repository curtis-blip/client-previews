I am especially passionate about helping out-of-state buyers find a house and transition to my favorite place I've ever lived, Martinsville and Henry County. I know what it feels like to move here from somewhere else because I did it.

The Buying Process

Eight Steps From First Conversation to Closing Day.

Here's what working together looks like, in order, from the day you reach out.

Step PhotoDiscovery call · video meeting
Step 01

Discovery Conversation.

We talk about what you're actually looking for: budget, timeline, location, must-haves, deal-breakers. If you're out of state, we do this on Zoom.

Step PhotoLender meeting · pre-approval paperwork
Step 02

Pre-Approval.

I'll connect you with mortgage lenders I trust. You'll get a real pre-approval, not a pre-qualification. Sellers in Virginia will take you seriously.

Step PhotoDriving through a Virginia community
Step 03

Community Match.

Martinsville, Henry County, Danville, Smith Mountain Lake, Stuart, Rocky Mount. We pick the right one for your lifestyle, schools, and commute. I'll be honest about trade-offs.

Step PhotoIn-person home tour · Martinsville home
Step 04

Home Search and Tours.

I tour homes in person or send walking video tours if you're remote. I'll tell you the truth about what I see, not what you want to hear.

Step PhotoOffer paperwork · contract negotiation
Step 05

Offer and Negotiation.

We craft an offer with the right price, terms, and contingencies for the property and market. I negotiate on your behalf with the listing agent.

Step PhotoHome inspection · Virginia-licensed inspector at work
Step 06

Inspection and Due Diligence.

I coordinate the home inspection with a Virginia-licensed inspector I trust. We review findings together and decide what to request from the seller.

Step PhotoFinal walk-through · buyers reviewing the home
Step 07

Final Walk-Through and Closing Prep.

We do a final walk-through to confirm the property is in the agreed condition. Title work, insurance, and final loan approval happen in parallel.

Step PhotoClosing day · keys handed over to new homeowner
Step 08

Closing Day.

You sign, you get the keys, you're home. I show up because closings are milestones.

Questions I Get

Buyer FAQ.

We can do everything remotely. I tour homes for you with walking video, FaceTime, or scheduled video calls. Offers, inspections, and closings can all be handled by mail, e-sign, or with a local notary. About 30% of my buyers move here from out of state.
From first conversation to closing day, most buyers are in the 60 to 90 day range. The financing approval is usually the longest piece. Daniel Shelton went from looking to homeowner within a month, but that's faster than typical.
Strongly recommend it, yes. A pre-approval letter is what sellers in Virginia want to see with an offer. I can connect you with lenders before we tour anything.
Franklin County is known for its school system. Henry County, Patrick County, and Martinsville City each have their own districts. GreatSchools.org has ratings for each. I'm happy to walk you through what each district is known for.
Virginia Housing has several programs for first-time buyers, including down payment assistance and lower-interest mortgage products. Eligibility depends on income, location, and a few other factors. I'll point you to the ones worth applying for.

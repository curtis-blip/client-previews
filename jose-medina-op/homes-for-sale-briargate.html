Homes For Sale In Briargate

Live listings from the Pikes Peak MLS, updated automatically. Curated by Jose Medina, Vantegic Home Team at HomeSmart.

Briargate listings run from established two-story family homes on curved suburban streets to newer builds in the outer sub-neighborhoods, almost all of it inside Academy District 20. Buyers here are usually balancing schools, commute, and a walkable stretch of the Promenade Shops, and the inventory turns quickly when a home is priced right. Questions about what your budget buys here? Ask Jose directly or read the Buyer's Guide first.

Why this exists

Would Sell It

Most brokers in Teller County will refer you to a management company. I run one. That matters on the day selling stops being the obvious answer, because I can price the rental scenario as carefully as I price the sale, and then actually operate it for you.

If you are weighing the two, the Seller's Guide covers the sale side, and I will give you both sets of numbers rather than assuming which one you want. You can read more about how I work, or see what past clients have said.

Property management is a licensed activity in Colorado. My licence is verifiable through the Colorado Division of Real Estate, and you can look it up directly using the link in the footer of every page on this site.

For owners

What Management Covers

Marketing and screening

Listing the unit, showing it, and applying written screening criteria consistently to every applicant. Credit, income, rental history and background.

Rent and reporting

Online collection, arrears follow-up, monthly owner payouts and statements your accountant can work from without rebuilding them.

Maintenance

Triage, local trades, and supervision. At altitude that also means freeze protection, snow removal arrangements and roof and gutter checks before winter.

Compliance and inspections

Lease drafting, required notices, habitability standards and periodic condition inspections, kept current with Colorado law as it changes.

For residents

Renting From Us

Applications are handled online and reviewed against the same written criteria for everyone. Once you are in, rent and maintenance requests both go through the tenant portal, and requests get triaged the day they arrive rather than sitting in an inbox.

We do business in accordance with the Federal Fair Housing Act and the Colorado Anti-Discrimination Act. If you need a reasonable accommodation at any point in the application process, tell us and we will work it out with you. Your rights as a renter are set out by HUD's fair housing office and enforced in this state by the Colorado Civil Rights Division.

Looking for a rental in a particular area? The communities page covers all nine places we work, and Woodland Park is where most of our inventory sits.

Management questions

Frequently Asked

Management fees are quoted per property, because a single-family home in Woodland Park and a unit down in the Springs are not the same amount of work. You will get the fee structure and what it covers in writing before you sign anything.

Credit, verifiable income, rental history and a background check, applied the same way to every applicant. Consistent written criteria are both better screening and how we stay on the right side of fair housing law.

Residents report maintenance through the tenant portal, we triage it, and we send a local trade who will actually drive up the pass in February. You are consulted before anything over an agreed threshold, and emergencies are handled first and reported immediately.

Rent is collected online, and owner payouts go out on a set schedule each month with a statement you can hand straight to your accountant. Everything is visible in the owner portal.

Ask me. Short-term rental rules differ between the City of Woodland Park, unincorporated Teller County and Colorado Springs, and licensing and occupancy limits vary. I would rather check the specific address than give you a general answer.

That is the conversation I am best set up for, because I can price both. We look at what the property would sell for today and what it would realistically rent for, against your loan and your timeline. Sometimes the answer is sell, and I will say so.

About the Area

The largest town on the Coastside with a walkable downtown along Main Street. Half Moon Bay offers a mix of coastal estates, ranch properties, and family neighborhoods. Known for the annual Pumpkin Festival, world-class surfing at Mavericks, and a thriving local food scene.

The downtown strip includes independent shops, restaurants, and galleries. Residents enjoy proximity to beaches, trails, and some of the best produce in the Bay Area from local farms.

About the Area

A tight-knit community perched above Pillar Point Harbor. El Granada offers some of the best ocean views on the Coastside with a mix of mid-century homes and newer construction. The harbor area is home to fishing boats, kayak launches, and waterfront dining.

Residents enjoy quick access to trails, tidepools, and some of the Coastside's best restaurants. The elevated neighborhoods in El Granada Highlands provide sweeping Pacific panoramas.

About the Area

A small, unincorporated community between Pacifica and Moss Beach known for its quiet, rural character. Montara offers larger lots, more privacy, and direct access to Montara State Beach and McNee Ranch State Park.

Homes range from classic beach cottages to modern hillside builds with panoramic ocean views. For buyers who want space, nature, and solitude without sacrificing proximity to San Francisco, Montara delivers.

Stephanie lives here and knows the neighborhood intimately. The area attracts families, nature enthusiasts, and professionals who value coastal living without the bustle of a larger town.

About the Area

The northernmost Coastside community and the closest to San Francisco. Pacifica combines surf culture with suburban convenience. Linda Mar Beach draws surfers year-round, while the Pedro Point and Rockaway Beach areas offer walkable shops and restaurants.

More accessible price points make Pacifica a popular entry point for Coastside living. The city has invested in trail improvements, downtown revitalization, and community programming in recent years.

About the Area

A larger Peninsula city offering urban amenities with access to the Coastside via Highway 92. San Mateo has a vibrant downtown with diverse restaurants, shops, and entertainment. Caltrain access makes it a commuter hub for Silicon Valley and San Francisco.

While not on the coast itself, San Mateo residents are 30 minutes from Half Moon Bay beaches. The city appeals to buyers who want walkability, dining, and nightlife during the week with easy weekend escapes to the ocean.

Questions About Living on the Coastside

It depends on what you want. Half Moon Bay proper has Main Street walkability and Half Moon Bay State Beach. El Granada sits above Pillar Point Harbor with some of the best ocean views on the coast. Montara has larger lots and direct access to McNee Ranch State Park. Moss Beach centers around the Fitzgerald Marine Reserve. Pacifica is closest to San Francisco with surfing at Linda Mar. San Mateo offers Caltrain access and a dense restaurant scene on the Peninsula side of Highway 92.
Housing ranges from Pacifica condos in the $700Ks to oceanfront estates above $3M. Half Moon Bay median listing is approximately $1.69M. Property taxes follow San Mateo County rates.
Half Moon Bay, El Granada, Montara, and Moss Beach all fall under Cabrillo Unified School District, which runs Hatch Elementary, Cunha Intermediate, and Half Moon Bay High School. Pacifica has the Pacifica School District for K-8 and feeds into Jefferson Union High School District. San Mateo uses the San Mateo-Foster City School District, which includes well-regarded schools like Aragon High School. Stephanie can walk you through the specific attendance boundaries for any address.
From Pacifica, take Highway 1 north and you are in Downtown SF in about 20 minutes. From Half Moon Bay, most commuters take Highway 92 east to I-280 north, which runs 40 to 50 minutes depending on traffic. Highway 1 through Devil's Slide is scenic but slower and can close in heavy storms. From San Mateo, Caltrain gets you to SF in about 30 minutes without driving.
Half Moon Bay and Moss Beach are popular with families. Kids in Cabrillo Unified go to school together from elementary through Half Moon Bay High School, so the community stays tight. There is youth soccer, Little League, and surf lessons at Pacifica. The Coastside Trail connects towns by bike. Events like the Pumpkin Festival and the Fourth of July parade bring the whole coast out. Stephanie raised her family here and can speak to the day-to-day of Coastside family life.
Prices vary significantly by community. Pacifica starts lower, while oceanfront properties in Montara and El Granada can exceed $3M. Half Moon Bay median is approximately $1.69M.

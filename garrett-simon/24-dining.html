Dining

The Telluride restaurants Garrett sends clients to

Garrett keeps it to one pick per category, the way he would answer a text from a friend in town for the weekend.

Allred's and Gorrono Ranch are up on the mountain; the rest are in and around the Town of Telluride, most of them on or just off Colorado Avenue. When a place closes, it comes off the list.

Staying in a house and would rather eat in? Garrett can put you in touch with a private chef or a caterer through Concierge Services.

On the mountain

Lunch at Gorrono, dinner at the top of the gondola

Gorrono Ranch is a historic ranch turned mid-mountain lodge under the Village Express (Lift 4), with the Wilson Range in view from its deck. Allred's sits at the gondola's San Sophia Station, and the free gondola reaches it from either town.

The resort's dining page covers both, along with the restaurants in Mountain Village. Gorrono also hosts weddings in summer.

Homes for Sale in Pilsen, Chicago

A historic Mexican-American neighborhood on the Lower West Side, where vibrant murals, galleries, and the 18th Street corridor meet classic Chicago two-flats and graystones. Browse current Pilsen listings below, updated daily, with a broker who knows the blocks and the buildings.

The neighborhood

A day in Pilsen

Mornings begin with coffee along 18th Street, where bakeries, taquerias, and cafes set the rhythm of the day. A short walk takes you past some of the most photographed murals in Chicago, painted across viaducts, storefronts, and the sides of classic brick buildings.

The National Museum of Mexican Art anchors the cultural heart of the neighborhood, with free admission and one of the largest collections of Mexican art in the country. Galleries, studios, and music at Thalia Hall keep the arts district alive well past sundown.

By evening the patios and family-run kitchens fill up, and the Pink Line makes the rest of the city easy to reach. Pilsen is proud, creative, and tightly knit, with a community identity you can feel on every block.

The homes

What you'll find in Pilsen Chicago homes for sale

Pilsen is a neighborhood of classic Chicago housing stock. Brick two-flats and three-flats line the side streets, many ideal for owner-occupants who want to live in one unit and rent the others, alongside ornate graystones with the kind of detail that is hard to find at this scale anywhere else in the city.

Former factories and warehouses have become spacious converted lofts, bringing timber beams and tall windows to buyers who want character and room to spread out. Rehabbed single-family homes and new infill construction round out the mix.

Whether you are after a first home with rental income, a loft near the galleries, or an investment property in one of Chicago's most distinctive neighborhoods, there is real range here to fit a true Chicago lifestyle.

Neighborhood highlights

Why people love Pilsen

National Museum of Mexican Art

One of the largest collections of Mexican art in the country, with free admission year-round.

18th Street corridor

The neighborhood's main street, lined with taquerias, bakeries, bars, and family-run shops.

Murals & galleries

Open-air street art and independent galleries make Pilsen one of Chicago's defining arts districts.

Thalia Hall

A landmark 1892 music hall and venue, with live shows and dining just off 18th Street.

Cafes & dining

From classic Mexican kitchens to new cafes and patios, the food scene is a destination in itself.

Pink Line CTA

The 18th and Damen stops connect Pilsen straight to the Loop and the rest of the city.

What Is My Home Worth in Apache Junction

A real number from recent nearby sales, not an automated guess from a portal that has never seen inside your house.

01 What drives the number

Why the portals get it wrong

An automated estimate reads public records and recent sales in your zip code. It has never been inside your house, it does not know the lot backs onto a wash, and in this valley it does not know which utility district you are in.

The number that matters comes from three things: what actually closed near you in the last sixty days, what condition your house is in against those, and what is currently competing with it on the market.

02 How the number is reached

Four steps to a real figure

Step 01

Price from three closed sales

Within half a mile, inside sixty days. Never from a portal estimate, which does not know what your house looks like inside.

Step 02

Prepare the rooms that sell

Counters to three objects, a third of the furniture out, every bulb matched. It is unglamorous and it moves the number.

Step 03

Photograph it properly

Professional photography, twilight exteriors where the house earns it, and a floor plan buyers can trust.

Step 04

Launch where buyers already are

MLS syndication, then paid social and search aimed at the people actually shopping this price band in this corridor.

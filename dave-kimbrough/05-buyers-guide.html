Buyer's Guide

Buying a home in Grand Junction

Financing, the buyer agreement, the search, the offer and every deadline after it, in the order they come.

Before the first showing

Know the order before you fall for a house

Buying a home in Grand Junction runs on dates. Once a seller accepts your offer, the Colorado contract sets a deadline for the inspection, the appraisal, your loan and the title, and every one of them is a date to keep.

Dave Kimbrough and The Kimbrough Team sell homes across Mesa County, from the Redlands to Palisade. He settles your financing first, so you are ready to write an offer when the right house comes up, then keeps track of every date between the accepted offer and the closing table.

Step 1 of 8

Get pre-approved before you shop

A lender looks at your income, savings and credit and puts in writing how much it will lend. That letter sets your price range, and it goes in with the offer when you write one. Buying your first home? Ask the lender about CHFA's programs now, before you choose a loan.

02

Step 2 of 8

Sign the buyer agreement with Dave

Since August 17, 2024, buyers sign a written agreement with their agent before touring a home together, in person or on video. In Colorado it is a Commission-approved form, and it spells out what Dave does for you and how he is paid.

Buyer's agent compensation is negotiable and set in your written buyer agreement. Sometimes the seller agrees to pay part or all of it.

03

Step 3 of 8

Decide where in the valley to look

The Grand Valley runs from Mack and Loma in the west to Palisade in the east, with the Redlands below the Colorado National Monument, Orchard Mesa across the river from downtown and Whitewater to the south. Tell Dave your commute, the lot you want and the kind of house, and he narrows the list.

05

Step 5 of 8

Write the offer

Colorado brokers write offers on the Commission's Contract to Buy and Sell Real Estate. Dave fills in the price, your earnest money and the date for each deadline with you, explains what each term commits you to before you sign, then negotiates the seller's counterproposal.

06

Step 6 of 8

Inspect the house

Once the offer is accepted, you hire an inspector. If the report turns up problems, you answer in writing before your inspection deadline with the Commission's Inspection Objection Notice, and Dave negotiates the repairs or a credit with the seller's agent.

07

Step 7 of 8

Appraisal, loan and title

Your lender orders the appraisal, the title company sends the title commitment, and any HOA documents arrive for you to read. Each has its own date in the contract, and a low appraisal or a title problem gets a written objection of its own. Dave keeps those dates in front of you and your lender.

08

Step 8 of 8

Close on the house

Your lender must send the Closing Disclosure, with the final loan terms and costs, three business days before closing. Read it against your Loan Estimate, then sign at closing. Dave checks the numbers with you before that day.

Your first home

First-time home buyer in Colorado? Start with CHFA

The Colorado Housing and Finance Authority, CHFA, offers home loans through a statewide network of participating lenders, with grants or second loans that can help with the down payment or the closing costs.

Every CHFA borrower takes a CHFA-approved homebuyer education class before closing, and each borrower on the loan needs a certificate of their own. Classes run in person and online, in English and in Spanish. Take one early: waiting can delay a closing.

Programs, limits and who qualifies change, so get the current terms from CHFA or one of its participating lenders before you choose a loan.

Questions

What buyers ask Dave

Do I have to sign an agreement before Dave shows me a house?

Yes. Since August 17, 2024, buyers sign a written agreement with their agent before touring a home together, in person or on video. Visiting an open house on your own needs none. Buyer's agent compensation is negotiable and set in your written buyer agreement. Sometimes the seller agrees to pay part or all of it. NAR explains written buyer agreements.

Which contract does a Colorado offer use?

Colorado brokers are required to use the contracts and forms the Colorado Real Estate Commission approves. A home purchase is written on the Contract to Buy and Sell Real Estate (Residential), and the inspection, appraisal and title answers each have a Commission form of their own. See the forms at the Division of Real Estate.

What is earnest money?

It is the deposit that goes with your offer to show the seller you mean it. The amount and the date it is due are terms in your contract, and Dave explains when it is returned and when it is at risk before you sign.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan, so the lender is the one to ask. If you are buying your first home, ask about CHFA too: its loans can come with help toward the down payment or closing costs. CHFA.

Does The Kimbrough Team sell condos, land and small rentals?

Yes. In Dave's words, the team sells "everything but trailers": condos and townhomes, 2 to 4 unit buildings, land and lots, farm and ranch, equestrian property, 55+ communities, second homes and investment property, at every price across Mesa County. See condos and townhomes and land and lots.

Which school serves a house I like?

Mesa County Valley School District 51 serves the valley, and schools are assigned by address. Confirm the school for a specific home with the district.

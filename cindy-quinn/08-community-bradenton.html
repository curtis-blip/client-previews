Bradenton

The mainland alternative to Anna Maria Island. Waterfront communities along the Manatee River and Palma Sola Bay, gated neighborhoods, and a lower price point per square foot than the island.

About the City

The mainland version of island living.

Bradenton is the mainland city just east of Anna Maria Island, connected by two bridges across Palma Sola Bay. It's the larger, more urban counterpart to the island, with significantly more inventory and a broader range of price points.

For buyers who want the lifestyle without the island premium, Bradenton offers waterfront homes along the Manatee River, gated communities on Perico Island, and bayfront properties that see the same sunsets at a fraction of the island price.

Access to the island is direct via the Manatee Avenue (Highway 64) or Cortez Road bridges, putting the beaches within a 10-minute drive. For many buyers, mainland Bradenton is the quiet solution to wanting island proximity without island prices.

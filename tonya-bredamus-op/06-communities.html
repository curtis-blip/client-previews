My Approach

How I Help You Pick the Right Community.

Each of these towns has a different rhythm. Martinsville is small and walkable, with serious community pride. Danville has comeback energy and a riverfront. Stuart is mountain-quiet. Rocky Mount has the Harvester and the Blue Ridge. Smith Mountain Lake is a lifestyle, not a town.

When we talk about which one fits you, we'll cover the obvious stuff (price, schools, commute) and the less obvious stuff (how loud, how social, how rural, what's open on a Sunday morning). I won't recommend a community I don't know personally.

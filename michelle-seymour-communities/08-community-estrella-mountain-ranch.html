Living in Estrella

Lakes, mountains and trails in south Goodyear

Estrella, still widely known as Estrella Mountain Ranch, is a 20,000-acre master-planned community in the foothills of the Sierra Estrella in Goodyear, about 17 miles west of downtown Phoenix. Mountain Ranch was its first village. Lucero, Montecito and the 55+ community CantaMia followed, and more than 8,000 homes are built.

Residents share two private lakes, with Yacht Club boats included in the HOA, two residents' clubs and more than 65 miles of trails. DRB Homes, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers and William Ryan Homes are building now. For a look inside one Estrella home, see this pool home with owned solar.

Questions

Estrella questions I hear most

Is Estrella Mountain Ranch a 55+ community?

No. Estrella is a master-planned community open to all ages. CantaMia is the gated 55+ community inside it.

What are HOA fees in Estrella?

Estrella's HOA starts at $125 a month (2025), a little more in gated neighborhoods, and it includes Yacht Club boat use. CantaMia has its own dues. I confirm the exact amount for any home you are considering.

Which schools serve Estrella?

Estrella is in the Liberty Elementary School District. Northern neighborhoods are zoned to Estrella Mountain Elementary and southern ones to Westar Elementary, both kindergarten to grade 8. High school students attend Estrella Foothills High School in the Buckeye Union High School District.

How It Works

Three steps, and you have a real number.

01 Submit Your Details

Tell Jerre about the home.

Fill out the short form above with your address, square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, and any recent renovations. The more detail you share about upgrades, the more accurate the comp work.

If you're weighing a rental conversion, check that box and Jerre will run a parallel lease projection.

02 Jerre Pulls Comps and Analyzes

Real comps, micro-market context.

Jerre pulls active, pending, and sold comparables from the last 90 days within your micro-market, not the whole zip code. She layers in current inventory, average days on market, and how renovations are pricing locally right now.

This is the same analysis she runs before listing any of her own clients' homes.

03 You Get a Written Report and a Follow-up Call

A real conversation, not a robot email.

You get a written valuation report with the comp set, a recommended pricing range, and notes on what's driving each number. Then Jerre calls to walk through it, answer questions, and talk through your timing.

If you decide not to list, that's fine. The report is yours either way.

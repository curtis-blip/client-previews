Greenway Park at a glance

An established neighborhood in central Broomfield

Greenway Park sits just south of West 120th Avenue and east of Lamar Street, around a nine-hole golf course that opened in 1973.

County records count 549 homes across five plats, about 94 percent of them built between 1970 and 1989. US-36 and the US 36 & Broomfield Station, in Arista, are about a mile west.

01

Around a nine-hole course

Greenway Park Golf Course, a nine-hole, par-27 course that opened in 1973, sits in the middle of the neighborhood, with a pool and clubhouse run through the neighborhood HOA. The City's 5.5-acre Greenway Park adds fields, a playground and trails.

02

Homes from the 1970s and 1980s

Most of the 549 homes were built between 1970 and 1989, so expect established trees and houses that range from original condition to fully updated. Ask Steve which is which before you tour.

03

Schools and getting around

Greenway Park is in Jeffco Public Schools' Standley Lake area: Ryan Elementary, Mandalay Middle and Standley Lake High. Some listing portals name Boulder Valley schools instead, so confirm any address with Jeffco. US-36 and the US 36 & Broomfield Station are about a mile west.

04

What to check before you offer

  • The HOA and the courseThe golf course, pool and clubhouse run through the neighborhood HOA. Confirm whether membership comes with the home and what it costs.
  • The school districtSome portals show Boulder Valley schools here; the district's own map places Greenway Park in Jeffco. Check the address.
  • Age of the systemsMost of these homes are 35 to 55 years old. Look at the roof, windows, furnace and electrical panel, and add a sewer line scope to the inspection.
  • Remodels and permitsWhere a home has been remodeled, ask for permits on finished basements and structural work.

Questions

Greenway Park FAQ

Which schools serve Greenway Park?

Jeffco Public Schools, in the Standley Lake articulation area: Ryan Elementary, Mandalay Middle and Standley Lake High. Some listing portals show Boulder Valley instead, so confirm a specific address with Jeffco.

Who runs the golf course and pool?

The course, pool and clubhouse are run through the neighborhood HOA. Whether membership comes with a particular home is worth confirming before you buy.

How old are the homes?

County parcel data lists 549 homes built from 1970 to 1999, about 94 percent of them between 1970 and 1989.

How do people get to Denver and Boulder?

US-36 is about a mile west, and the Flatiron Flyer stops at the US 36 & Broomfield Station in Arista.

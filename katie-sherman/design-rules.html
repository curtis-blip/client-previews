This is the standard body copy style. It runs at a generous line-height because most blocks carry meaningful paragraph content (about teasers, agent bio, FAQ answers, community descriptions). Width caps around 65 characters per line for readability.
Typography Hard Rules
  • Heading and body font families MUST be loaded via Google Fonts link at top of every page.
  • Never substitute a system font fallback as the primary face, only as the secondary in font-stack.
  • Never introduce a THIRD font family for "accent" or "display", heading + body only.
  • No italic-serif accent spans inside H1s ("real estate, reimagined" is banned).
  • No em dashes anywhere in copy, replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.

Buttons and Link Labels

Square corners everywhere. Buttons are uppercase body type, 600, letter-spaced.

Button (default)
Transparent with a brown (terracotta, #9c5539) outline and brown label; fills brown with a white label on hover. The header's Contact button is the same (Curtis, 2026-10-23). Class btn.
Over a photo or dark band
btn btn--fill (solid terracotta) or btn btn--light (white outline). The closing band pairs one of each.
Link label
tlink: uppercase label in ink that ends in a small terracotta wave, which slides on hover. The wave means "go to another page": never on phone numbers, emails or in-page jumps (Curtis, 2026-10-23, the contact page).
In-page cue
src-cue: a quiet uppercase label with a small down chevron, used for "Sources for Step N", which opens that step's tab in the sources directory.
Button Hard Rules
  • Border-radius 0 on buttons, cards, chips, tabs and photos.
  • One primary action per hero, at most two.
  • Two stacked buttons in a narrow column share one width (acts--stack).
  • Every control keeps a visible :focus-visible outline in the accent.

Motion

Slow, subtle motion. LP-style register. Never bouncy. Never fast.

TokenValueUse
--motion-duration-fast200msLink underline color changes, small UI feedback
--motion-duration-default240msButton hover, accordion expand
--motion-duration-slow800ms (reveal), 900msSection fade-in, photo zoom on hover
--motion-easing-defaultcubic-bezier(.22, .68, .12, 1)All hover transitions
Motion Hard Rules
  • No bounce easing. No springs. No spinning loaders inside content.
  • Photo hover zoom maxes at 1.03 (3%). More feels gimmicky.
  • Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, disable photo-zoom and section fade-in when set.

Voice and Content Reminders

Content writers and designers both need to remember these voice rules. Even if it's the writer's job, a designer who notices an em dash or "your trusted partner" should flag it before publish.

Copy Hard Rules
  • Never use em dashes (`—` or `--`), replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.
  • Never use generic AI phrases: "dream home", "seamless experience", "every step of the way", "your trusted partner", "in today's market", "with confidence".
  • Never use generic city descriptors: "thriving", "vibrant", "nestled", "perfect blend", "world-class", "rich history", "something for everyone".
  • Never use parallel triplets ("Clear, calm, confident"), hyphenated compound metaphors ("courtroom-trained eye"), or conditional buildups ("Whether you're A, B, or C...").
  • Every page H1 references at least ONE specific item from the client's brief (years, credentials, niche, personal story).

Full doctrine: references/copy-voice.md in the Optimus Prime plugin.

This Site's Patterns

Decided with Curtis on 2026-10-23; the locked pages (Home, About Us, Sarasota, Privacy Policy, Buyer's Guide) are the reference for each.

Florida touches
Palm shadow (palm, palm--left) on sand bands only, one or two a page, never two in a row. The breeze-block screen (screen, screen--sea) behind one held photo a page, on the photo's outside corner, away from the words. The header bar's "Sunset on Siesta Key tonight" line with the day's time.
Interior hero
Photo with a teal wash, darkest on the left and under the header. Breadcrumbs: no box; from 1100px they run up the hero's left edge (rotated, Home at the bottom); below 1100px plain small capitals above the eyebrow.
Steps
Full-bleed alternating photo and text rows, sage step numerals, one parallax statement between steps 4 and 5.
Tabs
The sources directory's step tabs and the Investment page's property-type chips both use the site script's tab module (.ct__tabs, role=tab, is-on on the open panel). In WordPress each property-type tab is an IDX Broker saved link.
Tile rows
A full-bleed tile row that ends its section meets the next band directly: no white strip under it.
Section height
No section's content taller than one 1440x900 screen without a reason (Curtis, 2026-10-23); long lists become rows of cards or tabs.
Contact
One wave (under the eyebrow); phones and email as plain rows, name left, number right. Only Katie 321-429-9031 and Ryan 321-429-9022 and coastalbreezelivingfl@gmail.com; never a brokerage phone or palmparadisegroup.com address.
Words
No "team" anywhere until they change brokerages ("Ryan and Katie", "both of them"). Copy speaks to every visitor, not only people moving here.

Anti-Patterns Summary

The shortlist of "do not do this." Every rule below was learned the hard way on past builds.

Never Do These
  • Em dashes anywhere in copy
  • Emojis as icons (inline SVG only)
  • Rounded corners on cards, buttons, images
  • Carousel sliders in heroes
  • Italic-serif "register lift" spans inside H1
  • 3+ CTAs in a hero (1 default, 2 max)
  • Split heroes anywhere except a clean home-page exception
  • Two dark-background sections in a row
  • Aggressive form or large CTA inside the hero (exception: Home Valuation page widget)
  • Gradient placeholders past first draft
  • Raw shortcode embeds without styled container
  • Flat text-only service boxes (must have SVG icons)
  • Zigzag timeline layouts for process steps (use alternating image+text rows)
  • "Recently Sold Nearby" on Home Valuation page
  • 4-step process blocks shipped as production (always default to 8)
  • FAQ mode: faq on About page (use mode: expertise, Areas of Expertise)
  • Communities home-page treatment on inner pages (V2 home-only)
  • About teaser block on /about (always use full Agent Bio)

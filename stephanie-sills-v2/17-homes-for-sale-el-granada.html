Living in El Granada

El Granada is one of the most sought-after coastal communities on the San Mateo County Coastside, known for its ocean views, harbor access and relaxed outdoor lifestyle.

Daniel Burnham, who oversaw the design of Chicago's 1893 World's Fair, laid out its curving streets for the Ocean Shore Railroad, which meant it as a seaside resort. The railroad gave up the plan, and El Granada grew into a residential village instead: unincorporated, served by San Mateo County, with Pillar Point Harbor and Princeton below it and the hills of Quarry Park above.

El Granada Neighborhoods and Kinds of Homes

Burnham's plan still shapes the town: its streets run in arcs from Avenue Alhambra up the hillside. Lots higher on the hill trade a longer walk to the beach for views over the harbor.

Most homes are single-family houses on their own lots, from small cottages to newer, larger houses, with a few condos and townhomes. Princeton, on the flats beside the harbor, is a working waterfront with restaurants and marine businesses.

02

Pillar Point Harbor and Princeton

Below the town, Pillar Point Harbor shelters the Coastside's fishing fleet behind its breakwater. The San Mateo County Harbor District runs it, and in season boats sell fish and crab straight off the dock.

Princeton's restaurants, including Half Moon Bay Brewing Company, sit along the waterfront, and Pillar Point Bluff, a county park, has trails out toward the point above the harbor mouth.

03

Surfer's Beach and Quarry Park

At the south end of town, Surfer's Beach sits beside the harbor's breakwater, and the coastal trail follows the shore south toward Half Moon Bay.

Up the hill, Quarry Park covers 517 acres of county parkland in El Granada, with play areas, picnic sites, a community garden and trails that climb through eucalyptus and cypress to the ridges.

What to Check Before You Buy in El Granada

  • Who issues permitsEl Granada is unincorporated, so building permits come from San Mateo County Planning and Building, and the town is in the state's coastal zone, where many projects need a coastal development permit.
  • Water and sewerWater comes from Coastside County Water District; sewer and garbage service from the Granada Community Services District.
  • Hillside lotsUp the hill, check the slope, the drainage and any geologic report, and what the lot allows if you plan to add on.
  • Insurance, earlyGet a homeowners quote during your inspection period. Some coastal and hillside homes end up on the California FAIR Plan.
  • Your new tax billProperty tax resets to the price you pay, and San Mateo County sends a supplemental bill for the rest of the fiscal year.

Questions

El Granada Real Estate Questions

Is El Granada part of Half Moon Bay?

No. El Granada is an unincorporated community in San Mateo County, just north of the City of Half Moon Bay, so the county handles planning and permits. Its addresses use ZIP code 94018 or 94019, and it shares Cabrillo Unified School District with Half Moon Bay.

What is El Granada known for?

Pillar Point Harbor below the town, Daniel Burnham's curving street plan, Surfer's Beach and the trails of Quarry Park. Most homes are single-family houses, many with a view of the harbor or the ocean.

Which schools serve El Granada?

Cabrillo Unified School District. El Granada Elementary is in town, and older students go on to Cunha Intermediate and Half Moon Bay High School. Boundaries can change, so confirm any address with the district.

How far is El Granada from San Francisco?

About 25 miles north on Highway 1, through the Tom Lantos Tunnels and Pacifica. Silicon Valley is over the hill by Highway 92 and I-280.

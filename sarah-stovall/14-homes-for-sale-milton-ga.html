Living in Milton

Horse Farms and One-Acre Lots

Milton is a city at the northern tip of Fulton County, formed from unincorporated Fulton County and incorporated on December 1, 2006.

It is named for Revolutionary War hero John Milton, who also gave his name to the former Milton County, which existed from 1857 until it was folded into Fulton County in the early 1930s. The city borders Cherokee County to the north and west, Forsyth County to the east, Roswell to the southwest and Alpharetta to the south.

Land, Zoning and Homes in Milton

Milton covers more than 39 square miles, and about 85 percent of it is zoned agricultural, which means residential lots must be at least one acre. The city was formed in 2006 from parts of unincorporated Fulton County, and its comprehensive plan uses eight character areas: Arnold Mill, Bethany, Birmingham, Central Milton, Crabapple, Deerfield, Milton Lakes and Sweetapple.

Horse farms are dispersed widely between neighborhoods, and the Milton Equestrian Committee is a formal city committee. Roads named Mayfield, Dinsmore, Broadwell, Rucker, Nix, Hembree and Cogburn recall early farming settlers of the 1800s.

02

Crabapple, Deerfield and the Parks

Crabapple, in the southwestern corner, is called the heart of Milton and holds City Hall, the Milton Library and many of the city's schools. Crabapple Fest takes place there each fall. Deerfield, in the southeastern corner near Windward Parkway and GA 400, is the city's most heavily commercial district.

City parks include Milton City Park and Preserve, the former Milton Country Club bought in 2018, with a pool, a tennis center, a community center and a 2.5-mile loop trail; Providence Park, with a fishing pier on Providence Lake; Birmingham Park; and Bell Memorial Park.

What to Check Before You Buy in Milton

A few questions are worth settling before you write an offer on a Milton home.

Sarah's Buyer's Guide

Zoning and lot size
About 85 percent of Milton is zoned agricultural, so residential lots there must be at least one acre. Ask how a property is zoned before you plan a barn, a guest house or a pool.
School assignment
Fulton County Schools assigns schools by address. Confirm the zoned schools for the specific home before you write an offer.
Homestead exemption
Once you own and live in the home, file for the homestead exemption with Fulton County; Georgia's deadline is April 1.
Horses
Horse farms are spread between neighborhoods, and the city has a formal Equestrian Committee. If you plan to keep horses, ask what the zoning and any homeowners' association allow.

Questions

Questions About Living in Milton

Which county is Milton in?

Fulton County, at its northern tip. Milton borders Cherokee County to the north and west, Forsyth County to the east, Roswell to the southwest and Alpharetta to the south.

When was Milton incorporated?

December 1, 2006, after 85 percent of voters approved a referendum creating the city. It is named for Revolutionary War hero John Milton, who also gave his name to the former Milton County.

Which school district serves Milton?

Fulton County Schools. Milton High School and Cambridge High School are both in the city.

Does Milton have a downtown?

Crabapple, in the southwestern corner, is the city's central gathering area with City Hall and the Milton Library, and the city's 2017 plan for it is titled the Downtown Milton/Crabapple Placemaking Plan.

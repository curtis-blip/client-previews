Polo Fields at a Glance

Homes Around a Private Golf Club

The Polo homeowners association calls the private golf course and country club central to the neighborhood, and Polo Fields Parkway meets Bethelview Road.

You will see the community called Polo Fields, Polo Golf and Country Club or just Polo. The club is run by Invited (ClubCorp) and sells its own memberships. The homeowners association, managed by Community Management Associates, handles the neighborhood side, including the approval that changes to a home need. Tell Louis and Rhonda what you are looking for, and they can help you sort out what to ask the club and the association before you make an offer.

Polo Fields in four chapters

01 / 04

What the Club Runs

Polo Golf & Country Club has a championship golf course designed by Joe Lee, a 30,000-square-foot clubhouse with several places to eat, two swim centers, one with a lap pool and one with a waterslide and a snack bar, and a tennis center with ten lighted courts, eight Har-Tru and two clay.

The club sells its own memberships in three categories: Full Golf, Associate Golf and Swim/Tennis. Ask the club which one fits, and what it means for a particular address, starting on its membership page.

02 / 04

Who Handles What

The neighborhood side belongs to the Polo Golf and Country Club Homeowners Association, managed by Community Management Associates. Its website says every change to a home needs an architectural request and approval and posts the design standards.

The same site points agents to the management company for the community fees and disclosures exhibit used in a sale, so ask for that paperwork early.

03 / 04

Where Polo Fields Sits

Polo Fields Parkway meets Bethelview Road, and the club's own address is on Polo Club Drive. The association's site names Vickery Village and The Collection as the nearby places for shopping, dining and entertainment; our Vickery page covers the village.

The neighborhood has a Cumming mailing address, so homes for sale in Cumming is the wider page to read next.

04 / 04

Before You Make an Offer

County records show most homes here were built from 1988 to 2012, so ask about the age of the roof and the heating and cooling when you tour. The Georgia buying guide and the Forsyth County property tax guide cover the rest, and what your home is worth helps if you are selling first.

Ask the club what a membership involves for the address, and ask the association for its documents.

Questions

Polo Fields FAQ

Does a Cumming mailing address put Polo Fields inside the city?

Polo Fields is in Forsyth County and uses a Cumming mailing address. A mailing address does not show whether a home is inside the Cumming city limits, so check a specific address with the county. For the wider area, see homes for sale in Cumming.

Which school district covers Polo Fields?

Forsyth County Schools is the public district for Forsyth County. Ask the district which campuses serve an address, since assignments can change.

Does buying in Polo Fields include a club membership?

The club lists its own membership categories, Full Golf, Associate Golf and Swim/Tennis, and describes itself as open to members and their guests. Ask the club what membership involves for a specific address before you make an offer.

What approval do changes to a home in Polo Fields need?

The homeowners association's website says all home changes need an architectural control request and approval and posts the design standards. Read them before you plan any change.

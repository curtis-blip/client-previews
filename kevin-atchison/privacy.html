Privacy Policy

How this site collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated September 2026.

Who we are

This site is operated by Kevin Atchison, Broker Associate with Property Professionals, 1430 Railroad Ave, Suite A, Rifle, CO 81650. You can reach us at kevin@kevinatchison.com or (970) 379-0199.

What we collect

We collect information you give us directly, which means the name, email address, phone number and message you enter into a contact or property enquiry form, and any address you submit to the home valuation tool.

We also collect limited information automatically when you browse, including your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the site that referred you. This comes from standard web server logs and from analytics software.

If you save a property search or create an account through the IDX property search, that service collects and stores your search criteria and saved listings so it can send you updates.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, to send you property information you asked for, to provide the real estate services you requested, and to improve how this site works. If you ask for listing alerts, we use your saved criteria to send them.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with parties who help deliver the service: our brokerage, the IDX provider that powers the property search, our email and customer management software, and analytics providers. Where a transaction requires it, we share information with lenders, title companies, inspectors and other professionals involved in your purchase or sale, and with anyone we are required to share it with by law.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies to remember your preferences and to understand how the site is used. You can refuse cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the property search may not work properly if you do.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will action it. Every marketing email we send carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing does not stop us replying to an enquiry you have made.

Colorado residents have additional rights under the Colorado Privacy Act, including the right to opt out of targeted advertising and the sale of personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Fair Housing

We are committed to the letter and spirit of the Fair Housing Act and the Equal Opportunity Act. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status, national origin, or any other class protected under Colorado law.

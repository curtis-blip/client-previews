What is my home worth?

Type your address for an estimated value, a range and a confidence score, then have Mitch price it in person.

Your number

Know your number before you list

An online estimate reads public records and recent sales. It cannot see your kitchen, your roof or the work you have put in, so treat it as a starting point.

A real price starts with your home's condition. Mitch walks through it, prices it against recent Roanoke Valley sales, and tells you which fixes pay for themselves and which do not.

Ask Mitch for a Price

What goes into a price

What moves a home's value

  • Recent sales nearby

    Homes like yours that sold in the last few months, on similar streets.

  • Condition and updates

    The roof, the systems, the kitchen and baths, and what a buyer would have to fix.

  • The lot and the setting

    Size, slope, views and the street itself.

  • Timing

    How many similar homes are for sale when yours lists.

Questions

Questions about your home's value

How accurate is an online estimate?+

It is a range from public data. It does not know your home's condition, so the in-person price can be higher or lower.

Does Mitch charge for a price opinion?+

No. Ask for one whenever you are thinking about selling.

Which updates add the most value?+

It depends on the house. Mitch looks at what buyers in your area pay more for and what they would redo anyway.

How soon can I list after the price?+

As soon as the house is ready. Mitch can line up photography within days.

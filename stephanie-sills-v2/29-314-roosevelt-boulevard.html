About This Home

A Post-Modern House Steps From Roosevelt Beach

Welcome to 314 Roosevelt Blvd in Half Moon Bay, a distinctive post-modern coastal retreat nestled in Miramar, one of the Coastside's most coveted neighborhoods. Steps away, you'll find tranquil Roosevelt Beach, featuring effortless access via a gentle slope to the sand, no stairs! This 3-bed, 3-bath home offers ~2,300 sq ft of light-filled living space and blends clean lines with timeless character. A former 4th bedroom is now a loft, ideal for an office or overnight guests.

The airy great room features soaring 17 ft ceilings, exposed wood beams, and seamless deck access. Expansive windows frame the ocean, visible from the great room, loft, and front deck, an ideal spot to unwind while watching stunning sunsets. The refreshed kitchen features new flooring and new stainless steel appliances.

A ground-floor bedroom and full bath offer versatility for guests or multi-generational living. Upstairs, the primary bedroom features 12 ft wood ceilings, huge walk-in closet, ensuite bath, and private deck. A third bedroom, additional deck, and full bath complete this level. The spacious 7,000 sq ft lot provides several spaces for entertaining and relaxation, or ADU potential. Close to beaches, trails, and downtown HMB. Experience the ultimate coastal lifestyle in this exceptional home!

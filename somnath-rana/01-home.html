Property management

The half of the business nobody advertises

Most of what goes wrong with a rental is slow and boring. A lease rolls over at the wrong rent. A repair waits too long. A vacancy sits a month longer than it needed to. Management is mostly about not letting those accumulate.

  • Tenant placement and screening
  • Leasing and renewals
  • Rent collection and owner reporting
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Move-in, move-out and periodic inspections
  • Long-term portfolio review

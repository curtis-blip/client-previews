Meet Millie Gwin, Your Space Coast Real Estate Expert

Millie Gwin

Florida Real Estate Expert

Millie Gwin grew up in the Chicagoland suburbs, where long winters and the pull of something warmer planted the seed for a life change. After years of dreaming about sun-filled mornings and coastal living, she made the move to Florida's Space Coast and never looked back. That personal experience of starting fresh in a new state shapes everything she does as a realtor in Titusville, Florida.

She understands the questions, the excitement, and the uncertainty that come with relocating. What neighborhoods have the best schools? Where can you find a waterfront home that fits your budget? How does the buying process differ in Florida? Millie has answered these questions firsthand and uses that knowledge to guide every client through a seamless transition.

Specializing in the Space Coast and Brevard County area, Millie serves communities stretching from Titusville to Melbourne Beach. Her territory includes family-friendly neighborhoods like Canaveral Groves, waterfront living on Merritt Island, and the growing communities near Lake Nona. Whether you are relocating from the Midwest, downsizing from the Northeast, or buying your first investment property in Florida, she brings a personalized, client-first approach to every transaction.

Millie is driven by a simple belief: buying or selling a home should feel like a partnership, not a process. She takes the time to understand your priorities, communicates proactively at every stage, and works tirelessly to make sure your goals are met.

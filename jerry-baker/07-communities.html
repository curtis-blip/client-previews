Communities

Eight towns in the Gilpin County corridor

From Black Hawk and Central City out to Nederland and Pinecliffe, down through Idaho Springs to Golden and Evergreen. Each one trades differently.

Start here

The town decides more than the house does

Access, water, commute and how long property sits all change from one town to the next. Open any of them for what to expect, then look at what is listed.

The honest version

How these towns actually differ

Golden and Evergreen sit in Jefferson County and behave most like the metro. More inventory, faster sales, and more competition for anything good. If you want liquidity, that is where it is.

Black Hawk and Central City are the gaming corridor, which means a different economy and a different mix of residential stock, Victorian through modern. Idaho Springs runs up Clear Creek Canyon with its own character and its own commute.

Nederland, Rollinsville and Pinecliffe are the high country proper. Thinner inventory, more land and cabins, more diligence required, and the places where a broker who knows which roads get plowed earns the fee.

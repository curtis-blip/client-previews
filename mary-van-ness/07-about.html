About

Mary K. Van Ness

Broker · Certified Probate Real Estate Specialist · AARE

Mary K. Van Ness is a broker at AARE (Andrew Arroyo Real Estate) based in Somerset, New Jersey. She holds the certified probate real estate specialist designation and works with executors, heirs, families, and first-time buyers across five Central New Jersey counties.

Her practice is built around one phrase: service with integrity. That means honest pricing, clear timelines, and no pressure. It also means being reachable when clients have questions, especially when those questions come up during an estate sale or a probate process that already feels overwhelming.

Before real estate, Mary built a career on listening carefully, solving problems patiently, and treating people the way she would want her own family treated. Those habits carry into every listing, every buyer consultation, and every walkthrough of a home that has been in one family for forty years.

She serves Somerset, Middlesex, Mercer, Burlington, and Union counties, from Bridgewater and Hillsborough in the north to Mount Laurel and Moorestown in the south. If you are thinking about buying, selling, or facing a probate sale, Mary is one phone call away at 732-207-7515.

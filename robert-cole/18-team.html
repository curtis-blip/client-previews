The Cole Home Team

Built around one idea: tell people the truth about the number.

Mandy and Robert Cole built the team out of Terrell and it now covers Kaufman, Henderson, Rockwall, Collin and Dallas counties. Between them the team closed 88 homes in the last twelve months, roughly $25.5M in volume, across a $75,000 to $1.6M range.

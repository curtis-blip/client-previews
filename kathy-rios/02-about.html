About Kathy Rios

Twenty years in one market

Kathy Rios, REALTOR®

Your trusted real estate partner

Licensed since 2004, Kathy brings over two decades of in-depth real estate experience to every transaction. Whether you are a first-time homebuyer, a seasoned investor, or getting ready to sell, she offers guidance built around your goals rather than a script.

As a full-time agent at Russ Lyon Sotheby’s International Realty she consistently closes around thirty transactions a year, and in the past three years has sold over $35 million in volume. She reads that as a reflection of her clients’ trust and of the negotiating that keeps them coming back.

Real estate is not just her job. She would be honored to guide you through your next move, and to show you why so many of her clients become clients for life.

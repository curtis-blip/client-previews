Laurens County

Dublin, GA

The county hub. Downtown, established neighborhoods, new construction, walkable shops.

Dublin is the heart of Laurens County, the city that anchors everything else around it. Downtown carries the historic blocks, the courthouse, the local restaurants, and the small-business stretches that give the city its character. Just past downtown, established neighborhoods like the Hillcrest corridor and the streets near the country club offer the kind of mature, leafy living that the metro headlines do not cover.

On the edges, new construction subdivisions are growing into the demand from buyers relocating from Atlanta, Macon, and Warner Robins. The mix means a Dublin GA home search can include a 1950s craftsman, a 2024 build with the latest finishes, or a half-acre property on the city limits, all in the same week.

  • Population: ~16,000 city · Laurens County ~48,000
  • Commute: Macon ~1 hr · Warner Robins ~90 min · Savannah ~2 hrs
  • What you get here: Established neighborhoods, walkable downtown, new construction on the edges, hospital + main shopping
  • Best for: Buyers who want everything in one city, in-town life, or a primary employer base nearby

West Laurens

Dudley, GA

Quiet, small-town setting along Highway 80, ten minutes west of Dublin. Land, privacy, easy access.

Dudley sits about 8 miles west of Dublin along Highway 80, in the kind of quiet small-town setting most buyers picture when they say "homes with land outside Dublin." Quarter-acre starter homes are rare here. Most Dudley GA real estate sits on a half-acre minimum, with full-acre, 5-acre, and 10 to 50-acre family tracts showing up on the MLS regularly.

The trade-off is straightforward. You get land, privacy, and a slower pace, and you keep Dublin within a 10-minute drive for groceries, hospital, schools, and main shopping. The Hwy 80 corridor is the connector. Most Dudley buyers I work with have either grown up here and are coming back, or are commuting professionals who want acreage without the long drive.

  • Distance to Dublin: ~8 miles · ~10 minutes via Hwy 80
  • Typical lot size: 0.5 to 5 acres, with 10-50 acre family parcels regularly available
  • What you get here: Real land, established country properties, quiet roads, mature trees
  • Best for: Buyers who want acreage without a long commute, families looking for a slower pace

North Laurens

Dexter, GA

Country atmosphere a short drive from Dublin. Open space, relaxed pace, real acreage homes.

Dexter is a quiet small town in the northern half of Laurens County, surrounded by open Georgia pine and family farmland. Most of the Dexter GA real estate market is acreage properties, country homes set back from the road, and the kind of land tracts that get passed down through families rather than turning over on the MLS every few years.

If you are looking for a Dublin GA home search that opens up to wider lots and a quieter daily pace, Dexter is one of the first places I will show you. Dublin is a short drive for everything you need, the schools, hospital, main shopping. Dexter is where you come home and unwind.

  • Distance to Dublin: ~15 minutes
  • Typical lot size: Acreage homes standard, larger tracts available
  • What you get here: Open space, country atmosphere, family-land properties, no traffic
  • Best for: Buyers who prioritize land + privacy and want a small-town feel

South Laurens

Rentz, GA

Farmhouse and acreage along Highway 257. Some of the best value-per-acre in Laurens County.

Rentz is one of Laurens County's smaller incorporated communities, tucked into the southern half of the county along Highway 257, about 12 miles south of Dublin. The town itself is small, fewer than a few hundred residents inside the city limits, but the broader Rentz GA real estate market wraps a much wider rural ZIP that pulls in farmhouse property, family land, and steady acreage homes that buyers miss when they only search Dublin proper.

What makes Rentz interesting strategically is the position. You are 12 minutes from Dublin via Hwy 257, which keeps hospital, schools, and main retail accessible. You are also a short drive from I-16, which puts Macon and Savannah on practical reach. Most Rentz buyers I see closing want more land, more privacy, and less hustle, and they would rather drive 12 minutes for groceries than pay Dublin prices for a postage-stamp lot.

  • Distance to Dublin: ~12 miles · ~12 minutes via Hwy 257
  • Highway access: I-16 within reach for Macon + Savannah commutes
  • What you get here: Farmhouse-and-pasture setups, real acreage, best value-per-acre in the area
  • Best for: Buyers who want land + a short hop to bigger markets without paying Dublin proper prices

South Laurens

Cadwell, GA

Family land and private acreage along Highway 117 in the southern stretch of the county.

Cadwell sits in the southern stretch of Laurens County, a small incorporated community of a few hundred residents along Highway 117. The wider rural ZIP pulls in family land, pasture, and the kind of acreage homes that almost never make it onto the front page of a Zillow search. If you are searching Cadwell GA homes for sale, you have already done some homework. This is not a market for casual scrollers, it is a market for buyers who know exactly what they want.

The character of Cadwell is privacy and small-community closeness. Most everyone knows most everyone. Lots are large by Dublin standards, drives are long-and-straight, and the pace is unmistakably southern-rural. Dublin is roughly 20 minutes by car, close enough to keep the city services in reach without bringing the city to your door.

  • Distance to Dublin: ~20 minutes via Hwy 117
  • What you get here: Real privacy, family land, the kind of acreage that holds value
  • What is rare: Newer construction is uncommon, most homes are established
  • Best for: Buyers who want privacy + community, often coming home or settling near family

Oconee River

East Dublin, GA

River-side homes and commuter-friendly neighborhoods just across the Oconee from downtown.

East Dublin sits across the Oconee River from downtown Dublin, with its own grain of personality. The river drives a lot of the character here, river-side properties, the access to fishing and boating, and a real sense of being just-outside-of-town without being far from it. Practically speaking, you are minutes from downtown Dublin, the hospital, and the Hillcrest shopping stretch.

East Dublin GA real estate ranges from established neighborhoods to riverfront properties to commuter-friendly homes that pull in workers heading to Dublin itself, Macon, or Warner Robins. It is a popular area for buyers who want river access or the slight psychological shift of being on the other side of the bridge while still keeping everything inside a 10-minute drive.

  • Distance to Dublin: Across the bridge, ~5 minutes to downtown
  • River access: Oconee riverfront properties available
  • What you get here: Mix of established homes + riverside lots, easy commuter access
  • Best for: Buyers who want river access or quick Dublin access in a slightly different neighborhood mix

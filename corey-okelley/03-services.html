Commercial Real Estate Services · Pensacola, FL

Full-service commercial advisory.

Representation on either side of the table, plus valuation, leasing, and investment advisory, managed with a disciplined, execution-focused approach.

O’Kelley CRE provides full-service commercial real estate advisory across Pensacola and the Gulf Coast, backed by the platform and reach of SVN Southland Commercial. Whether you own a retail center, lease office space, or build an investment portfolio, Corey works every phase of the transaction with the same goal in mind, to protect your position and deliver measurable results.

That work spans both sides of the table. Corey represents landlords and sellers who need their property positioned and marketed to qualified prospects, and he represents tenants and buyers who need an advocate through site selection, negotiation, and closing. Alongside representation, his investment advisory brings cap rate, NOI, and hold-strategy discipline to acquisitions and dispositions. Every engagement is structured to mitigate risk and quantify the outcome, not just close a deal.

Questions

Common questions about working with Corey.

In landlord representation, Corey works for the property owner to market space, qualify prospects, and negotiate terms that protect the owner’s value. In tenant representation, he works for the occupier to find the right space, evaluate options, and negotiate the lease entirely on the tenant’s behalf. Corey represents one side per transaction so your interests come first.

A Broker Opinion of Value, or BOV, is a broker’s informed estimate of what your commercial property is worth, grounded in income, comparable sales, and current cap rates. It is free and far faster than a formal appraisal, which makes it a practical first step before a sale, refinance, or strategic decision. You can request a free BOV here.

Yes, through a partnership. Day-to-day property management is delivered by our trusted partner, Blue Commercial Properties, which handles operations, tenant relations, and maintenance. That arrangement lets Corey stay focused on advisory, leasing, and sales while owners still get dependable, professional oversight of the asset.

Lease advisory is strategic guidance on the structure and economics of a commercial lease. Corey advises landlords and tenants on terms such as NNN versus gross rent, escalations, renewal and expansion options, and restructures, so the lease reflects current market conditions and supports your longer-term plan rather than just filling space.

Investment advisory guides buyers and sellers of income-producing commercial property through acquisition and disposition. Corey analyzes cap rate, net operating income, tenancy and lease term, and hold strategy, then frames the numbers so you can weigh risk and return with discipline and decide with confidence.

Corey serves Pensacola, Northwest Florida, and the wider Gulf Coast, with a primary focus on retail and office assets and adjacent investment and land work. With more than eight years building relationships in the local market, he pairs that on-the-ground knowledge with the reach of SVN Southland Commercial.

Living in Silt, CO

Between Rifle and New Castle, and the quietest of the three. Newer subdivisions on the river side, older lots on the bench, and a straightforward commute either direction.

OverviewLocal knowledgeGood to knowMeet Kevin

00 Orientation

Silt is the quiet one between Rifle and New Castle

Silt does one thing and does it well: it puts you ten minutes from Rifle and ten minutes from New Castle, on the river, for less than either. There is a grocery store, a school and a post office, and for anything beyond that you are driving, which most people here decided was a fair trade. The town is small enough that inventory is genuinely thin, so this is not a market you browse for six months. What comes up is usually either a newer house on the river side or an older lot up on the bench, and those are two different purchases with two different sets of questions.

01

The river side

Newer subdivisions between the highway and the Colorado. Straightforward houses, town water and sewer, and a ten minute drive either direction to Rifle or New Castle.

02

Up on the bench

Older lots and small acreage above town, where you get the view and the space and take on a well and a septic system to get them. The bench also sits above the valley fog that settles on the river in winter, which people notice in January and never think about in July.

01 Before you offer

What I would check for you

Silt is small, which means inventory is thin and the right house does not wait. These are the things I check before we write.

Town water or a well

Inside the town limits you are on town water. Outside, you are not, and the two are different purchases with different long-term costs.

The septic field, on its own

A separate inspection, never folded into the home inspection. A failed leach field is a five-figure surprise and it is entirely avoidable.

Irrigation and ditch rights

Some of the older parcels carry ditch shares. What conveys with the deed and whether it is enough to irrigate what you are picturing are two questions.

Who maintains the road

Town street, county road or a private easement shared with neighbours. Worth knowing before the first winter.

Mineral rights

Often severed here. It rarely changes a decision but it belongs in the title review rather than at the closing table.

What the bench does in weather

Access above town is different in February than it is in July. If you are moving from the Front Range, drive it once in bad weather before you commit.

02 Day to day

Good to know

Schools

Garfield School District Re-2 serves Silt. Boundaries change, so confirm a specific address with the district rather than assuming.

Garfield Re-2

Everyday

Groceries and the basics are in town. For a bigger shop it is Rifle, about ten minutes west, or Glenwood Springs, about half an hour east.

Town of Silt

Getting around

I-70 is at the edge of town, which makes the commute either direction genuinely short. There is no bus route.

Garfield County

Outdoors

Harvey Gap sits north of town, and the Colorado River runs the length of the valley with public access points either side of Silt.

Wildomar at a glance

An 1880s railroad town, a city since 2008

Wildomar lies along Interstate 15 between Lake Elsinore and Murrieta, in a valley with the Santa Ana Mountains to the west and rolling hills to the east. It became a city on July 1, 2008, with about 28,000 people, and its boundaries take in some 24 square miles.

The name joins the first letters of three founders: William Collier, Donald Graham and Margaret Collier Graham, who also founded Lake Elsinore. Wildomar stayed a farming and horse-ranching community for most of the 20th century, and the City's General Plan describes a mix of large-lot ranch homes and newer housing tracts today.

01

A railroad stop named Wildomar

Wildomar began in 1884 as a California Southern Railroad stop called Car B Station, six miles south of Elsinore Junction. When Franklin Heald, William Collier and Donald Graham divided their La Laguna ranch in 1885, Collier and Graham took the land southeast of Corydon Road. They platted a town called Wildon, then recorded a new map in 1886 under the name Wildomar.

The original townsite lay between Palomar Street and Grand Avenue. Rail service did not last: floods took the line through Temecula Canyon for the last time in 1892, and after the Railroad Canyon track washed out in 1927 the trains stopped for good. Interstate 15 brought the next growth, and the newer tracts with it.

02

Ranch lots beside new tracts

Large-lot ranch homes are still part of Wildomar, between newer tracts and the townhomes along the main roads. The City's General Plan allows agriculture and animal keeping in its rural and lower-density residential designations, which run from one home per ten acres to two homes per acre.

The designation and the zoning decide what a parcel can keep and build, more than the size of the lot does. Before you buy for horses or a workshop, ask Jim to pull both for the address.

03

The Plateau and the faults

The City calls Wildomar the gateway to the Santa Rosa Plateau. From Interstate 15, Clinton Keith Road runs about four miles southwest to the Santa Rosa Plateau Ecological Reserve, which the state's wildlife department describes as oak woodland, chaparral and native grassland. It became an ecological reserve in 1994.

The Elsinore fault zone also passes through the city, west of Interstate 15. The City's Safety Element places one strand, the Wildomar fault, about 2,000 to 4,000 feet west of the freeway, and the land beside it lies inside a state earthquake fault zone, which a seller has to disclose.

04

Water, sewer and septic

Elsinore Valley Municipal Water District provides water and sewer service in Wildomar, as it does in Lake Elsinore and Canyon Lake, but not every house is on the sewer. In the Sedco Hills, between Mission Trail and Interstate 15, the district is converting about 700 septic customers to sewer lines.

Flooding in Wildomar is tied mostly to Murrieta Creek and to flash floods, the Safety Element says, and localized flooding is most common on the west side of the city, where the storm drains are fewest and oldest.

05

What Jim would check in Wildomar

  • Sewer or septic Ask whether the house is on the district's sewer or a septic system. On septic, get the records and an inspection, and ask whether the parcel is in a conversion area.
  • Earthquake fault zones Look the address up on the state's EQ Zapp map. Inside an earthquake fault zone, a new building has to stay clear of the fault itself.
  • Zoning and animals The land use designation and zoning decide whether horses or other animals are allowed and what you can add. Jim checks both before you write an offer on a large lot.
  • Flood and fire maps Look the address up on FEMA's flood maps, especially near Murrieta Creek, and on CAL FIRE's fire hazard maps for the hillsides.

Questions

Wildomar FAQ

When did Wildomar become a city?

On July 1, 2008, with about 28,000 people. The town itself is far older: its map under the name Wildomar was recorded in 1886.

Which school district serves Wildomar?

Lake Elsinore Unified School District, which lists Wildomar among the cities it serves. Its online locator lists the schools for a street address, so confirm a specific address there.

Are there homes on septic in Wildomar?

Yes. Elsinore Valley Municipal Water District provides sewer service, but some houses still use septic systems, and the district is converting about 700 septic customers in the Sedco Hills to sewer.

How far is Wildomar from San Diego and the airports?

By road from City Hall, downtown San Diego is about 68 miles away, Ontario International about 43 miles and John Wayne Airport about 58 miles.

Is Longmont a Good Place to Live?

Five downsides nobody tells you, from a REALTOR® who lives here.

13:17 · Published October 16, 2026 · Watch on YouTube

The quick answer

For a Lot of People, Yes

Longmont is a city of about 100,000 people, about 16 miles from Boulder and 35 miles from Denver. The trade-off: the mountains are a drive, and the nightlife is smaller than Boulder or Denver.

Local context

Filmed in Longmont, 2026. The St. Vrain Greenway runs about 8 miles through town, compared with about 5.5 miles for Boulder’s Creek Path (City of Longmont, City of Boulder). The Longmont Farmers Market runs Saturdays 8 AM to 1 PM, April through November, at the Boulder County Fairgrounds. Lyons is about 12 miles away and Estes Park, the gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park, is under an hour with no interstate.

Questions this video answers

Is Longmont, Colorado a good place to live? For many people, yes. It’s a city of about 100,000 people about 16 miles from Boulder, with mountain views, about 8 miles of greenway trail and city-owned fiber internet. The downsides are a smaller nightlife scene and a drive to the mountains. (U.S. Census Bureau, Visit Longmont, 2026)

How much is a house in Longmont, Colorado? The median single-family home sold for about $606,000 from January to August 2026, about half of Boulder’s roughly $1.3 million and about $75,000 more than Loveland. (IRES MLS, Jan to Aug 2026)

Is it a good time to buy a house in Longmont? Buyers have more room than in 2021. Single-family homes in Longmont took about 2 months to sell and closed at about 99% of asking from January to August 2026, so price, repairs and seller concessions are negotiable again. (IRES MLS, Jan to Aug 2026)

Market figures are from IRES MLS, single-family detached, January to August 2026, and change monthly.

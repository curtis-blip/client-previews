Living in Suwanee

A Railroad Town with Two Centers

Suwanee is a city in the northwest part of Gwinnett County, about 30 miles north of Atlanta.

It began as a Native American village on the Chattahoochee River and grew around a railroad depot built in 1871, and the city was incorporated in 1949. Its Old Town historic district runs along Main Street beside the railroad, and Town Center sits at Buford Highway and Lawrenceville-Suwanee Road.

01

Old Town and Town Center

Suwanee grew around a railroad depot built in 1871. Old Town, a historic district along Main Street beside the Norfolk Southern tracks, has commercial and residential buildings from 1871 to the early 1960s, along with PlayTown Suwanee, a Gwinnett County Public Library branch and a historic red caboose. Town Center sits at Buford Highway and Lawrenceville-Suwanee Road on a former industrial site and is anchored by Town Center Park, with the Big Splash interactive fountain and an amphitheater stage, and by City Hall, which opened in 2009.

The district includes 147 townhome and condominium units and 85 single-family homes. Town Center on Main & DeLay Nature Park, which opened in 2024, extends the area.

02

Parks and the Suwanee Creek Greenway

The city maintains eight parks, and voters approved a bond for open space in 2001. The Suwanee Creek Greenway is a hard-surface trail through four miles of woods and wetlands, with a trailhead at Suwanee Creek Park, which also has an 18-hole disc golf course. Sims Lake Park has a lake with a dock and a 1.2-mile looping trail.

White Street Park, in the Old Town Historic District, holds the Harvest Farm community garden and a public orchard. Gwinnett County manages George Pierce Park inside the city, and its Peachtree Ridge Park and Settles Bridge Park are also nearby.

What to Check Before You Buy in Suwanee

A few questions are worth settling before you write an offer on a Suwanee home.

Sarah's Buyer's Guide

City or county
The City of Suwanee is in Gwinnett County, and many homes with a Suwanee mailing address sit in unincorporated Gwinnett or Forsyth County. Check the city's address map first.
School assignment
Gwinnett County Public Schools assigns schools by address. A Suwanee address in Forsyth County is served by Forsyth County Schools. Confirm the zoned schools for the specific home before you write an offer.
Homestead exemption
Once you own and live in the home, file for the homestead exemption with the county the home is in, Gwinnett or Forsyth; Georgia's deadline is April 1.
Homeowners' associations
If a home is in a subdivision with a homeowners' association, read its covenants and dues before you write an offer.

Questions

Questions About Living in Suwanee

Which county is Suwanee in?

The City of Suwanee is in Gwinnett County. Some properties with Suwanee mailing addresses are in unincorporated Gwinnett or Forsyth County instead, so the city's address map should be checked for any specific property.

Which school district serves Suwanee?

Gwinnett County Public Schools serves students living in the City of Suwanee. Because some Suwanee-addressed properties are in Forsyth County, confirm the county of any specific address first.

Does Suwanee have a downtown?

Yes. Old Town is the historic district along Main Street, and Town Center, at Buford Highway and Lawrenceville-Suwanee Road, is the mixed-use hub with Town Center Park and City Hall.

How far is Suwanee from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport?

About 43 miles by road from City Hall, mostly on I-85. Suwanee is about 30 miles north of Atlanta.

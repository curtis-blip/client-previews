Before you offer

What we would check for you

None of this is special to Whitney. It just comes up here more than it does in a city.

The shoreline is federal

Lake Whitney belongs to the Corps of Engineers. Docks and retaining walls are permitted through them, not the county. We find out what actually transfers to you.

The water line moves

The lake is run for flood control first, so the level shifts through the year. We will tell you what the shoreline looks like at both ends of that.

Weekend place or year-round

A lot of what went up near the water started life as a weekend place. Some have been made into full-time homes and some have not. Ask us which is which.

Well and septic

Outside the city limits, expect a well or rural water and a septic system. We have both checked on their own, never folded into the home inspection.

Who maintains the road

County road, private easement or association street. Three different answers, and three different costs across the years you own the place.

The association paperwork

White Bluff and several lake developments have property owners associations. We get you the dues and the governing documents while you can still act on them.

Day to day

Good to know

Schools

Whitney ISD runs four campuses, all of them in town. Boundaries change and an address outside the city limits is not automatically included, so check a specific property with the district.

Whitney ISD

Everyday

Groceries and a handful of longtime restaurants are in town. For a bigger shop it is Hillsboro, twelve miles east at the interstate, or Waco, about thirty miles south.

Getting around

State Highway 22 runs through town and meets I-35 at Hillsboro. There is no bus route, though the Heart of Texas Rural Transit District runs a weekday dial-a-ride across the county.

Rural transport

The lake

About 23,500 acres of the Brazos, held back by a Corps of Engineers dam since 1951. Lake Whitney State Park sits on the east shore with camping and public water access.

Living in Roswell

A Mill Town on Vickery Creek

Roswell is a city in northern Fulton County, north of Atlanta, with its southern boundary along the Chattahoochee River.

It grew around a cotton mill that Roswell King planned on Vickery Creek in the 1830s, and its charter was approved on February 16, 1854. The city moved from Cobb County to Fulton County in 1932 and includes a National Register historic district around its town square.

Historic Roswell and the Homes Around It

Roswell's oldest buildings sit in the Roswell Historic District, which was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1974. Its buildings are largely Greek Revival and Late Georgian in style, and its features include the town square, Roswell Presbyterian Church and The Old Bricks, built as housing for mill workers.

The City of Roswell owns and operates three historic house museums: Barrington Hall, Bulloch Hall and the Archibald Smith Plantation Home.

02

Town Square and Gathering Places

The city runs Town Square at 610 Atlanta Street, with an outdoor stage for concerts and a picnic area, and Heart of Roswell Park at 925 Canton Street. Vickery Creek Old Mill Park has a covered bridge, trails to the old mill ruins, a historic machine shop and an overlook of the Vickery Creek Dam and waterfall, and admission and parking are free.

The Chattahoochee Nature Center at 9135 Willeo Road sits along the river with trails, gardens and wildlife. Trails in the city include the Roswell Riverwalk Trail, the Big Creek Greenway and the Vickery Creek Trail.

What to Check Before You Buy in Roswell

A few questions are worth settling before you write an offer on a Roswell home.

Sarah's Buyer's Guide

Historic district
The Roswell Historic District, on the National Register since 1974, takes in the town square and The Old Bricks. Before you plan exterior changes to an older home near downtown, ask the city whether any design review applies.
School assignment
Fulton County Schools assigns schools by address. Confirm the zoned schools for the specific home before you write an offer.
Homestead exemption
Once you own and live in the home, file for the homestead exemption with Fulton County; Georgia's deadline is April 1.
Near the river
For a Roswell home near the Chattahoochee, look up its flood zone before you write an offer.

Questions

Questions About Living in Roswell

Which county is Roswell in?

Roswell is in northern Fulton County. It was part of Cobb County until it was transferred to Fulton County effective May 9, 1932.

Which school district serves Roswell?

Fulton County Schools. The district's site lists Roswell High School and Centennial High School and has a tool to find a school by home address.

How far is Roswell from the airport and downtown Atlanta?

By road from city hall it is about 33 miles to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport and about 21 miles to downtown Atlanta, mostly on Georgia 400 and I-85.

Does Roswell have a historic downtown?

Yes. The Roswell Historic District was added to the National Register of Historic Places on May 2, 1974, and includes the town square. The city operates Town Square at 610 Atlanta Street and three historic house museums.

Questions

Flat-fee questions

What is the MLS?

The Multiple Listing Service: the database real estate agents use to find homes for their buyers. Listings flow from it to the major home search sites.

Why list on the MLS if I am selling it myself?

Most buyers work with an agent, and agents search the MLS first. A listing puts your home where they are already looking.

Do I still pay a buyer's agent?

That is between you and the buyer. Buyer's agent compensation is negotiated, and you decide whether to offer any of it.

Do I still need a seller's disclosure?

Yes. Most Texas sellers complete the Seller's Disclosure Notice whether or not an agent is involved.

What if I decide I want help?

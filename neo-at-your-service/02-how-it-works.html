Setup

The only setup step: call forwarding

There's no app to download, no new phone, and no number to give up. You flip on call forwarding once, and Neo takes it from there.

Forwarding takes about 60 seconds

You keep the number your customers already know. Forwarding it to Neo is a quick setting on your phone, usually a short code or a toggle in your carrier app, and it's done in about a minute. Nothing to install, nothing to port, and no change to how your customers reach you. If you'd rather not do it alone, help is available to walk you through it step by step.

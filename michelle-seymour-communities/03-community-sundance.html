Living in Sundance

55+ and all-age neighborhoods around a golf club and a park

Sundance has a 55+ section, Sundance Adult Village with about 1,100 homes, and all-age neighborhoods in the same community, each with its own homeowners association. It is built around the public Sundance Golf Club, south of Interstate 10 and about two miles from the Costco and new shopping at Verrado Way.

On its south edge, the City of Buckeye's Sundance Park grew to 68 acres in 2023, with the city's first public fishing lake, a splash pad, lighted basketball courts and sports fields. For the bigger picture on the city, read Moving to Buckeye: Pros and Cons.

Questions

Sundance questions I hear most

Is Sundance a 55+ community?

Partly. Sundance Adult Village is the 55+ section, with about 1,100 homes, and the rest of Sundance is open to all ages. I can tell you which section a home is in before you see it.

What are HOA fees in Sundance?

Sundance has three homeowners associations, so dues depend on the section. Most homes run about $100 to $175 a month, and I confirm the exact amount for any home you are considering.

Which schools serve Sundance?

Most of Sundance is in the Buckeye Elementary School District, zoned to Sundance Elementary north of Yuma Road and Inca Elementary south of it. The east edge is in the Liberty Elementary School District (Freedom Elementary). All of Sundance is zoned to Youngker High School.

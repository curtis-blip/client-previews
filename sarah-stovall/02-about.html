An Atlanta native helping buyers and sellers across north metro Atlanta for more than 30 years.

Meet Sarah

Three Decades of North Atlanta Real Estate

Born and raised in Atlanta, I bring over three decades of real estate experience, and I’ve guided over 1,000 families through every kind of transaction.

Whether someone is buying their first home, selling a long-held family property, purchasing a second home, or building an investment portfolio, I love being part of their story. The numbers matter far less to me than making each deal seamless and stress-free for my clients. Real estate is often a person’s largest asset, so I handle each detail with the care and professionalism it deserves.

I believe in giving back to the profession that has given me so much. I’m an active member of both my local and state REALTOR® boards and have served in several leadership roles, including president of my local board in 2020. I also contribute at the national level through committee work and support the REALTORS® Political Action Committee at the Crystal level, and I hold the Certified Residential Specialist (CRS) designation through the Residential Real Estate Council.

Off the clock, I’m a proud mom of two adult children and a huge animal lover. Our household is ruled by Cooper and Boomer, our energetic dogs, and our two cats, Charlie and Paprika. When I’m not helping clients, you’ll find me in my Jeep exploring new neighborhoods, enjoying a glass of good wine or bourbon, or at one of our outdoor concerts.

Home/Seller's Guide

Selling a Home in Whitney, Texas

Eight steps, in order, and an honest answer about what your place is worth.

Before you start

The first decision is whether to sell at all

Sometimes the answer is not this year. We would rather tell you that than take a listing that sits for six months and expires, which helps nobody and quietly costs you money.

If it is the right time, the difference between a good sale and an average one is mostly decided before the sign goes in the yard.

The process

What happens, in order

Same process on a lake house as on an acre in town, with different homework at each step.

01

Before you list anything

We walk the house with you and tell you what is worth doing and what is not. Half of what people plan to fix before selling does not return the money. Joanna is direct about which half.

02

What it is actually worth

Not an algorithm. Recent closings on comparable property, what is sitting unsold and why, and what a buyer in this market will genuinely pay in the next ninety days.

03

Getting it ready

Paint, declutter, and the two or three repairs that matter. Michael handles the trades. We are not going to send you into a full renovation to sell a house.

04

Photography and copy

Shot properly, in the right light, with drone work on acreage and waterfront. The listing description gets written by a person who has walked the property.

05

Pricing and going live

Priced to draw traffic in the first ten days, which is when you get your real market feedback. We syndicate everywhere buyers actually look.

06

Showings and feedback

We tell you what people are saying, including the parts you will not enjoy. Silence after two weeks is information and we act on it.

07

Offers and negotiation

We read the whole offer, not just the number. Financing strength, timeline, what they are asking you to pay for, and how likely it is to actually close.

08

Under contract to closing

Inspection responses, appraisal, survey and title. This is the stretch where deals fall apart, and where having someone who has done it several hundred times matters most.

Common questions

Answered

What does it cost to sell?

Commission is negotiable and we will put ours in writing before you commit to anything. Beyond that, expect title, survey if you do not have a current one, and any repairs you agree to. We give you a net sheet up front so there are no surprises at closing.

How long will it take?

Priced correctly, most properties here go under contract inside thirty to sixty days, then thirty to forty five more to close. Acreage and high end waterfront take longer, sometimes considerably, because the buyer pool is smaller.

Should I renovate before selling?

Usually no. Paint, deep clean, declutter and fix anything that will show up on an inspection. Full kitchen and bath renovations rarely return what they cost in this market.

What about selling a lake property?

Dock condition and permit status, the Corps line, and shoreline access all get scrutinised by buyers here. We get ahead of those questions before they become a renegotiation.

Do I need to be there for showings?

No, and it is better if you are not. We will coordinate around your schedule and give you feedback after.

Can you help me buy at the same time?

That is most of what we do. Buying and selling in one season has to be sequenced together, and our Buyer's Guide covers the other half.

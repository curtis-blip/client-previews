Market reports

What makes the Telluride market tick

Garrett's six reasons this market behaves differently from other resort towns. His current reports live on the market reports page.

Read the market reports

  1. A year-round economy

    Festivals in spring, summer and fall and the outdoors in every season bring buyers here all year, not only in ski season.

  2. Little land to build on

    National Forest and BLM land surround the region, so private ground is scarce, and most of it sits under a master plan.

  3. Quality control

    The Town's Historic and Architectural Review Commission and Mountain Village's design review hold new building to strict guidelines.

  4. Rare physical beauty

    Two towns and the mountains between them, joined by one ski area and a gondola instead of a bus route.

  5. Road and air access

    Easier than the town's reputation suggests, and that reputation is part of why it never sprawled.

  6. Historic character

    Telluride is a National Historic Landmark District: a Victorian-era mining town that still looks like one.

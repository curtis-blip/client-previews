A town a month,
on camera.

Market reports town by town, property tours and straight answers about buying and selling out here. Filmed where the houses are, not at a desk.

01 Why I make these

Numbers for this valley are hard to find and easy to misread

National headlines describe a market that does not exist here. Even Colorado numbers are dominated by the Front Range, which behaves nothing like the Western Slope.

So once a month I take one town, pull what actually sold, what sat and what reduced, and explain what it means if you are the person buying or selling there. Rifle one month, New Castle the next. Over a year it builds into a picture of the whole corridor that you cannot get from a portal. If you want the underlying detail rather than the video, the communities pages carry it town by town, and the valuation page will give you a number for your own house.

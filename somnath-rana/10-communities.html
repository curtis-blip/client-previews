Where I work in the valley

Six areas across Las Vegas, Henderson and North Las Vegas, each with its own page and its own live search.

How to use this

Pick the area before you pick the house

The Las Vegas Valley is not one market. Build era, lot size, HOA structure and whether you are inside a city or in unincorporated Clark County all change from one side of it to the other, and they change what a given budget buys.

Each page below covers what the housing stock in that area is actually like, and carries a live search filtered to it.

Summerlin

Master-planned villages on the western edge, backed onto the Red Rock escarpment.

Henderson

Its own city to the southeast, established neighborhoods plus newer build-out south.

North Las Vegas

Where a lot of the valley's newer tract construction and rental yield sits.

Southwest Las Vegas

Newer subdivisions running out toward the mountains, heavy on single-story plans.

Centennial Hills

Northwest, established, with quick access to the 95 and to Kyle Canyon.

Skye Canyon

Newer master-planned build-out at the northwest edge, mostly builder inventory.

Glendale at a glance

Stadiums, Westgate and Catlin Court

Glendale is home to State Farm Stadium, where the Arizona Cardinals play, and to the Westgate Entertainment District.

The city-owned Desert Diamond Arena anchors Westgate, and the Los Angeles Dodgers and the Chicago White Sox share spring training at Camelback Ranch-Glendale.

Questions

Glendale FAQ

Where do the Arizona Cardinals play?

At State Farm Stadium in Glendale.

What is Westgate?

Glendale's entertainment district, anchored by the city-owned Desert Diamond Arena.

What is Catlin Court?

One of the two neighborhoods of Historic Downtown Glendale, where old bungalows now hold shops.

Which school districts serve Glendale?

Several, among them Peoria Unified, Washington Elementary and, for high school, Glendale Union High School District. Confirm the schools for any address with the district.

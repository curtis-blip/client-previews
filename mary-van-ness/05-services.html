Real estate services across Central New Jersey

Probate, residential, first-time buyer, luxury, and investment support for Somerset, Middlesex, Mercer, Burlington, and Union counties. One broker, all five counties, every stage of life.

Whatever the reason you are moving, I can help

Real estate happens in seasons of life. A first home. A growing family. A downsize. A parent's passing. An investment property. Each of those sales calls for a slightly different playbook, and after years in Central New Jersey, I have run every one of them.

Below is what I actually do, day to day. Pick the one that fits your situation, or call and we will talk through it together.

Six Ways I Work

Services, in detail

Probate Real Estate

Certified probate real estate specialist guidance for executors, administrators, and heirs. I work alongside your attorney, manage the property, and move the sale through court timelines. Family dynamics handled with care.

Selling Your Home

First-Time Buyers

Move-Up & Relocation

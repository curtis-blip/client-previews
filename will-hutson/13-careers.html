Before You Apply

Career questions, answered

Georgia requires you to be at least 18, complete a 75-hour pre-license course, pass the state exam, and clear a background check before you activate a salesperson license. The Georgia Real Estate Commission lists the full, current pre-license requirements, and we are glad to point you toward approved course providers and help you plan the timeline around the rest of your life.

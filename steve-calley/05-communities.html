Broomfield · Steve's home base

Midway between Denver and Boulder on US-36

Population
79,174 (Census, 2025 estimate)
County
City and County of Broomfield, consolidated in 2001
Schools
Six districts overlap, mainly Adams 12, Boulder Valley and Jeffco
Getting around
US-36 to Denver and Boulder, the Northwest Parkway and I-25

A day in Broomfield

Broomfield is organized around its parks and trails. Broomfield County Commons and the Paul Derda Recreation Center anchor the middle of the city, FlatIron Crossing sits off US-36, and the Flatiron Flyer stops at the US 36 & Broomfield Station for the ride to Denver or Boulder.

What you'll find

Neighborhoods from every decade since the 1950s: the original Broomfield Heights streets, 1970s and 1980s homes in Greenway Park, and newer master-planned neighborhoods like Anthem in the north. With six school districts in one city, check the district for any address you're serious about.

Highlights

  • Broomfield County Commons
  • Paul Derda Recreation Center
  • FlatIron Crossing
  • Broomfield Depot Museum (1909)
  • Anthem Community Park
  • Interlocken business park

Anthem Highlands · Broomfield

The north Broomfield neighborhood Steve calls home

Where
North Broomfield, ZIP 80023, from State Highway 7 south across the Northwest Parkway
Schools
Adams 12: Thunder Vista P-8 and Legacy High for the addresses checked
HOA
Anthem Highlands Community Association
Getting around
Sheridan Parkway and Lowell Boulevard, with the Northwest Parkway to I-25 and US-36

A day in Anthem Highlands

Life here runs through the parks and trails. The Parkside Community Center has the pool and fitness center, the City's Anthem Community Park sits at Sheridan Parkway and Lowell Boulevard, and Thunder Vista P-8 is inside the neighborhood.

What you'll find

Pulte and Del Webb planned Anthem, and county records show homes built here from 2005 to 2024. The homes Steve has sold here have mostly been two-story single-family homes.

Highlights

  • Parkside Community Center pool
  • Parkside fitness center
  • Anthem Community Park
  • Thunder Vista P-8
  • Neighborhood trails and open space
  • Northwest Parkway access

Greenway Park · Broomfield

An established neighborhood around its own nine-hole course

Where
Central Broomfield, south of W. 120th Avenue and east of Lamar Street
Schools
Jeffco Public Schools: Ryan Elementary, Mandalay Middle, Standley Lake High
Homes
549, built 1970 to 1999 (county parcel data)
Getting around
US-36 and the US 36 & Broomfield Station, about a mile west

A day in Greenway Park

Greenway Park Golf Course, a nine-hole, par-27 course that opened in 1973, sits in the middle of the neighborhood, with a pool and clubhouse run through the neighborhood HOA. The City's 5.5-acre Greenway Park adds fields, a playground and trails.

What you'll find

County parcel data counts 549 homes, about 94 percent of them built between 1970 and 1989. Expect established trees and houses that range from original condition to fully updated; ask Steve which is which.

Highlights

  • Greenway Park Golf Course, 9 holes
  • Neighborhood pool and clubhouse
  • Greenway Park, 5.5 acres
  • Standley Lake articulation area
  • US 36 & Broomfield Station
  • Quick access to US-36

Arvada · where Steve grew up

Olde Town, the G Line and the foothills to the west

Population
122,901 (Census, 2025 estimate)
County
Jefferson County, with a small Adams County portion (City of Arvada)
Schools
Jeffco Public Schools for about 99 percent of the city
Getting around
The G Line from three Arvada stations to Union Station; I-70 and I-76 to the south

A day in Arvada

Olde Town Arvada is the center of it: the historic district around the G Line station, with the Arvada Center for the Arts and Humanities up Wadsworth Boulevard. Majestic View Nature Center and the Two Ponds National Wildlife Refuge are close by for a quiet morning outside.

What you'll find

Established neighborhoods close to Olde Town, and newer ones stretching west toward the foothills and the Apex Center. Steve was born and raised in Arvada, so ask him about any corner of it.

Highlights

  • Olde Town historic district
  • Arvada Center (opened 1976)
  • Majestic View Nature Center
  • Two Ponds Wildlife Refuge
  • Apex Center
  • G Line, opened 2019

Westminster

Between Denver and Boulder, with Standley Lake on the west side

Population
116,182 (Census, 2025 estimate)
County
City of Westminster, in Adams and Jefferson counties
Schools
Split three ways: Jeffco, Adams 12 and Westminster Public Schools
Getting around
US-36 with Flatiron Flyer buses, I-25, and the B Line from Westminster Station

A day in Westminster

Standley Lake Regional Park is the west side's backyard, with trails and boating (no swimming: the lake is a drinking-water supply). The Butterfly Pavilion is on 104th Avenue, the Orchard Town Center anchors the north end, and Downtown Westminster is growing on the old mall site at 88th and Sheridan.

What you'll find

Homes from several decades across two counties and three school districts, so the district can change from one neighborhood to the next. Ask Steve to check the district for a specific address before you get attached to a house.

Highlights

  • Standley Lake Regional Park
  • Butterfly Pavilion
  • The Orchard Town Center
  • Downtown Westminster
  • Westminster Station, B Line
  • The 1908 “Westminster Castle”

Thornton

Along I-25 north of Denver, with the N Line to Union Station

Population
147,766 (Census, 2025 estimate)
County
City of Thornton, Adams County
Schools
Mostly Adams 12, with 27J in the east and Mapleton and Adams 14 in the south
Getting around
I-25, E-470 and three N Line stations

A day in Thornton

Two recreation centers, Carpenter on Colorado Boulevard and Trail Winds on Holly Street, anchor the west and north. Denver Premium Outlets sits just off I-25, and the N Line runs from three Thornton stations to Union Station.

What you'll find

Thornton began in 1953 as a planned suburb off Washington Street and has grown north ever since, so homes range from the original neighborhoods in the south to newer ones near E-470.

Highlights

  • Carpenter Recreation Center
  • Trail Winds Recreation Center
  • Denver Premium Outlets
  • N Line, three stations
  • I-25 and E-470
  • Original Thornton, begun 1953

Lafayette

A Boulder County coal town with an Old Town on Public Road

Photo: Jeffrey Beall, CC BY 4.0
Population
30,116 (Census, 2025 estimate)
County
City of Lafayette, Boulder County
Schools
Boulder Valley School District for the whole city
Getting around
US-287 and State Highways 7 and 42, with the Northwest Parkway nearby

A day in Lafayette

Old Town Lafayette centers on Public Road and Festival Plaza. Waneka Lake Park has a loop trail with a mountain view, the Bob L. Burger Recreation Center is on Baseline Road, and the Coal Creek Trail runs through town.

What you'll find

Miners' cottages near Old Town, and newer neighborhoods toward the edges of the city. The Lafayette History Museum, in an 1890s miner's cottage on Simpson Street, is a good first stop.

Highlights

  • Old Town and Festival Plaza
  • Waneka Lake Park
  • Bob L. Burger Recreation Center
  • Lafayette History Museum
  • Coal Creek Trail
  • US-287

Denver

Every neighborhood in the city, from LoDo to Washington Park

Population
740,613 (Census, 2025 estimate)
County
City and County of Denver, consolidated in 1902
Schools
Denver Public Schools
Getting around
I-25 and I-70, and the A Line from Union Station to the airport

A day in Denver

Union Station is the hub: the A Line reaches Denver International Airport in about 37 minutes, and the B, G and N lines run out to Westminster, Arvada and Thornton. City Park holds the Denver Zoo and the Museum of Nature & Science, and Washington Park has two lakes and a loop trail.

What you'll find

Lofts in LoDo's old brick warehouses, older homes around City Park and Washington Park, and newer townhomes near the rail lines.

Highlights

  • Union Station
  • Larimer Square (1971)
  • City Park
  • Washington Park
  • Cherry Creek
  • A Line to DIA

Boulder

Beneath the Flatirons, 25 miles up US-36 from Denver

Population
105,689 (Census, 2025 estimate)
County
City of Boulder, Boulder County
Schools
Boulder Valley School District
Getting around
US-36 and the Flatiron Flyer to Union Station; State Highways 119 and 93

A day in Boulder

Chautauqua Park is the gateway to the Flatirons, the Boulder Creek Path runs 5.5 miles through town, and the Pearl Street Mall has been the heart of downtown since 1977.

What you'll find

In 1967 Boulder voters approved the country's first sales tax dedicated to buying open space. The city now holds more than 46,000 acres of it, with about 155 miles of trails.

Highlights

  • Chautauqua Park
  • Pearl Street Mall (1977)
  • Boulder Creek Path
  • Boulder Reservoir
  • University of Colorado Boulder
  • Flatiron Flyer to Denver

Superior

A day in Superior

Downtown Superior and Superior Marketplace sit at US-36 and McCaslin Boulevard, Community Park is on Coalton Road, and the Coal Creek Trail starts at South 66th Street.

What you'll find

In December 2021 the Marshall Fire destroyed 393 homes in Superior, most of them in Original Town and the Sagamore neighborhood. If you're looking at a home there, ask Steve about its history, permits and insurance.

Highlights

First-Time Home Buyer Programs in Georgia

Georgia Dream, Peach Plus and Peach Advantage: what each one offers a buyer in Forsyth County.

Programs From DCA

Georgia Programs for a Forsyth County Buyer

First-time home buyer programs in Georgia include the Georgia Dream mortgage products from the Georgia Department of Community Affairs (DCA).

This page covers three of them, Georgia Dream, Peach Plus and Peach Advantage, with the limits listed for Forsyth County as of 2026.

Limits and rules change. Confirm every figure here with a participating lender before you rely on it.

This page is not legal or tax advice. For questions about your own situation, ask a Georgia real estate attorney or a tax professional. If you are still planning the purchase itself, start with the Georgia buying guide.

Forsyth County, 2026

Price and Income Limits for Forsyth County

These are the 2026 limits for Forsyth County, taken from DCA's Georgia Dream mortgage products page. They change, so confirm them with a participating lender.

  • Georgia Dream: a purchase price up to $625,000. Income up to $137,555 for one or two people and $158,188 for three or more.
  • Peach Plus: FHA and VA loans, and you do not have to be a first-time buyer. Purchase price up to $725,000. Income up to $206,333 for one or two people and $237,282 for three or more.
  • Peach Advantage: a conventional loan. Purchase price up to $725,000. Income up to 150% of the area median income.

Eligibility

Who Qualifies for Georgia Dream

DCA's Georgia Dream FAQs list what a buyer needs. As of 2026, you qualify when you meet all of these:

  • You are a first-time buyer, you have not owned a home in the past 3 years, or you are buying in a targeted area.
  • Your income is under the program's limit.
  • Your liquid assets are under the program's cap.
  • Your middle credit score meets the program's minimum, listed as 640.

Peach Plus does not require you to be a first-time buyer. The rules change, so confirm them with a participating lender.

Down Payment Help

How the Down Payment Help Works

Georgia Dream down payment help is a second mortgage at 0% interest with no monthly payment. You repay it when you sell, refinance or no longer live in the home as your primary residence. See DCA's Georgia Dream mortgage products page.

The amounts changed on July 8, 2026. The standard amount is 5% of the purchase price, up to $10,000. Protectors, educators, nurses and the Choice option get 6%, up to $12,500.

DCA's own pages do not all agree yet: its Peach Plus page still shows 3.5% and 4%. Confirm the current percentages with a participating lender before you plan around them.

Before You Apply

Homebuyer Education Is Part of the Program

Georgia Dream requires homebuyer education.

Ask a participating lender which education providers are accepted and how to enroll.

Once a program fits, put the monthly payment next to the tax on a home you like. The Forsyth County property tax guide explains the bill, and Home Search shows what is for sale.

Questions

Quick Answers About Georgia Dream

Do I have to be a first-time buyer to use Georgia Dream?

A buyer qualifies as a first-time buyer, as someone who has not owned a home in the past 3 years, or as a buyer in a targeted area. Peach Plus does not require first-time buyer status. Confirm your situation with a participating lender.

Do I have to pay back the down payment help?

Yes, later. It is a second mortgage at 0% with no monthly payment, repaid when you sell, refinance or no longer live in the home as your primary residence. Confirm the current terms with a participating lender.

Does Forsyth County have its own first-time buyer program?

We found no Forsyth County program of its own. That does not prove none exists, so ask a participating lender.

What credit score does Georgia Dream ask for?

DCA's Georgia Dream FAQs list a middle credit score of 640. Limits and rules change, so confirm the number with a participating lender.

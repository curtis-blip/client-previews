Seller's Guide

Selling a House in Indiana, Start to Close

What actually happens between deciding to sell and signing, including the parts that go wrong and what to do about them.

The short version

Price is the whole game, and the first two weeks are most of it

A house that is priced right draws the buyers who were already looking and ready to move. A house that is priced on hope draws nobody for a month, then gets reduced, and by then the reduction reads as a problem rather than a correction. Jennifer holds the SRS designation, the list-side credential, and the sequence below is how she works a listing.

01

Find out what it is actually worth

Not the Zestimate. A number built from sales Jennifer can defend, walked in person. Start with a valuation.

02

Decide what to fix and what to leave

Not every repair returns its cost. She will tell you which ones move the number in this market and which are money you will not see again.

03

Price it from the comps, not from hope

Overpricing the first two weeks costs you the buyers who were ready. In a thin-comp market that window is most of your leverage.

04

Prepare and photograph it properly

Staging within reason, then real photography. The listing photos are the showing for most buyers, because they decide from a phone whether to drive out.

05

Go live where buyers actually look

MLS through MIBOR first, syndicated out from there, plus her own channels. Timing the live date matters more than people expect.

06

Show it, and gather real feedback

Not just how many walked through, but what they said and why the ones who passed passed. That feedback is what tells you if the price is wrong.

07

Negotiate the whole offer

Price, closing date, inspection window, financing type, what conveys. The highest number is not automatically the best offer, and sometimes it is the worst one.

08

Inspection, appraisal, close

Where deals wobble. Repair requests get negotiated, appraisals occasionally come in short, and both are survivable if you know in advance what you will and will not do.

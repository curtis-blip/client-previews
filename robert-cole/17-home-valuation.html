Home/Sell/Home Valuation
Why It Matters Here

In Texas, the sale prices are not public.

Texas is a non-disclosure state. What a home actually sold for is not in the public record, which means every online estimate you have seen is a model working without the one input that matters most. In the smaller North Texas markets those estimates are routinely off by a margin that would change your decision.

Licensed agents can pull real closed figures from the NTREIS MLS. That is what we price from, and we will show you the comparable sales so you can check our work.

We closed 88 homes in the last twelve months at an average of $351,000, so the comparables we are drawing on are frequently our own.

What You Get

How we arrive at the number

Pull the real closed sales

Actual sold figures from the NTREIS MLS for your street and your submarket, filtered to homes genuinely comparable to yours rather than whatever is nearest on a map.

Adjust for what the data cannot see

Condition, updates and their age, lot position, orientation, outbuildings, whether the property backs onto a road or a greenbelt. This is the part an algorithm cannot do.

Account for the local tax layer

A MUD or PID assessment changes what a buyer can afford to pay for your home. If your neighbourhood carries one, it belongs in the pricing conversation.

Give you the range and the reasoning

A written opinion of value with the comparables attached, plus where we would list and why. If the honest answer is that now is not the moment to sell, we will say that too.

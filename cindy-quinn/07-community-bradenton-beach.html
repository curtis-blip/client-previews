Bradenton Beach Real Estate

The southernmost town on Anna Maria Island. Historic Bridge Street, the municipal pier, and the most rental-friendly zoning on the island. Popular with investors and buyers who want weekly rental eligibility.

About the City

Bridge Street, beaches, and the rental-friendly south end.

Bradenton Beach occupies the southern two miles of Anna Maria Island. It's the smallest of the three municipalities by land area, but has one of the densest mixes of condos, rental properties, and historic cottages.

Bridge Street is the cultural heart: a walkable district of shops, restaurants, and the Bradenton Beach Pier. It's home to long-running local favorites and the most tourist-forward of the three island towns.

Rental rules are the most investor-friendly of the three municipalities. Many Bradenton Beach condos and single-family homes have established weekly rental histories. For buyers focused on income or vacation-rental strategy, this is where most transactions happen.

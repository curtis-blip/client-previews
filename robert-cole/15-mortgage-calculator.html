Read the Assumptions

Three numbers worth checking yourself

Property tax rate

The calculator defaults to 1.8%, a reasonable Kaufman County working assumption. Your actual rate depends on the city, county, school district and any special districts your address falls inside. The county appraisal district publishes the real figure, and we will pull it for any specific address.

MUD and PID assessments

If the home sits inside a Municipal Utility District or Public Improvement District, that assessment comes on top of the rate above. In parts of Forney and Terrell this is the single largest variable between two otherwise identical homes. It is not in the default figure.

Homeowners insurance

Defaulted to $2,400 a year. North Texas hail exposure pushes premiums well above the national average, and the number moves a lot with roof age, deductible and carrier. Get a real quote early, not at closing.

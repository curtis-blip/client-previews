Everything You Need to Know About Lyons, Colorado

23:15 · Noah’s Lyons guide, 2024

Start here

Get Your Bearings in Lyons

Lyons was founded by quarry workers, and several buildings on Main Street date to the late 1800s. The town’s sandstone buildings are a National Register historic district, and the 1881 sandstone schoolhouse is now its museum. The whole town is in Boulder County.

Stop 1

Main Street and the Historic District

Start on Main Street. The Town says Lyons was founded by quarry workers, and several Main Street buildings date to the late 1800s. A few facts from the Town:

  • The Lyons Sandstone Building District is on the National Register of Historic Places
  • Several Main Street buildings date to the late 1800s
  • The Town points visitors to a self-guided Historic District Walking Tour

Stop 2

Lyons Redstone Museum

The Lyons Redstone Museum is in the school building at 340 High Street, built in 1881 and 1882 as a one-room schoolhouse. The museum’s own history page says:

  • Enlarged in 1895 and 1902
  • Turned into the museum by the Lyons Historical Society after classroom use ended in the 1970s
  • Free admission, donations welcome
  • Open daily June 1 to September 30 and some weekends in May

Stop 3

Bohn Park and the Dog Park

Bohn Park is at 199 2nd Avenue. The Dog Park at its south end is the only designated off-leash area in Lyons, and the Town warns that some map apps route you to a private road on 5th Avenue, so navigate to 199 2nd Avenue. The Town lists:

  • Ball fields
  • A skate park
  • A pedestrian bridge to 4th Avenue
  • A restroom

Before you buy

What to Check on Any Lyons Address

Four things that change from one side of town to the other. Each takes a few minutes to look up and is worth doing before you write an offer.

Five Hidden Costs Before Moving to Northern Colorado

12:37 · Costs

  1. Which county is it in?

    Lyons is entirely in Boulder County, and land outside the town limits falls under the county. Look any address up in the Boulder County property search.

  2. Which schools go with the address?

    St. Vrain Valley Schools serves Lyons. The district’s maps and boundaries page returns the neighborhood schools for an address, and it says its maps are not legal documents, so confirm the campus before you write an offer.

  3. Who provides the water?

    The Town of Lyons serves about 1,000 customer accounts and bills its own customers. The drinking water comes from the City of Longmont.

Quick answers

Lyons Questions Buyers Ask

What county is Lyons in?

Boulder County. The whole town is inside it.

Which school district serves Lyons?

St. Vrain Valley Schools. The district’s boundary lookup shows the neighborhood schools for any address.

Where does Lyons get its water?

From the City of Longmont. The Town serves about 1,000 customer accounts, and the water comes from the St. Vrain watershed, stored in Ralph Price Reservoir before pipeline delivery to Longmont’s treatment plant.

What is there to see in Lyons?

The Main Street historic district, the Lyons Redstone Museum in the 1881 schoolhouse, and LaVern M. Johnson Park, which holds the Lyons Whitewater Park, camping and a splash pad.

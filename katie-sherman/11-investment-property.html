Start With What the Rules Allow, Not What a Rental Might Earn

On the Suncoast, a house that can rent by the week in one zoning district may rent only by the month a few streets away. The city, the zoning district, the condo or homeowners association and the county tax collector each have a say.

Vacation rentals on the islands

Anna Maria Island Has Three Sets of Rental Rules

Three cities share Anna Maria Island, and each regulates vacation rentals its own way, on top of the state license.

  • Anna Maria

    The city registers and inspects each vacation rental, and a new registration cannot be advertised or rented until it passes. The application asks for the state license, a Department of Revenue certificate and a Manatee County Tax Collector account.

  • Holmes Beach

    Every rental needs the city's Vacation Rental Certificate, with an inspection. Minimum stays follow the zoning district: 30 days in R-1 and R-1AA, seven days in R-2 through R-4.

  • Bradenton Beach

    A unit rented more than three times a year for short stays, or advertised as regularly rented, needs state registration and the city's Transient Public Lodging license.

  • The state license

    Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants licenses vacation rentals rented as a whole unit more than three times a calendar year for less than 30 days, or advertised as regularly rented.

On Siesta, Lido and Longboat Key, check the rules for the exact address before you write an offer.

Taxes on short stays

Two Taxes on Every Stay of Six Months or Less

Florida's sales tax and the county's tourist development tax both apply to rentals of six months or less. The state's share goes to the Department of Revenue, and the county's goes to the county tax collector.

In Sarasota County, anyone renting for six months or less registers with the Tax Collector and collects both taxes. Manatee County has no agreement with booking platforms to collect its tourist tax, so the owner collects it and sends it to the Manatee County Tax Collector.

Second homes inland

A Second Home Doesn't Get the Homestead Exemption

The homestead exemption and the Save Our Homes cap are only for your permanent residence, so a second home or a rental is assessed without them.

Florida caps yearly increases in the assessment of other residential property at 10 percent and resets it at just value after a sale. Inland, check the homeowners association's rental rules and any community development district assessments before you buy.

Small multifamily

Looking at a Sarasota Duplex for Sale?

A duplex can be two long-term rentals, or your home with a rental next door. The leases run under Florida's Residential Landlord and Tenant Act.

Stays of six months or less owe the tourist development tax; a written lease for longer than six months can be exempt. Check the flood zone on FEMA's maps and get an insurance quote on the building before you write the offer. The duplexes for sale now are under the first tab of the listings above.

Condos as rentals

Renting Out a Condo? Read the Rules First

The association's documents decide how often, and for how long, you can rent a unit, and a rule adopted before you buy binds you.

In a building three or more habitable stories tall, Florida also requires a structural integrity reserve study and, once the building turns 30 (or 25 where the local building official requires it), a milestone inspection. Ask for both.

Questions

What Investors Ask

Can Ryan and Katie tell me what a rental will earn?

No. They can check the rules for an address and show you recent sales of similar properties, but they don't predict rental income or returns.

Do I need a license to rent my place on Airbnb?

In Florida, renting a whole unit more than three times a calendar year for less than 30 days, or advertising it as regularly rented, calls for a vacation rental license from the Division of Hotels and Restaurants. The city may have its own registration too.

  • Vacation rental licensing, from the DBPR
Can my homeowners association stop me from renting?

It depends on when the rule was adopted. A new rental restriction generally binds only owners who consent to it and owners who buy after it takes effect, but an association can restrict rentals shorter than six months, or more than three a year, for every owner.

  • HOA rental amendments, Florida Statutes section 720.306
Can I use a 1031 exchange to buy here?

Swapping one investment property for another can put off the tax on the gain under Section 1031, as long as both are held for business or investment. A house bought mainly to flip doesn't count, cash you take out in the trade is taxable, and the IRS wants the exchange on Form 8824. Ask your tax adviser about the deadlines.

  • Like-kind exchanges, from the IRS
How is a vacation home I also use taxed?

IRS Publication 527 covers rental income and expenses, including a vacation home you use yourself part of the year. Your tax adviser can apply it to your situation.

  • Residential rental property, IRS Publication 527

Neighborhoods

Carlsbad Neighborhoods

Carlsbad splits into four quarters. Here is what you will find in each one.

Northwest Carlsbad

The Village and Barrio

  • The Village

    Downtown, with the restaurants, shops and hotels. The old 1907 Santa Fe depot is now the visitor center.

  • Barrio

    Together with the Village, the City's historic heart, between the railroad and I-5.

  • Carlsbad Ranch

    The Flower Fields, LEGOLAND and the outlets, east of I-5 on Paseo del Norte.

Northeast Carlsbad

Calavera Hills and Robertson Ranch

  • Calavera Hills

    Master-planned homes near Carlsbad Village Drive and Tamarack Avenue, with Calavera Hills Community Park on Glasgow Drive.

  • Robertson Ranch

    Planned in 2006, north of El Camino Real on both sides of Cannon Road. A new city park is designed for it.

  • Quarry Creek

    Approved in 2013 for 656 homes, south of Highway 78 on the Oceanside border, near the Marron Adobe.

Southwest Carlsbad

Aviara and Poinsettia Shores

  • Aviara

    Built around the Park Hyatt Aviara and its Arnold Palmer golf course, with a public trail along Batiquitos Lagoon.

  • Poinsettia Shores

    On the north side of Batiquitos Lagoon, by the Poinsettia Coaster station.

Southeast Carlsbad

La Costa, Bressi Ranch and Rancho Carrillo

  • La Costa

    Grew up around the La Costa resort, which opened in 1965 and has had 36 holes of golf since 1984.

  • Villages of La Costa

    The Greens, the Oaks and the Ridge: homes, a school site, a community park and open space.

  • Bressi Ranch

    At Palomar Airport Road and El Camino Real, with a village center of shops and places to eat.

  • Rancho Carrillo

    Between Palomar Airport Road and San Marcos, beside Leo Carrillo Ranch Historic Park.

Living Here

What It's Like to Live Here

The places people in Carlsbad go on a weekend.

  • The Flower Fields

    Fields of ranunculus in bloom each spring, from March into May.

  • LEGOLAND California

    The theme park, water park and SEA LIFE Aquarium.

  • Museum of Making Music

    A hands-on music museum on Armada Drive.

  • Carlsbad Strawberry Company

    Pick your own strawberries in spring and early summer, pumpkins in fall.

  • Batiquitos Lagoon

    Docent-led walks on the north shore trail.

  • Agua Hedionda Lagoon Discovery Center

    Nature exhibits and trails on Cannon Road.

  • Carlsbad State Beach

    Below the bluffs on Carlsbad Boulevard.

  • Leo Carrillo Ranch Historic Park

    Adobe buildings, windmills and gardens, a California Historical Landmark.

Shopping and Dining

Where to Shop and Eat

Shopping

  • Carlsbad Village

    Boutiques, cafes and the farmers market on State Street.

  • Carlsbad Premium Outlets

    More than 85 outlet stores on Paseo del Norte.

  • The Shoppes at Carlsbad

    The indoor mall on El Camino Real, with a cinema.

  • The Forum Carlsbad

    An open-air center at El Camino Real and Calle Barcelona.

  • The Beacon La Costa

    The La Costa shopping center, renovated in 2015.

  • Bressi Ranch Village Center

    Shops, dining and services on Gateway Road.

Schools

Which School Goes With Your Address

Four school districts serve Carlsbad, and the address decides which one. Confirm with the district before you buy.

  • Carlsbad Unified

    Serves most of the city, kindergarten to grade 12. Look up an address.

  • San Marcos Unified

    Serves part of the city, kindergarten to grade 12. District site.

  • Encinitas Union

    The elementary district for part of the city, kindergarten to grade 6.

  • San Dieguito Union High

    The middle and high school district for the same part of the city.

Before You Buy

Before You Make an Offer

Check these on any home before you write an offer.

  1. 01

    Coastal Zone

    About 37 percent of the city. Most projects there need a coastal permit. The City's lookup.

  2. 02

    Special Taxes

    Community Facilities Districts add to some tax bills. Read the county tax bill for the address.

  3. 03

    Flood and Bluffs

    Near the coast and the lagoons, read the City's sea level rise study and FEMA's flood maps.

  4. 04

    Water and School Districts

    Three water districts and four school districts serve the city. Both depend on the address.

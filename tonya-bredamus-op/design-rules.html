Tonya's Signature: Hot Pink

Tonya's brand is built around pink as the dominant accent. The color IS the brand. Per her own brief: "Pink isn't soft on you, it's power pink. It says: I'm professional, memorable, and I do things my way."

Hot pink (#EF5ECF) appears on every primary CTA, link hover, brand moment, and accent. Never desaturate it to "feel safer." Never substitute a muted pink. The pink should hit.

Typography Hard Rules
  • DM Sans is the only font family. Heading and body share it.
  • H1s are Title Case, not UPPERCASE. This is the alternate per the H1 capitalization rule, chosen because Tonya's brand is warm/signature, not cold-luxe.
  • Never introduce a third font, never use italic-serif accents inside H1s.
  • No em dashes anywhere in copy. Replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.

Color Hard Rules
  • Pink is the constant. Don't desaturate, don't substitute, don't tint.
  • Never invent additional colors. Only use the 8 canonical aliases above.
  • Hover states: lighten/darken by 8-12%, never shift hue.
  • Body text contrast on all backgrounds meets WCAG AA.

Buttons

Primary buttons use Tonya's signature pink. Secondary buttons are black-bordered ghost buttons. NO rounded corners.

Primary
Pink solid background, white text, UPPERCASE, sharp corners. On hover, darkens to #D63FB4. Does NOT invert to ghost (different from default sparse template).
Schedule a Conversation
Secondary
Black border, transparent fill, black text. Used as the second CTA when the pink primary already exists in the same block.
Read My Story
Button Hard Rules
  • Border-radius: 0. Sharp geometry, no rounded buttons.
  • Never use 3+ buttons in a single hero. One primary, optionally one secondary.
  • Pink does NOT invert to ghost on hover. It darkens. Different from default sparse template.
  • Transition duration 400ms with cubic-bezier easing on hover.
  • Always preserve `:focus-visible` indicator for accessibility.

Voice and Content Reminders

Content writers and designers both need to remember these. Even if the writer's job, a designer should flag any AI-tell phrasing before publish.

Copy Hard Rules
  • Never use em dashes (`—` or `--`). Replace with comma, period, colon, or middle dot.
  • Never use generic AI phrases: "dream home", "seamless experience", "every step of the way", "your trusted partner", "with confidence", "in today's market".
  • Never use generic city descriptors: "thriving", "vibrant", "nestled", "perfect blend", "world-class", "rich history".
  • Never use parallel triplets, hyphenated compound metaphors, or conditional buildups ("Whether you're A, B, or C...").
  • Every page H1 references a specific Tonya differentiator: out-of-state buyer specialty, 24-year second career, pink signature, film producer angle.
  • First-person where Tonya's brief supports it. Her bio is gold; quote it verbatim.

Tonya-Specific Anti-Patterns

Never Do These for Tonya's Site
  • UPPERCASE H1s (wrong register — Title Case throughout)
  • Black/gold luxury-canon styling (she's signature-pink-warm, not lux-austere)
  • Raleway 200 / Montserrat fonts (DM Sans only)
  • Desaturated "muted pink" to feel safer (use #EF5ECF at full saturation)
  • Separating the film identity onto a hidden page (Film is in primary nav)
  • Italic-serif accent type, anywhere
  • Em dashes, anywhere in copy
  • Over-explaining design choices to Tonya (she wants confident judgment, not 5 options)
  • Carousel sliders in heroes (banned across all builds)
  • Split heroes anywhere (banned except clean home-page exception)
  • "Recently Sold Nearby" on Home Valuation page (data access gap)
  • 4-step process blocks shipped to production (8 is the default)
  • FAQ "mode: faq" on About page (use "mode: expertise" — Areas of Expertise)
  • Home-page Communities V2 treatment on inner pages
  • About teaser block on /about (use full Agent Bio)

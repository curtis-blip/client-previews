Meet Melo-de

A Personalized Approach to Real Estate

For over 25 years, Melo-de Savage has helped clients navigate every corner of San Diego County. Whether you are transitioning from a cool mountain retreat in Julian to sun-drenched coastal living in Del Mar, or discovering San Diego for the very first time, her deep local knowledge bridges every landscape in the region.

Melo-de believes that real estate is about more than property. It is about lifestyle, timing, and trust. She works closely with each client to understand what matters most, then delivers honest guidance backed by decades of hands-on experience across mountain, inland, and coastal communities.

Philosophy

Melo-de takes an education-first approach to every client relationship. She believes that informed clients make better decisions, so she invests time in explaining the market, walking through comparable data, and providing honest guidance even when the answer is not what someone wants to hear. Transparency builds trust, and trust is the foundation of every successful transaction.

Client Approach

Every engagement begins with listening. Melo-de takes the time to understand your lifestyle priorities, financial goals, and timeline before suggesting a single property or pricing strategy. She recognizes that buying or selling a home is deeply personal, and she navigates the complexity of San Diego's diverse market with patience, clarity, and genuine care for each client's outcome.

Melo-de has been a licensed California real estate agent since 2006, bringing over 25 years of combined real estate and professional experience to her clients. She has worked across every micro-market in San Diego County, from backcountry mountain towns to premier coastal communities.

Her countywide knowledge is especially valuable for clients moving between these distinct lifestyles.

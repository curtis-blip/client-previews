About Corey O’Kelley

A FINANCE DISCIPLINE MOST BROKERS DON’T bring.

A commercial real estate broker in Pensacola who pairs two decades of specialty finance with local market depth, focused on retail and office across the Gulf Coast.

Meet Corey

Built in finance, grounded in pensacola.

Corey O’Kelley brings more than 20 years of experience in specialty finance to commercial real estate, a background built on structuring complex transactions, reading risk, and negotiating optimal outcomes. Raised by Realtors, he grew up around property and developed an early, instinctive understanding of how markets move.

For the past eight years he has lived and worked in the Pensacola area, building a deep network of professional and personal relationships across Northwest Florida and the Gulf Coast. He specializes in retail and office assets, advising owners and occupiers through sales, leasing, and investment strategy.

Corey is currently pursuing the CCIM designation, the commercial real estate industry’s recognized standard for financial and market analysis. It is a natural extension of the disciplined, numbers-first approach he already brings to every engagement.

My Approach

How Corey works.

Every engagement starts with strategy. Before a property is marketed or an offer is shaped, Corey works to understand your objectives, your constraints, and what a successful outcome actually looks like for you, so the plan is built around your position rather than a generic playbook.

Transparency and accountability run through everything. You will know where a deal stands, what the numbers say, and what the trade-offs are at each decision point. Negotiation is handled with discipline, never emotion, and always backed by data: rents, comparable sales, cap rates, and the underlying economics of the asset.

The goal is the same on every transaction. Protect your value, reduce your risk, and deliver results you can measure, not just a deal that closes.

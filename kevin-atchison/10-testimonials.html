01 The pattern

Every one of these mentions communication

I did not ask them to. It is what people notice, and it is the part of this job I care most about getting right.

These are published in full rather than trimmed to the flattering sentence. One of them runs several hundred words and describes a transaction that nearly fell apart. That one is worth reading most.

I am so incredibly grateful we found Kevin! Initially, we were unsure of where we wanted to buy, and Kevin was incredibly patient with us. His solid guidance was a cornerstone of our experience, as he showed remarkable dedication and perseverance, taking us to about 15 properties throughout Rifle, New Castle, and Parachute before we found “the one.” Given the different geographic areas we were considering, I was impressed with his in-depth knowledge of each market. He offered invaluable insights on each property and location, helping us make well-informed decisions. From the start, Kevin’s communication skills were exceptional. He kept us informed every step of the way, not only answering all of our questions but also anticipating concerns we hadn’t even thought to consider. Our transaction was a bit tricky, given the listing agent didn’t have his act together. However, Kevin’s expertise and diligence kept the deal on track. I am confident that the whole thing would have fallen apart had Kevin not been such an incredible agent! He truly went above and beyond to ensure everything went smoothly from the time we submitted an offer until we closed. Beyond just helping us secure our home, Kevin shared an incredible list of referrals for contractors and introduced us to local neighborhood gems like restaurants and mountain bike trails. His local knowledge added so much value to our experience and made our transition into the new neighborhood seamless. If you’re looking for a real estate agent who is professional, knowledgeable, and genuinely invested in your best interests, Kevin is your guy. We highly recommend him and are grateful for all his guidance and expertise throughout our home-buying journey. And, he will definitely be our agent anytime we sell or buy in the future. Thank you, Kevin!

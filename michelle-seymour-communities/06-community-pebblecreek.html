Living in PebbleCreek

Two golf clubs, two clubhouses and a full calendar

PebbleCreek is Robson Communities' 55+ active adult community in Goodyear. It opened in 1993, Robson still builds and sells new homes here, and Robson counted more than 10,000 residents in 2023.

Life runs through two clubhouses. The Eagle's Nest Clubhouse has a fitness center, a resort-style pool, a lap pool, a ballroom, a library and billiards. The Tuscany Falls Clubhouse adds the 332-seat Renaissance Theater, and the Tuscany Sports and Aquatics Complex has an indoor lap pool. I compared PebbleCreek with other 55+ communities in my August 2024 market update.

PebbleCreek questions I hear most

Is PebbleCreek a 55+ community?

Yes. PebbleCreek is an age-restricted 55+ community. Ask me how the age rules apply to any home you are considering.

What are HOA fees in PebbleCreek?

Most PebbleCreek homes run about $250 to $350 a month. Fees vary by neighborhood, so I confirm the exact amount for any home you are considering.

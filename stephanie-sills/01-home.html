About Stephanie

About Stephanie Sills

MBA, MIM, REALTOR® | Principal Agent, Palermo Properties Team at Compass

Stephanie became a top producer in her first year selling real estate in Half Moon Bay. Before that, she spent 20 years in technology, data management, and consulting, where she led client relationship and contract negotiation teams across the Bay Area. That background gives her buyers and sellers a strategic advantage in competitive markets.

As a Moss Beach homeowner and principal agent with Palermo Properties Team, ranked among the Leading 100 Top Performers in Bay Area real estate, Stephanie combines deep Coastside knowledge with Compass's industry-leading marketing and technology platform.

She holds a BFA, MBA, and Master of International Management, and supports educational nonprofits in the Half Moon Bay community.

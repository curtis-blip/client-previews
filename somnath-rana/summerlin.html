Master-planned villages on the western edge, backed onto the Red Rock escarpment.

The area

What Summerlin actually is

Summerlin runs along the western edge of the valley, up against the Red Rock escarpment. It is not one neighborhood but a series of villages built out in phases since the early 1990s, which is why the housing stock and the price bands vary so much across it.

Housing stock

What you are likely to find

  • Villages built in phases from the early 1990s through to current construction in Summerlin West
  • Everything from attached townhomes to custom lots on the higher ground
  • Most villages carry their own HOA, and the fees differ village to village
  • Golf, the trail network and Downtown Summerlin retail sit inside the footprint

Because the build eras are decades apart, two homes of the same size in Summerlin can sit in very different price bands. Worth deciding which village you are actually shopping before you start looking at square footage.

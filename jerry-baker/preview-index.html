Still needed

Six things before this can launch

Colorado licence numbers

Both the individual licence and the brokerage licence. State advertising rules require them and the current site has neither. This is the main blocker.

Office hours

The footer office card has a slot for them.

Community photography

Golden, Nederland and Rollinsville are covered. Black Hawk, Central City, Evergreen, Idaho Springs and Pinecliffe are not.

Sell page figures

Sales volume, average days on market and list to sale ratio. Shown as labelled placeholders rather than invented.

Showcase IDX credentials

Needed to wire the search, featured listings and the eight saved searches behind the hotsheets.

Confirm the X handle

The supplied handle looks personal rather than business, so it is not linked anywhere yet.

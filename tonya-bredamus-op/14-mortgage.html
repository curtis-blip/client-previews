The Basics

What to Expect From the Financing Process.

Most homebuyers need a mortgage. Here's the rough sequence: you get pre-approved, you start touring homes, you make an offer with the pre-approval letter attached, you do an inspection and an appraisal, your lender does final underwriting, and on closing day the money moves and you get the keys.

A few key terms worth knowing. Pre-approval is a real letter from a lender saying they've underwritten your file. Pre-qualification is just a credit check, which sellers don't take seriously. Escrow is the third-party account that holds money during closing. Earnest money is the deposit you put down when you make an offer. Closing costs are typically 2-5% of the home price and cover title, taxes, recording, and lender fees.

Want the longer version with examples? The CFPB's Owning a Home guide is genuinely useful and free.

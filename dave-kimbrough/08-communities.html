How Dave works the county

Grand Junction neighborhoods and the towns around them

Dave has been licensed in Colorado since 2003, and his sales run from Fruita and Loma at the west end of the valley to Clifton and Palisade in the east, with Grand Junction in the middle.

Wherever the house is, he prices it the same way: against the homes that have actually sold around it. A home on acreage near Loma is measured against Loma sales, not against a subdivision in town, and each page below carries the homes he has sold there when the MLS has them.

Grand Junction neighborhoods without a page of their own, North Grand Junction among them, are sections of the Grand Junction page. Collbran, De Beque, Gateway and Glade Park are on the Mesa County page.

The whole county at once: 3,329 square miles of land and 155,703 people at the 2020 Census.

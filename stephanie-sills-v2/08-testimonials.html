Eight Offers

“The Process She Constructed”

“Stephanie helped us decide if we wanted to sell, the exact right time to do it (based on her knowledge of market activity), whether we should do it on or off market, what work we needed to do (vs didn't have to do) on the house, whether or not we needed to stage, how to live through showings in a seller-occupied home, and several pricing and negotiation strategies. She even put in place back-up offers to set our mind at ease. In the end, we received 8 great offers (all well above asking), thanks to the process she constructed, how she communicated with other realtors, and the way she presented our home.”

Chelsea H. · Seller

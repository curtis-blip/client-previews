Your Victoria Real Estate Experts

Meet The Orr Group

Three agents, one team, and nearly a decade of closings across Victoria County and the South Texas coast.

Why A Team

Someone is always available

A solo agent is one person's calendar. When a listing needs a showing on a Tuesday morning and an inspection response is due that afternoon, one calendar is a bottleneck. Three agents who all know your file is not.

We split the work by what each of us is genuinely better at. Melissa runs the process and the pricing, Jason takes contracts and anything with land, and Caleb carries first-time and relocating buyers. You get all three.

It works. We are currently the #3 team in Victoria for both sales volume and transactions according to RealTrends Verified, and Melissa has been named to the Coldwell Banker International President's Circle three times.

What This Is

What Is a Home Value, and Why Does It Matter?

Your home's value is what a real buyer will pay for it on a real date, given the current state of the market. It's not an opinion. It's a calculation grounded in recent comparable sales, your property's condition, and the buyer pool actively shopping right now.

Why does it matter? Two reasons. First, if you're selling, getting the price right in the first two weeks is the single biggest factor in how fast and how high your home sells. Second, even if you're not selling, knowing your real number helps with insurance, taxes, and financial planning.

What I send you is different from what Zillow's Zestimate or Redfin's automated tool sends you. Those tools average regional data. I look at your specific block.

How I Do It

How I Determine Your Home's Value.

Four things I look at, in this order, every time.

Recent Local Comparables

Three to five comparable sales within a half-mile radius from the last 60 days. Not regional averages.

Property Condition

Adjustments up or down based on your specific home's condition versus the comps. Updated kitchen vs original? New roof vs needed roof? Each counts.

Current Market Dynamics

Days-on-market trends, list-to-sale ratios, interest rates, season. The market moves; the valuation has to be timed to today, not last summer.

Targeted Buyer Pool

Who's actively shopping for a home like yours right now? Out-of-state relocators? First-time buyers? Downsizers? The buyer matters as much as the comps.

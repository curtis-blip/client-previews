A Day in Mahomet

A morning at the Lake of the Woods Forest Preserve, a walk through the Mabery Gelvin Botanical Garden, then back into town for lunch. Mahomet is the kind of place where families put down roots and stay, with top-rated schools and new construction going up to meet the demand. Russ has lived here his whole life, so this is home ground in the truest sense.

What You'll Find

A steady mix of established neighborhoods and newer subdivisions, with lots still being developed on the edges of town. Buyers come to Mahomet for the schools, the forest preserve, and the small-town feel that holds up even as the village keeps growing. It draws families looking for room to grow without leaving Champaign County.

A Day in Champaign

Coffee downtown, an afternoon shaped by whatever the University of Illinois has going on, and dinner from a restaurant scene that pulls in cooking from all over the world. Champaign is the busy half of Champaign-Urbana, with the energy of a college town and the diversity that comes with a major research university at its center.

What You'll Find

One of the widest ranges of housing in the county, from historic homes near campus to newer builds on the west side. Buyers come to Champaign for the restaurants, the university, and a community that draws people from across the country and around the world. There is something here at almost every price point.

A Day in Savoy

A quiet morning in a newer subdivision, a short drive to everything Champaign offers, and the convenience of Willard Airport just down the road. Savoy sits right on the southern edge of Champaign and gives buyers a calmer pace without giving up easy access to the city. It is one of the steadiest spots in the county for newer construction.

What You'll Find

A lot of newer homes and planned development, which makes Savoy a natural fit for buyers who want move-in-ready without the wear of an older house. The quiet streets and the airport nearby appeal to families and frequent travelers alike. You get suburban calm with Champaign minutes away.

A Day in Urbana

A Saturday at the farmers market, an afternoon among the galleries and tree-lined streets, and the easy walk to campus that defines this side of Champaign-Urbana. Urbana is the artsy, creative half of the metro, with a character all its own and a strong sense of place. It is a favorite for people who want personality in their neighborhood.

What You'll Find

Older homes with real character, leafy streets, and a community that leans creative and independent. Buyers come to Urbana for the farmers market, the arts, and the proximity to campus without the busiest of the college crowd. It rewards people who care about the feel of a neighborhood as much as the floor plan.

A Day in Monticello

A walk through the gardens and woods at Allerton Park, a stop at the shops and restaurants downtown, and an afternoon at one of the town parks. Monticello is a true small town just west of Champaign County, with top-rated schools and a quality of life that keeps families here for the long haul. Allerton Park alone draws visitors from across the region.

What You'll Find

A welcoming small-town core with dining, shopping, and parks, surrounded by neighborhoods that families settle into for the schools. Buyers come to Monticello for Allerton Park, the strong schools, and a slower pace within an easy reach of the Champaign-Urbana metro. It suits people who want a real town center, not just a subdivision.

A Day in Fisher

A quiet morning surrounded by farmland, a stop in a town where people still know their neighbors, and come summer, the whole community out for the Fisher Fair. Fisher is a small agricultural town in northern Champaign County, the kind of place where roots run deep and life moves at a steadier pace. The fair is the heartbeat of the year here.

What You'll Find

A tight-knit rural community with farmland all around and a real small-town center. Buyers come to Fisher for the space, the slower pace, and the kind of neighborly feel that is getting harder to find. It is a fit for people who want room to breathe and do not mind a short drive into the metro.

Choosing a Town

Finding the Right Champaign County Community for You

Most buyers do not start with a town. They start with a life: kids who need good schools, a commute that has to work, a budget, a preference for new construction or older character. The right Champaign County community falls out of those answers, and that is the conversation Russ starts with.

After more than fifty years across these towns, Russ knows the trade-offs that listings never show. How Mahomet and Monticello compare on schools. Where the newer homes in Savoy hold up. What the commute from one side of the county to the other really feels like at 7:45 in the morning. He helps you weigh it honestly so you choose a town you will still be glad about in ten years.

My job is not to sell you on a town. It is to help you pick the one you will be happy in for years.

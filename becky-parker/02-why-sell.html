Meet Becky Parker

A Georgia Realtor Who Treats Your Move Like It Matters.

I started this career in 2006 because I truly love helping people. Eighteen years and roughly 760 closings later, that has not changed. I am the agent who picks up the phone on a Saturday morning, who walks your home before I price it, who reads every line of every contract with you. Not because the script says to, because that is how I would want it done.

I grew up in Georgia. I raised my family here. I know which schools the relocating parents are actually asking about, which neighborhoods quietly hold their value, and which streets are worth driving twice before you write an offer. Most of my buyers and sellers find me through the people I sold a home to ten years ago. That is the practice I built, one relationship at a time.

The math is straightforward. More than 100 of my past clients took the time to leave a five-star Zillow review. Not because I asked, because the move felt right. Whether you are listing a paid-off family home in Monroe, buying your first place in Athens, or moving the kids closer to grandparents in McDonough, you get the same thing from me: a calm process, honest pricing, and a single point of contact who answers her own phone.

What you will not get is a junior assistant fielding your questions on a Tuesday afternoon. You will not get a marketing pitch instead of a market read. You will not get a price that is high to win the listing and then a price reduction conversation three weeks later. I will tell you the number that matches the market the first time we meet. If you do not like it, we do not have to list. That is on me to earn.

Why List With Becky

Three Things I Will Do Differently.

01

Honest Pricing

I walk your home before I price it. I pull comps from your actual neighborhood, not your ZIP. If the number does not match the market, I will say so the first time we meet, not three weeks into a stale listing. That is the only way pricing should work.

02

Real Marketing

Professional photography, written-by-me listing descriptions, and a syndication plan that reaches the buyer pools I have built across Northeast Georgia since 2006. No generic MLS copy and a hope. I have a Facebook business page called "Call Today and Start Packing" because that is the goal.

03

Hands-On Negotiation

One agent at the table, on the phone, in the inspection. I have negotiated 760 of these. I know what concessions a Georgia buyer will actually push for, what the appraisal can carry, and where to hold the line. You get my read on every counter, in plain language, before you sign.

Investors and complex deals

Real Estate Investing in Rifle, CO

Rentals, land with water, 1031 exchanges, tenant-occupied sales and deals that have to line up, in Rifle and along the I‑70 corridor.

01 Where most of the work is

Investment property in Rifle and the towns around it

Most of my work is with investors and with deals that have more moving parts than usual. A rental with a tenant in place, acreage where the water decides the value, a 1031 exchange on a deadline, or a sale and a purchase that have to close together.

Rifle sits in the practical middle of the corridor, with Silt, New Castle, Parachute and Battlement Mesa on either side, so a portfolio here usually spans more than one town. Living in Rifle, CO covers the town itself, and the communities cover the rest.

02 What I handle

Six kinds of deal, each with its own checklist

Each one below changes what gets checked, in what order, and who else needs to be at the table.

01

Rentals and small multifamily

Single family rentals, duplexes and small multifamily in Rifle, Silt and Parachute. The lease, the rent roll and the condition of the systems are read before the price is, because they decide what the property earns.

02

Land and acreage with water

On the Western Slope the water is owned separately from the land. What conveys, the Well Permit on record and the access to the parcel are confirmed before you commit.

03

1031 exchanges

Selling one investment property and buying the next on the IRS like-kind exchange timeline. Your qualified intermediary and tax advisor run the exchange; I line up the two transactions so the dates work.

04

Tenant-occupied sales and purchases

Selling a rental with the tenant in place, or buying one with the lease attached. Showings, notice and the lease terms are planned around the tenant from the first day.

05

Buying and selling at the same time

Selling in Rifle and buying in Glenwood Springs, or the reverse. The contingencies and the closing dates are set together, so neither side waits on the other.

06

Estate, trust and shared ownership sales

Inherited property, a house held in a trust, or several owners who all have to sign. Your attorney handles the legal side; I handle pricing, preparation and the sale.

The value is in the details a listing leaves out

Water, the lease, the septic and the title decide what an investment property is worth here.

03 What I check before you write

Six checks on every investment purchase

01

Water and the well

Town tap, a shared well or a permitted well, and what the permit allows it to be used for.

02

Septic

The transfer inspection Garfield County requires, and the remaining life of the system.

03

Leases and tenants

The lease term, the deposit, the rent and what the tenant is owed at sale.

Taxes and districts

Property tax and any metro district, from the Garfield County Treasurer.

04 Questions investors ask

The ones worth answering before we meet

Can I do a 1031 exchange into property in Rifle?

Yes, when the property you sell and the one you buy are both held for investment or business use. The rules and the deadlines are set by the IRS, and a qualified intermediary holds the funds between the two sales. Read the IRS guidance and talk to your tax advisor before you list.

Can you sell my rental with the tenant still in it?

Yes. The lease decides most of it: how much notice the tenant gets for showings, whether the lease carries over to the buyer, and when the buyer can take possession. We read it together first and plan the showings and the closing date around it.

How do I buy and sell at the same time without carrying two properties?

By setting the two contracts up together: a contingency on the purchase, a closing date on the sale that comes first, or a rent back so you stay in place after you sell. Which one fits depends on the market in each town and what your lender allows.

What makes land on the Western Slope harder to value?

The water, the access and what is under the ground. Two parcels of the same size can be worth very different amounts because one has a permitted well and legal access and the other has neither. Both are checked before you write an offer.

Do you work with investors who live out of the area?

Yes. Documents are signed electronically and closings can be arranged by mail, so you do not need to be in Rifle for most of the transaction. Call or text and we will set up how you want updates.

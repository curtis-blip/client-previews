The problem

Why an automated estimate misses in this county

An automated valuation needs neighbours. It wants three or four properties that sold recently, sit close by, and look broadly like yours. In a subdivision that is easy. In Gilpin County it frequently does not exist at all.

So the model reaches further out, picks up something on a different road with different access and a different water situation, and returns a number with confident styling and no basis. That is worse than no estimate, because people make decisions on it.

How I find your value

Access, slope, exposure, outbuildings, what the well and septic are doing, how it reads in bad weather. None of that is in a database and all of it moves the number.

Assessor data, well permits, septic use permits, easements and any mining claim history. Surprises here change value materially and they are all public.

Show you the working

You get the comparables, the adjustments and the reasoning, not just a figure. If you disagree with an adjustment you can see exactly which one.

Three different numbers

Appraised, assessed and market value are not the same thing

Appraised value

What a licensed appraiser certifies for a lender, on a specific date and for a specific loan. Conservative by design, and the number most likely to hold up a mountain closing.

Assessed value

What the county uses to calculate property tax. It lags the market, follows its own schedule, and is almost never what your property would sell for.

Market value

What a willing buyer would actually pay right now, given the access, the utilities and how few alternatives exist. This is the one that matters when you list.

When to ask

Reasons to get a real valuation

  • You are thinking about selling

    Even a year out. Knowing the number changes what you fix and what you leave alone.

  • Refinancing or a line of credit

    Lenders will order their own appraisal, but going in with a realistic figure avoids a wasted application.

  • Estate, divorce or partition

    These need a defensible number with the analysis attached, not a screenshot from a website.

  • You want to challenge your assessment

    If the county's figure looks wrong, a market analysis is where an appeal starts.

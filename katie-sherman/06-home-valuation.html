The instant estimate

What the Instant Estimate Can and Can't See

Enter your address in the box above and press Get Home Value. Public property records and sales data produce three answers.

  • An estimated value

    A single figure from an automated valuation model, a program that prices homes from public records and recent sales.

  • A likely range

    The spread between a low and a high figure. The wider it is, the thinner the data on homes like yours.

  • A confidence score

    A measure of how well the records match your home. If the data is too thin, which happens with new construction, the estimator tells you and points you to Ryan and Katie's analysis.

No program can open your front door. The new roof, the impact windows and the view across the canal are invisible to it.

What moves value

Reading your numbers

Three Values: Market, Assessed and Appraised

Your TRIM notice, an appraisal and a list price can all show different figures for the same house, and each is right for its own purpose.

  • Market value

    The price a buyer would pay today, judged from comparable sales. Florida's property appraisers call it just value, and it is where a list price begins.

  • Assessed value

    The value the county property appraiser uses for property tax, as of January 1. With the homestead exemption, Save Our Homes keeps it from rising more than 3 percent or the change in the Consumer Price Index each year, whichever is less, which is why owners who have been in their homes a long time often see a big gap between the two. Your August notice of proposed property taxes lists your values.

  • Appraised value

    The written opinion of a licensed appraiser, which the buyer's lender typically orders before approving the loan. Florida licenses appraisers through its Real Estate Appraisal Board, and the borrower has a right to a copy.

Questions

Questions About Your Home's Value

How close is the instant estimate?

Close enough to start a conversation. Public records and sales data can't tell it about the condition, the updates or the view; Ryan and Katie's market analysis can.

Why does my TRIM notice show a lower value than the estimate?

Save Our Homes. After the first year with the homestead exemption, your assessed value can rise no more than 3 percent or the change in the Consumer Price Index each year, whichever is less, while market value follows what buyers pay.

  • Save Our Homes, from the Florida Department of Revenue
Does asking for an estimate affect my property taxes?

No. Only the county property appraiser sets the values your taxes follow, as of January 1 each year. A sale, not an estimate, is what resets them, for the buyer, the January 1 after closing.

  • Property taxes for first-time Florida homebuyers, from the Florida Department of Revenue
What if the buyer's appraisal is below our contract price?

The CFPB tells buyers a low appraisal is strong evidence the price was above market value, so expect a request to lower it. Depending on the contract, the buyer may be able to cancel.

  • When an appraisal is less than the price, from the CFPB
Can I take my Save Our Homes benefit to my next home?

If the next home is a Florida homestead, you may be able to transfer all or part of it, up to $500,000, when you establish the new homestead within three years of January 1 of the year you left the old one.

  • Save Our Homes and portability, from the Florida Department of Revenue
  • Portability, from the Manatee County Property Appraiser (2026)
The estimator found no value for my home. Why?

Automated estimates need enough public data about similar homes, and a brand-new or one-of-a-kind house may not have it. The estimator tells you so and points you to Ryan and Katie's analysis.

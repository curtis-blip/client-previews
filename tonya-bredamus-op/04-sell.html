The Selling Process

Eight Steps to a Sold Sign in the Yard.

Here's how I work with sellers from the first conversation to the closing table.

1

Listing Consultation

We sit down, walk the house, talk about what you're trying to accomplish and on what timeline. I'll be honest about what the market will pay.

2

Pricing Strategy

I pull recent comparable sales within a half-mile radius from the last 60 days. Pricing too high in the first two weeks loses the buyers who were ready.

3

Prep & Staging

What to fix, what to leave alone, what to clear out, what to repaint. I'll tell you the truth, not a wishlist.

4

Professional Photography

Real photography of your home, taken in the best light. Photos are 80% of why a buyer clicks the listing.

5

Marketing & Launch

MLS, social, my personal network, and the Showcase IDX feed on this site. Coordinated launch so the listing hits everywhere on day one.

6

Showings & Offers

I manage scheduling, follow up with every showing agent, and walk you through each offer when they come in.

7

Negotiation & Contract

Counter-offers, contingencies, repair requests after the buyer's inspection. I negotiate on your side.

8

Closing Day

You sign, the wire hits, you hand over the keys. I'm there because closings are milestones.

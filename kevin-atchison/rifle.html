Living in Rifle, CO

My office town, and the practical middle of this corridor. Far enough down valley that a family income still buys a house with a yard, close enough that Glenwood is a forty minute drive.

00 Orientation

Rifle is the practical middle of this corridor

Everything on this valley floor is a trade between what you pay and how far you drive, and Rifle is where that trade balances for most people. It is far enough down valley that a normal income still buys a house with a yard, and close enough that Glenwood Springs is about twenty five minutes on the interstate. It also has the things a town needs to work on its own: a hospital, a high school, a state park up the road and enough retail that you are not driving east for groceries. That combination is rarer on the Western Slope than people assume, and it is why I have put more families here than anywhere else I work.

In town

The grid either side of Railroad Avenue, where my office is. Square lots, sidewalks, and a walk to the school or the grocery store. This is the part of Rifle that works if you want a normal town and a normal commute rather than a project.

02

Up on the bench

Ten minutes and you are on acreage either side of the river, with a well, a septic field and room for animals. Different price, different questions, and a different set of things that have to be checked before you write.

01 Before you offer

What I would check for you

None of this is unique to Rifle. It just comes up here more than it does in a city.

Where the water comes from

Town tap inside the city, well or shared well outside it. Each has a different cost, a different legal position and a different resale. I find out which one a property actually has before we tour it.

Shared wells need paperwork

A shared well with three houses on it and no written agreement is a problem you inherit. It is more common here than you would think, and it is checkable in an afternoon.

The septic field, on its own

Outside the city limits expect a septic system. I have it inspected separately rather than folded into the home inspection, because the number that matters is the remaining life, not whether it passes today.

Who maintains the road

City street, county road or private easement. Three different answers and three different costs across the years you own the place.

Mineral rights are often severed

Frequently split off from the surface in this part of Colorado. It rarely stops a purchase, but it should never be a surprise found at closing.

Wildfire insurance, priced early

Carrier appetite has tightened across the Western Slope. On some properties above town the premium is now the deciding number, so price it while you are still under contract.

02 Day to day

Good to know

Schools

Garfield School District Re-2 runs the schools in Rifle. Boundaries change and an address outside the city limits is not automatically in the campus you expect, so check a specific property with the district.

Garfield Re-2

Health

Grand River Health runs the hospital in Rifle, which is the reason a lot of people down valley end up living here rather than further west.

Grand River Health

Getting around

I-70 runs through town, so Glenwood Springs is about 25 minutes and Grand Junction about 90. Highway 13 heads north toward Meeker.

City of Rifle

Outdoors

Rifle Falls and Rifle Gap sit north of town on Highway 325, with the reservoir, camping and the falls themselves inside about fifteen minutes.

Rifle Falls State Park

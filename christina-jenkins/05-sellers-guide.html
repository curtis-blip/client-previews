Questions sellers ask

Before you list

The roof, the flood disclosure, repairs and what comes after an offer: the questions sellers ask before they list.

How is my home's price set?

The list price comes from what comparable homes have sold for and from what is particular to yours. A roof near the end of its life, a flood zone, a seawall or dock, or the association's rules can all change what a buyer will pay.

Do I have to disclose flooding?

Yes. Florida law requires a seller to complete a flood disclosure and give it to the buyer at or before the time the contract is signed (s. 689.302, F.S., read October 27, 2026). Christina can go through it with you.

Should I repair things before I list?

Fix what an inspector is likely to flag, such as a roof near the end of its life, and price the rest. Christina can walk the home with you and say which is which.

What happens after an offer is accepted?

The buyer's inspectors visit, the lender's appraiser looks at the home, and any repairs are negotiated. Christina can help you decide which repairs to make.

What does an older roof mean for my sale?

Buyers and their insurers look at a roof's age. The 4-Point and Roof guide explains what an insurer asks for.

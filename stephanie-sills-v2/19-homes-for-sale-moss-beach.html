Living Here

Living in Moss Beach

Stephanie lives in Moss Beach: a small town on the bluffs between Montara and Princeton, home to the Fitzgerald Marine Reserve and a quiet stretch of coast.

Moss Beach began as a resort, with its first hotel built in 1881, and grew with the Ocean Shore Railroad. Today it is a residential town of about 3,200 people, with cypress groves along the bluffs, the tide pools of the marine reserve below them and the Half Moon Bay Airport at its south end.

Moss Beach Neighborhoods and Kinds of Homes

Moss Beach runs from the highway down to the bluffs. Streets west of Highway 1 sit close to the ocean, under cypress and pine, with the marine reserve at the end of Nevada Avenue; east of the highway, homes climb the slope toward the hills.

Most homes are single-family houses, from older coastal cottages to newer houses on larger lots. Seal Cove, near the bluffs, is a neighborhood of its own.

02

The Fitzgerald Marine Reserve

The Fitzgerald Marine Reserve is a San Mateo County park on the Moss Beach shore, inside the Montara State Marine Reserve, a protected area under the state's Marine Life Protection Act. At low tide its reef opens into tide pools.

Rangers lead tide pool tours, and nothing may be collected. Check the tide table before you go: the best pools show at the lowest tides.

03

The Cypress Grove and the Bluff Trail

Above the reserve, a grove of tall cypress, known locally as the Enchanted Forest, shades the bluff-top trail, which follows the edge of the bluffs south toward Pillar Point.

04

What to Check Before You Buy in Moss Beach

  • Who issues permitsMoss Beach is unincorporated, so permits come from San Mateo County Planning and Building, and the town is in the state's coastal zone.
  • Water and sewerThe Montara Water and Sanitary District supplies water, sewer and garbage service. Confirm what serves any parcel at the edge of town.
  • Bluffs and Seal CoveAlong the bluffs and in Seal Cove, read the geologic report and the county's setback rules. A Seal Cove buyer wrote that Stephanie brought them updated information about the geological issues in the area before they negotiated.
  • The airportHomes at the south end of town sit near the Half Moon Bay Airport; ask about flight paths if quiet matters to you.
  • Insurance, earlyGet a homeowners quote during your inspection period. Some coastal and hillside homes end up on the California FAIR Plan.
  • Your new tax billProperty tax resets to the price you pay, and San Mateo County sends a supplemental bill for the rest of the fiscal year.

Questions

Moss Beach Real Estate Questions

Where is Moss Beach, California?

On the San Mateo County coast about 20 miles south of San Francisco, between Montara to the north and Princeton, El Granada and Half Moon Bay to the south.

Is Moss Beach part of Half Moon Bay?

No. It is an unincorporated community, so San Mateo County handles planning and permits, and the Montara Water and Sanitary District serves it. Its ZIP code is 94038, and its public schools are part of Cabrillo Unified.

Can you collect shells or tide pool animals at the Fitzgerald Marine Reserve?

No. The reserve is inside a state marine reserve, where shells and living things stay where they are. Rangers lead tide pool tours, and the county posts the rules before you go.

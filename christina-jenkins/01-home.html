Meet Christina

Florida Sales Associate · Dalton Wade Real Estate Group

I don't simply open doors and write contracts. I help my clients understand the entire picture behind a real estate decision.

Christina Jenkins

In Sarasota, the things that decide whether a home is right for you rarely show in the listing photos: what the insurance will cost, the flood zone and the elevation, the age of the roof, the state of the seawall and what the association allows. Christina raises them early, while there is still time to adjust the offer, and lines up the inspectors and contractors who can answer them.

Design Patterns & Behaviors

The non-obvious rules that make the site feel consistent.

  • Hero: Full-viewport height, dark overlay on background image, fixed/parallax background attachment, vignette around text.
  • Header: Transparent sticky header on hero pages. Solid dark on scroll past 100px.
  • Eyebrow labels: Always preceded by a 60px orange line, uppercase, 2px letter-spacing.
  • Process steps: 2-column zigzag timeline with a center accent line and numbered circles. Buy and Sell use an 8-step version. Never a straight vertical list of numbered boxes.
  • FAQ: Interactive accordion, smooth expand/collapse, rotating plus/minus icon. 6 FAQs per core page.
  • Cards: Every card has hover lift (translateY -4 to -8px), shadow elevation, and a border or background color shift.
  • Section rhythm: Alternate backgrounds in the sequence light → alt → dark to create visual rhythm.
  • Icons: SVG only, 1.8px stroke width, orange color. Never emoji. Never icon fonts.
  • Images: Real client/staging imagery when available, Unsplash fallback otherwise. Never gradient placeholders.

Anti-Patterns

Things to avoid, always.

  • No em dashes in any copy. Replace with commas, periods, or restructure the sentence.
  • No emoji characters anywhere. SVG icons only.
  • No flat text-only service boxes with just borders. Every service item needs an icon.
  • No walls of bullet points without visual structure (cards, grids, or timeline).
  • No raw embeds. Matterport, IDX, video, forms: always wrap in a styled container.
  • No generic stock copy. Every sentence should reference Phoenix, Scottsdale, or Mark's 30+ years specifically.

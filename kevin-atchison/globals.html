Option B throughout. Plus Jakarta Sans for everything: headings 300 in the case they are written, body 400, labels, buttons and the nav 600 in tracked capitals. Kevin's own #121212 is the text colour and every dark ground; warm white #f8f6f1 and sage sand #e7e4da are the light grounds. The accent #1F5F5B is never text or a line on #121212 (2.54:1); on the dark ground use the light text colour. The --ka- tokens drive the header, footer and home page; the pack tokens drive the interior pages; both carry the same values. No IDX: the community pages are knowledge only, with no search or listings.

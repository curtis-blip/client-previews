Englewood at a glance

An Old Main Street on Lemon Bay, With Manasota Key Across the Water

Englewood sits on Lemon Bay, with Manasota Key between the bay and the Gulf. Its old downtown is Dearborn Street, the main street of Old Englewood Village, where galleries and antiques shops share the blocks with a Thursday farmers market from October through May.

Dearborn Street began as a path and oxen trail worn by the William and Mary Goff family, who moved to Lemon Bay in 1878. Herbert, Howard and Ira Nichols, brothers from Englewood, Illinois, platted the town in 1896 and named the old trail Dearborn. They pictured a grove town for lemons until hard freezes killed the trees, and the founders turned to fishing and the water instead.

01

Dearborn Street and Old Englewood Village

Visit Sarasota calls Dearborn Street the original downtown of Old Englewood Village. The Arts Alliance of Lemon Bay keeps a gallery here among the antiques shops, the Englewood Farmers Market sets up at Pioneer Plaza every Thursday from October through May, and on the first Saturday of each month Cruisin’ on Dearborn fills the street with cars and the people who love them. Past the shops, it turns into a neighborhood of historic homes, and the Lemon Bay Historical Society tells its story, from the Goff family’s oxen trail to the 1920s, when Dearborn was part of the first route of the Tamiami Trail.

02

Englewood Beach at Chadwick Park

Charlotte County runs Englewood Beach at Chadwick Park, at 2100 North Beach Road on Manasota Key. Built in 2001, it has a 900-foot boardwalk with three covered lookouts, restrooms, showers, volleyball and basketball courts, a playground, picnic shelters with grills, and beach wheelchairs to borrow. It is a pay-to-park lot, and the county does not allow dogs on its beaches.

03

Manasota Beach, Blind Pass and Stump Pass

Elsewhere on the key, Manasota Beach has dunes and mangroves, and Blind Pass Beach runs nearly 3,000 feet between the Gulf and the bay. Stump Pass Beach State Park, on the key’s southern peninsula, is known for the driftwood and tree stumps on its beach and a trail through the natural land behind it. Walk the water line on the key’s beaches and look for shark teeth: small, dark and triangular.

04

Lemon Bay, Lemon Bay Park and Indian Mound Park

Lemon Bay, between Englewood and Manasota Key, is a network of mangroves, marsh grasses and seagrass meadows that Visit Sarasota says is home to more than 150 species of birds and 200 species of fish. On its shore, Lemon Bay Park and Environmental Center, at 570 Bay Park Boulevard, has nature trails through pine and scrubby flatwoods, a butterfly garden, a canoe and kayak launch, and guided nature and bird walks through the year. Indian Mound Park, at 210 Winson Avenue, is on the Trail of Florida’s Indian Heritage and has a boat ramp and a launch for small boats.

05

Two counties, two school districts

The county line runs through Englewood. Most of it is in Sarasota County, served by Sarasota County Schools, and the rest, including Englewood Beach, is in Charlotte County, served by Charlotte County Public Schools, so check any address with its district before you count on a school. For books and programs, the Elsie Quirk Library, a Sarasota County library, opened to the Englewood community in 1962 and took Elsie Quirk’s name in its 2001 renovation.

Questions

Englewood FAQ

Is Englewood in Sarasota County or Charlotte County?

Both. The county line runs through Englewood: most of it is in Sarasota County, and the rest, including Englewood Beach on Manasota Key, is in Charlotte County. Englewood has no city government, so the county you are in sets the rules. Check the parcel with that county's property appraiser.

Which school district serves an Englewood address?

It depends on the county. Sarasota County Schools serves the Sarasota County side and Charlotte County Public Schools the Charlotte County side. Confirm the school for any address with its district, since attendance zones change.

Where can you find shark teeth near Englewood?

On the Manasota Key beaches. Visit Sarasota says shark teeth wash ashore there and suggests looking along the water line for small, dark, triangular teeth.

When is the Englewood Farmers Market?

Every Thursday from October through May, at Pioneer Plaza on Dearborn Street in Old Englewood Village.

About Home Values

Why Magic Valley Home Value Matters.

Magic Valley markets vary by town. Twin Falls, Kimberly, Buhl, and Jerome each move differently. Pricing what works in one doesn't always work in another. A strategy that produces multiple offers in Twin Falls can sit untouched in Buhl, and the opposite happens just as often.

An accurate listing price is the single biggest factor in a successful sale. Underpriced leaves money on the table. Overpriced sits, then sells anyway for less than a correctly priced listing would have brought. The first two weeks on market do most of the work, and the price you set decides what those two weeks look like.

Online estimators are a useful starting point. They are not a strategy. The real number comes from comparable closed sales, current buyer activity, your home's specific features, and the way your home should be positioned in the market.

How I Find The Number

Four Inputs Into Your Home's Value.

No single data point sets the right price. The right price is the intersection of these four.

Comparable Sales

Closed sales of similar Magic Valley homes in your town from the past 6 months, weighted by recency, condition, lot size, and features.

Current Conditions

What's selling now, what's sitting, what's pending. Buyer activity in your price range and town tells me where the market is moving today.

Property Specifics

Your home's actual features, upgrades, condition, lot, view, age. Not the broad average for your ZIP code.

Strategic Positioning

Where to anchor in the market to bring real offers, not just showings. List price strategy depends on the buyer pool active right now.

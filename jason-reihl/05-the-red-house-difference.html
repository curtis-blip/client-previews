Our Philosophy

Our Commitment to Property Owners

Red House is a boutique property management firm, not a volume shop. We intentionally keep our portfolio focused so every property gets the personal attention it needs. That's the core of what makes us a different kind of San Diego property manager.

We combine modern technology with hands-on local knowledge. Every owner gets access to their AppFolio portal for real-time financials, automated reporting, and document storage. Behind the screen, our team is on the ground handling inspections, coordinating vendors, and staying ahead of maintenance issues.

Based in Solana Beach, we're within 15 minutes of most properties in our portfolio. When something needs attention, we respond in person, not from a call center three time zones away.

This combination of technology, proximity, and personal service is why our owners stay with us year after year, and why we can offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee with complete confidence.

The Standard This Website Is Built To

This website is meant to work for everyone who visits it, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, screen magnification or voice control.

It is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, the standard published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). That is the goal for every page; this statement does not claim that every page meets it in full. If something falls short, the contact details below are the way to report it.

What Is Built In

The pages are built so that:

  • Every page has one main heading, and the headings under it run in order, so a screen reader can jump from section to section.
  • Photos that show something have a written description; purely decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text meets WCAG's color contrast ratios against its background, photos included.
  • Everything you can click also works from a keyboard, and whatever has focus shows a clear outline.
  • On touch screens, buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall, and no text anywhere is under 12 pixels.
  • Pages fit a phone screen without scrolling sideways.

Home Search, Listings and Other Websites

Home Search, the map and the listing pages are Showcase IDX's, a separate company, and carry listing information from the MLS. Showcase IDX builds those pages, not Team Giarraffa Real Estate or the people who built this website, so they may fall short of the standard above. Other websites this one links to are outside it too.

When a search or a listing will not work for you, call or email Team Giarraffa Real Estate with the address or the area you have in mind, and you will get the details by phone or email.

Report a Barrier

If something on this website is hard to use, or you need information in another format, contact Team Giarraffa Real Estate:

Say which page you were on and what got in your way, and, if you use assistive technology, which one.

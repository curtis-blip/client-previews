Living Here

Living in Montara

Montara is the first town on the coast south of the Devil's Slide tunnels: a small, quiet place between Montara Mountain and the sea.

A lighthouse has stood at Point Montara since 1875. The town became a stop on the Ocean Shore Railroad in 1905 and grew slowly, mostly after the 1950s, and it keeps its rural feel. Homes sit on a grid of numbered streets above the highway, Montara State Beach runs below them, and Rancho Corral de Tierra, nearly 4,000 acres of the Golden Gate National Recreation Area since 2011, rises behind.

Montara Neighborhoods and Kinds of Homes

Most of Montara sits between Highway 1 and the hills, on a grid of numbered streets that climbs toward Montara Mountain. West of the highway, a smaller group of homes stands on the bluffs near the lighthouse, above coves and tide pools.

Homes are mostly single-family houses, from older cottages to newer, larger houses, with few condos. Lots at the top of town border open space.

02 / 04

Point Montara and Montara State Beach

The Point Montara lighthouse, at the south end of town, is now a hostel run by Hostelling International, and the tide pools along this shore are part of the Montara State Marine Reserve.

At the north end, Montara State Beach is close to a mile of sand below the bluffs. California State Parks lists it for beachcombing, shore fishing and surfing; the beach drops off steeply, so swimming is risky.

03 / 04

Montara Mountain and Rancho Corral de Tierra

Behind the town, Rancho Corral de Tierra is nearly 4,000 acres of hills and canyons, part of the Golden Gate National Recreation Area since 2011, with trails into the hills.

From the north end of the beach, the McNee Ranch trails of Montara State Beach climb Montara Mountain, 1,898 feet, for wide views on a clear day.

What to Check Before You Buy in Montara

  • Who issues permitsMontara is unincorporated, so permits come from San Mateo County Planning and Building, and the town is in the state's coastal zone, where many projects need a coastal development permit.
  • Water and sewerThe Montara Water and Sanitary District supplies water, sewer and garbage service to Montara and Moss Beach. On the edges of town, confirm what serves the parcel before you buy.
  • Steep and narrow lotsSome lots climb the hillside or sit on narrow streets; check access, drainage and what the lot allows before you plan to build or add on.
  • Insurance, earlyGet a homeowners quote during your inspection period. Some coastal and hillside homes end up on the California FAIR Plan.
  • Your new tax billProperty tax resets to the price you pay, and San Mateo County sends a supplemental bill for the rest of the fiscal year.

Questions

Montara Real Estate Questions

Where is Montara, California?

On the San Mateo County coast about 20 miles south of San Francisco, just south of the Tom Lantos Tunnels at Devil's Slide and north of Moss Beach. Half Moon Bay is about 8 miles south.

Is Montara part of Half Moon Bay?

No. Montara is an unincorporated community, so San Mateo County handles planning and permits, and the Montara Water and Sanitary District serves it. Its ZIP code is 94037, and its public schools are part of Cabrillo Unified, with Half Moon Bay's.

Which schools serve Montara?

Cabrillo Unified School District. Farallone View Elementary is in Montara, and older students go on to Cunha Intermediate and Half Moon Bay High School. Confirm any address with the district.

What is there to do in Montara?

Walk Montara State Beach, hike Montara Mountain or the trails of Rancho Corral de Tierra, see the tide pools along the shore below the Point Montara lighthouse, and stop at Sage Bakehouse on the highway.

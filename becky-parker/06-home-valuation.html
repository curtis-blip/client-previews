How A Free Valuation Works

An Instant Estimate, Then The Real Number.

Submit your address and a few contact details. The widget pulls comparable sales the moment you enter your address and returns an instant estimate on screen. That number orients you, useful if you are just curious or planning ahead. Within 24 hours, Becky follows up with a written CMA she prepares by hand, factoring your home's actual condition, the current buyer pool, and the seasonal pace of Northeast Georgia. The instant number tells you the neighborhood. Becky's CMA tells you the price you can actually list at. Both come from the same form-fill.

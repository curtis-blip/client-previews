Market Positioning

Why Sell on the Coastside Now

The San Mateo County Coastside continues to attract buyers relocating from San Francisco, Silicon Valley, and the broader Bay Area who are drawn to the lifestyle, ocean proximity, and relative value compared to Peninsula communities. With approximately 77 homes currently on the market and a median listing price near $1.69M, seller positioning remains strong.

Limited inventory on the Coastside means well-prepared homes generate meaningful buyer interest. Properties in Half Moon Bay, El Granada, Montara, and Moss Beach benefit from persistent demand and relatively low competition when priced and presented correctly.

Stephanie uses a data-driven pricing strategy informed by current comparable sales, absorption rates, and neighborhood-level trends to position your property for the strongest possible outcome.

Common Questions

Questions About Selling on the Coastside

How much is my Half Moon Bay home worth?

Home values on the Coastside depend on location, condition, lot size, and views. Half Moon Bay median listing is approximately $1.69M. Stephanie provides a detailed comparative market analysis for your specific property. Contact Stephanie for a home evaluation.

How long does it take to sell a home on the Coastside?

Well-priced Coastside homes typically receive offers within 2-4 weeks. Total timeline from listing to closing is usually 45-75 days.

What does it cost to sell a home in San Mateo County?

Seller costs include agent commission, staging/preparation, title and escrow fees, transfer taxes, and potential repair credits. Stephanie reviews all expected costs during your initial consultation. Learn about Compass Concierge.

When is the best time to sell a home in Half Moon Bay?

Spring and early fall tend to see the strongest buyer activity on the Coastside. However, limited inventory means well-priced homes attract interest year-round. Stephanie can advise on timing based on current conditions.

Pittsylvania County, Virginia

Living in Danville.

A comeback city with character. Historic charm, riverfront views, and economic momentum.

The Town

The Dan River Runs Right Through Downtown.

Historic architecture, beautifully restored brick buildings, boutique shops, and locally owned restaurants. The River District along the Dan River offers scenic walking and biking trails on the Danville Riverwalk, riverfront views, and community events year-round.

Downtown's River District is full of renovated warehouses, loft apartments, local restaurants, breweries, and small businesses. The addition of Caesars Virginia has brought new energy, jobs, and visitors to the area.

Danville sits in Pittsylvania County, surrounded by Chatham, Blairs, South Boston, and Ringgold. If you're looking at Danville for the historic feel but want more land or quieter streets, those surrounding communities are worth a look.

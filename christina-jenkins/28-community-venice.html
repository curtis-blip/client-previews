Planned in 1925

Venice was laid out in 1925 by John Nolen

Venice is a city in Sarasota County. Dr. F. H. Albee bought 2,196 acres in 1925 and retained the planner John Nolen, and the Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers then bought the land and kept Nolen to finish the plan. Downtown still has its 1920s Mediterranean Revival buildings.

The choice here is between older streets near downtown and houses nearer the beaches and the inlet. Christina works in Venice as well as Sarasota, and on a waterfront house she looks at the seawall and the dock along with the flood zone.

Questions

Venice: the name, the plan and the trail

Where does the name Venice come from?

The area was first called Horse and Chaise, after a stand of coastal trees. In the 1880s settlers applying for a post office name wanted something shorter and chose Venice because of the community's many waterways.

Why is Venice a planned city?

John Nolen planned three Florida towns in undeveloped areas, Nokomis, Clewiston and Venice, and Venice had the most comprehensive plan.

Can I really find shark teeth in Venice?

Fossilized shark teeth turn up at Caspersen Beach and Venice Beach, and the shops in historic downtown sell shark tooth sifters.

Where does the Legacy Trail start?

The paved Legacy Trail starts at the Historic Venice Train Depot, which dates to about 1927.

How do I get from Venice to Sarasota without a car?

Breeze Transit Route 17 runs between the Venice Train Depot and the Downtown Sarasota transfer station.

Should I check the flood zone before I buy in Venice?

Near the inlet, a beach or a waterway, yes, and look up the house, not the street. The flood zone guide shows how to find the zone and the elevation.

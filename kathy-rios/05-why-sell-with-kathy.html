Why sell with Kathy

A global network doing the reach, and one agent doing the neighborhood. Sellers need both.

The argument

Two kinds of marketing

Most listings get one kind. Syndication out to the portals, a sign in the yard, and then waiting.

Kathy runs both. The Sotheby’s International Realty network puts a Scottsdale listing in front of an audience that is genuinely international, which matters in a market where a meaningful share of buyers are arriving from somewhere else. Then she does the part that no network can do for you: postcards into the right streets, pop-by gifts that keep her in front of past clients who know somebody, and cross-promotions with Scottsdale businesses that put your home in front of people who were not looking at portals at all.

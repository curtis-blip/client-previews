Jerry Baker · MCNE · COS

Founder, broker and owner

The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development recruited Jerry in 1988 as a real estate specialist and project manager. He stayed twenty years, working both the single family and the multifamily disposition portfolios, and also served as a financial analyst.

He came out of that career with more than thirty five letters of professionalism, merit and performance citations, along with the Downtown Denver Multifamily Project Manager of the Year Award and a Colorado Multifamily Loan High Sales Volume Award. He is listed in Who's Who and is a HUD Homes authorized broker.

In 2010 he founded Black Hawk Real Estate. In his own words, from his intake:

I chose the Real Estate Profession for Goal and Performance Driven Results and Achievements. The desire to help my clients succeed their objectives through completion of their real estate wants and needs. Founded Black Hawk Real Estate to better serve local homes, business and community.
Jerry Baker

What the record shows is narrower and more useful than a mission statement. Jerry sells high country ground. Vacant land, mountain lots, cabins, mining claims, and the occasional manufactured home. He has done it out of one office in Black Hawk for sixteen years, and he answers his own phone.

Areas of expertise

What Jerry actually gets hired for

The largest share of the transaction record. Vacant land in Black Hawk and Central City, gulch acreage, patented mining claims, and parcels whose only access is an unmaintained jeep road. Reviewers name this specialty more often than anything else.
Cabins and homes where the well, the septic, the road and the power are all questions rather than assumptions. One client bought an off grid mining claim outright. Another needed the septic use and well permit implications explained before pricing a sale.
Two reviewers bought their first property with Jerry and both singled out the same thing, that he explained the process slowly and answered questions without making them feel rushed. First purchases in the mountains carry more unknowns than a suburban closing does.
Several clients have bought or sold without being in Colorado. One family purchased from another state after finding him through a search, and a repeat client completed a fully remote sale. It works because the diligence gets done and reported, not because it is easy.
Twenty years inside HUD disposition and a HUD Homes authorized broker designation. If a property is coming through a federal or institutional channel, Jerry has seen that paperwork from the other side of the table.

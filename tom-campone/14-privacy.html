Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tom Campone Real Estate ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit this website (the "Site") or interact with our services. Tom Campone is a licensed real estate broker affiliated with Keller Williams ONE Chicago, serving the South Loop, Pilsen, Bridgeport, and the greater Chicago area. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways when you use the Site:

  • Information you provide directly when you submit a contact form, request a home valuation, save a property search, schedule a showing, or reach us by email or phone. This may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any details you include in your message.
  • Property preferences when you use our listing search tools, save favorite listings, or set up saved searches, including the neighborhoods, price ranges, and property types you are interested in.
  • Transaction information when you become a client, including details reasonably necessary to help complete a real estate transaction, such as property details and financing or pre-qualification status.
  • Information collected automatically, such as pages visited, search queries, time on the Site, referring web address, device and browser type, and IP address.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise device geolocation unless you grant your browser permission to share it.

How We Use It

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • Send property alerts, saved-search updates, and market information you have asked to receive.
  • Help facilitate real estate transactions on your behalf.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site and our services.
  • Comply with our legal, licensing, and brokerage obligations in the State of Illinois.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, security incidents, and other misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes without your consent.

Cookies & Analytics

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies to function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may limit some features such as saved searches and form pre-fill. We generally use the following categories:

  • Strictly necessary cookies required for the Site to operate, including session, security, and form submission.
  • Analytics cookies, such as Google Analytics or a similar service, used to understand aggregate, anonymized usage so we can improve the Site.
  • Functional cookies that remember your saved searches, favorite listings, and preferences.
  • Marketing cookies, which may include retargeting tools used to show relevant ads on other sites. You can opt out through your ad-preference settings or recognized industry opt-out tools.

IDX/MLS Data

Property listings shown on the Site are provided through the local Multiple Listing Service (MLS) by way of Internet Data Exchange (IDX). This listing data is publicly sourced and displayed under MLS attribution and display rules. When you use the search tools, your saved searches and favorites may be stored by our IDX provider to deliver the features you request. Listing data is updated periodically and may not always reflect the current status of a property. All property details should be independently verified.

Lead Forms & Communications

When you submit a form or otherwise contact us, you are asking us to follow up with you. We may contact you by email, phone, or text message in response to your request and to provide the services or information you sought. If you opt in to ongoing updates, every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP to any text message to opt out of future texts. Standard message and data rates may apply.

Third-Party Services

We work with service providers who help us operate the Site and run our business, and we share information with them only as needed for those purposes. These may include our brokerage, Keller Williams ONE Chicago, for licensing compliance and transaction supervision; our IDX listing provider; email, customer relationship management, and form tools; website hosting; and analytics providers. We may also share information with other transaction participants, such as lenders, title companies, inspectors, or attorneys, when you have engaged us in a transaction and sharing is necessary to complete it. We may disclose information when required to comply with the law or to protect rights, safety, or property.

Your Choices

You have choices about your information:

  • Access and update. You may ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Deletion. You may ask us to delete your personal information, subject to legal and transaction-related retention requirements.
  • Opt out of marketing. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time and reply STOP to opt out of text messages.
  • Cookie controls. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below. Residents of states with applicable privacy laws may have additional rights, which we honor upon a verified request.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us right away.

Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general, adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

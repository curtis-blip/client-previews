## 1. Client identity

**Jonathan Sweat** — nationally respected mortgage loan officer, sales coach, and industry leader. 21 years as a loan officer, 30+ years in financial services. Currently Branch Manager and Mortgage Loan Originator at Integrity Home Mortgage Corporation.

Leads **The Legacy Team** with Dixie Lowe (MLO) and Ellyson Spickard (LOA). Also runs **Compass Sales Coaching** (compasssalescoaching.com), where he coaches Realtors and loan officers nationally.

Markets: Roanoke Valley (Virginia) + Lakeland and Tampa (Florida). Dual-state licensed.

## 2. Positioning

Personal-brand mortgage advisor. Not a transactional rate-shopper. Not a corporate lender face. The pitch is **trust, certainty, and legacy** — backed by 21 years and a no-pre-qualification policy that Realtors recognize.

The coaching arm (Compass Sales Coaching) is positioned as the "second act" of the brand — not a hard cross-sell on the mortgage site, but a credibility amplifier that proves Jonathan teaches what he practices.

## 3. The differentiator (most important brand asset)

> **"We only do pre-approvals. Not pre-qualifications."**

When a Realtor sees Jonathan's name on a pre-approval letter, they know the deal will close. Most lenders run a basic credit check and call it a pre-qual. The Legacy Team underwrites the full file before the offer goes out.

This deserves headline real estate. Should be a major section on home, the title of its own page, and reinforced in the Apply / Get Started page.

## 4. Audience

**Primary (mortgage):**
- Roanoke Valley homebuyers — first-time, move-up, retirees
- Lakeland / Tampa homebuyers — relocators, second-home buyers, retirees
- Refinance candidates with equity in current home
- Realtors and listing agents who refer (B2B trust signal)

**Secondary (coaching cross-sell):**
- Loan officers nationally who want to grow production
- Realtors looking for sales / business coaching

## 5. Brand context

**Through-line: Legacy + Compass / Navigation.** Confirmed across both businesses:
- The Legacy Team (mortgage)
- Compass Sales Coaching (coaching)
- Latitude Podcast
- PinMarkers Blog
- "Charting Your Future · Changing Your Legacy"
- "Learn to Live Freely and Lightly"
- "Financial legacy" framing in his About copy

**Voice:** Trusted advisor. Plain-spoken. Slight editorial weight via serif headlines. Confident but warm. Faith mentioned only on the coaching brand, not on the mortgage site.

## 6. Visual direction

**Palette:** deep navy + warm gold + burgundy accent + cream
- Navy because his logo is white-on-dark by design (logo dictates dark backdrops)
- Gold for accent — premium, legacy, restraint
- Burgundy as secondary — pulled from his headshot blazer, distinguishes from generic mortgage navy
- Cream for alt sections — warmth, editorial register

**Type:** Fraunces (display, editorial serif with italic accent variants) + Inter (body)

**Register:** "Bank-trust legacy advisor", not "agent personal brand". Closer to a boutique wealth advisor or executive coach site than a generic mortgage template.

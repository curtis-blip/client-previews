On This Coast

What Moves a Coastside Home's Value

Four things an online estimate misses and a walk-through catches.

  • The View

    An ocean or open-space view moves value more than any finish inside the house.

  • The Lot and Its Permits

    Lot size, slope, setbacks and what the coastal permit history allows next.

  • Condition and Presentation

    Paint, floors and staging, often paid through Compass Concierge.

  • Insurability

    A newer roof, ember-resistant vents and cleared defensible space make the house easier to insure.

Questions

Home Value Questions

How accurate is an online home value estimate?

It is an automated range, so it is closest for typical homes and furthest for unusual ones: an ocean view, a large lot, a remodel or a home with few comparable sales. On the Coastside many homes are unusual.

How does Stephanie price a home?

Comparable sales first, then the homes yours would compete with and what buyers are doing this month. Her post on pricing explains the thinking.

Does an estimate commit me to anything?

No. The estimate is yours to keep, and a walk-through is a conversation, not a listing agreement.

Where can I see what homes are selling for?

In Compass's market reports, or ask Stephanie for the recent sales on your street.

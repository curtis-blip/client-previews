Meet Tonya

I've Been Obsessed With Real Estate Since I Was a Kid.

I got my real estate license at 18 in Florida, then moved to Virginia after college and started raising kids. Twenty-four years later, I came back to my first love. Now I help families buy and sell homes across Martinsville, Henry County, and Southern Virginia, especially out-of-state buyers who are making the same transition I did.

I love a good house hunt. I love listing a house so perfectly it goes under contract in a day or two. And I love what Martinsville is, which is why I want to show it to you.

Where I Work

Three Communities Worth Knowing in Southern Virginia.

Martinsville is the heart of it. Danville has the riverwalk and a comeback story. Smith Mountain Lake is where everyday life feels like vacation.

Community 01

Uptown Martinsville

Uptown Martinsville is the heart of the city. Historic architecture, walkable streets, festivals, parades, live music. Full of character, small-town charm, and a close-knit community feel. It's where business owners still know your name.

Explore Martinsville →
brand assetDowntown Danville · Dan River + brick warehouses
Community 02

Downtown Danville

Historic charm with serious comeback energy. Restored brick buildings, boutique shops, locally owned restaurants. The River District along the Dan River has scenic walking trails, riverfront views, and year-round events. Caesars Virginia brought new jobs and visitors to the area.

Explore Danville →
Community 03

Smith Mountain Lake

20,000 acres of clear water with the Blue Ridge as backdrop. Boating, fishing, waterfront dining, shoreline homes. A year-round destination for full-time residents and vacationers. SML residents know life is better at the lake.

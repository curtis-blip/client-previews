Who We Help

When You Need To Sell Your House Fast In Colorado Springs

Most sellers who call us have a house that turned into a problem. These are the ones we hear about most.

A Vacant House

Every month it sits empty, you keep paying the mortgage, taxes and upkeep while it loses value.

A House That Will Not Finance

A buyer’s lender turns it down for its condition, the sale falls through and months go by. We do not need a loan.

A Fixer-Upper

Roof, furnace, fire or water damage, mold, code violations. Leave the repairs to us.

Behind On Payments

Missed payments, back taxes, liens or a past bankruptcy can cloud the title. If foreclosure has started, call sooner rather than later.

Financial Hardship

Medical bills, a lost job or a slow year in your business. Every family is different, so every solution is too.

Divorce Or A Death In The Family

A house that has to sell for financial or legal reasons. If it is an estate, ask your attorney where probate stands, then call.

PCS Orders

Orders out of Fort Carson, Peterson, Schriever or the Academy rarely wait for a listing.

A Move On A Short Clock

A new job, a next house under contract, a date that cannot slide. If a listing’s timeline does not fit yours, cash may.

Questions

Selling Your House Fast In Colorado Springs, Answered

How does the process work?

Tell us about the house with the form on this page, which is our intake form, or by call or text. We reach out right away and set up a time to come see the house. Once we have seen it, done our research and learned what matters most to you, we make our best cash offer and walk you through every detail. You decide what happens next.

Who is actually buying my house?

Colorado Guys Buy Homes, the real estate investment business run by Jose Medina. Jose is a licensed Colorado real estate broker and the founder of the Vantegic Home Team at HomeSmart.

Do you actually close with cash?

Yes. The title company pays you at closing, by wire to your account or by bank check.

When can you close?

In as little as 5 to 7 days, or on your timeline. Most sellers want time to line up where they will live next, so most of our purchases close 30 to 45 days after the agreement is signed. You pick the date.

Do you really buy as-is?

Yes. Do not clean and do not repair. Take what you want and leave what you do not. We look at what a house can become, not the condition it is in today.

What if my home needs a lot of work?

That is why we exist. Many houses cannot sell the usual way because a buyer's lender will not finance them in their condition, and not every owner has the money, the know-how or the time for a renovation. We buy them for cash, fix what needs fixing and put them back on the market.

Who do you typically work with?

Owners in pre-foreclosure, going through a divorce, relocating, handling probate or an inheritance, or dealing with title or lien problems, fire damage, mold or code violations. Owners of vacant, abandoned or unwanted houses. Whatever your reason, nothing surprises us. We are here to listen, help and close when you want to.

How do you decide what to offer?

We research the property, run the numbers and find out what matters most to you. We make our money by renovating and reselling, and nobody accepts a low-ball offer, so it is in our interest to make the highest cash offer we can. On the offer call we show you how we got to our number.

Will a cash offer be lower than listing?

Usually, yes. A cash buyer pays for speed and certainty and takes on the repairs and the risk of resale. If your house is in good shape and you have time, a listing will often net you more, and we will tell you so. Get your home value so you have a number to compare.

Do I pay a commission or fees?

No. There is no agent commission and no fee on a direct sale to Colorado Guys Buy Homes. Your contract and closing statement will show every number before you sign.

Is Jose my agent in a cash sale?

No. In a direct cash sale the buyer is on the other side of the deal from you and does not represent you. You are welcome to have your own attorney or real estate agent review any contract before you sign.

Why work with Colorado Guys Buy Homes?

Because we only do deals where both sides win. We can structure a purchase more than one way, including our Turn-Key Partner Program, so you get a couple of options and choose the one that works best for you.

What if I am not ready to decide?

Then do not decide. Requesting an offer does not obligate you in any way, and nothing commits you until you sign a contract. If the timing is not right, we check in over the following weeks and months with no pressure. If a listing suits you better, the Seller's Guide walks through how the Vantegic Home Team runs one.

Which areas do you buy in?

Colorado Springs and the surrounding communities, including Briargate, Broadmoor, Northgate, the Powers Corridor, Monument, Fountain, Peyton and Black Forest. If your house is nearby but not on that list, ask.

The Listing Plan

How We Sell Your Home in North Georgia

Four parts, one morning of prep, one goal: the highest net in the fewest days. Pick a tab.

Priced to the Comps, Not the Portal

List price is a strategy, not a guess. We price against homes we have actually sold nearby, weigh the current absorption rate for your zip, and set a number that draws showings in the first weekend. Overpricing costs you more than a slow start; it costs you the buyers who were watching on day one.

Marketing Day Sets the Stage

Before a single photo, we run Marketing Day: photography, staging notes, and the launch plan handled in one morning at your house. Clean, bright, and camera-ready is what turns a scroll into a showing. See exactly how it works on Marketing Day.

Where Your Listing Shows Up

Your home goes out in the Zillow Showcase format, syndicates to FMLS and Georgia MLS, and runs paid social aimed at buyers already shopping your price band. Bigger photos, an interactive floor plan, and top search placement put the home in front of the people who can act on it.

Reading Offers Past the Top Line

The highest number is not always the best offer. We read the financing, the contingencies, and the close date, then negotiate the net you keep. Not every sale fits the open market either, so ask about a cash offer or a probate home sale when the situation calls for it.

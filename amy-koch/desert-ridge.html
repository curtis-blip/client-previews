About the Community

North Phoenix Living, Master-Planned

Desert Ridge is a 5,700-acre master-planned community in north Phoenix, just off the Loop 101 and Tatum Boulevard. Home to more than 50,000 residents, it blends resort living, regional shopping and dining, championship golf, and major employment campuses in one of the Valley's most sought-after locations. National homebuilders including Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers, and Pulte have shaped its neighborhoods, from gated single-family enclaves to lock-and-leave condominiums. Desert Ridge sits within the highly rated Paradise Valley Unified School District.

Lifestyle & Amenities

Resort Living at Your Doorstep

Desert Ridge Marketplace

An open-air, super-regional shopping and dining center with national anchors, more than 25 restaurants and eateries, an AMC theater, Dave and Buster's, and seasonal splash pads. It is the social heart of the community, just minutes from every neighborhood.

JW Marriott Resort and Wildfire Golf

A AAA Four Diamond resort with more than 950 rooms across 316 acres, home to the Wildfire Golf Club and its two 18-hole championship courses designed by Arnold Palmer and Nick Faldo. Dining, spa, and fitness are open to residents and guests alike.

Location

Direct access to the Loop 101, regional employment campuses, and minutes to Scottsdale and Paradise Valley. Desert Ridge puts work, recreation, and the wider Valley all within easy reach, which is a large part of why it stays in such high demand.

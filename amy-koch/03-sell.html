Your Selling Journey

How to Sell a Home in Phoenix the Right Way

Selling a home in the Phoenix market requires more than a sign in the yard and a listing on the MLS. How your property is priced, presented, and marketed determines how many offers you receive, how strong those offers are, and whether the deal actually closes. Amy Koch's selling process covers every detail from the initial pricing strategy through the final walk-through.

Pricing Strategy

The Right Price Changes Everything

Pricing Impact on Days on Market

Priced Right 14-21 days
5% Over Market 45-60 days
10%+ Over Market 90+ days

Based on Phoenix metro MLS averages

Overpricing drives buyers away and leads to price reductions that signal desperation. Underpricing leaves money on the table. Amy uses a data-driven approach to find the pricing sweet spot that attracts the most qualified buyers and generates competitive offers.

Her pricing analysis includes recent comparable sales within a half-mile radius, current active and pending inventory, buyer demand trends in your specific neighborhood, and the impact of your home's condition and upgrades relative to the competition.

Name and age

The neighborhood and the park share a name

Arlington Park is a neighborhood inside the City of Sarasota, and the park it is named for is a City park. The City's parks map lists the park and its Community Center and Aquatic Complex.

Most of the housing was built from the 1950s, with a handful of 1920s bungalows and some Sarasota School homes on Hawthorne Street. In a house that age, the roof and the wiring decide the repair budget.

People sometimes call the area East of the Trail and mix it up with Alta Vista, so confirm which neighborhood an address is in before you rely on the name.

Park and houses

A City park with a pool, and houses from the 1950s

  • A park with a pool

    Arlington Park and Aquatic Complex is on the City's parks map. The Sarasota Observer described a 50-meter pool, a 25-yard pool and a wading pool there in 2016. City parks map

  • Established houses

    Most of the housing dates from the 1950s, according to Sarasota Magazine in 2016, so the age of the roof matters on almost every house you tour.

  • A neighborhood that made its own map

    The Arlington Park Neighborhood Association made a parcel-by-parcel map in 2017, with the City's help, showing every sidewalk and bike route.

Questions

Questions about the neighborhood and the park

Is Arlington Park its own town?

No. It is a neighborhood inside the City of Sarasota, and the county's neighborhood directory lists an Arlington Park Neighborhood association.

Is Arlington Park the same as the park?

No. The neighborhood and the City park share a name.

What should I check in a house built in the 1950s?

The roof first, then the wiring and the flood zone. Insurers ask about the roof's age as much as its condition, and 4-Point and Roof Inspections explains what the inspector looks at. Sarasota Flood Zones shows how to look up the zone.

Who made the neighborhood map?

The Arlington Park Neighborhood Association, in 2017, with help from the City's GIS coordinator and a City communications grant. It shows every parcel, sidewalk and bike route.

Which school does an Arlington Park address go to?

The Sarasota County School District assigns schools by address.

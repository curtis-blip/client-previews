About Us

About Ryan and Katie Sherman, Sarasota Realtors

A husband and wife who moved from St. Louis in 2022 and now help buyers and sellers from Anna Maria Island to Englewood.

Meet Ryan and Katie

Katie and Ryan, in Their Own Words

You get both of them: Katie on the details, Ryan on the market and the negotiating. They wrote these bios themselves.

Katie Sherman

REALTOR · 321-429-9031

Katie loves helping clients turn the idea of coastal living into real life. A strong advocate with an eye for the details, she keeps her clients informed, protected, and confident while making the process feel as smooth and enjoyable as possible. She believes real estate should be exciting, not overwhelming, and brings plenty of personality along with the strategy. For Katie, it’s about more than finding the right home. It’s helping clients create their own version of the coastal dream.

Ryan Sherman

REALTOR · 321-429-9022

Ryan brings a relaxed, easygoing energy to real estate and loves helping clients discover what makes life on Florida’s coast so special. Known for staying calm, keeping things simple, and making the process genuinely fun, he pairs his laid-back style with strong market knowledge and smart negotiation. Whether it’s finding the perfect home near the water or making a move toward a new lifestyle, Ryan is all about helping clients enjoy the journey and live the coastal dream.

Their move

From St. Louis to Sarasota in 2022

When Ryan and Katie left St. Louis they could have moved anywhere. The beaches and the tropical feel made Sarasota the one.

Ryan was licensed in 2022. Having made the move themselves, they know what a newcomer wants to know about a town, and what it takes to buy a home before you live here.

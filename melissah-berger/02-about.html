How I Work

Most agents on the Front Range pick a side of Ute Pass. I did not, because my clients do not. A family selling a five-acre parcel outside Divide to get into Wolf Ranch for the schools is making one move, not two transactions, and it goes considerably better when the same person understands the septic inspection on one end and the metro district mill levy on the other.

The second thing that makes this practice unusual in Teller County is that I run a real property management operation alongside the brokerage. That matters on the day selling stops being the obvious answer. If holding the house and renting it out is the better decision, I can price that scenario as carefully as I price the sale, and then actually run it for you.

What I Know That The MLS Does Not

Which streets hold their value through a hard winter. Which lots drain badly in spring. Which driveways are a genuine problem in January and which just look steep in the photos. Which neighborhoods down the pass are worth the drive for the schools alone. That is the part you get from living somewhere for years, not from pulling comparables.

Outside of work, most of my volunteering goes to youth organizations now, after years in school and church activities while my own family was coming up through the district. Woodland Park has been good to us, and that is not a line in a bio, it is the reason I am still here.

I am a licensed Colorado broker, and my licence and any disciplinary history are public record through the Colorado Division of Real Estate. There is a direct lookup link in the footer of every page here, and I would encourage you to use it on any agent you are considering.

If you are weighing a move in either direction, the Buyer's Guide and the Seller's Guide lay out how each side works, the communities page covers all nine places I work, and the search shows what is on the market right now. Owners weighing a rental should start with the management side, and past clients describe how all of this actually goes. I also write up the questions that keep coming back over on the blog. Or just get in touch.

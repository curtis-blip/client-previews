Questions

What people ask about Robert

What does a broker of record actually do?

A broker of record is the licensee legally responsible for the transactions under their license. Most agents work under someone else's. Robert holds his own, so the person advising you is the person accountable for the file.

Why does the mortgage credential matter?

Deals stall on financing more often than on price. Holding the NMLS originator credential means Robert can read a loan file the way the underwriter reads it, and tell you what has to happen next. More on home financing.

Do you only work with distressed property?

No, though it is the specialty. REO, HUD and VA foreclosures and short sales have been core work since the 1990s; ordinary purchases and sales are the larger share of any year. See investment property.

Do you work with first-time buyers?

Often. The teaching background is most useful here: financing first, so you know your real budget before you fall for a house. Start with the Buyer's Guide.

Can you help me relocate to Texas?

Yes. Robert lists and sells in San Antonio and Boerne and can compare neighborhoods against your budget, commute and schools. Because he is also licensed in California and Florida, he can advise on the sale at the other end.

What does the insurance license have to do with real estate?

Buying a house is usually the moment a household's financial picture changes. Robert can talk through life insurance and protection in the same conversation. See insurance.

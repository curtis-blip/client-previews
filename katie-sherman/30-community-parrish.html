Parrish at a glance

Pasture, Oaks and New Neighborhoods North of the Manatee River

Parrish is unincorporated Manatee County, so the county runs its parks and sets its rules. The Florida Railroad Museum's trains still roll out of Parrish on weekends, and since 2017 the Fort Hamer Bridge has carried Fort Hamer Road across the Manatee River.

The museum's track was the first rail line through Manatee County. The Seaboard Air Line Railroad built it in the early 1900s; after the Atlantic Coast Line and the Seaboard merged in 1967, the line was downgraded and finally abandoned in 1986. Today museum members run it as volunteers, six miles between Parrish and Willow.

02

The Florida Railroad Museum

The Florida Railroad Museum, at 12210 83rd Street East, runs diesel trains each weekend over six miles of track between Parrish and Willow, in open-air, open-window and air-conditioned cars. Museum members run the trains as volunteers, giving thousands of hours a year, and the ticket office and gift shop sit in a refurbished rail car. Around the holidays, the museum runs a North Pole Express trip to visit Santa.

03

Fort Hamer Park and the Fort Hamer Bridge

Fort Hamer Park, at 1605 Fort Hamer Road, is a Manatee County park with a boat ramp, a canoe and kayak launch, a dock and two pavilions beside the rowing facility, plus a fishing pier at the kayak launch. The Fort Hamer Bridge opened to traffic on October 18, 2017, linking Fort Hamer Road in Parrish with Upper Manatee River Road across the Manatee River, with 5-foot sidewalks and 4-foot bike lanes.

04

Rye Preserve on the Manatee River

Rye Preserve, at 905 Rye Wilderness Trail, is open every day from sunrise to sunset. Its trails cross four ecosystems: sand pine scrub, xeric oak scrub, oak hammocks and the river community, where you may see a gopher tortoise. A canoe and kayak launch on the Red Trail puts you on the Manatee River, tent camping runs on Friday and Saturday nights from November through April, and the Rye Family Cemetery is the last reminder of the old Rye River community.

05

Parrish Community Park and the school district

On Fort Hamer Road, Parrish Community Park has an amphitheater for the county’s music series and other events, a splash pad that opens seasonally, a playground and a boardwalk. The Manatee County School District runs the public schools. Confirm the school for any address with the district.

Questions

Parrish FAQ

Is Parrish a city?

No. Parrish is unincorporated Manatee County, so the county handles zoning, permits and parks, and there is no Census place named Parrish. Check any parcel with the Manatee County Property Appraiser.

Which school district serves Parrish?

The Manatee County School District. Attendance zones change, so confirm the school for any address with the district.

Is there a bridge across the Manatee River from Parrish?

Yes. The Fort Hamer Bridge opened to traffic on October 18, 2017. It links Fort Hamer Road in Parrish with Upper Manatee River Road on the far side of the river, with sidewalks and bike lanes.

When do the trains run at the Florida Railroad Museum?

On weekends. The museum's diesel trains run six miles between Parrish and Willow; check frrm.org for the current schedule and the holiday trips.

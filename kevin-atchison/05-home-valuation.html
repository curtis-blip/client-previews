An instant estimate in about a minute, then a real analysis from someone who has sold on your street. Start with the first one.

01 Start here

Instant estimate

Enter your address and get a range

It takes about a minute and there is no call attached to it. What you get back is a range drawn from public records and recent sales nearby.

02 What that number is

A good starting point, and not a price

An automated estimate reads public records and recent sales. That gets you close in a subdivision where fifty houses are near enough identical. Out here it is a starting point, because the things that move a Western Slope price are exactly the things no algorithm can see.

01

It cannot see your water

A house on town tap and a house on a shared well with no written agreement look identical in the record and sell for different money.

02

It cannot see condition

A roof replaced last year and a roof with two years left are the same roof to a computer.

03

It cannot see the comparable it used

In a thin market one distressed sale three streets over drags the whole estimate down, and the algorithm will not tell you it did that.

04

It cannot see your acreage properly

Irrigated pasture, a usable outbuilding and bare hillside are all just land in the record.

05

It cannot see a metro district

In Battlement Mesa the monthly cost changes what a buyer can pay, and the estimate does not account for it.

06

It cannot see what buyers said

I know which houses sat, which ones had three offers, and what the difference was. That is not in any public record.

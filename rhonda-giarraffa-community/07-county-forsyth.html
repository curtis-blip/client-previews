ABOUT FORSYTH COUNTY

What it is like to live here

On Lake Lanier, buyers weigh the water before the floor plan: how deep it is at the slip, how the boat is kept, what the view looks like in winter and how the lot falls to the shore.

Forsyth County holds roughly a third of Lake Lanier, along its western shore, with Cumming as the county seat and GA-400 as the road south. Homes for sale here range from neighborhoods with a community dock to lakefront homes with a private dock, and some listings sit inland, so we check how each one reaches the water.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE BUYING

Two ways to own on Lake Lanier

No two lake properties are the same. The first question is how you reach the water.

In a lake community

A neighborhood with a shared dock or marina. Your boat lives in a slip, and the community's rules decide how slips are owned, rented or assigned.

  • We confirm in writing which slip, if any, comes with the home.
  • We read the dock rules, the dues and any wait list with you.
  • We check what the HOA dues include.

A lakefront home of its own

A home on its own lot, often with a private dock on Corps shoreline. The dock permit ends at the sale, so the new owner applies to the Corps in their own name.

  • We check the permit, the dock's condition and the shoreline rules.
  • We look at depth at the dock against the lake's lows.
  • We walk the lot: the slope to the water matters every day.

AROUND FORSYTH COUNTY

THE LAKE

The Army Corps of Engineers manages Lake Lanier, its shoreline and its dock permits.

The Corps posts the lake level every day.

QUESTIONS

Questions buyers ask about Forsyth County

Is every Forsyth County listing on the lake?

No. The county search shows homes across Forsyth County. The four searches above narrow it to homes with a slip, a lake view, waterfront or a lot.

Why does a Forsyth County home have a Gainesville address?

Mailing cities cross county lines near the lake. Schools and property taxes follow the county, so we confirm it for every address.

Does a dock permit transfer when I buy a lake home?

Not on Lake Lanier. A private dock's Corps permit ends when the home sells, and the new owner applies in their own name.

How deep is the water at a Forsyth County dock?

It depends on the cove and the lake level, which the Corps manages and posts daily. We check depth at the dock against the lake's recent lows before you commit.

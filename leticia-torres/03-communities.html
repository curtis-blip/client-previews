The Region

What the Region Shares

The six towns have their own character, and a few things in common.

The Coast

  • Carlsbad and Oceanside sit on the Pacific, with public beaches along Oceanside's shoreline.

  • The COASTER commuter train stops in Oceanside and Carlsbad on its way to downtown San Diego.

The Inland Valleys

  • San Marcos, Vista, Escondido and Fallbrook sit inland, in the hills and valleys east of the coast.

  • Each town has its own city hall, schools and neighborhoods, and its own feel.

The 78 Corridor

  • The SPRINTER light rail runs along Highway 78, connecting Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos and Escondido.

  • Interstate 5 runs through Oceanside, and Interstate 15 runs north to Temecula.

Transit details are from North County Transit District. Check the current schedules with them before you plan a commute.

Typography Hard Rules
  • Load Raleway (200 to 600) and Montserrat (400 to 700) from Google Fonts on every page; never a system face as primary.
  • Headings are uppercase at light weights (200 to 400). Never bold a heading; never mixed case.
  • Headings keep pairs together: join real-estate terms, place names and brand names with non-breaking spaces (Forsyth&nbsp;County, Cumming,&nbsp;GA) so no heading ends on a lone last word. Apply it to every heading you build, plus text-wrap: balance.
  • "Chattahoochee River Club" is written Chattahoochee&nbsp;<wbr>River&nbsp;Club: it may break only before "River".
  • No em or en dashes in any copy. No italic accent spans inside H1s.

Color Hard Rules
  • Small gold text on a light ground is #726540, never #bcaf8a (fails contrast). Links are #726540 and turn #0d0d0d on hover.

Button Hard Rules
  • No solid-fill buttons at rest, no blue, no rounded corners. One style for primary and secondary.
  • At most two buttons in a hero. Stack them vertically below 760px.
  • Text links (.tlink) are Montserrat 600, 12.5px, tracking .24em, uppercase, underlined on hover only.
  • Keep the focus ring: 2px Gold Ink outline, 3px offset, on every focusable element.

Deliberate mobile-only departures

  • Header: the desktop nav and the phone and email strip hide below 1280px; a burger menu replaces them, and a fixed two-button call bar (.callbar) pins to the bottom of the phone screen.
  • Directory tab row scrolls sideways below 1280px; hub tiles stack one per row and their one-line descriptions hide below 760px.
  • Community chapters: the fixed side plate and its sticky photo hide at 1024px; each chapter shows its own 4:3 photo instead.
  • Parallax photo bands (background-attachment: fixed) become normal scroll on phones.
  • Footer columns stack to one column at 760px. The legal table of contents hides below 900px.

Anti-Patterns Summary

Never Do These
  • Blue anywhere, or gold #bcaf8a as small text on white
  • Solid-fill buttons at rest, rounded buttons, cards or images
  • Em or en dashes in copy; emojis as icons (inline SVG only)
  • A third font family, bold or mixed-case headings
  • A heading ending in a lone last word; breaking "Chattahoochee River Club" anywhere but before "River"
  • A different footer on any page
  • Visible photo credits or captions; outside links in the home page body
  • Rebuilding a locked page (home, About, Cumming) instead of matching it
  • Pasting a raw Showcase shortcode outside its labelled, styled zone

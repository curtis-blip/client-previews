What decides a Telluride home value

The two towns and the mesas sell to different buyers for different reasons, so a sale in one says little about a property in another.

In the Town of Telluride

Where it sits in the historic grid, the sun it gets, the walk to the lifts and Colorado Avenue, parking, and what the Historic and Architectural Review Commission will let you change.

In Mountain Village

How close it is to the lifts and the gondola, whether it is truly ski-in/ski-out, the HOA and what it allows, and the rental history if it has one.

On the mesas

Acreage and the view, water and septic, the road and who plows it, the covenants and the drive to town in winter. Few sales match, so each property gets a closer look.

Questions

What owners ask

How accurate is the instant estimate?

It is a starting point. An automated estimate works from property records and past sales, and it cannot see the view, the condition or the walk to the lift. In a market this small, with few recent sales like yours, it has less to work from than it would in a city. For a number to list at, ask Garrett for a walkthrough and a full market analysis.

What goes into Garrett's market analysis?

Garrett walks the property, then compares it with the closest recent sales and with what is for sale now, and adjusts for the differences that move prices here: location, views, access, condition, the HOA and any rental history. You get a recommended price and the sales it rests on.

Is the estimate the same as the assessor's value?

No. The San Miguel County Assessor values property for taxes on a two-year cycle, from market data gathered before the cycle begins, so the assessed value is not a current market price.

How is a market analysis different from an appraisal?

Garrett's analysis is a pricing recommendation for a sale. An appraisal is an independent appraiser's opinion of value, usually ordered by a lender for a purchase or a refinance.

I live out of state. Can Garrett value the property without me there?

Yes. Garrett or Jessica can meet your property manager or caretaker at the property, then go over what they saw with you by phone.

Does a rental history help?

It matters to buyers who plan to rent, so keep the booking and management statements together. When a Mountain Village condo with no parking was hard to sell, Garrett kept its rental history in front of buyers until it sold. Read how he markets a listing.

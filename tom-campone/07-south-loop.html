The lifestyle

A day in the South Loop

Mornings start on the Lakefront Trail, Lake Michigan on one side and the skyline on the other. A short walk south brings you to the Museum Campus, home to the Field Museum, the Shedd Aquarium, and the Adler Planetarium, with Soldier Field and Northerly Island just beyond.

Grant Park, Chicago's front yard, sits at your doorstep, hosting Taste of Chicago, the Chicago Blues Festival, and Lollapalooza each year. The rest of the time it is open green space, Buckingham Fountain, and room to breathe in the middle of the city.

Best of all, you are a short walk or a single CTA stop from the heart of the Loop, so work, dining, and the theater district are never far.

A neighborhood with a story

From Millionaires Row to modern lofts

Few Chicago neighborhoods carry as much history as the South Loop. Along the Prairie Avenue District, the preserved mansions of the old Millionaires Row still line the blocks where the city's 19th-century industrialists built their homes.

Just west, the Motor Row District recalls the era when this was the country's largest auto showroom district, its terra-cotta facades now home to music venues and restaurants. Printers Row, once the heart of the Midwest publishing trade, has traded its presses for timber-loft condos, bookstores, and cafes.

Today a mix of longtime residents, downtown professionals, and students gives the South Loop a relaxed, walkable energy: historic bones and modern life, a few minutes from both the lake and the Loop.

The homes

What you'll find in South Loop condos for sale

The South Loop is condo country. Glass high-rises along the lake offer floor-to-ceiling views, while mid-rise buildings and converted timber lofts in Printers Row bring character and space at a different price point.

New construction keeps adding modern towers with full amenity packages, and you will still find classic loft conversions for buyers who want something with history. From a first home steps from the lake to a low-maintenance city base or a rentable investment property, the range here fits a real Chicago lifestyle.

South Loop questions, answered

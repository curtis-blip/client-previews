Moving to Sarasota

Is Sarasota a Good Place to Live?

Ryan and Katie asked the same question before they moved here from St. Louis in 2022. Here are the towns, the costs and the weather, with the sources.

Their move

Two Transplants From St. Louis, Now on the Suncoast

When Ryan and Katie left St. Louis in 2022, it was the beaches and the tropical feel that brought them to Sarasota.

Ryan was licensed that same year and Katie soon after, and many of their clients now make a move like theirs: Katie's Zillow reviews include buyers from up north, a buyer from Tennessee who settled in Parrish and one who bought sight unseen.

So is Sarasota a good place to live? For a lot of people, yes, and the honest answer comes with trade-offs: insurance is a real line in the budget, summer is the rainy season, and hurricane season runs six months of the year.

Twelve towns

How the Twelve Towns Differ

From the islands at Anna Maria to the beaches at Englewood and the master-planned communities, each town lives differently. Start with how you want to spend a weekday, then pick the town.

  • Sarasota

    The bayfront city, with its downtown on Sarasota Bay and the airport about four miles north.

  • Siesta Key

    An island known for Siesta Beach's nearly pure quartz sand, named the top U.S. beach by Tripadvisor in 2025 and by Dr. Beach in 2017.

  • Anna Maria Island

    Three small cities on one island: Anna Maria, Holmes Beach and Bradenton Beach.

  • Bradenton

    On the Manatee River, where the Pittsburgh Pirates have made LECOM Park their spring home since 1969.

  • Venice

    A Gulf Coast city planned in the 1920s by the city planner John Nolen.

  • Lakewood Ranch

    The top-selling multigenerational master-planned community in the US, per RCLCO's 2025 ranking.

  • Wellen Park

    A master-planned community west of I-75, partly in the City of North Port and partly in unincorporated Sarasota County.

  • Lido Key and St. Armands

    St. Armands Circle, which began as John and Mable Ringling's plan, with Lido Beach to the south and the free Bay Runner trolley to downtown.

  • Longboat Key

    A barrier island between Sarasota Bay and the Gulf, in Manatee County to the north and Sarasota County to the south.

  • Nokomis

    Near Venice, with Nokomis Beach, which Visit Sarasota calls Sarasota's oldest public beach, on Casey Key.

  • Englewood

    At the south end of Sarasota County, with Olde Englewood Village and the four beaches of Manasota Key.

  • Parrish

    In north Manatee County, with weekend train rides at the Florida Railroad Museum and new neighborhoods such as North River Ranch.

No State Income Tax, and How Property Taxes Work

Florida does not impose a personal income tax, so there is no state return to file. Property tax is where owning a home shows up: the county property appraiser values every home as of January 1, and the tax bill arrives in late October or November.

If the home is your permanent residence on January 1, the homestead exemption can take up to $50,000 off its assessed value, and Save Our Homes then limits how fast that value can rise. A second home gets neither.

See the Sources

02

Insurance and Flood Zones

Budget for homeowners insurance before anything else, and get a quote on the actual house: the roof's age, the windows and shutters, the elevation and the flood zone all move the price, and qualifying wind features earn discounts under Florida law.

Flood is a separate policy, since most homeowners insurance does not cover it. Look up any address on FEMA's flood maps. If private insurers turn a home down, Citizens Property Insurance, created by the Legislature, covers owners who cannot find insurance in the private market.

See the Sources

03

HOA Dues and CDD Assessments

If a neighborhood has a mandatory homeowners association, Florida requires a disclosure summary before you sign: you will owe its assessments, they can change, and unpaid assessments can become a lien on the home.

Some newer communities also sit in a community development district, which levies its own taxes or assessments for public facilities and services. Its board sets them each year, they come on top of county taxes, and your contract has to say so in bold type.

See the Sources

04

Hurricane Season and Evacuation Levels

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30. Each county sets evacuation levels; Manatee County letters its levels A to E and reminds residents that an evacuation level is not the same as a flood zone.

Look up the address before you buy, not after. The state's Know Your Zone map takes any Florida address, and its plan-and-prepare pages cover the supply kit and the alerts to sign up for.

See the Sources

05

The Summer Rain

Summer is the rainy season. The National Weather Service office for Tampa Bay dates it from mid to late May into October, with almost daily showers and thunderstorms fed by the sea breeze, and frequent lightning.

Keep an umbrella in the car, and respect the lightning: when you hear thunder, come in off the beach and out of the pool.

See the Sources

06

Getting Around

Visit Sarasota calls I-75 the easiest way into the county, and U.S. 41, the Tamiami Trail, is the other main route. Sarasota Bradenton International Airport sits on the county line about four miles north of downtown Sarasota, with nonstop flights on several airlines.

Downtown, the free Bay Runner trolley links Sarasota with St. Armands Circle and Lido Key.

Questions

Questions Newcomers Ask

When do I need a Florida driver license?

Within 30 days of establishing residency, and your car needs a Florida title and registration within 10 days. The Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles lists what counts as establishing residency.

  • New residents, from the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (2026)
When can I file for homestead?

Once you own the home and live in it as your permanent residence on January 1. File with the county property appraiser by March 1 of the year the exemption is to begin.

  • Homestead exemption, from the Sarasota County Property Appraiser
  • Homestead exemption, from the Manatee County Property Appraiser
Is Sarasota in a flood zone?

Parts of it are. Flood zones are mapped address by address, so look the home up on FEMA's flood maps, and check its evacuation level separately: the two maps answer different questions.

  • Flood maps by address, from FEMA's Map Service Center
  • Evacuation levels, from Manatee County
Do I have to sell my home up north before I buy here?

No. You can buy first if your lender approves the loan with both homes, or make your offer contingent on selling yours, which the seller can accept or decline.

Can I buy without flying down?

Yes. Ryan and Katie tour homes with you on video, and a Florida online notary can notarize your signature over a video call wherever you are. Your lender and the closing agent decide if your closing can be signed that way.

  • Online notarization, Florida Statutes section 117.209

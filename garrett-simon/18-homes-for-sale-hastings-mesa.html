Hastings Mesa, Colorado

Homes for Sale on Hastings Mesa, CO

Ranch parcels and smaller lots between Sawpit and Dallas Divide, and how you reach each one in winter.

Hastings Mesa at a glance

From Sawpit to Dallas Divide

Hastings Mesa lies north of Last Dollar Mountain and east of Leopard Creek Canyon, in San Miguel County. County Road 58P climbs onto it from Sawpit and crosses it to CO-62 near Dallas Divide, and most of its subdivisions are parcels of 35 acres or more.

Every named subdivision on it maps to Telluride School District R-1 and the Telluride Fire Protection District. Dallas Divide, the CO-62 summit at its northeast corner, is where the Ouray County line runs; the Census puts the divide itself in Ouray County and the Ridgway School District R-2. In summer, Last Dollar Road continues over Last Dollar Pass to Deep Creek Mesa; the pass is closed to vehicles from December 1 to May 15.

01

Last Dollar Road from Dallas Divide

Last Dollar Road (County Road 58P) leaves CO-62 at the top of Dallas Divide. These three sit nearest that end, about 28 to 31 road miles from downtown Telluride.

  • Last Dollar TractsNine parcels of 35 to 67 acres off Last Dollar Road, with an American Farmland Trust easement recorded as Last Dollar Ranch, about 400 acres, just to the south. The Last Dollar PUD by the airport is a different place, on Deep Creek Mesa.
  • Hastings EstatesEleven parcels of about 35 to 39 acres. The road in, signed 61V, is not county-maintained, so ask who keeps it open in winter.
  • Hastings Mesa EstateNext door to the west: 43 smaller parcels, with a median of about 8.8 acres.

02

Cradle Park, San Juan Vista and Beaver Pond

On the east side of the mesa, toward Last Dollar Pass. The county does not plow the 4.2-mile branch of County Road 58P toward the pass, which runs past Cradle Park.

  • Cradle ParkTwenty-three parcels of about 35 to 73 acres in the open park below North Pole Peak, bordering National Forest, with American Farmland Trust and Nature Conservancy easements on its edge.
  • San Juan VistaUp the road through Cradle Park: 82 parcels of about 5 to 17 acres, median 6.5, at the highest sample point on the mesa, about 9,750 feet.
  • Beaver PondSeven parcels of about 36 to 88 acres on the National Forest edge, farther up toward the pass, about 2.5 miles west of Hayden Peak.

03

The south and west side, from Sawpit to CO-62

The southwest end sits above Sawpit, about 16.5 to 18 road miles from downtown, and County Road 56V comes in from CO-62 on the west side; the county plows both roads. Colorado's 35-acre rule explains the sizes: parcels of 35 acres or more fall outside the county subdivision definition (C.R.S. 30-28-101(10)).

  • West Hastings MesaTwelve parcels of about 40 to 52 acres on the southwest rim above Sawpit, bordering BLM land.
  • Old Elam RanchTwenty-two parcels of 35 to 108 acres on the south end, bordering BLM land.
  • Alder Canyon RanchesSixteen parcels of 35 to 79 acres along Alder Creek, on County Road 56V. Garrett's essay calls it Alder Creek Ranches; the county records Alder Canyon Ranches.
  • Little Lake RanchesEight parcels of 35 to 52 acres on the western benches, bordering BLM land.
  • Telluride PinesThirty-three parcels, nearly all about 5 acres, off County Road 56V. Its HOA runs the only community water system EPA lists on the mesa.
  • Finnegan RanchEight parcels of about 35 to 38 acres at the northwest corner, beside a 574-acre Nature Conservancy easement. The last stretch in, Green Meadow Lane (County Road 57U), is not county-maintained.

What Garrett would check on Hastings Mesa

  • Winter accessThe county plows about 7.1 miles of County Road 58P and 4.3 of County Road 56V, not the branch toward Last Dollar Pass, and some subdivision roads are private. Ask how a lot is reached from December to May.
  • Power and phoneNo public source shows which lots have power and phone at the lot line. Ask before you plan a build.
  • Water and septicTelluride Pines has the only community water system EPA lists here. Elsewhere, ask how a lot gets water; a new well needs a state permit, and septic a county permit.
  • The county lineThe Ouray County line runs near Dallas Divide. For a parcel at the northeast edge, confirm the county, then the zone in the county's Zone District Finder.

Questions

Hastings Mesa FAQ

Is Hastings Mesa in San Miguel County or Ouray County?

San Miguel County. Every named subdivision on the mesa is in San Miguel County in county records. The Ouray County line runs near Dallas Divide, at the mesa's northeast corner.

How do you reach Hastings Mesa in winter?

On County Road 58P from Sawpit, which the county plows for about 7.1 miles to CO-62, or on County Road 56V from CO-62. Last Dollar Pass is closed to vehicles from December 1 to May 15.

How big are the parcels on Hastings Mesa?

Most subdivisions are parcels of 35 acres or more in county records. San Juan Vista, Hastings Mesa Estate and Telluride Pines have smaller lots, with medians of about 6.5, 8.8 and 5 acres.

Is Last Dollar Tracts the same as Last Dollar on Deep Creek Mesa?

No. Last Dollar Tracts sits at the CO-62 end of Last Dollar Road, on Hastings Mesa. The Last Dollar PUD is a separate subdivision near the airport turnoff on Deep Creek Mesa, about 10 miles south.

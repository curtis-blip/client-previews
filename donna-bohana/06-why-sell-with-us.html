Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Selling in Malibu

How long does it take to sell a home in Malibu? ▾
The timeline depends on price point, location, condition, and market conditions. Luxury homes in Malibu typically sell within 60 to 120 days when priced correctly, though some properties in prime locations sell much faster. Donna will provide a detailed market analysis and realistic timeline during your initial consultation.
How does Donna price my Malibu home? ▾
Donna uses a comprehensive Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) combined with more than 20 years of local transaction history. She evaluates recent comparable sales, current inventory, micro-neighborhood factors like ocean views and bluff positioning, and broader market trends. Her record-breaking sales in the Palisades Highlands demonstrate her ability to price homes for maximum return.
What does Solstice's marketing plan include? ▾
Every listing receives professional architectural photography, drone footage, 3D virtual tours, targeted digital advertising, social media campaigns, PR outreach to luxury publications, broker open events, and exposure through Donna's international network. The plan is customized based on your property's unique selling points and target buyer profile.
What is Donna's commission structure? ▾
Commission is discussed during the listing consultation and varies based on the property and scope of services. Donna is transparent about costs and always explains the value behind the investment. With her track record of record-breaking prices, the return on working with Solstice consistently exceeds the commission cost. Contact Donna to discuss specifics.
Should I stage my home before listing? ▾
In Malibu's luxury market, staging can make a significant difference. Donna works with top staging professionals and will advise on whether your home needs full staging, partial updates, or is best shown as-is. The goal is always to present the property in a way that maximizes the buyer's emotional connection and perceived value.
When is the best time to sell a home in Malibu? ▾
Malibu's luxury market operates year-round because buyers are often relocating from other states or countries on their own timeline. Spring and early summer typically see the highest activity, but well-priced properties in desirable locations attract qualified buyers in any season. Donna will help you evaluate the current market conditions and choose the optimal listing window for your property.

Buying in the San Juans

Buying a Home, Ranch, or Land in Southwest Colorado

Many of our buyers come from out of state and fall for this place the way we did. We make the search, the showings, and the closing work from anywhere.

Why Buy With Us

Local Knowledge, Personal Service

We live here full time, fish these rivers, and know which roads close in winter. That local knowledge is the difference between a property that looks right online and one that is right for you.

The Process

How Buying Works With Us

Step 01

First Conversation

We start by understanding what you are looking for, your timeline, and whether this is a full-time move, a second home, or an investment.

Step 02

Financing & Proof of Funds

We connect you with local lenders who understand mountain, land, and rural properties, so your offer is ready when the right place appears.

Step 03

The Search

We line up homes, ranches, and land that fit, and we flag the details that matter out here: water rights, well and septic, access, and zoning.

Step 04

Touring, Near or Far

Many of our buyers are out of state. We walk properties on your behalf, send video, and make showings easy from a thousand miles away.

Step 05

Making an Offer

We help you write a competitive, well-structured offer and negotiate terms that protect you.

Step 06

Inspections & Due Diligence

For rural property that means wells, septic, access easements, and surveys, not just a standard home inspection.

Step 07

Appraisal & Financing

We coordinate with your lender and the appraiser to keep everything moving toward closing.

Step 08

Closing & Welcome Home

We are with you through signing and beyond, and we are happy to check on the place after the first snow.

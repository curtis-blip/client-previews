Why People Ask

A cash offer for my house, without the guesswork

Some sales cannot wait for staging, weekend open houses, and a 45-day mortgage timeline. A job starts in two weeks. An inherited house in Canton sits empty. A divorce needs to close. When that is the situation, a cash sale can be the right call, and we treat it as one option among several, never the only answer.

We are a real brokerage, not a billboard buyer. The Hutson Group at Century 21 Results has closed more than 750 transactions and over $200 million across Woodstock, Marietta, Cumming and the North Atlanta counties since 2017. We pull live comparable sales, bring you offers from buyers we have vetted, and walk you through the numbers so you can decide with the full picture in front of you.

How It Works

From first call to cleared and closed

Four steps. No repairs, no showings, and a closing date you choose.

01
Tell Us About the House

Share the address and the situation

Send us the property and what is driving the timeline. We look at the home, its condition, and recent sales nearby in your county, then tell you whether a cash buyer is realistic for your price point. No obligation, and no pressure to take it.

02
See Real Numbers

Get a vetted cash offer in writing

We bring offers from buyers we have actually closed with before, then put the figure next to a quick estimate of what the same home might net on the open market. You get both numbers side by side, so the choice is yours and it is informed.

03
Skip the Prep

No showings, no repairs, sold as-is

Cash buyers purchase the home in its current condition. Leave the dated kitchen, skip the weekend cleanups, and forget staging. There is nothing to fix and no parade of strangers walking through your living room on a Saturday.

04
Close When You Want

Pick the date, then move on

With no lender appraisal or loan underwriting holding things up, cash closings often land in one to three weeks. Need longer to find your next place? Say so. We build the timeline around your move, not the other way around.

FITS
When It Fits

Situations where a cash sale earns its keep

A cash offer is a tool. These are the times it tends to be the right one.

A Fast Relocation

A job moves you to another state next month. A certain closing date means you are not paying two mortgages while a listing sits.

An Inherited Property

You live out of town and the estate home in Cherokee or Cobb County needs to sell. A cash buyer takes it as-is, contents and all.

A Home That Needs Work

Major repairs you would rather not fund or manage. Selling as-is hands the renovation, and the cost, to the buyer.

Speed Over Top Dollar

When certainty and a quick close matter more than squeezing out the last few thousand, cash is hard to beat.

The straight advice

Here is the part other cash programs leave out: a listed home on the open market usually nets more, even after agent fees and a few weeks of prep. A cash offer trades dollars for speed and certainty. If your home is in good shape and you can give the sale a little runway, we will tell you to list it, and we will show you the Zillow Showcase marketing that helps homes here sell faster and for more. If speed is the priority, the cash route is genuinely the smarter move. Either way, you get the real numbers and a recommendation we would give our own family.

PROCESS
The Process

From first tour to final keys

Six clear steps, with you in control the whole way.

01
Step One

Initial Consultation

We start by understanding what you actually want, so the search is focused from day one.

  • Understanding your goals: your ideal home, preferred neighborhoods, and whether this is a starter, forever, or investment property.
  • Budget and timeline: a comfortable budget and a realistic move-in window.
  • Must-haves vs nice-to-haves: we separate the non-negotiables from the wish list.
02
Step Two

Pre-Approval

A pre-approval letter shows sellers you are a serious, qualified buyer. In competitive markets it can be the difference between winning and losing a home.

  • Strengthening your position: you are ready to act the moment the right home appears.
  • Trusted lenders: we partner with reputable local lenders who are responsive and clear.
  • Why it matters: it removes financing uncertainty and lets you move quickly.
03
Step Three

Home Search & Tours

This is the fun part, exploring homes and neighborhoods together.

  • Curated listings: properties matched to your criteria, constantly updated.
  • Property tours: scheduled around your life, with us pointing out features and concerns.
  • Expert evaluation: pricing trends, condition, comparables, and resale value.
04
Step Four

Submit an Offer

When you find the one, we position you to win.

  • Strategic pricing: built on comps, market conditions, days on market, and seller motivation.
  • Clear terms: contingencies and timelines that protect you and stay attractive to sellers.
  • Positioning to win: local relationships and negotiation skill, in any market.
05
Step Five

Negotiation & Binding Agreement

We advocate hard on your behalf, then get everything in writing.

  • Expert negotiation: counters on price, terms, and timelines.
  • Favorable terms: we protect your interests through every round.
  • Contract execution: once signed, you are officially under contract.
06
Step Six

Due Diligence

Your safety net before you finalize the purchase.

  • Inspection period: structure, roof, systems, plumbing, electrical, and HVAC.
  • Interpreting findings: we help you tell serious issues from routine maintenance.
  • Amendment negotiations: repairs, credits, or price adjustments where warranted.
LEND
Step Two, Made Simple

Know your budget before you fall in love with a home

Getting pre-approved is the single most powerful thing you can do as a buyer. We will connect you with trusted local lenders and walk you through every step.

Scottsdale Luxury Specialist

A Career Built on Relationships

Sarah Mitchell has been a trusted name in Scottsdale luxury real estate since 2008. With over $850 million in career sales volume and more than 400 families served, she brings an unmatched depth of market knowledge to every transaction across Paradise Valley, DC Ranch, North Scottsdale, and Grayhawk.

Before entering real estate, Sarah spent a decade in architecture and interior design in Chicago. That background gives her a trained eye for property value, spatial quality, and renovation potential that most agents simply cannot offer. She evaluates every home not just as a transaction, but as a living space with long-term investment implications.

Sarah holds the Certified Luxury Home Marketing Specialist (CLHMS) designation, is a member of the Institute for Luxury Home Marketing, and has been recognized as a top 1% Scottsdale agent for eight consecutive years. She is also a Certified Negotiation Expert (CNE), ensuring her clients receive optimal terms on every deal.

When she is not helping clients, Sarah volunteers with Habitat for Humanity Scottsdale and is an active member of the Scottsdale Cultural Council. She lives in North Scottsdale with her husband and two children.

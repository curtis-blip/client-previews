Questions

Insurance questions

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and a Medicare Supplement?

Medicare Advantage replaces Original Medicare with a private plan, often with a network and extra benefits. A Supplement works alongside Original Medicare and helps pay the costs it leaves. Which fits depends on your doctors, budget and travel.

When can I enroll in Medicare?

Your Initial Enrollment Period runs for seven months around your 65th birthday: the three months before, your birthday month and the three months after.

What is final expense insurance?

A small whole life policy meant to cover funeral costs and final bills, often with simplified or guaranteed acceptance.

Can I get health insurance outside open enrollment?

Usually only after a qualifying life event, such as a move, a marriage, a new baby or losing other coverage.

Is there any cost for a consultation?

No. Robert's consultations and plan comparisons carry no obligation.

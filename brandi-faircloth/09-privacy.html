Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Faircloth Home Team respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any details you share when you contact us, request a home valuation, save a property search, or sign up to receive listing updates.

We also collect limited information automatically as you browse, including your IP address, browser type, device, the pages you view, and the dates and times of your visits. This helps us understand how the site is used and how we can improve it.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, send you property listings and market updates you have requested, schedule appointments, provide our real estate services, improve our website, and comply with applicable laws. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in to saved searches, and measure site performance. We may use analytics tools to understand visitor behavior in aggregate. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function as intended.

Third-Party Services

Property search and listing data on this site are provided through Showcase IDX, our MLS listing search partner. When you save searches or create an account through that tool, your information is handled under its own privacy practices in addition to ours.

We may also share limited contact details with trusted lender partners and service providers when you request financing information or related services. These partners are expected to protect your information and use it only for the purpose for which it was shared.

Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any message we send. You may request that we update, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law.

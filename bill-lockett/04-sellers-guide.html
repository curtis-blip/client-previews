Selling a home in Chaffee County.

Twenty-five years listing across Buena Vista, Salida, and the Collegiate Peaks corridor. The #1 producing agent at Coldwell Banker Collegiate Peaks Realty.

Why list with Bill

Twenty-five years of local closings.

Bill has personally listed and sold properties from Leadville to Westcliffe since 1999. He knows which buyer pool is active in March versus October, what a clean well report does to your offer count, and where a strategic price reduction creates a bidding war instead of signaling weakness.

The eight-step process below is the one he runs personally on every listing, no junior-agent handoff.

The Process

Eight steps from valuation to closing.

Pricing strategy, marketing, showings, negotiation, closing. Bill handles every stage personally.

01

Initial valuation conversation

Bill walks the property, reviews recent comparable sales, and gives you a realistic price range. The instant estimate from the widget orients the conversation; this is the listing-price layer Bill builds on top of it. A direct read on what your home should list for.

02

Pricing strategy and CMA

A full comparative market analysis: what's listed, what's pending, what just sold, and what makes your property comparable or different. Pricing slightly under, at, or over market each has a different outcome. Bill recommends based on your timeline and goal.

03

Prep and staging recommendations

What to fix, what to leave alone, what to declutter. Mountain properties have specific staging considerations: maximize the views, downplay the deferred maintenance, show how the home lives in winter and summer.

04

Professional photography and video

Drone footage of the property, the surrounding peaks, and the seasonal context. Interior photography that flatters the home without overselling it. Twilight shots for properties where light is the differentiator.

05

Marketing launch

MLS, Realtor.com, Zillow, BillLockett.com, the brokerage email list, social media, and Bill's personal referral network. The Collegiate Peaks buyer pool is national and the marketing reflects that.

06

Showings and buyer-agent coordination

Bill personally coordinates every showing. Feedback gets relayed to you, not buried. If something needs adjusting (price, staging, hours), the conversation happens promptly.

07

Offer review and negotiation

Every offer reviewed in detail. Price, contingencies, financing strength, timeline, earnest money. Bill explains the tradeoffs, recommends a response, and negotiates aggressively but without burning the deal.

08

Closing

Inspection, appraisal, underwriting, and final walkthrough all coordinated by Bill personally. At closing he's there in person to hand over keys, confirm the wire, and close the file.

Home Valuation

What Is My Home Worth? Try the Home Value Estimator

An instant estimate: type your address for a likely value, a range and a confidence score.

How it works

What the Estimate Sees, and What It Can’t

The estimator reads public records and recent sales to give a likely value, a range and how confident it is.

It cannot see your renovations, your lot or your view. For a number you can plan around, ask Sarah for a comparative market analysis.

Beyond the estimate

Two Ways to Pin Down Value

A comparative market analysis
Sarah compares your home with similar homes that sold nearby, adjusts for the differences, and adds what she sees in person: condition, updates, lot and setting. It is free.
An appraisal
A licensed appraiser inspects the home and writes an independent report. Lenders order one for a purchase or a refinance.

When it helps

When a Valuation Helps

Planning a sale
Know your likely price and your equity before you decide on timing.
Refinancing
Your home’s value sets how much you can borrow and the terms you are offered.
Renovating
See how your home compares with others nearby before you spend on improvements.
A home equity line
Lenders look at your equity to decide whether you qualify and for how much.

Questions

Home Value Questions

How accurate is the instant estimate?

It is a good starting point. It cannot account for recent renovations or features public records do not show, so Sarah's analysis is the number to plan around.

What is the difference between a CMA and an appraisal?

A comparative market analysis is Sarah's read of the market for pricing. An appraisal is an independent report a lender relies on.

Does a valuation cost anything?

The instant estimate and Sarah's comparative market analysis are both free.

Can Sarah give me a more precise number?

Yes. Ask for a comparative market analysis and she will look at your home in person.

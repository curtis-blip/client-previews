Our Approach

The Team Behind Every Transaction

At Vantegic Home Team, we know that every move comes with big decisions, changing priorities, and important goals. That's why we focus on making the process straightforward, transparent, and tailored to what matters most to you.

Our team combines local market knowledge, strong communication, and creative marketing strategies to help buyers, sellers, and investors make informed decisions. What sets us apart is our hands-on approach, we take the time to understand your goals, stay accessible throughout the process, and work hard to create results that make sense for your situation.

Meet Jose

Jose Medina, Founder And Lead Broker

I have spent more than 20 years selling real estate in Colorado Springs. In that time my team and I have guided over 1,200 families through a move, which placed me in the Top 1% of producers across the region. The ranking is nice. The number that actually matters is the one on my client's closing statement.

Real estate was never about the transaction for me. It is about protecting the equity families spend their whole lives building. First home, Broadmoor listing, PCS orders to Fort Carson, Peterson SFB, Schriever SFB, or the Air Force Academy, or a new build going up in Northgate, the work is the same: read the market honestly, negotiate hard, and keep you informed at every step.

I list and sell across Colorado Springs and the wider Pikes Peak region, from Briargate and Northgate to Monument, Black Forest, Peyton, Fountain, and the Powers Corridor. You can see how I read each of those areas on the Colorado Springs communities page, and hear it in my clients' own words across 91 verified reviews.

If you are earlier in the process, start with the Buyer's Guide or the Seller's Guide. Both walk through what actually happens, step by step, before you ever pick up the phone. Full service in English and Spanish, start to finish.

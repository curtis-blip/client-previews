Living in Victory

A 55+ district with Verrado at its door

Victory is the 55+ district inside Verrado, in Buckeye in the foothills of the White Tank Mountains. It opened in 2015 with no gates or walls, so residents have a club and amenities of their own plus everything the larger Verrado community offers: Main Street, the trails and the new Verrado Marketplace. I compared the two in Verrado vs. Victory.

Homes are single-story designs, from duplexes and cottages to homes on 80-foot homesites. Builders over the years have included Lennar, Taylor Morrison, K. Hovnanian and Landsea, now Risewell Homes. David Weekley, K. Hovnanian, Risewell and William Ryan are selling new homes now.

"I have lived in Verrado for over 12 years and have toured Victory more times than I can count. I can tell you which streets have the best sunset views, which floor plans buyers love, and how each builder's homes hold their value."Michelle Seymour · Verrado resident

Questions

Victory questions I hear most

Is Victory a 55+ community?

Yes. Victory is the age-restricted 55+ village inside Verrado. Ask me how the age rules apply to any home you are considering.

What are HOA fees in Victory?

Most Victory homes run about $200 to $300 a month. Fees vary by neighborhood and builder, so I confirm the exact amount for any home you are considering.

Who is building in Victory now?

David Weekley (as Encore), K. Hovnanian, Risewell Homes and William Ryan Homes are selling new homes now. Earlier builders include Lennar and Taylor Morrison, so resales come in many styles.

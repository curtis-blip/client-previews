Who We Are

A Team Built for the Coastside

Palermo Properties Team is a group of eight dedicated real estate professionals at Compass, serving buyers and sellers across the San Mateo County Coastside and Peninsula. Our team operates from two offices, one in Half Moon Bay and one in Burlingame, covering everything from oceanfront properties and hillside retreats to neighborhood homes in El Granada, Montara, Moss Beach, Pacifica, and San Mateo.

What brought us together was a shared belief that real estate on the Coastside requires something more than a standard approach. The communities here are distinct. Market conditions shift quickly. Buyers relocating from San Francisco, Silicon Valley, and beyond need advisors who understand both the lifestyle and the data. That is what we deliver.

We combine deep local roots with the full resources of Compass, giving our clients access to industry-leading marketing, technology, and a national network of top agents. Whether you are buying your first home or selling a property you have loved for decades, our team is built to guide you through every step with clarity and care.

Principal Agent

Meet Stephanie Sills

Stephanie Sills brings a rare combination of academic depth and professional range to her work in Coastside real estate. She holds three graduate-level degrees, a BFA, an MBA, and an MIM (Master of International Management), reflecting her commitment to continuous learning and strategic thinking. Before entering real estate, she spent over 20 years in the corporate world, building expertise in Internet Software, Data Management, and Consulting for clients across multiple industries.

Her corporate career centered on Client Relationship Management and Customer Success, where she developed a client-first approach that carries directly into how she serves buyers and sellers today. She understands complex negotiations, contract details, and the importance of clear communication at every stage of a transaction. That professional discipline, paired with genuine care for her clients, is what sets her apart.

Stephanie became a top producer from her very first year in real estate. She chose to focus on the San Mateo County Coastside because she lives here. As a Moss Beach homeowner, she knows these neighborhoods at a personal level, from the schools and local businesses to the hiking trails and tide pools that make this area special.

As a Principal Agent with Palermo Properties Team at Compass, Stephanie works alongside a team of experienced agents who share her commitment to client-centered service. Outside of real estate, she proudly supports several educational nonprofits on the Coastside, investing in the community she calls home.

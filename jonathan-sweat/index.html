The Pre-Approval Difference

We only do pre-approvals.
Not pre-qualifications.

Most lenders run a soft credit pull, ask three questions, and call it a pre-qual. It looks the same on paper. It plays out very differently when you're competing for a house.

Our team underwrites your full file before you ever make an offer. Income, assets, credit, the whole picture. So when a Realtor sees Jonathan's name on a pre-approval letter, they know one thing for certain: the deal will close.

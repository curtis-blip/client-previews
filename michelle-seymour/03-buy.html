THE PROCESS

First Time Home Buyer Arizona: What to Expect

Buying a home in the West Valley is one of the best decisions you can make, but the process can feel overwhelming if you don't know what to expect. That's where I come in. I've guided hundreds of buyers through every stage, from pre-approval to closing day, and I make sure you understand exactly what's happening at each step.

The West Valley offers something for everyone: master-planned communities like Verrado and Sterling Grove, 55+ resort-style living at PebbleCreek and Victory, and affordable family neighborhoods in Tartesso and Sundance. The key is matching the right community to your lifestyle, budget, and timeline.

KNOW THE NUMBERS

Relocating to Phoenix AZ: What It Costs

Down Payment Ranges

Most buyers in the West Valley put 3-20% down depending on loan type. FHA loans start at 3.5%, conventional at 5%, and VA loans offer 0% down for eligible veterans. I'll help you explore all your options.

Closing Costs

Expect to budget 2-3% of the purchase price for closing costs in Arizona. This includes title insurance, escrow fees, lender fees, and prepaid items like property taxes and homeowner's insurance.

Pre-Approval Timeline

Getting pre-approved typically takes 1-3 business days with a responsive lender. I recommend starting this process before you begin touring homes so you know exactly what you can afford.

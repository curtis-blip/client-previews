What is my home worth

Not an algorithm's guess off a satellite photo. A number from someone who has been inside the houses yours will be compared to.

01 / The difference

Why the online estimate is usually wrong here

Automated valuations work reasonably well in dense subdivisions where a thousand near-identical houses have sold recently. They work badly in rural Georgia, where the nearest genuine comparable might be four miles away on a different sized lot with a different outbuilding situation.

The models cannot see that you replaced the roof, that the back two acres flood, or that the house across the road has been for sale for eleven months. A person who works this market every week can.

02 / What we look at

Six things the algorithm cannot see

01

Actual condition

Roof age, HVAC, foundation, windows. What a buyer's inspector is going to find and price against.

02

The land

Acreage, road frontage, drainage, outbuildings, timber. In this market the lot often moves the number more than the house.

03

Real comparables

Not the county average. The specific houses that sold near you, and what was different about them.

04

What is sitting

Active listings that have not moved are as informative as sold ones. They tell you where the ceiling is.

05

Local demand

Fort Stewart rotations move Hinesville. Vidalia moves on its own schedule. The counties do not track together.

06

Timing

What the same house is worth in March versus November, and whether waiting is worth it.

03 / No obligation

A valuation is not a listing agreement

Plenty of people ask because they are curious, or planning two years out. You will get the number and an honest read either way.

04 / How it works

Three steps, about a week

01

You tell us about the house

Address, rough condition, anything you have changed. Five minutes on the phone or by email.

02

We walk it

In person where we can. Photos and a call where we cannot. This is the part the websites skip.

03

You get a range and the reasoning

A realistic range, the comparables behind it, and what would move it up before listing.

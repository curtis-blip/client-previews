What a Home Valuation Actually Tells You

An instant estimate is a starting point, not an answer. Automated tools read public records and recent sales and hand you a range. That range can swing tens of thousands of dollars because the software cannot see inside your house. It does not know you redid the kitchen, finished the basement, or that the roof is one storm from replacement. Treat the number as a rough bracket, then narrow it with someone who knows the street.

A real valuation weighs the things the algorithm misses: the condition of your home, the updates you have made, where you sit within Champaign County, and what buyers are actually paying right now. A ranch in Mahomet near Lake of the Woods and a similar square-foot ranch in Champaign do not carry the same value, and a tool that averages them will tell you neither. Two houses on the same block can appraise differently because one has been kept up and the other has not.

Demand is the part people forget. The same home is worth more when buyers are competing for it and less when listings sit. Russ has priced homes through high markets and slow ones across the 217 area since 1972, so the figure he gives you reflects where the Champaign County market is this month, not a number a server generated last quarter.

Our Method

How We Determine Your Home's Value

When I value a home, I work through four things in person. None of them show up cleanly in an instant estimate, and together they are where the real number comes from.

Comparable Sales

I pull recent sales near you that genuinely match yours on size, age, and layout, then adjust for the real differences between those homes and yours rather than leaning on a raw average.

Condition and Updates

I walk the house and account for what an algorithm cannot see: the new furnace, the updated kitchen, the finished basement, or the deferred work a buyer will price in.

Location and Lot

Where you sit within Champaign County matters: the school district, the proximity to campus or Lake of the Woods, the lot size, and the street all move the number up or down.

Current Demand

I factor in what buyers in the 217 area are paying this month, how fast comparable homes are going under contract, and how much inventory you would be competing against.

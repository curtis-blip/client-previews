Buyer Questions

Scottsdale Home Buying FAQ

The median home price in Scottsdale is approximately $750,000 as of 2026. Luxury properties in Paradise Valley and DC Ranch typically range from $1.5M to $10M+, while communities like Grayhawk offer entry points starting around $500,000. Beyond the purchase price, buyers should budget 2-5% for closing costs.

Most Scottsdale home purchases close within 30-45 days from accepted offer. The full journey from initial search to closing typically takes 2-4 months, depending on financing complexity and market conditions. Cash transactions can close in as few as 14 days.

While not legally required, a dedicated buyer's agent is strongly recommended in Scottsdale's luxury market. An experienced agent provides access to off-market listings, handles complex negotiations, and protects your interests throughout the transaction. Sarah's clients consistently save money through her negotiation expertise and market knowledge.

Most conventional loan programs require a minimum credit score of 620, though scores above 740 qualify for the best interest rates. Jumbo loans, common in Scottsdale's luxury market, typically require scores of 700 or higher. Sarah works with lenders who specialize in high-value financing and can advise on preparation strategies if needed.

Closing costs in Arizona typically range from 2-5% of the purchase price and include title insurance, escrow fees, lender origination fees, appraisal costs, and recording fees. On a $1M purchase, expect approximately $20,000-$50,000 in closing costs. Sarah provides a detailed cost breakdown early in the process so there are no surprises.

Key areas include roof condition (critical in Arizona's climate), HVAC systems, pool equipment, foundation integrity, and plumbing. Sarah's architectural background helps her identify structural concerns that standard inspectors might overlook. She attends every inspection alongside her clients and recommends specialists for any flagged issues.

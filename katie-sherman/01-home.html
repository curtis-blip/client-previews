Moving to the Suncoast

Moving to Sarasota From Out of State

Ryan and Katie did it from St. Louis. Their clients have bought from Tennessee and the northern states, and one family bought sight unseen.

  1. Pick the town first, then the house

    Siesta Key and Lakewood Ranch feel nothing alike. Ryan and Katie start with how you want to live day to day, then help you narrow twelve towns down to the two or three that fit.

  2. Video walk-throughs when you can't be here

    They walk a home with you on a video call; Katie’s reviews mention Zoom walk-throughs and answers on weekends.

  3. A closing you can sign from home

    Florida allows remote online notarization, so many buyers sign from where they live now and drive down to keys that are already theirs.

How buying works

Eight Steps From First Search to Keys

Each step names what you bring to it, so you know what comes next.

  1. Step 1

    Tell Noah what you are looking for

    A first call or note. You share the kind of home, the towns you are drawn to and when you hope to move, and Noah tells you what he sees in those towns.

    What it needsYour must-haves, a rough timeline and the towns on your list.

  2. Step 2

    Line up your financing

    Before you tour in earnest, talk with a lender about what you can borrow and what a monthly payment looks like. If you are not sure what income a home takes, his post on what salary you need to buy a house in Longmont is a good place to start.

    What it needsA conversation with a lender and the income and savings paperwork they ask for.

  3. Step 3

    Set up your search

    Noah sets up your search so new homes that fit come to you. You can also look on your own in the home search at any time.

    What it needsPrice range, bedrooms, the towns you want and anything you will not compromise on.

  4. Step 4

    See the homes, in person or on video

    For every home you are serious about, Noah films the whole house, the good, the bad and the ugly, so you can judge it from wherever you are. In town, you walk it with him.

    What it needsA short list of homes you are serious about.

  5. Step 5

    Make an offer

    When a home is right, Noah helps you decide what to offer and what terms to ask for, then negotiates for you.

    What it needsYour price ceiling and the terms that matter most to you.

  6. Step 6

    Inspect the home

    This is the one trip for out-of-state buyers: you fly out for the inspection. Noah goes through the inspector’s findings with you and what to ask the seller to fix.

    What it needsYour time on site for the inspection, and the inspector’s report.

  7. Step 7

    Clear the paperwork

    Appraisal, title and the loan all move toward closing together. Noah keeps the dates straight, and if you are still out of state he sends someone to your current home to sign closing documents.

    What it needsYour signatures and any documents your lender or the title company ask for.

  8. Step 8

    Close and move in

    A final walk-through, the closing itself and the keys. Noah’s promise is support from the first conversation to closing day and beyond.

    What it needsYour closing funds and your moving date.

Questions

Buying a Home, Answered

Can I buy a home without visiting first?

You can see every home you are serious about before you travel, because Noah films each one. Out-of-state buyers fly out once, for the inspection.

Which towns does Noah cover?

Nineteen, from Boulder and Longmont to Fort Collins, Windsor and Commerce City. Each has its own page, listed under “where to buy” above.

I am still deciding whether to move. Where do I start?

Start with Noah’s guide to moving to Colorado, then watch the town guides for the places on your list.

Do I need a lender before I start looking?

Talking with a lender early tells you what you can comfortably borrow, which narrows the search before you fall for a house outside it.

I have a home to sell first. Can Noah help with both?

Yes. Noah works with sellers too. Start with what your home is worth and plan the two moves together.

How can I check that Noah is licensed?

He is a Colorado real estate broker, license 100088958, with eXp Realty. Look him up with the Colorado Division of Real Estate.

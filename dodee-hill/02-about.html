MEET DODEE · SOUTHSIDE SPECIALIST · CORPUS CHRISTI REALTOR®

Meet Dodee Hill, the Southside Specialist.

WYOMING ROOTS, CORPUS HOME

Raised in Wyoming. Rooted in Corpus Christi since 2012.

Dodee Hill grew up in Wyoming, where she learned the lessons that still shape how she works today. Hard work. Word is bond. Attention to detail. Her family later transferred from the Black Hills of South Dakota to Corpus Christi, Texas in 2012, trading mountain pine for salt air and finding a community they have called home ever since.

The Hill family camps, volunteers around the local community, and lives the beach-life rhythm that draws so many people to the Coastal Bend. That lived-in perspective shows up in every transaction. Dodee has walked the relocation path her clients are walking, packed the boxes, learned a new market, and figured out which neighborhoods fit which kind of life.

As a full-service REALTOR® with AARE (Andrew Arroyo Real Estate Inc), Dodee is best known as the Southside Specialist. Kings Crossing, the Cimmaron and South Staples corridor, larger lots, custom homes, and mature trees are her home turf. She also works closely with military families relocating to NAS-CC, island and coastal-lifestyle buyers, and out-of-state families making the move to South Texas. She listens first, communicates plainly, and negotiates with steady hands.

WHY WORK WITH DODEE

What it looks like to work with the Southside Specialist.

Coastal Bend Local

Dodee knows the Coastal Bend at the street level. Southside Corpus Christi and Kings Crossing are her home turf. She works the NAS-CC area for military buyers, the island communities of North Padre and Port Aransas for coastal-lifestyle moves, and the Rockport and Port A market for waterfront and second homes. You get a guide who has actually driven the streets, not just pulled the listings.

She Lived the Move

Dodee moved her family from Wyoming to the Black Hills of South Dakota and then to Corpus Christi in 2012. She knows what it feels like to land in a new market without a network, weigh schools sight unseen, and pick a neighborhood from a hundred listings online. Relocating buyers, especially military and out-of-state families, get a REALTOR® who has walked the same path.

Plain-Spoken, No Pressure

No scripts. No sales-y push. Dodee listens first, asks questions that matter, and tells you what she would do if it were her family. Clients describe her as steady, organized, available, and the kind of agent who makes you feel like family. AARE backs her with full-service brokerage infrastructure so you get personal attention and the systems to back it up.

Henry County, Virginia

Living in Martinsville.

A small town with heart. Where people still wave, neighbors show up, and community actually means something.

The Town

Uptown Martinsville Is the Cornerstone.

Seasonal festivals, farmers market, coffee shop, restaurants and boutique shops. It's a place where business owners still know your name. Full of historic architecture, walkable streets, and a close-knit community feel.

Martinsville sits at the heart of Henry County, surrounded by Collinsville, Bassett, Fieldale, Stanleytown, Ridgeway, and Axton. If you're a buyer looking at a Martinsville address, you should also know what's available in those surrounding communities. They're often more affordable and just a 10 to 15 minute drive from Uptown.

Meet Tonya

About Tonya Bredamus.

client photoTonya Bredamus · professional portrait · 4:5

Hey, I'm Tonya Bredamus and I'm obsessed with real estate. I have been since I was a kid. I loved looking at all of the homes in the monthly home buyer magazines.

At the age of 18 I got my real estate license in Florida. I ended up moving to Virginia after college and then started having kids. Fast forward 24 years and I finally got back to my first love, real estate.

I love a good house hunt and the thrill of listing a house so perfectly that it goes under contract within a day or two. I have a heart for helping people and I am especially passionate about helping out of state buyers find a house and transition to my favorite place I've ever lived, Martinsville and Henry County, Virginia.

Whether you are buying or selling, I am here to work hard so you don't have to.

Where I Focus

Areas of Expertise.

The five things I get asked to do most often, and what I bring to each.

I've made the out-of-state move myself. I walk California, Colorado, and Florida buyers through the entire process: picking the right Virginia community for their lifestyle, scheduling virtual tours, coordinating with mortgage and insurance, and making sure their inspection contingencies are timed right when they can't be on the ground.
First-time buyers get extra time from me. I explain pre-approval, closing costs, escrow, and inspection results in plain language. I know which Virginia first-time-buyer programs are worth applying for and which aren't.
I work across eight named communities, from Martinsville's Uptown to Smith Mountain Lake's shoreline to Patrick County's no-zoning rural acreage. I host an ADTV episode about each one. Each market prices and markets differently, and I won't recommend a community I don't know personally.
My favorite listings are the ones that sell in a day or two. That happens when pricing, prep, photography, and timing all line up. Alice Farmer's house sold the same day the sign went in the yard. That's the goal every time.
When you're moving to Martinsville from out of state, you don't just need a home. You need a mortgage broker, a home inspector, an insurance agent, sometimes a moving company, sometimes a contractor. I've built relationships with all of them locally. Gloria Larravide's testimonial mentions exactly this.

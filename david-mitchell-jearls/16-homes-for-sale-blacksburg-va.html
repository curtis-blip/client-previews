About Blacksburg

What it is like to live in Blacksburg

Blacksburg is an incorporated town in Montgomery County, with 44,826 residents in the 2020 Census. William Black gave 38 acres in 1797 for a town laid out as the Sixteen Squares, and the town was incorporated in 1798.

Virginia Tech's main campus is here, with its Center for the Arts and the Hahn Horticulture Garden. The Huckleberry Trail, a paved path, runs from the Blacksburg library on Miller Street toward Christiansburg.

Homes close to campus and downtown tend to have a long history of different owners and uses. Mitch, who renovated and resold homes himself, asks the same questions on every one: what has been replaced, what has only been painted, and what a seller can show you on paper.

Highlights

What sets Blacksburg apart

  • Historic Smithfield

    A historic site at 1000 Merry Oak Way with tours, a museum, gardens and grounds. It is open Wednesday to Saturday and closes from mid-December to early April. Historic Smithfield

  • Huckleberry Trail

    A paved trail linking Blacksburg and Christiansburg, with access points at the Christiansburg Recreation Center and the New River Valley Mall. Town of Christiansburg

  • Alexander Black House

    The Blacksburg Museum and Cultural Foundation's home at 204 Draper Road SW, with rotating art and history exhibitions. Blacksburg Museum

  • Hahn Horticulture Garden

    Nearly six acres of teaching and display gardens run by Virginia Tech, on Garden Lane. Virginia Tech

  • Center for the Arts at Virginia Tech

    A performance hall with a 1,260-seat theatre, galleries and studios on Alumni Mall. Virginia Tech

Questions

Questions buyers ask about Blacksburg

Which school division serves a Blacksburg address?+

Montgomery County Public Schools serves the town, pre-K to 12. Confirm the campus for any address with the division before you buy.

Is Blacksburg its own city?+

No. Blacksburg is an incorporated town inside Montgomery County, so county services and county schools apply alongside the town's own.

Where does the Huckleberry Trail start?+

The mile marker zero sits at the Blacksburg library on Miller Street, and the paved trail runs toward Christiansburg. The Town of Christiansburg's trail page lists the access points.

What should I ask about a house near campus?+

Ask how the house has been used, what has been replaced and when, and for receipts. Mitch can walk the house with you and point out what to have inspected.

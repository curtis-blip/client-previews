I Chose This Town,
Then Built A Life In It

I moved to Woodland Park and found what most people find here eventually, that the town is small enough to know and good enough to stay in. My family grew up through these schools. I spent years in school and church activities, and these days most of my volunteering goes to youth organizations.

That is the part of this work you cannot learn from the MLS. Which streets hold their value through a hard winter, which lots drain badly in spring, and which neighborhoods down the pass are worth the drive for the schools alone.

“I help homeowners turn their biggest asset into their biggest opportunity.”

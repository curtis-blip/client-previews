The Process

8 Steps to Buying Your Phoenix Home

1

Initial Consultation

Discuss your goals, budget, timeline, and what you're looking for in a home. This sets the foundation for a focused search.

2

Mortgage Pre-Approval

Connect with a lender to determine your price range and get pre-approved. This strengthens your position when making offers.

3

Property Search

Receive curated listings matched to your criteria. Tour homes, compare neighborhoods, and evaluate properties based on condition, price, and location.

4

Make an Offer

Build a competitive offer using market data. Price, contingencies, timelines, and seller concessions are all structured to give you the best position.

5

Inspection & Due Diligence

A professional inspection reveals the home's true condition. Results are reviewed and any necessary repairs or credits are negotiated.

6

Appraisal & Loan Processing

The lender orders an appraisal to verify the home's value. Underwriting reviews your financials and the property details to finalize loan approval.

7

Final Walkthrough

Walk through the property one last time to confirm agreed repairs are complete and the home is in the expected condition before closing.

8

Closing Day

Sign the final documents, transfer funds, and receive the keys. Your lender, title company, and agent coordinate to make sure everything is in order.

Closing Costs

What Closing Costs to Expect in Phoenix

Closing costs typically range from 2% to 5% of the purchase price. Here's a breakdown of what buyers commonly pay when closing on a Phoenix home.

Loan Origination Fee

Charged by your lender for processing the mortgage. Typically 0.5% to 1% of the loan amount.

Appraisal Fee

Covers the professional appraisal to verify the home's market value. Usually $400 to $600 in the Phoenix area.

Home Inspection

A thorough evaluation of the property's condition. Expect $350 to $500 depending on home size.

Title Insurance & Search

Protects against ownership disputes and verifies the property's title history. Varies by purchase price.

Escrow & Recording Fees

Administrative costs for managing the transaction and recording the deed with the county.

Prepaid Property Taxes

A prorated share of property taxes from closing date through the end of the tax period.

Homeowners Insurance

Your first year's premium is typically due at closing. Shop rates early to compare coverage options.

HOA Transfer Fees

If the property is in an HOA community, transfer and document fees may apply. Common in Phoenix master-planned communities.

On a $400,000 home, expect roughly $8,000 to $20,000 in closing costs. Mark helps you estimate these costs early so there are no surprises at the closing table. The CFPB's homebuying guide walks through the full closing process in detail.

FAQ

Phoenix Home Buying Questions, Answered

How long does it take to buy a home in Phoenix?
Most purchases take 30 to 45 days after an accepted offer, depending on financing, inspections, and document completion timelines.
What should I prepare before starting?
Review your credit score, save for a down payment and closing costs, and get pre-approved by a lender to confirm your price range.
How competitive is the Phoenix market?
Competition varies by area, price range, and inventory. Some homes receive multiple offers when priced correctly. Each situation is evaluated using current data.
What happens after my offer is accepted?
Inspections, appraisal, and lender requirements are completed. Repairs may be negotiated based on results. Paperwork is finalized for closing.
What costs should I expect?
Expect to cover the down payment, closing costs (2-5% of purchase price), inspection fees, appraisal fees, and loan-related expenses.
Can I negotiate price or terms?
Yes. Price, contingencies, closing timelines, repair requests, and seller concessions can all be adjusted based on market conditions and property demand.

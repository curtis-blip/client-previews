Lafayette at a glance

A Boulder County coal town with an Old Town

Mary E. Miller founded Lafayette in 1888 and named it for her late husband, Lafayette Miller. It incorporated on April 2, 1889 and grew as a coal-mining town.

Today about 30,000 people live here, with Old Town on Public Road, Waneka Lake Park on the north side of town, and Boulder Valley schools across the whole city.

01

Old Town on Public Road

Old Town Lafayette centers on Public Road and Festival Plaza. The Lafayette History Museum, an 1890s miner's cottage at 108 E. Simpson Street, is a good first stop.

02

The homes

Miners' cottages near Old Town, and newer neighborhoods toward the edges of the city. Ask Steve how the two compare before you tour.

03

The lake and the trail

Waneka Lake Park, at 1600 Caria Drive, has a loop trail with a mountain view. The Bob L. Burger Recreation Center is on Baseline Road, and the 14-mile Coal Creek Trail runs through town between Superior and Erie.

04

What to check before you offer

  • Older homes in Old TownMany were built in the coal-mining era. Ask about the foundation, sewer line and electrical, and add a sewer scope to the inspection.
  • Metro district and HOA costsNewer Colorado neighborhoods often sit in a metropolitan district that adds to the property tax bill, and many carry HOA dues. Get both figures in writing before your contract deadlines.
  • The schoolBoulder Valley serves the whole city; confirm the assigned schools for the address.

Questions

Lafayette FAQ

Which school district serves Lafayette?

Boulder Valley School District serves the whole city. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district.

Where is Old Town Lafayette?

On Public Road, around Festival Plaza.

Where is Waneka Lake?

At Waneka Lake Park, 1600 Caria Drive, with a loop trail around the lake.

Is Lafayette in Boulder County?

Yes, the whole city is in Boulder County.

Why Price Upon Request

Texas does not publish sold prices.

Texas is one of a handful of non-disclosure states, which means sale prices are not part of the public record. Portals estimate them, and those estimates are frequently wrong by a wide margin. A licensed agent can pull the actual closed figures from the MLS. That is the single biggest reason a Texas seller should not price from an online estimate.

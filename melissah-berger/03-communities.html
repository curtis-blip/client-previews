Thirty minutes on US-24 separates those two lists, and they are genuinely different places to live. If you are deciding between them, the Buyer's Guide covers what changes about the purchase itself, or you can search every active listing across both.

Schools decide it for a lot of families, and the two districts publish their own boundary and performance information: Woodland Park School District RE-2 up the pass and Academy School District 20 on the north side of the Springs. If you are selling in one of these before buying in another, the Seller's Guide covers that half, and I can manage a property you decide to keep.

Woodland Park

Teller County · 8,465 ft
The city above the clouds
8,465 ftElevation
US-24Main corridor
RE-2School district
18 miTo Colorado Springs

Living here

Woodland Park is the practical center of Teller County and the reason most people can live up here at all. The grocery stores, the hospital, the high school and my office are all inside the same few square miles. Housing runs from in-town lots on paved streets to five-acre parcels on the ridges above, and the difference in commute, snow load and price between those two is larger than newcomers expect.

What I would check first

Ask about the driveway before you ask about the kitchen. Grade, aspect and who plows it decide what January feels like. In town you will usually be on city water and sewer, and further out you are on a well and a septic system that both need their own inspections. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and City of Woodland Park is the best primary source on the area.

Divide

Land, quiet, and the way into Mueller
9,165 ftElevation
US-24 / CO-67Crossroads
MuellerState park
10 miTo Woodland Park

Living here

Divide is a crossroads rather than a town center, and that is the appeal. Parcels are larger, neighbors are further off, and the entrance to Mueller State Park is minutes away. People move here for elbow room and for the quiet, and they tend to stay a long time once they have it.

What I would check first

Everything runs on a well and a septic system, so the water report matters as much as the inspection. Winter access is a real question on the unpaved county roads, and so is whether the property has a defensible space cleared around the structure. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Mueller State Park is the best primary source on the area.

Florissant

Teller County · Land
The largest parcels in the county
8,200 ftElevation
Fossil BedsNational monument
35+ acCommon parcel size
18 miTo Woodland Park

Living here

Florissant is where the acreage buyers land. The Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument sits at its center, and around it are some of the largest private parcels in Teller County, many of them 35 acres or more. Value here depends far more on the specific lot, its water, its access and its views, than on the street it sits on.

What I would check first

Two properties a mile apart can differ enormously in price for reasons that are invisible from the listing photos. Well yield, legal access, mineral rights and whether the parcel can be split are the questions worth asking first. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument is the best primary source on the area.

Green Mountain Falls

Ute Pass · Village
A hundred and forty years of mountain village
7,756 ftElevation
1880sFounded
LakeVillage center
14 miTo Colorado Springs

Living here

Green Mountain Falls is the oldest feeling place on the pass, a small village built around a lake and a gazebo, with trailheads running straight out of town. Inventory is genuinely scarce, and a good deal of it is historic, which means charm and maintenance in the same purchase.

What I would check first

Lot sizes are small and older homes often carry older systems. Check the foundation, the electrical and the roof carefully, and understand where the property sits relative to the creek. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Town of Green Mountain Falls is the best primary source on the area.

Cascade

Ute Pass · Pikes Peak
At the foot of the Pikes Peak Highway
7,400 ftElevation
Pikes Peak HwyTrailhead
US-24Corridor
10 miTo Colorado Springs

Living here

Cascade sits at the bottom of the Pikes Peak Highway, the closest of the pass communities to Colorado Springs. That makes it the practical choice for people who want to live in the trees but still drive to work in the city most days.

What I would check first

Proximity to US-24 is the whole trade here. Some streets are quiet and some hear the highway, and that difference does not show up in a listing. Walk it at rush hour. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Pikes Peak Highway is the best primary source on the area.

Briargate

North Colorado Springs · Master-planned
District 20 and everything within reach
6,400 ftElevation
District 20Schools
1980s+Built
30 minTo Woodland Park

Living here

Briargate is the large master-planned area on the north side of Colorado Springs, and for many families it is shorthand for Academy District 20 schools. Shopping, medical and the interstate are all close, and the housing stock spans several decades of construction, which matters more than the neighborhood name.

What I would check first

Confirm the actual school assignment for the specific address rather than the area, because boundaries move. Check which HOA or metro district the property falls under and what it costs. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Academy School District 20 is the best primary source on the area.

Wolf Ranch

North Colorado Springs · Newer build
Newer construction around a lake and trails
6,700 ftElevation
District 20Schools
2000s+Built
MetroDistrict financing

Living here

Wolf Ranch is one of the newer master-planned communities on the north edge of the Springs, built around a lake, a trail network and neighborhood parks. Much of the inventory is recent construction, and there is usually a live builder presence alongside the resale market.

What I would check first

New construction and resale compete directly here, and the builder's incentives can make a resale look overpriced on paper. Understand the metro district mill levy before you compare a payment here with one up the pass. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Academy School District 20 is the best primary source on the area.

Built around a community center

District 20Schools
2006+Built
ClubCommunity center

Living here

Cordera was planned around a community center and an active events calendar, which is genuinely the thing residents talk about. Trails connect the neighborhoods and the elementary school sits inside the community, so a lot of daily life happens without getting in the car.

What I would check first

The community center and the programming are funded through the association, so read what the dues cover and what they have done over the last few years. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Academy School District 20 is the best primary source on the area.

Flying Horse

North Colorado Springs · Golf
Golf, club and the top of the north-side market
7,000 ftElevation
District 20Schools
2004+Built
ClubGolf and athletics

Living here

Flying Horse is the highest price band on the north side, built around a golf course and a private club with athletics and dining. Custom and semi-custom homes dominate, and the views back toward the Front Range are the reason many of the lots command what they do.

What I would check first

Club membership structures and what they cost are a separate question from the house, and they vary. Lot position relative to the course and the ridgeline drives a lot of the price difference between otherwise similar homes. The Buyer's Guide covers the full checklist, and Academy School District 20 is the best primary source on the area.

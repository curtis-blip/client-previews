Three luxury communities Michael knows by name.

Three neighborhoods where Michael has done the most work in the luxury tier, and where he can walk you through floor plans, lots, and HOA quirks before you ever pull up to a gate.

The River Club

Suwanee's flagship private community, built around a Greg Norman-designed golf course set along the Chattahoochee River. Gated, ultra-private, and consistently the address luxury buyers ask Michael about first. Homes here typically trade in the mid $1M range up through $5M+, on estate lots with mature landscaping and meaningful frontage.

View Suwanee

Laurel Springs

A Cumming staple known for its Jack Nicklaus signature golf course, 24-hour gated security, resort-style amenities, and family-luxury balance. Most luxury inventory here moves in the mid $700s to north of $2M. For buyers who want country-club living without trading school district or commute, Laurel Springs is the short list.

View Cumming

Edinburgh

A quieter Suwanee neighbor to The River Club, Edinburgh trades on large lots, mature trees, and a classic golf-course-community feel. Architecture leans traditional rather than trend-driven, which is exactly what its repeat buyers come back for. Luxury homes generally land in the mid $700s to around $2M depending on lot, finish, and view.

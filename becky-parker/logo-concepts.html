Logo Concepts · For Review

Becky Parker · Logo Directions

Four typographic directions, each rendered on light and dark backgrounds and shown at display, header, and small (favicon) sizes. All use the brand system: Montserrat / Inter, teal-green #1f514c, beige-gold #dac5a7. Pick a direction (or mix elements) and I'll refine into a final lockup.

Concept 01

Stacked Wordmark

Classic, low-risk, reads premium
Display · 220px
Header · 140px
Favicon · 48px (BP)
Why this works: stacked-wordmark is the LP-canon default for high-end residential brokers (Jade Mills, Compass agents). Reads premium, scales cleanly from business card to billboard, and the gold hairline gives Becky's brand color a moment without overpowering the type. Tradeoff: low personality — relies on context (the rest of the site) to feel distinctly her.
Concept 02

Inline Wordmark with Gold Bar

Horizontal, modern, header-friendly
Display · 280px
Header · 180px
Favicon · 48px (BP)
Why this works: the gold vertical bar gives a clean separator between brand and credential. Wide-format reads great in a header, business card, and email signature. Title and subtitle can be edited (e.g. "REALTOR · 18 YEARS" or "REALTOR · BROKER LICENSED 2007") without re-doing the mark. Tradeoff: needs horizontal space — doesn't reduce well to a tiny square unless the BP monogram favicon variant is used separately.
Concept 03

Monogram Badge + Wordmark

Personality, scales to favicon naturally
Display · 280px
Header · 180px
Favicon · 48px (badge only)
Why this works: the BP badge functions as a standalone mark (favicon, social avatar, app icon, listing watermark) while the full lockup carries the brand name. "Since 2007" inside the badge anchors her 18-year credibility without needing extra copy. Tradeoff: two-element lockup means slightly more design surface; better suited to a brand-confident execution.
Concept 04

Initial-Led with Regional Lockup

Statement, distinctive, scales aspect ratios
Display · 280px
Header · 180px
Favicon · 48px (B alone)
Why this works: the large gold B carries instantly-recognizable personality. The full lockup wraps regional + credential context cleanly. Favicon strips down to just the B, which becomes a memorable single-letter mark across business cards, social avatars, and yard signs. Tradeoff: highest personality / lowest neutrality — works great for a single-agent brand, would look out of place if Becky later joined a team.
Next Steps

Pick a Direction, or Mix Elements.

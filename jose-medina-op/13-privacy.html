Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly through the forms on this site. That typically includes your name, email address, phone number, a property address if you request a home value, and whatever you write in a message field. We also collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring site.

How We Use It

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, send property information or market updates you requested, prepare a home value estimate, and improve the site. If you ask for listing alerts, we use your criteria to send matching properties.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

Service Providers

We share information with providers who help operate this business, including our IDX listing provider, our customer relationship management system, email delivery services, and website analytics. These providers only receive what they need to perform their function. When a transaction moves forward, information may be shared with lenders, title companies, and other transaction partners with your knowledge.

Cookies And Analytics

This site uses cookies to keep the site working properly and to understand how visitors use it. Most browsers let you refuse cookies or alert you when one is set. Refusing cookies may limit some site functionality, including saved property searches.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in any message. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email jose@vantegic.com and we will respond.

Text Messages

If you give us your phone number, we may contact you by call or text about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Privacy questions can go to jose@vantegic.com, (719) 302-0602, or 1755 Telstar Dr #300, Colorado Springs, CO 80920. You can also use the contact page.

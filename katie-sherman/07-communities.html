Island towns, a downtown on the bay, beach towns to the south and new villages inland, each with a page of its own.

Choosing a town

Twelve Towns Between Anna Maria Island and Englewood

Ryan and Katie sell homes in twelve towns across Sarasota and Manatee counties. Some sit on barrier islands with the Gulf on one side and a bay on the other; others are inland, built around lakes, ballparks and town centers.

Each town has its own page: daily life there, the schools by district, the drive times and the homes for sale now. Holmes Beach, Bird Key, Palmer Ranch and the other neighborhoods buyers ask about by name are covered on their town's page.

Neighborhoods

Holmes Beach, Bird Key, Palmer Ranch and Other Neighborhoods

Buyers often search for these by name. Each one is part of a larger town, so its homes and daily life are on that town's page.

Anna Maria Island

  • Holmes Beach, incorporated in 1950 on land the Cobb family homesteaded in 1896
  • Bradenton Beach, where Bridge Street and the Historic Old Town District sit at the old bridge's landing
  • Anna Maria Island homes and daily life

Lido Key and St. Armands

  • Bird Key, joined to the mainland by the bridge the Ringlings had built in 1926
  • Lido Shores, developed around 1950 by Philip Hanson Hiss, home of Paul Rudolph's 1953 Umbrella House
  • Lido Key and St. Armands homes and daily life

Sarasota

  • Gulf Gate and West of the Trail, two of the neighborhoods Katie points buyers to
  • Palmer Ranch, where Culverhouse Nature Park opens onto the Legacy Trail
  • Sarasota homes and daily life

Nokomis

  • Casey Key, reached by Albee Road from the south or a historic one-lane swing bridge at Blackburn Point
  • Nokomis Beach, Sarasota County's oldest public beach, is on Casey Key
  • Nokomis homes and daily life

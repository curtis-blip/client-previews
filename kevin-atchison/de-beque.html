Living in De Beque, CO

The far west end of the corridor, where Garfield County hands over to Mesa County. A few hundred people, wild horses on the bench above town, and the cheapest ground I can put you on.

00 Orientation

De Beque is the cheapest ground on the corridor, and it is cheap for a reason

A few hundred people at the point where Garfield County hands over to Mesa County, with canyon country in every direction and wild horses on the bench above town. There is a school and very little else, which is the point. Grand Junction is about thirty minutes west and Parachute about fifteen east, and I-70 through De Beque Canyon is the only route either way, so weather matters more here than it does further up the valley. If small is what you want, nothing else I sell comes close on price. If it is only the price you want, be honest with yourself about the distance before you buy.

01 Before you offer

What I would check for you

De Beque is where Garfield County hands over to Mesa County, and the price reflects how far it is from everything. Here is what I check.

Access, before anything else

On the parcels up the creeks this is the first question, not a detail. County road, BLM access or private easement, and what that means in March.

Water, and whether there is any

A permitted well, a shared system or hauled water are three very different propositions. Confirm which one before you price the land.

What the parcel can actually hold

A building envelope, a septic design and a power run all have to work before raw ground becomes a house. Price the whole project, not the lot.

Mineral rights and energy activity

A long energy history here. Severed minerals are the norm rather than the exception and the title work deserves real attention.

Distance, honestly

Grand Junction is about thirty minutes and Parachute about fifteen. Everything else is further. That is the trade you are making for the price.

Resale

Thin market, few buyers, longer timelines on the way back out. I will tell you what that looks like before you buy.

02 Day to day

Good to know

Schools

De Beque School District 49JT serves the town and runs its own school. The district does not maintain a public website, so confirm boundaries and enrolment with the school or the county directly.

Mesa County

Everyday

Very little in town by design. Parachute is about fifteen minutes east and Grand Junction about thirty minutes west.

Town of De Beque

Getting around

I-70 runs through De Beque Canyon, which is the only route in either direction and does close in bad weather. Check conditions before you commit to a showing in winter.

CDOT road conditions

The wild horses

The Little Book Cliffs Wild Horse Range sits on the bench above town, one of a small number of ranges of its kind in the country, managed by the BLM.

Living in Buford

On the Southern Shore of Lake Lanier

Buford is a city in northern Gwinnett County with a northern portion in Hall County, on the southern shore of Lake Lanier.

It was named for Algernon Sidney Buford, president of the Atlanta and Richmond Air-Line Railroad, and was incorporated as the Town of Buford on August 24, 1872 before becoming the City of Buford in 1896. The city runs its own school system, and Lake Lanier is its primary water supply.

01

Where Buford Sits and Who Runs What

Buford straddles two counties: most of the city is in Gwinnett County and a northern portion is in Hall County. It sits on the southern shore of Lake Lanier, and the lake is its primary water supply. Buford runs an independent city school system, Buford City Schools, for residents inside the city limits, while Gwinnett County Public Schools and Hall County Schools serve addresses outside the limits.

For a specific address, the county and school system can be confirmed with each district's locator.

02

Downtown Buford and Gathering Places

Buford grew along the railroad and became known for leather, and the Bona Allen Company was a major part of that industry. The Buford Community Center at 2200 Buford Highway houses the Sylvia Beard Theatre, the Buford Museum and the Town Park Amphitheatre, and the city lists Main Street Park and Amphitheater at 400 East Main Street and the Garnett Street Walking Trail at 9 New Street.

The Bona Allen Mansion at 395 Main Street was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1983, and the Bona Allen Shoe and Horse Collar Factory is also listed. The Tannery Row Artist Colony has used a former Bona Allen shoe factory building since 2003.

What to Check Before You Buy in Buford

A few questions are worth settling before you write an offer on a Buford home.

Sarah's Buyer's Guide

School system
Buford City Schools serves homes inside the city limits, while Gwinnett County Public Schools and Hall County Schools serve addresses outside them. Confirm which side of the line a home is on.
Two counties
Most of Buford is in Gwinnett County and a northern portion is in Hall County, so the county, its tax bill and its services depend on where a home sits.
Homestead exemption
Once you own and live in the home, file for the homestead exemption with the county the home is in, Gwinnett or Hall; Georgia's deadline is April 1.
Lake lots and docks
On any Lake Lanier lot, the shoreline is managed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, and a private dock needs a Corps permit. Ask whether a lake lot already has one.

Questions

Questions About Living in Buford

Which county is Buford in?

Most of Buford is in Gwinnett County, and a northern portion is in Hall County.

Which school district serves Buford?

Buford City Schools, an independent city school system, serves residents inside the city limits. Gwinnett County Public Schools and Hall County Schools serve addresses outside the city limits.

How far is Buford from the airport?

By road from city hall it is about 48 miles to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport and about 37 miles to downtown Atlanta, mostly on I-985 and I-85.

Does Buford have a downtown?

Yes. Buford has a historic downtown district, with the Buford Community Center and Town Park, Main Street Park and Amphitheater and the Garnett Street Walking Trail nearby.

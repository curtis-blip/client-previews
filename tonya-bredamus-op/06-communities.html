My Approach

How I Help You Pick the Right Community.

Each of these towns has a different rhythm. Martinsville is small and walkable with serious community pride. Danville has comeback energy and a riverfront. Stuart is mountain-quiet with no zoning anywhere in Patrick County. Rocky Mount has the Harvester and the Blue Ridge. Smith Mountain Lake is a lifestyle, not a town.

When we talk about which one fits you, we'll cover the obvious stuff (price, schools, commute) and the less obvious stuff (how loud, how social, how rural, what's open on a Sunday morning). I won't recommend a community I don't know personally , I host an ADTV episode about each one.

A Day in the Life

Morning coffee in Uptown, Saturday at the farmers market, seasonal festivals on the courthouse green. Friday night football is genuinely the whole town. Weekends spent at Philpott Dam or the Smith River. The kind of place where business owners actually know your name.

What You'll Find

Historic architecture in Uptown, mid-century neighborhoods radiating out, and newer construction in the surrounding county. Lot sizes vary from compact in-town to several acres outside the city. Affordable relative to most of Virginia, especially compared to Roanoke or the lake.

A Day in the Life

Walk the Dan River trail in the morning, lunch in the River District at a renovated warehouse, an evening at Caesars Virginia. Year-round community events along the riverfront. Boutique shops and locally owned restaurants in beautifully restored brick buildings.

Historic architecture downtown, loft apartments in renovated warehouses, established residential neighborhoods, and newer construction in the county. The River District is the cultural anchor. Danville is a comeback city with serious economic momentum.

A Day in the Life

Coffee at a local diner around the historic Patrick County Courthouse. Drive the Blue Ridge Parkway in the afternoon. Stop by a family-owned shop on Main Street. Seasonal festivals on the courthouse green. The kind of place where life genuinely slows down.

What You'll Find

Mountain homes for sale across Patrick County with serious acreage. The county has no zoning anywhere, which means rural flexibility , keep horses, build a workshop, run a small farm. Includes Stuart, Critz, and Woolwine. Worth knowing if you want mountain land without restrictions.

A Day in the Life

Morning coffee on the dock, paddle in the quiet cove before the wind picks up, lunch at a waterfront restaurant, sunset boat ride. Boating, fishing, kayaking, paddleboarding all part of normal weekends. SML residents know life is better at the lake.

What You'll Find

Waterfront homes with private docks, quiet coves perfect for kayaking, open water ideal for boating. Over 500 miles of shoreline. With the Blue Ridge as backdrop, scenery is peaceful year-round. Fall colors reflect off the water, summers are lively and social, winters are calm and cozy.

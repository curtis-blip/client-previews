Patrick County, Virginia

Living in Stuart.

Peaceful mountain living with deep roots and real community connection. Where life slows down and people genuinely look out for each other.

The Town

The Blue Ridge Mountains Frame Everything.

Stuart is located in beautiful Patrick County and sits near the Blue Ridge Mountains, offering incredible scenery all year-round. Downtown Stuart is centered around the historic Patrick County Courthouse and classic Main Street charm. Local diners, family-owned shops, seasonal festivals and community gatherings.

One thing buyers should know about Patrick County: there is no zoning anywhere in the county. That means rural acreage with serious flexibility. If you want to keep horses, build a workshop, run a small farm, or just have neighbors who are far enough away, Patrick County is your answer.

The county includes Stuart, Critz, and Woolwine. All worth knowing if you're searching for mountain land.

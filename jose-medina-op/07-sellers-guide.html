The Selling Process

How Selling Works, Step By Step

Eight steps, in order, so you always know what happens next. Selling a home in Colorado Springs is not complicated when someone runs the process instead of reacting to it.

The Listing Consultation

We start at your kitchen table, not on a spreadsheet. I walk the home, ask what you are trying to accomplish and by when, then show you exactly what my listing and marketing program will do for this specific property. You leave that meeting knowing the plan, the timeline, and the numbers.

Pricing Your Home With Data

Price is the single biggest lever you control. I build a comparative market analysis from recent, genuinely comparable Pikes Peak region sales, then position your home where it draws the most qualified attention in the opening two weeks, when buyer interest runs highest. Overpricing costs sellers more than any repair ever will.

Prep, Repairs, And Presentation

Not every repair earns its money back. I walk the house room by room and tell you what buyers in this market actually respond to, what to fix, what to leave alone, and where one weekend of work changes the offer. My team coordinates the vendors so you are not chasing contractors.

04

Photography And Marketing Launch

Professional photography, video, and floor plans are the baseline, not the upgrade. My marketing specialist builds the full campaign before the home ever goes live, so the listing launches everywhere at once instead of trickling out over a week.

photo Vantegic sign going up in front of a Springs home · 4:5
05

Live On The PPMLS

Your home goes active on the Pikes Peak REALTOR® Services Corp MLS and syndicates out to the major search portals and my own buyer database. Showings start immediately, and every inquiry routes to a real person on my team, not a voicemail box.

photo Buyers touring an open, well lit home · 4:5
06

Showings And Real Feedback

You get told the truth, not comfortable news. We track showing activity and agent feedback week by week and review it with you on a set schedule, so if something needs to change, price, presentation, or access, we catch it early instead of after the listing has gone stale.

photo Jose reviewing offer paperwork with sellers · 4:5
07

Offers And Negotiation

An offer is more than a number. I lay out net proceeds, financing strength, inspection and appraisal terms, and closing dates side by side, then negotiate hard on the terms that actually protect your equity. This is the part of the transaction where two decades in this market pays for itself.

Inspection, Appraisal, And Closing

Under contract is not sold. My transaction coordinator tracks every deadline, the inspection resolution, the appraisal, the loan milestones, and the title work, while my lending and title partners keep the file moving. You show up to the closing table with no surprises.

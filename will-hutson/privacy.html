Privacy Policy

How The Hutson Group at Century 21 Results collects, uses, and protects the information you share when you use this website.

Last updated: [PLACEHOLDER, attorney review pending]

This Privacy Policy explains how The Hutson Group at Century 21 Results ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit our website or contact our North Georgia team. We respect your privacy and only collect what we need to help you buy or sell a home.

This policy is a standard template provided for review by the firm's attorney before publication. It is written to be generally accurate for a Georgia real estate brokerage team and does not replace legal advice.

01Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways when you use the Site:

Information you give us

  • Contact details you submit through a form, a home valuation request, or a saved-search sign-up, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Details about what you are looking for, such as your price range, preferred counties or cities, and notes you include in a message.
  • Anything else you choose to send us when you call, text, or email the team.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data, such as your browser type, operating system, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Your approximate location and the website that referred you, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

We do not ask for sensitive information like a Social Security number through this website. If a transaction later requires it, that is handled separately through secure channels.

02How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and follow up about buying or selling a home in North Georgia.
  • Send the listings, saved searches, and home valuation results you request.
  • Provide and improve the Site, understand which pages are useful, and fix problems.
  • Send updates or marketing messages about our services when you have asked to hear from us.
  • Meet our legal, tax, and recordkeeping obligations as a licensed Georgia brokerage team.

03Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Like most websites, this Site uses cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the Site remember your preferences, keep you signed in to an IDX search, and measure how the Site is used. Some cookies are set by us, and some are set by the services we rely on, such as the IDX search platform and website analytics.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you turn cookies off, parts of the Site, including the property search, may not work the way they should.

04How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • With service providers who run parts of the Site for us, such as website hosting, the Showcase IDX platform, our customer relationship management (CRM) system, and email or text messaging tools.
  • With a lender or other partner only when you ask us to connect you, or otherwise give your consent.
  • With our brokerage, Century 21 Results, and members of our team who are helping with your transaction.
  • When the law requires it, such as responding to a valid legal request, or to protect our rights, our clients, or the public.

The providers we work with are only allowed to use your information to perform services for us.

05Your Choices and Rights

You have control over your information:

  • You can opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in any message, and stop text messages by replying STOP.
  • You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings at any time.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and we will honor that request as the law allows.

To make a request, contact our team using the details at the bottom of this page.

06Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No website or method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, though, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please be careful about the information you send through online forms, and contact us by phone if a matter is sensitive.

07Third-Party Links

The Site may link to other websites and tools that we do not operate, including lender pages, social media profiles, and the IDX search platform. This Privacy Policy applies only to this Site. When you follow a link to another company, that company's privacy practices and policies govern, and we encourage you to read them.

08Children's Privacy

This Site is meant for adults shopping for or selling a home, and it is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or the law change. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be noted on the Site. Please review this page from time to time so you know how your information is handled.

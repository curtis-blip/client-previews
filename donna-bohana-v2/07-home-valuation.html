Common Questions

Home Valuation FAQ

What is a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)? ▾
A CMA is a detailed report that compares your home to similar properties that have recently sold, are currently on the market, or were listed but did not sell in your area. Donna uses local transaction data, property condition, location specifics, and current market trends to determine the most accurate price range for your home.
How is Donna's valuation different from a Zillow Zestimate? ▾
Automated valuation models like Zillow's Zestimate use algorithms based on public data. They cannot account for ocean view quality, lot positioning, renovation quality, fire zone status, or the unique characteristics that drive Malibu pricing. Donna's valuations are built on 20+ years of local expertise and personal knowledge of comparable properties, delivering a far more accurate picture of your home's value.
How long does it take to receive my home valuation? ▾
Most valuations are delivered within 24 to 48 hours of receiving your property details. Complex properties or unique situations may take slightly longer as Donna researches additional comparable sales and market factors to ensure accuracy.
What factors affect my Malibu home's value? ▾
Key factors include location and proximity to the beach, ocean view quality and angle, lot size, home condition and recent renovations, fire zone designation, Coastal Commission restrictions, school district, neighborhood character, and current market supply and demand. Donna evaluates all of these factors when preparing your valuation.
Is the home valuation really free? ▾
Yes. Donna provides complimentary home valuations with no obligation to list your property. It is part of the boutique service approach at Solstice International Realty. Whether you are considering selling now or simply want to understand your home's current market position, the valuation is yours at no cost.
What should I expect after I request a valuation? ▾
After you submit your request, Donna will review the property details and may follow up with a brief phone call to confirm specific features. She will then prepare a detailed CMA report that includes comparable sales, market positioning, and a recommended price range. You will receive this report via email along with an invitation to discuss the findings.

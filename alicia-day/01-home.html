02 / The Agent

Alicia Day

Twenty years on the numbers before the houses

Twenty years in accounting before real estate, which changes the order of the conversation. Most agents show you the house and work out the money afterwards. Alicia starts with what the month actually looks like once you own it.

She holds a BSBA and works the ten counties of the Altamaha basin and out to the coast, alongside Randy Day and Kathy Rogers. Between them they cover showings, listings and the paperwork that keeps a contract from falling apart in week three.

Buying or selling a house is not a transaction to her clients. It is the largest financial decision most of them will make. She treats it that way, and she says the honest thing about a house even when the honest thing loses her the sale.

Home value

What is my home worth?

Type your address for an instant estimate. When you want a number to list at, Dave walks the house and prices it himself.

What comes back

An instant estimate, then a price from Dave

The estimator returns an estimated value for your address, a likely range and a confidence score, with the home's facts on record. It asks for nothing but the address.

It is a computer's read of the records on file, so it cannot see the kitchen you redid, the roof you replaced or the view from the back porch. That is where Dave comes in: he walks through the house, prices it against the homes that have actually sold around it, and tells you which repairs would change the number.

Ask for that private valuation from the estimate's report, or call Dave at (970) 261-3266.

This evaluation was prepared by a licensed real estate broker and is not an appraisal. This evaluation cannot be used for the purposes of obtaining financing.

How Dave prices a home

Priced against what has actually sold

Dave's number starts in the house, not on a screen.

He walks it with you and says plainly which repairs would pay for themselves in the price and which would not. Then he prices it against the homes that have sold around it, and sets the plan for preparing and presenting it at the same time.

If a sale is on your mind, that price comes with the plan to reach it: the work list, the photography, drone, video and virtual tour, and the launch.

Questions

Home value questions

How accurate is the instant estimate?

It is a starting point. The estimator reads the records on file for your address and returns a value, a range and a confidence score, and the score tells you how sure it is. It cannot see updates, condition or the view, which is why Dave walks the house before he puts a price on it.

Why might the estimate come back with no number?

A new build, or a home with little on record, can leave the estimator with nothing to go on. When that happens it says so and offers Dave's own valuation instead.

Is the county's value the same as a sale price?

No. The Mesa County Assessor's values are set for property tax. A sale price comes from what buyers pay for homes like yours now, and that is what Dave prices against. Look up the assessor record.

Will someone call me after I get an estimate?

Only if you ask. The estimate's report has a short form for a private valuation; send it and Dave gets in touch to set a time to see the house.

Can I use Dave's valuation for a loan or a refinance?

No. This evaluation was prepared by a licensed real estate broker and is not an appraisal. This evaluation cannot be used for the purposes of obtaining financing.

A Pool, Courts and Clubhouse Run by Neighbors

The CRC Homeowners Association runs its pool, clubhouse, tennis, pond and playground through volunteer committees, with a management company handling billing and requests.

The association's own site goes by CRC, and its swim team practices at the neighborhood pool. Mail goes under Cumming, and the homes are in Forsyth County. Chattahoochee River Club in four chapters

01 / 04

Neighbors on the Board, a Firm in the Office

The CRC Homeowners Association is run by volunteers, with committees for the pool, clubhouse, tennis, pond and playground. Access Management Group handles the day-to-day: its owner portal takes payments, clubhouse reservations, architectural requests and service requests.

The association's website has the board contact and the links into the portal.

02 / 04

Summer Swim and ALTA Tennis

The neighborhood pool is home to the CRC Stingrays, a summer team in the Atlanta Swim Association since 2008. The swim team page has the season details.

Tennis has its own committee, and teams from the neighborhood play in ALTA in the spring and fall.

03 / 04

Windermere Parkway and the County Park

River Club Drive meets Windermere Parkway, and Overlake Drive runs off River Club Drive. Forsyth County's Windermere Park is at 3355 Windermere Parkway.

The mailing address is Cumming, so homes for sale in Cumming is the wider page. Other South Forsyth neighborhoods are Olde Atlanta Club and Laurel Springs.

04 / 04

Looking for Homes on the Water?

The Chattahoochee River runs along this side of the county, so ask about water access for each home rather than going by the community's name. Homes on Lake Lanier are on a separate Team Giarraffa site, homeonlakelanier.com.

County records show most homes here were built from 1997 to 2004. The Georgia buying guide, the first-time buyer programs and the property tax guide cover the next steps.

Questions

Chattahoochee River Club FAQ

Is Chattahoochee River Club inside the Cumming city limits?

The mailing address is Cumming and the association places the community in Forsyth County. A Cumming mailing address does not show whether a home is inside the city limits, so check a specific address with the county.

Where do I confirm schools for Chattahoochee River Club?

With Forsyth County Schools, the public district for Forsyth County. Ask the district which campuses serve an address, since assignments can change.

Does the name mean river access?

The name does not tell you whether a home has water access, so ask about each home. Homes on Lake Lanier are on a separate site, homeonlakelanier.com.

Who handles clubhouse reservations and requests?

The association's website routes them through Access Management Group's owner portal, which takes clubhouse reservations, architectural requests and service requests. The board has its own contact on the same site.

Lakewood Ranch at a glance

A Master-Planned Community Across Two Counties

Lakewood Ranch is an unincorporated community that spans Manatee and Sarasota counties, built by Schroeder-Manatee Ranch since 1994. It was second in the nation for new home sales in 2025 and the top-selling multigenerational master-planned community in the US (RCLCO, 2025).

The Uihlein family, original owners of the Schlitz Brewery, have owned the land since the early 1900s, when it held cattle, citrus groves, tree and turf farms and aggregate mines. The Sarasota Polo Club came in 1991, and in February 1994 the family's company, Schroeder-Manatee Ranch, moved into building the community, which it runs through LWR Communities and LWR Realty.

01

Waterside Place and the Sunday farmers' market

Waterside Place is a town center on Kingfisher Lake, in the Waterside village, with restaurants, shops and events through the week, among them Ranch Nite Wednesday and live music. The Farmers’ Market at Lakewood Ranch sets up there every Sunday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., all year, with more than 100 vendors, morning yoga at Waterside Park and a Kids’ Zone at The Yard. Waterside Park itself is an island park on the lake, reached by a footbridge, with volleyball courts, a splash park, a playground, hammocks and an event lawn.

02

Main Street, The Green and the Publix plazas

The Ranch has three town centers. Main Street at Lakewood Ranch is an open-air street of cafes, boutiques and restaurants with Lakewood Ranch Cinemas and the information center, and it hosts concerts and festivals through the year, among them Music on Main. The Green, nearest the northern villages, has a fitness center, a specialty grocery, shops, restaurants and apartments. Seven neighborhood plazas anchored by Publix cover the weekly errands.

03

Polo, soccer fields and a stadium

The Sarasota Polo Club has played here since 1991, before the first homes, on seven Bermuda grass fields. The Premier Sports Campus has 23 FIFA regulation fields and a stadium with more than 3,000 seats, home to the Sarasota Paradise soccer club.

04

Parks, lakes and nature trails

The parks are built around the lakes and wetlands. Bob Gardner Community Park, on White Eagle Boulevard, runs about a mile between wetland preserves, with walking paths, soft trails, sports courts, disc golf, pavilions and dog parks. James L. Patton Park circles Lake Patton and has a canoe launch, Heron’s Nest Nature Park opens onto Heron Lake and Summerfield Lake for fishing and bird-watching, and Braden River Nature Park loops around Trophy Lake, known for bass, and Otter Lake, which is catch and release.

05

Villages, new homes and two school districts

Ryan and Katie find that each village has its own feel, and for buying from a builder there is New Construction. Because the Ranch spans two counties, the address decides the school district: the Manatee County School District serves the Manatee County side, which is most of the Ranch, and Sarasota County Schools serves the Sarasota County side. Lakewood Ranch is unincorporated, so county rules apply, from Manatee County or Sarasota County depending on which side of the line a home sits.

Questions

Lakewood Ranch FAQ

Is Lakewood Ranch a city?

No. Lakewood Ranch is an unincorporated community that spans Manatee and Sarasota counties, so the county a home sits in provides services and sets the rules. Schroeder-Manatee Ranch, the developer, runs the community through LWR Communities and LWR Realty.

Which school district serves Lakewood Ranch?

Two do. The Manatee County School District serves the Manatee County side, which is most of the Ranch, and Sarasota County Schools serves the Sarasota County side. Confirm the school for any address with the district.

How did Lakewood Ranch rank for new home sales?

Lakewood Ranch was second in the nation for new home sales in 2025 and the top-selling multigenerational master-planned community in the US (RCLCO, 2025). RCLCO ranks master-planned communities by new home contracts, net of cancellations.

When is the Lakewood Ranch farmers' market?

Every Sunday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., all year, at Waterside Place, with more than 100 vendors.

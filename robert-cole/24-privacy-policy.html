Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026. This policy explains what The Cole Home Team collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

The Cole Home Team is a real estate team operating in North Texas, with an office at 1091 Lone Star Blvd, Terrell, TX 75160. You can reach us at (469) 260-1550 or mandy@thecolehometeam.com.

Information we collect

We collect two kinds of information.

Information you give us. When you submit a contact form, request a home valuation, save a property search or create a search account, we collect what you enter: your name, email address, telephone number, the property address you asked about, and anything you write in a message field.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours records IP address, browser and device type, referring page, the pages you view and how long you spend on them. This comes from cookies and similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To respond to you, which is the main reason we ask.
  • To send property matches and saved-search alerts you have asked for.
  • To prepare a home valuation you requested.
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations as licensed real estate agents in Texas.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful, in aggregate.

We do not sell your personal information.

Calls, texts and email

If you give us your telephone number through a form on this site, you are agreeing that we may contact you by phone, text message and email about your enquiry and about properties that may interest you. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. Opting out of marketing does not stop us contacting you about a transaction you are actively part of.

Who we share it with

We share information with service providers who help us run the business, and only so far as they need it:

  • MLS and IDX providers. Listing data on this site comes from the North Texas Real Estate Information Systems (NTREIS) MLS. If you create a search account, your saved searches and favourites are held by the IDX provider that powers it.
  • Customer relationship management. Enquiries are routed into our CRM so that they reach the right agent and nothing gets missed.
  • Analytics and hosting. Providers who host the site and give us aggregate traffic data.
  • Other parties in a transaction. If you go under contract, we share what is necessary with the title company, lender, inspectors and the other side's agent.
  • Where the law requires it. Including our obligations under Texas real estate licensing rules.

Cookies

Cookies keep you logged in to a search account, remember your saved properties, and let us measure traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Parts of the property search will stop working properly if you do.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email mandy@thecolehometeam.com and we will respond. Some information we are required to keep for a period under Texas real estate record-keeping rules, and we will tell you if that applies.

Texas residents, and residents of states with comprehensive privacy statutes, may have additional rights including the right to opt out of certain processing. Contact us using the details above to exercise them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No website or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-party links

This site links to other organisations, including city websites, school districts, appraisal districts and the Texas Real Estate Commission. Their privacy practices are their own, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be flagged on the site.

Contact

Questions about this policy: mandy@thecolehometeam.com, (469) 260-1550, or 1091 Lone Star Blvd, Terrell, TX 75160.

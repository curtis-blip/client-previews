Why Ask a Local Broker

Better than a Zillow estimate.

A Zillow Zestimate uses an algorithm that doesn't know your kitchen was just redone, that the lot floods, or that the house next door went over asking last week. It's an average pulled from public data, and averages miss the things that actually move price.

A real CMA from a local broker accounts for actual recent comps, current condition, and what's happening in your specific Dublin GA neighborhood right now. That's the difference between a guess and a Dublin GA home valuation you can plan around.

Valuation Questions

What people ask about home valuations.

How accurate is your home valuation?
Much more accurate than an automated estimate, because it accounts for your actual home and the actual current Dublin GA market. The range I give reflects what your home would realistically sell for if listed today, given recent comparable sales and condition.
How is this different from Zillow's Zestimate?
Zillow's algorithm averages broad data and can't see inside your home. My valuation pulls real recent neighborhood sales, considers condition, and reflects what's selling right now, not what sold six months ago in a different price climate.
Is there any obligation to list with you after?
Zero obligation. The valuation is free and yours. Plenty of homeowners use mine just to understand where they stand. If you decide to list, I'd love the conversation. If not, that's fine too.
How long does it take to get my valuation?
Within 48 hours in almost every case. If your situation is more complex, I'll let you know upfront and give you a real timeline.
Do you actually visit the home?
For most valuations I can do a strong analysis from the address, photos, and quick details. If you want a more precise number, I'm happy to walk through the home in person. Either is free.
What information do you need from me?
Address, basic property details (beds/baths, approx square footage, home type), and a quick note about anything notable (recent renovations, condition issues, special features). The form covers everything I need to start.

What This Is

What Is a Home Value, and Why Does It Matter?

Your home's value is what a real buyer will pay for it on a real date, given the current state of the market. It's not an opinion. It's a calculation grounded in recent comparable sales, your property's condition, and the buyer pool actively shopping right now.

Automated tools like Zillow's Zestimate average regional data. The calculator above gives you a starting point. What I send you when you fill out the form is different: a real number from your specific block, looking at homes that sold within a half-mile in the last 60 days.

How I Do It

How I Determine Your Home's Value.

Four things I look at, in this order, every time.

Recent Local Comparables

Three to five comparable sales within a half-mile radius from the last 60 days. Not regional averages.

Property Condition

Adjustments up or down based on your home's condition versus the comps. Updated kitchen vs original? New roof vs needed roof?

Current Market Dynamics

Days-on-market trends, list-to-sale ratios, interest rates, season. The market moves; the valuation has to be timed to today.

Targeted Buyer Pool

Who's actively shopping for a home like yours right now? Out-of-state relocators? First-time buyers? Downsizers?

Homes For Sale
In Briargate

Briargate is the large master-planned area on the north side of Colorado Springs, and for many families it is shorthand for Academy District 20 schools. Shopping, medical and the interstate are all close, and the housing stock spans several decades of construction, which matters more than the neighborhood name. More on the area, including what it is like day to day, is on the Briargate community page. For reference, Academy School District 20 is the best primary source on the area itself.

Before you buy here: Confirm the actual school assignment for the specific address rather than the area, because boundaries move. Check which HOA or metro district the property falls under and what it costs.

On this side you are weighing builders, covenants and metro district financing. The Buyer's Guide walks through it, and El Paso County Assessor holds the property records.

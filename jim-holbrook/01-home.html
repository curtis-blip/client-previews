Exposure creates competition

Don't just put it on the market. Put it in the spotlight.

Simply putting a home on the MLS isn't a marketing strategy. Competition is what drives a price up, and competition comes from exposure: more qualified buyers seeing your home, sooner, at its best.

  1. 01

    Presentation

    Staging advice, then professional photography, so the first showing happens online and goes well.

  2. 02

    Pricing

    Priced against the homes buyers will actually compare it with, and adjusted on evidence, not hope.

  3. 03

    Exposure

    Online and offline marketing and targeted advertising, aimed at the buyers most likely to pay.

  4. 04

    Negotiation

    Proactive outreach to buyers and their agents, then Jim negotiates every offer himself.

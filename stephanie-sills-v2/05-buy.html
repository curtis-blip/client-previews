Buying Here

Why Buying on the Coastside Is Different

Buying a home on the San Mateo County Coastside offers a lifestyle that is difficult to replicate elsewhere in the Bay Area, within commuting distance of San Francisco and Silicon Valley.

It also comes with considerations of its own. California disclosure packages are extensive, coastal properties may have environmental factors to review, and competition for well-located homes can be strong. Homes range from oceanfront estates and hillside properties with sweeping views to beach cottages and rural parcels in the Santa Cruz Mountains.

Start with the towns: Half Moon Bay, El Granada, Montara, Moss Beach and Pacifica.

Costs to Plan For

Property Taxes and Insurance on the Coastside

Property Tax

Your tax bill resets to the price you pay: Proposition 13 sets the base on the purchase price, plus any voter-approved bonds and local assessments. San Mateo County then sends a supplemental bill for the rest of the fiscal year.

Look up a home's current assessment with the San Mateo County Assessor.

Homeowners Insurance

Get a quote during your inspection period, not the week before closing. Some coastal and hillside homes end up on the California FAIR Plan with a second policy for what it leaves out.

Stephanie wrote about it in detail: Wildfire Risk and Your Home.

Questions

Buying a Home on the Coastside: Questions

Can I see homes before they reach Zillow?

Often, yes. Compass Coming Soon homes appear on Compass.com and Redfin.com first, and private exclusives are shared only with Compass agents and their buyers.

Can I buy before I sell my current home?

Often, with bridge financing. Compass Bridge Loan Services can help qualified buyers use the equity in their current home to buy the next one first. See Services.

How does a buyer pay their agent?

Since August 2024, buyers sign a written agreement with their agent before touring, and it sets out how the agent is paid. Stephanie explains the options at your first meeting.

What is in a California disclosure package?

Usually the seller's disclosure statements, a natural hazard report, any inspections the seller ordered and the property's permit history. Stephanie reads them with you before you write an offer.

How long does buying take?

Escrow on a financed purchase usually runs about a month. The search before it depends on how many homes fit what you want; the home search shows what is available now.

The Connection

I Get to Show This Region to a National Audience.

American Dream TV is a nationally syndicated series. Each region has a host who showcases the local communities, businesses, hidden corners, and the people who make the area worth knowing. I'm the host for Southside Virginia.

When I tell a buyer that I know a neighborhood, I really mean it. I've produced a TV episode about most of them.

PhotoTonya on a Martinsville episode shoot
What ADTV Is

A Syndicated Series That Celebrates Local.

American Dream TV is a half-hour series that airs nationally and online, with regional editions hosted by local agents who actually know their markets. The Southside Virginia edition is mine.

Why It Matters

Why ADTV Makes Me a Better Realtor.

When I'm helping a buyer decide between Martinsville and Rocky Mount, or between Patrick County acreage and a Smith Mountain Lake waterfront, I'm not pulling community knowledge from a brochure. I've literally produced a TV episode about each one. I've interviewed the business owners. I've stood in the streets at festival time. That's the level of local knowledge I bring to every real estate conversation.

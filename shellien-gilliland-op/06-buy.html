The Buyer Process

How I Help You Buy.

Eight steps, no surprises.

photo Lender meeting, pre-approval paperwork · 4:5
01

Pre-Approval

Before we tour anything, you talk to a lender and get pre-approved. I can recommend Magic Valley lenders or work with one you already trust. Knowing your real budget shapes everything that follows.

photo Buyer wishlist meeting, notebook + map · 4:5
02

Define Your Magic Valley Wishlist

We talk about what matters: commute, schools, lot size, budget, must-haves, deal-breakers. I'll tell you honestly which Magic Valley towns fit and which don't.

photo Virtual tour video call, laptop walk-through · 4:5
03

Tour Strategy

I build a tour list around your wishlist and your timeline. Out-of-state? Virtual walk-throughs come standard. In-person? We plan efficient tour days that don't waste your weekend.

photo Buyer in-person home tour with agent · 4:5
04

Tour Day

We see homes in person or on video. I point out what's negotiable, what's not, what'll cost you later, and what's a deal. No filler showings, only serious contenders.

photo Buyer reviewing purchase offer paperwork · 4:5
05

Make An Offer

When we find the right home, I walk you through the offer terms. Price, contingencies, financing, closing timeline, earnest money. The strategy depends on the seller, the home, and the market.

photo Realtor on phone negotiating counter-offer · 4:5
06

Negotiation

Counter-offers, inspection negotiations, repair requests. I represent you, not the seller. The goal: best terms, not just best price.

photo Inspector at work, buyer reviewing report · 4:5
07

Inspection And Due Diligence

Schedule the inspection, review the report together, decide what's a real concern versus a normal finding, and negotiate any repairs. Title review, lender follow-up, appraisal coordination.

photo Closing day, keys handed to new buyer · 4:5
08

Closing Day

Final walk-through, signing, funding, keys. My transaction coordinator manages every deadline so closing day is calm, not chaotic. Then we celebrate.

Nokomis at a glance

The County's Oldest Public Beach, and a Swing Bridge to Casey Key

Nokomis Beach, on Casey Key, is Sarasota County's oldest public beach. Its 1950s plaza is the work of Jack West, one of the architects of the Sarasota School.

John Nolen, the planner who laid out Venice, also drew the plan for Nokomis, according to the National Park Service. Nokomis still has no city government of its own; Sarasota County governs it, and the Legacy Trail passes through it.

01

Nokomis Beach and its 1950s plaza

Nokomis Beach, at 100 Casey Key Road, has year-round lifeguards, a boardwalk, a canoe and kayak launch, a bayside boat ramp and dock, picnic shelters, a playground, volleyball and free parking. Jack West designed and built its plaza in the 1950s, and it was renovated in 2008. On Wednesdays and Saturdays, a drum circle gathers at the beach.

02

North Jetty, at the end of the beach

North Jetty sits at the end of Nokomis Beach. Visit Sarasota calls it a popular spot for fishing from the rocks, and when the conditions line up, people surf the small Gulf waves there. Kayak and paddleboard rentals are close by.

03

Casey Key and the Blackburn Point Bridge

Visit Sarasota describes Casey Key as a narrow island known for its privacy, with Nokomis Beach and North Jetty Park as its beaches. You reach it two ways: Albee Road at the south end, near Venice, or Blackburn Point Road at the north, over the Blackburn Point Bridge. The Sarasota County Libraries say the one-lane swing bridge is on the National Register of Historic Places and has been in service for nearly 100 years.

04

The Legacy Trail and Nokomis Community Park

The Legacy Trail runs through Nokomis, and Nokomis Community Park is one of its access points, with grills, a fishing pier, a community garden, a kayak launch, a picnic pavilion, restrooms, and pickleball and tennis courts. From the Nokomis Riverview Park trailhead, the North Port Connector sets off on what Visit Sarasota calls a newly paved, tree-lined trail.

05

Schools and county services in Nokomis

Because Nokomis has no city government, Sarasota County handles zoning, permits and parks, and Sarasota County Schools runs the public schools. Nokomis Beach and Casey Key sit outside that boundary but share the Nokomis mailing address, so check any parcel with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser before you count on a school or a rule.

Questions

Nokomis FAQ

Is Nokomis its own city?

No. Nokomis is unincorporated, so Sarasota County governs it. Nokomis Beach and Casey Key share the Nokomis mailing address but sit outside the Census boundary of Nokomis, so check any parcel with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser.

Which school district serves Nokomis?

Sarasota County Schools. Confirm the school for any address with the district, since attendance zones change.

How do you get onto Casey Key?

Two ways: Albee Road at the south end, near Venice, or Blackburn Point Road at the north end, over the one-lane Blackburn Point swing bridge.

Does Nokomis Beach have lifeguards?

Yes. Visit Sarasota lists year-round lifeguards at Nokomis Beach, along with free parking, restrooms, picnic shelters and a playground.

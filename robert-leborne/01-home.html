Questions

Before you call

Do you only work in San Antonio?

San Antonio is the home market. Boerne, Austin and Houston are worked regularly, and relocation clients arrive from California and Florida, where Robert also holds a license and can handle the sale at the other end.

Can you help me buy a foreclosure, or a HUD or VA home?

Yes. It has been the specialty for three decades. Each type has its own bidding rules and timelines, and Robert walks clients through them before an offer goes in.

How do you help first-time buyers?

Financing first, so you know your real budget. Because Robert holds the mortgage originator credential, that conversation happens with him rather than being handed off.

Do you work with investors?

Yes. Distressed and wholesale property is the core of the investor practice, with curated deals, underwriting support and a clear read on value after repairs.

What does a broker of record actually do?

They are the licensee legally responsible for the transactions under their license. Most agents work under someone else's. Robert holds his own, so the person advising you is the person accountable.

Why does a real estate broker hold an insurance license?

Buying a house is usually the moment someone's financial picture changes. Robert can talk through protection and long-term planning as part of the same conversation instead of handing you to a stranger.

Meet Amy Koch

Thoughtful real estate guidance across Greater Phoenix's most desirable communities.

The Story

From Canada to the Valley

Amy Koch's path to Greater Phoenix real estate started in Canada. Born in Ontario, she grew up in Vancouver and spent years in Calgary before she and her husband made the decision to relocate to Arizona in 2006. They settled in the Arcadia area of Scottsdale and never looked back.

That cross-border experience shaped the foundation of Amy's career. She understood firsthand what it meant to move to a new country, navigate unfamiliar markets, and build a life from scratch in the desert. When Canadian buyers started asking for help making the same transition, she was uniquely qualified to guide them.

Over the past 15 years, Amy has built her business entirely through referrals and repeat clients. She co-founded Desert Ridge Real Estate Advisors and Kreate Property Group before joining The Brokery, one of Arizona's most respected independent brokerages. Her focus has always been the same: know the neighborhoods at a granular level and give every client the kind of attention that turns a transaction into a relationship.

Today, Amy serves buyers and sellers across Desert Ridge, North Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Fountain Hills, Old Town Scottsdale, and North Phoenix. Her clients include first-time buyers, luxury homeowners, investors, and a steady stream of Canadian families making Arizona their new home.

The Brokerage

Backed by The Brokery

The Brokery is an independent, agent-owned brokerage built on the belief that real estate should be personal, professional, and rooted in real relationships. Unlike franchise brokerages, The Brokery gives agents the freedom to serve clients their own way while providing the infrastructure, marketing support, and collaborative culture that elevates every transaction.

For Amy's clients, this means access to The Brokery's network, marketing resources, and industry connections without the rigid systems of a corporate brand. It's the best of both worlds: boutique attention backed by serious capability.

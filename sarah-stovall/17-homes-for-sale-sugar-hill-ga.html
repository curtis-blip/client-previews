Living in Sugar Hill

A Downtown on West Broad Street

Sugar Hill is a city in northern Gwinnett County, near Lake Lanier and the foothills of the North Georgia mountains.

It was chartered as the Town of Sugar Hill in 1939 and became the City of Sugar Hill in 1975, and local tradition ties the name to a load of sugar that spilled on a hill. Its downtown is on West Broad Street, home to The Bowl at Sugar Hill outdoor amphitheater, the Eagle Theatre and the Sugar Hill Art Gallery & History Museum.

Sugar Hill's Setting and History

Sugar Hill lies in northern Gwinnett County, bordered by Buford to the northeast and Suwanee to the southwest. The town was chartered in 1939 and renamed a city in 1975. Local tradition says the name comes from a load of sugar that spilled on a hill. The city's history museum highlights gold mining and moonshining history, along with churches, schools and the volunteer fire department, and the Simmons Mine overlook at Gold Mine Park refers to mining that was active in the 1800s through the early 1900s.

The Sugar Hill Historic Cemetery is also listed on the city's site.

02

Downtown Sugar Hill

Downtown Sugar Hill is centered on West Broad Street. The Bowl at Sugar Hill, an outdoor amphitheater, and the Eagle Theatre share an address at 5029 West Broad Street and present the Sugar Hill Live On Stage concert series. The Eagle Theatre was built in 2018 with art deco styling and 406 seats. The city also lists a plaza called The Landing behind The Bowl, The Promenade with gas lanterns, water features and public art, Splash Park, the E Center and the Sugar Hill Art Gallery & History Museum at 5010 West Broad Street.

The Sugar Hill Greenway begins at Cornerstone Park at the corner of West Broad and Church Streets.

What to Check Before You Buy in Sugar Hill

A few questions are worth settling before you write an offer on a Sugar Hill home.

Sarah's Buyer's Guide

School assignment
Gwinnett County Public Schools assigns schools by address. Parts of the city are served by different high schools. Confirm the zoned schools for the specific home before you write an offer.
City limits
A Sugar Hill mailing address can reach past the city limits. Confirm a home is inside the city before you compare taxes and services.
Homestead exemption
Once you own and live in the home, file for the homestead exemption with Gwinnett County; Georgia's deadline is April 1.
Lake lots and docks
On any Lake Lanier lot, the shoreline is managed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, and a private dock needs a Corps permit. Ask whether a lake lot already has one.

Getting around

  • Georgia 20 runs through the center of the city and U.S. 23 along its southern edge; I-985 and I-85 are reached via Georgia 20 in Buford.
  • The Sugar Hill Greenway is a paved shared-use trail that connects downtown to neighborhoods, schools and parks.
  • Road distance from city hall: about 11 miles to Cumming, 37 miles to downtown Atlanta and 48 miles to Hartsfield-Jackson.

School information is factual and by district. Always confirm a specific address in the district's school finder.

Questions

Questions About Living in Sugar Hill

Which county is Sugar Hill in?

Sugar Hill is in northern Gwinnett County.

Which school district serves Sugar Hill?

Gwinnett County Public Schools. The district's school locator confirms the zoned schools for a specific address.

How far is Sugar Hill from the airport and downtown Atlanta?

By road from city hall it is about 48 miles to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport and about 37 miles to downtown Atlanta, mostly on I-985 and I-85.

Does Sugar Hill have a downtown?

Yes. Downtown Sugar Hill is on West Broad Street, with The Bowl at Sugar Hill, the Eagle Theatre, Splash Park, the E Center and the Sugar Hill Art Gallery & History Museum.

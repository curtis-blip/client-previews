Seller's Guide

Selling a House in San Diego, Step by Step

Six steps from getting the house ready to handing over the keys, with Leticia beside you.

Before You List

How Leticia Prepares a Sale

Leticia Torres helps homeowners across North County San Diego sell with a clear plan, so you always know what to expect and what comes next. She works in English and Spanish.

Here are the six steps of her process, from preparing the home to closing.

01

Step 1 of 6

Prep

Look at the home's condition and pick the simple improvements worth making. Cleaning, repairs and staging help the home show its best.

What Leticia does: She walks the home with you and organizes the work with local contractors.

02

Step 2 of 6

Pricing

A fair price is set with a Comparative Market Analysis, which looks at recent sales and nearby listings. Pricing well from the start builds early interest.

What Leticia does: She prepares the analysis for your neighborhood and talks through the price with you.

03

Step 3 of 6

Marketing

Professional photos, virtual tours, MLS exposure and digital advertising put the home in front of buyers.

What Leticia does: She plans and runs the marketing for your listing.

04

Step 4 of 6

Showings

Open houses and private tours are scheduled around your life, and interested buyers are checked for readiness.

What Leticia does: She sets up and hosts the showings and screens for buyers who are ready.

05

Step 5 of 6

Offers

Each offer is weighed for its price, contingencies and closing timeline, so you can decide with the full picture.

What Leticia does: She explains every offer clearly and negotiates on your behalf.

06

Step 6 of 6

Closing

Inspections, the appraisal and escrow come next, then final signatures and the transfer of ownership.

What Leticia does: She oversees the details and keeps everyone informed until the sale closes.

Questions

Questions Sellers Ask Leticia

How do I start the selling process?

Begin with a consultation. Leticia will go over your goals, answer your questions and outline the steps to list and sell.

What improvements are worth making?

Fresh paint, tidy landscaping and small kitchen updates are common choices. Leticia can recommend local contractors.

Do I need to stage my home?

Staging is not required, but it can help buyers picture themselves living there. Leticia offers staging advice or can connect you with professionals.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on the home, the price and market conditions. Leticia can give you a realistic picture once she has seen the house.

What if my home does not appraise?

If the appraisal comes in low, Leticia talks with the buyer and their lender and uses comparable sales to support your price.

What I do

Two practices, one office

Twelve years in Las Vegas and Henderson, split between helping people buy and sell, and managing rental property for owners. Most agents do the first. Most management companies do the second. Doing both is the reason I can tell an investor what a property will actually cost to run before they sign for it.

Property management

The half of the business nobody advertises.

Tenant placement and screening, leasing and renewals, rent collection and owner disbursement, maintenance coordination, move-in and move-out inspections, and a long-term look at whether the property is still doing what you bought it to do.

A good share of the owners I manage for live outside Nevada. The work is the same either way; what changes is who has to physically go and look.

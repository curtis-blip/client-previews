About

A brokerage built for the towns it sits in

Black Hawk Real Estate has been working the Gilpin County corridor since 2010. Two people, one office, on Lump Gulch Highway.

The practice

More than a home or a property, it is a lifestyle

Begin your real estate journey with a team who knows it is more than a home or a property. We are local to Colorado, connected to the vibrant cities and the beautiful Rocky Mountains, and invested in the community we work in.

What that means in practice is narrower than it sounds. We work one corridor, from Black Hawk and Central City out to Nederland, Rollinsville and Pinecliffe, down through Idaho Springs to Golden and Evergreen. Eight towns, each of which trades differently, and none of which behaves like the Denver metro forty five minutes east.

Where the method comes from

Two decades inside federal housing

The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development recruited Jerry in 1988 to sell and manage its single family and multifamily portfolios. He stayed twenty years, working as a project manager and financial analyst alongside the sales work, and collected over thirty five letters of merit and performance citations across that span.

Federal portfolios are unusual training for a mountain brokerage, and exactly the right training. They are full of property with no clean comparable, unusual condition, and a buyer pool that has to be found rather than waited for. That is Gilpin County with different paperwork.

He was named Downtown Denver Multifamily Project Manager of the Year and took a Colorado Multifamily Loan High Sales Volume Award. He remains an authorised HUD Homes sales broker.

Three ways this usually goes

Large franchise

The national brand

  • Agent may cover a whole metro area
  • Work often distributed across a team
  • Mountain diligence is unfamiliar ground

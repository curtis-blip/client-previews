Corpus Christi housing market: where the Coastal Bend stands going into Q3.

Q2 has been a steadier season across Corpus Christi, Flour Bluff, and Padre Island than the spring rush we saw last year. Days on market have stretched in some price bands while well-prepped Southside homes still see early offers. Inventory is loosening just enough to give serious buyers room to negotiate. Here is what the numbers and the showings are actually telling Dodee on the ground.

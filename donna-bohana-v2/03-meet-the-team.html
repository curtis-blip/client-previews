Frequently Asked Questions About Our Team

How many agents are on the Solstice International Realty team?
Solstice International Realty is a boutique brokerage led by Donna Bohana with a team of eight experienced agents. Each agent brings specialized knowledge of different communities across Malibu, Pacific Palisades, Westlake Village, and the greater LA Westside.
What areas does the Solstice team cover?
The team serves Malibu, Pacific Palisades, Westlake Village, Calabasas, Brentwood, Hollywood Hills, Manhattan Beach, Newport Beach, and select international markets. Our agents have deep local knowledge in each of these communities, with specialists focused on specific neighborhoods. Explore our communities.
Does the team handle both buying and selling?
Yes. The Solstice team handles buyer representation, seller representation, and luxury leasing across all of our service areas. Donna herself has set records for both the highest sale and the highest rental in the Palisades Highlands, and the team carries that same standard of performance across every transaction type.
What makes Solstice different from a large franchise brokerage?
Solstice International Realty is an independent, boutique brokerage founded by Donna Bohana in 2007. That means every client gets direct access to Donna and her team, with personalized attention, discretion, and the flexibility to tailor marketing strategies to each property. There are no corporate layers between you and the person handling your transaction.
Do you work with international buyers and sellers?
Absolutely. "International" is in our name for a reason. Donna's education in England and her experience with global properties in France, Spain, Italy, and London give the team a genuinely international perspective. Jessica Castaneda specializes in cross-border transactions, and the entire team is equipped to serve clients relocating from overseas or investing in LA from abroad.
How do I get in touch with a specific team member?
The best way to connect with any member of the Solstice team is through our contact page or by calling (310) 801-0265. Let us know what you are looking for, and we will pair you with the agent whose expertise best matches your needs and preferred market area.

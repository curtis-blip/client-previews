Meet Dodee

The Southside Specialist, settled in Corpus Christi since 2012

Dodee Hill is a full-service Corpus Christi REALTOR® who leads with listening. Wyoming-raised by way of the Black Hills of South Dakota, she landed in Corpus Christi in 2012 and never looked back. Her family is built on camping trips, beach days, and showing up for the local community.

The Southside is her home base, Kings Crossing, the Cimmaron and South Staples corridor, the larger lots and mature trees that define this part of town. Clients can expect plain-spoken communication, steady negotiation, and a guide who treats your move the way she would treat her own.

DC Ranch

A master-planned community organized as villages, with its own main street at the foot of the McDowell Mountains.

Home/ Communities/DC Ranch

The character

Villages, not a single tract

DC Ranch runs along the base of the McDowell Mountains and is among the most recognized master-planned communities in north Scottsdale. What separates it from a conventional development is the structure: it is laid out as a set of distinct villages rather than one continuous tract, which is why the architecture and the feel change noticeably from one neighborhood to the next.

Housing spans custom homes, standard single-family neighborhoods and luxury townhomes, so the entry point is considerably wider than the reputation suggests. Some enclaves are gated and others are not; that varies street by street rather than across the community.

Market Street is the community's own main street, with shops and restaurants inside the community rather than a drive away. Residents have community centers, pools and tennis, and desert trails connect directly to the McDowell Sonoran Preserve along the eastern edge.

The trade

Structure, and somewhere to walk to

DC Ranch is the answer for buyers who want a master-planned community with genuine amenity and a real center, rather than large lots and quiet. It is the opposite choice from Troon North, twenty minutes away.

Living here

The practical detail

Housing

Custom homes, single-family neighborhoods and luxury townhomes across the villages. Architectural character shifts between them, so it is worth touring more than one.

The center

Market Street carries shops and restaurants within the community. Community centers, pools and tennis courts serve residents, and private golf sits inside the community boundary.

Outdoors

Direct access to the McDowell Sonoran Preserve trail network along the eastern edge, plus the wider city parks system.

Schools

The area falls within Scottsdale Unified School District. Boundaries are assigned by address, so confirm on the district's own map for any specific property.

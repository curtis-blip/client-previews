Point Loma and Ocean Beach at a glance

A peninsula and the beach town beside it

Point Loma is the long ridge between the Pacific and San Diego Bay, and Ocean Beach is the grid of streets at its northwest foot, at the mouth of the San Diego River. Both are neighborhoods of the City of San Diego, in Council District 2.

The City plans them separately. Its Peninsula Community Plan, adopted in 1997, covers eleven Point Loma neighborhoods, and Ocean Beach has had a plan of its own since 1975. Every lot in both falls under the 30-foot height limit that the City's voters approved in 1972.

01

Where Cabrillo came ashore

Juan Rodriguez Cabrillo sailed into San Diego Bay in 1542 and probably landed at Ballast Point, on the bay side of the peninsula. The National Park Service counts him as the first European to set foot on what is now the West Coast of the United States.

The Old Point Loma Lighthouse was lit on November 15, 1855, 422 feet above the sea. Fog and low clouds hid it too often, and a lower light replaced it in 1891. A 1913 proclamation set the old lighthouse aside as Cabrillo National Monument, whose tidepools are best in fall and winter.

02

From La Playa to Loma Portal

La Playa, on the bay, was one of California's first working waterfronts under Spanish and Mexican rule, and its old trail is now Rosecrans Street. Roseville began to the north in 1869, and the trolley lines brought houses to Roseville and Loma Portal after 1910.

The Peninsula Community Plan describes the Wooded Area's large lots and narrow roads under eucalyptus. Fleetridge's one-story houses date from the 1950s, on bigger lots than other postwar tracts, and Loma Portal has winding concrete streets with old street lights set in the middle of intersections. Liberty Station is the former Naval Training Center, a Navy base from 1922 to 1997.

03

A resort town from 1887

Ocean Beach began as a resort subdivision. Billy Carlson and Frank Higgins filed its map with the County Recorder on May 28, 1887, with 25-foot lots on blocks split by alleys and avenues named after resort towns, Newport and Niagara among them. Summer cottages and a streetcar line followed.

Many of the cottages became year-round homes. The City's Ocean Beach Community Plan lists 72 buildings in the Ocean Beach Cottage Emerging Historical District, which covers cottages and bungalows built from 1887 to 1931.

04

The river mouth and Dog Beach

The San Diego River forms Ocean Beach's northern edge. Dog Beach, at the north end of the sand, is the City's only beach where dogs can run off leash around the clock. The Famosa Slough, inland, is kept as open space for its birds.

South of the pier, tidepools and small beaches run below the bluffs to Adair Street. There the City's 68-acre Sunset Cliffs Natural Park begins, with sea caves and arches carved into the bluffs along the western edge of Point Loma.

05

What Jim would check here

  • Which plan, which district The Peninsula Community Plan or the Ocean Beach plan, by address; both are in Council District 2. The plan shapes what a lot can become.
  • Coastal rules The 30-foot limit covers both. All of Ocean Beach and about 60 percent of the peninsula are in the Coastal Zone, where additions can need a Coastal Development Permit, and houses 45 years or older are screened for historic value before most exterior permits.
  • Airport noise Ocean Beach sits under the departure path most used at San Diego International. Check the address on the Airport Authority's Quieter Home Program maps, and ask whether the house was insulated under it.
  • Bluffs and flood maps On the cliffs, ask how far the house sits from the bluff edge. Near the river and the slough, look the address up on FEMA's flood maps.

Questions

Point Loma and Ocean Beach FAQ

Are Point Loma and Ocean Beach in the City of San Diego?

Yes. Both are neighborhoods of the City of San Diego, in Council District 2, with separate community plans: the Peninsula plan for Point Loma and the Ocean Beach plan.

Which school district serves Point Loma and Ocean Beach?

San Diego Unified School District. Its School Finder lists the schools for a street address, so confirm a specific address there.

Is there a height limit in Point Loma and Ocean Beach?

Yes. Proposition D, approved by the City's voters in 1972, limits buildings in the Coastal Zone to 30 feet, and both community plans apply it to every lot.

Is Ocean Beach under the airport flight path?

The City's Ocean Beach plan says the community sits under the departure path most often used at San Diego International. The Airport Authority's Quieter Home Program covers eligible homes inside the 65-decibel noise contour, and its maps show the line.

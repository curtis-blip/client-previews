The Three-Phase Launch

The 3-Phase Plan: Private Exclusive, Coming Soon, Launch

Concierge pairs with Compass's three-phase marketing, so the improvements and the pre-marketing run at the same time.

  1. Phase 1

    Private Exclusive

    Before the home is ready, it can be shared privately with Compass agents and their buyers. Early feedback shapes the price, and no days build up on the market.

  2. Phase 2

    Coming Soon

    As the improvements wrap up, the listing goes public on Compass.com as Coming Soon, building interest before the first showing.

  3. Phase 3

    Full Market Launch

    Once the work is finished, the home launches on the MLS and every major site, with feedback and demand already in hand.

Questions

Compass Concierge Questions

What is Compass Concierge?

Compass Concierge is a Compass program that pays for pre-sale improvements up front, such as paint, flooring, staging and repairs, through a loan from Notable Finance. Stephanie helps you choose the work and manages the vendors, and repayment is due at closing.

Are there services Concierge does not cover?

Concierge works with a wide range of vendors and covers more than 100 kinds of improvement. Stephanie confirms which services qualify for your property during the walk-through.

Does Compass Concierge charge a fee?

There is nothing to pay up front. Depending on your state of residence, fees or interest may apply, and your Compass Concierge loan agreement sets out any costs before work begins. Repayment is due when your home sells, your listing agreement with Compass ends, or 12 months pass from your Concierge start date, whichever comes first.

Who coordinates the vendors?

Stephanie does, with vendors she trusts on the Coastside. You approve the work and the budget, and she manages the schedule, the contractors and the quality.

How do I get started?

Call Stephanie at (650) 678-5707 or send her a note. She will walk the house with you and set out the plan.

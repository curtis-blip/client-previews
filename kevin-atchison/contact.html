Text is usually fastest

I am out showing houses most of the day, so a text often gets a quicker answer than a call does. If you leave a voicemail I will call you back, and if you send the form below it comes straight to my phone.

Tell me roughly what you are trying to do and where. If you already have a house in mind, send the address. If you are still deciding where, the communities will help you narrow it down. If you are selling, tell me the town and when you would want to be on the market, or start with an instant estimate. Either way you will get a straight answer, including if the answer is that now is the wrong time.

If you are just starting, the buying process and how I sell will tell you most of what you need before we speak.

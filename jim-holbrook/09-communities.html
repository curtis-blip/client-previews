Where Jim works

Eighteen communities, two counties

Jim's office is in Murrieta, and most of his sales are in Temecula, Murrieta and Menifee. The team covers the rest of Southwest Riverside County and much of San Diego County, from North County's coast to the city's older neighborhoods.

Each page covers what a buyer filters on: the county or city, the school district, the water district, the drive times, and the questions Jim asks before you write an offer.

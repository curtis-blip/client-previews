Our Services

What's Included in Our Property Management Services

San Diego property management services designed around what owners actually need, from listing to lease renewal and everything between.

We advertise your San Diego rental across 20+ platforms including Zillow, Apartments.com, and the MLS. Professional photography, detailed listings, strategic pricing based on local comps, and targeted social media exposure get qualified applicants to your door faster. Most properties lease within two to four weeks.
Every applicant goes through a multi-step screening process: credit checks, nationwide background reports, income verification (minimum 3x rent), and rental history references. We follow all fair housing laws and place tenants who are financially qualified and respectful of the property. This reduces late payments and turnover significantly.
Tenants pay rent online through AppFolio, and funds are deposited directly to your account. We enforce late fee policies consistently, track payment history, and handle any delinquency issues promptly. Owner portal access lets you view rent status, payment history, and upcoming deposits anytime.
Our 24/7 emergency maintenance line at (858) 808-2420 is always staffed. Routine and emergency requests are triaged, dispatched to our trusted local vendor network, and tracked through completion. We negotiate competitive rates, oversee quality, and keep you informed with photos and updates throughout the process. Regular property inspections catch small issues before they become costly repairs.
Monthly owner statements are delivered through your AppFolio portal, detailing income, expenses, maintenance costs, and net distributions. Year-end 1099 tax documents and annual summaries are provided automatically. You have 24/7 access to your financial dashboard from any device, giving you full visibility into your property's performance.
We perform detailed move-in and move-out inspections with timestamped photo documentation. Routine inspections are scheduled periodically throughout each tenancy to verify property condition, identify maintenance needs, and confirm lease compliance. Inspection reports are shared with you through the owner portal.

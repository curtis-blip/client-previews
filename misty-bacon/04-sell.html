Prep and Stage

Common Questions

Seller FAQ

I prepare a Comparative Market Analysis using recent sold properties in your specific Bay Area Houston neighborhood. I look at square footage, condition, upgrades, lot size, and current buyer demand. This gives you a data-backed price range, not a guess from a national algorithm. I walk you through the numbers so you understand exactly why we land where we do.
Average days on market in Bay Area Houston vary by price point and season. Well-priced homes in the $150K to $300K range often receive offers within the first two weeks. Condos and townhomes near Clear Lake and NASA typically move faster due to steady demand from aerospace professionals. I set realistic timeline expectations during our consultation based on current market conditions in your zip code.
Every listing gets professional photography, detailed property descriptions, MLS syndication to HAR, Zillow, and Realtor.com, targeted social media advertising, and broker-to-broker outreach. Depending on the property, I may also recommend virtual tours, drone photography, or open house events. The goal is maximum exposure to qualified buyers actively searching Bay Area Houston.
Full professional staging is not always necessary, but strategic preparation makes a measurable difference. I provide a room-by-room checklist covering decluttering, furniture placement, lighting, and curb appeal. In most cases, simple adjustments you can handle yourself will make your home photograph better and show better. If professional staging is warranted, I can connect you with local staging partners.
Typical seller costs in Texas include agent commissions, title insurance, prorated property taxes, and any negotiated repair credits. I provide a detailed net sheet during our consultation so you know exactly what to expect at closing. No surprises. I walk through every line item and explain where you have room to negotiate.
When multiple offers come in, I present each one side by side with a clear breakdown of price, financing type, contingencies, closing timeline, and buyer qualifications. Price is not always the deciding factor. A strong conventional loan with a flexible close date may net you more than the highest cash offer with restrictive terms. I advise on the full picture and negotiate to maximize your outcome.

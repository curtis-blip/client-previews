What the median price tells you, what it does not, and where to see the current numbers for San Antonio and Boerne.

How to read it

The middle sale, not the typical house

The median price is the middle sale of the month: half of the homes sold for more, half for less. It moves with the mix of homes that happened to sell as much as with prices, so one month can mislead.

Read the trend over several months, and your own neighborhood, before reading anything into it. For your home specifically, start with an instant estimate.

Why your home is not the median

Your price depends on your home's size, condition and street, and on the sales around it.

A neighborhood well above the city median can still have homes that sell below it, and the reverse. A valuation built from the comparable sales near you is the number that matters.

Questions

Market questions

What is the difference between median and average price?

The median is the middle sale; the average adds every price and divides. A few very expensive sales pull the average up, which is why market reports lead with the median.

What does days on market mean?

How long homes sat between listing and contract. Shorter usually means buyers are competing; longer means they have time to negotiate.

What are months of inventory?

How long the current listings would take to sell at the current pace. Around six months is usually read as balanced; fewer favors sellers, more favors buyers.

Where can I see official Texas housing data?

The Texas Real Estate Research Center at Texas A&M publishes monthly housing data for Texas metros, including San Antonio.

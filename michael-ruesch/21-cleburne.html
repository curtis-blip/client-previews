Before you offer

What we would check for you

None of this is special to Cleburne. It just comes up here more than it does in a city.

The commute is the market

A lot of demand here is driven by Fort Worth. That supports values and it also means traffic and pricing move with the Metroplex, not with Whitney.

Well and septic

Outside the city limits, expect a well or rural water and a septic system. We have both checked on their own, never folded into the home inspection.

Who maintains the road

County road, private easement or association street. Three different answers, and three different costs across the years you own the place.

Flood plain

Elevation decides the insurance premium, sometimes by thousands a year. We pull the map before you are attached to the house.

Good to know

Schools

Cleburne ISD serves the city, with other districts across the county. Confirm any specific address with the district.

Everyday

Full retail and services in town, and the whole of the Metroplex within an hour if you need something it does not have.

Getting around

The Chisholm Trail Parkway put downtown Fort Worth within roughly forty five minutes, which reshaped demand here.

Outdoors

Lake Pat Cleburne sits just southwest of the city, and Cleburne State Park is a short drive further out.

Common questions

Answered

Is Cleburne a Fort Worth commute?

For many buyers, yes. The Chisholm Trail Parkway makes downtown Fort Worth roughly forty five minutes.

How does pricing compare to the Metroplex?

Generally lower for comparable space, which is the trade people are making. It also means values here move with the Metroplex market.

Is there acreage nearby?

Plenty across Johnson County. Our Farm and Ranch page covers water, access and minerals.

What about the lake?

Lake Pat Cleburne is close and is a different animal from Lake Whitney, smaller and municipally managed.

Is there new construction?

Yes, particularly toward the parkway side of town.

How far is Lake Whitney?

About forty minutes south. Some buyers here keep a weekend place on the water.

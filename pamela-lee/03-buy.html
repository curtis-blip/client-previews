The Buying Process

Eight steps from first call to keys in hand.

Buying a home in Dublin, GA is a sequence of decisions, not a single one. Here is what working with Pamela Lee Realty looks like, start to finish.

01

Discovery call.

We start with a conversation. Budget, timing, the parts of Laurens County that interest you, what your day-to-day life needs to look like. No pressure to act, just clarity on where to begin.

02

Get pre-approved.

Talking to a lender early gives you a real budget and tells sellers you are serious. I can connect you with local lenders who know the Dublin market and respond fast. We get this done before the search heats up.

03

Set up a smart search.

I build a filtered MLS search around the criteria that actually matter for you. New listings hit your inbox the moment they go live. No public Zillow firehose, no homes you have already eliminated.

04

Tour the right homes.

We walk homes together. I tell you what is wrong with each one, not just what is right. Roof age, drainage, neighborhood quirks, future-resale concerns. The honest version is the only version that helps.

05

Make the right offer.

When you find the one, I prepare a comps-backed offer that protects your interests and gives you the best shot at acceptance. Contingencies that matter, terms that are clean, no boilerplate that creates risk later.

06

Inspection + due diligence.

Inspection coordinated with a Dublin-area inspector I trust. We read the report together, decide what is real, what is cosmetic, and what is worth pushing back on with the seller.

07

Appraisal + final approval.

Appraisal coordinated, lender on track, title work running. I keep all three lanes moving in parallel and stay on top of every deadline so nothing slips in the final 30 days.

08

Close + get the keys.

Walk-through the day before closing, signing at the closing table, keys in your hand. I show up. Most clients walk out of closing as friends, and most of my future business comes from this moment.

Buyer Questions

Things buyers ask me first.

How much do I need for a down payment in Dublin GA?
It depends on the loan. Conventional often runs 5-20%, FHA can be as low as 3.5%, and USDA loans (which cover much of rural Laurens County) can be zero down. The right answer comes after you talk to a lender. I'll connect you to one I trust.
How fast do homes sell in Dublin?
It varies by neighborhood and price point. Some homes go in days, others sit longer. The biggest factor is condition and pricing relative to comps. When you're ready to look, I'll show you what's actually moving and what's not.
I'm relocating to Dublin from out of state. How do you help?
A lot of my buyers are from out of state. We start with a video call about what you need, what your daily life is like, and which parts of Laurens County would actually fit. I can do video tours of homes you can't visit in person.
Should I get pre-approved before we start looking?
Yes, and not just for the obvious budget reason. In the Dublin market, sellers take pre-approved offers more seriously, and you'll be able to act fast when the right house comes up.
What if I need to sell first to buy?
That's common. We can run both sides at once. I'll work with you on the listing strategy for your current home and the timeline for finding the next one, so the move stays clean.
Do you only show me your own listings?
No. As your buyer's agent, I show you any home on MLS that fits, regardless of who has it listed. My job is finding the right house for you, not selling you a specific one.

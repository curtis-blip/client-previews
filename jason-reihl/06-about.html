About Us

About Red House Property Management

A boutique management company that takes pride in finding quality tenants, cutting-edge marketing, and sustainable solutions for San Diego rental properties.

Who We Are

Our Story

Red House Property Management was founded by Jason Riehl with a straightforward idea: San Diego rental owners deserve a property manager who actually communicates, uses modern technology, and treats every property like a personal investment. Not a number in a spreadsheet.

We built this company by rejecting the conventional property management approach. Too many firms collect their fees, go silent for months, and leave owners wondering what's happening with their own property. That's not how we operate. Every owner gets a direct line to our team, real-time access to financials through AppFolio, and honest answers when something comes up.

Based in Solana Beach, we specialize in single-family homes, condos, and 2-4 unit properties throughout San Diego County. Our focus is long-term unfurnished residential rentals with a 1-year minimum lease requirement, which means we attract stable, quality tenants who stay longer and take better care of your property.

What Drives Us

Our Mission at Red House Property Management

We exist to protect your investment and grow its value while providing quality homes for the tenants who live in them. Every decision we make balances owner returns with responsible property stewardship, because the best long-term results come from treating everyone in the equation with respect.

That means transparent financials, responsive maintenance, thorough tenant screening, and a team that picks up the phone when you call.

"Peace of Mind, One Door at a Time."

For buyers

Finding and Buying Your Home

From the first search to the keys, Noah handles the details so you can focus on the move itself.

Buying With Noah Search Homes

  1. Personalized home search

    A search built around what you want, in the towns that fit, not a list sent to everyone.

  2. Expert negotiation

    Noah negotiates for you, so a broker handles the price and the terms for you.

  3. Transaction management

    The deadlines, paperwork and inspections are tracked for you from offer to closing.

  4. Local market expertise

    He lives in Longmont and works the towns around it, so you hear what each one is like from a neighbor.

For sellers

Pricing and Selling Your Home

Start with what your home is worth, then talk through the next step with Noah.

Selling With Noah Home Value

  1. A free home valuation

    A detailed market analysis of your home, so the asking price rests on what is happening around it.

  2. Professional photography and wide marketing

    Your home is professionally photographed and marketed across all major online platforms, social media, and Noah’s exclusive network of buyers.

  3. Showings and open houses

    Noah runs the showings and open houses and keeps you posted after each one.

  4. Negotiation and closing

    He negotiates the offers and carries the sale through to closing day.

Out of state

Buying From Out of State

Noah bought his own house here sight unseen, from Hawaii. Now he helps others do it the right way.

  1. He films every home you are serious about

    The good, the bad and the ugly, so you see each house the way he does before you book a flight.

  2. You fly out once

    One trip, for the inspection.

  3. He handles the rest

    The details between your offer and the keys are his.

  4. Closing papers are signed where you live

    Noah sends someone to your current home to sign the closing documents.

Common Questions

Does Noah help buyers moving from out of state?

Yes. He films every home a buyer is serious about, the buyer flies out once for the inspection, and he handles the rest, including sending someone to the buyer’s current home to sign closing documents.

Does Noah help sellers too?

Yes. It starts with a free home valuation, then professional photography, marketing across the major online platforms and social media, showings, negotiation and closing. See selling with Noah.

Which towns does Noah work in?

Longmont and the Northern Colorado towns around it. Each has its own page, listed on the communities page.

How do I start?

Call Noah or send a note. The contact page has both.

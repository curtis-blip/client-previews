Home Valuation

What Is My Home Worth in Shelby County

A valuation walked in person, with the comparable sales it came from and the number she would actually list at.

The short answer

An automated estimate has never driven down your street

It does not know that the house three doors down backs onto the rail line, or that the school corporation boundary runs behind your fence, or that the roof was replaced two years ago. Automated models work well in uniform subdivisions with a steady stream of near-identical sales. Most of the nine towns Jennifer works are the opposite of that, which is exactly where those models are least reliable.

So she walks the house, pulls the sales she can defend, and gives you a range plus the single number she would list at. It is free, there is no obligation, and you keep the comparables either way.

What comes back to you

  • The three to five comparable sales the price came from, with the actual addresses
  • A range that is honest about uncertainty, and the single number she would list at
  • What she would fix before listing, and what she would leave alone
  • An estimate of net proceeds, so you know what actually reaches you

How she gets to a number

Four steps, none of them automated

01

She walks the house

Not the photos, the house. Grade, roof line, mechanicals, what has been maintained and what has been deferred. An algorithm cannot see any of that and it is frequently the difference between two identical-looking listings.

02

She pulls the comparable sales she can defend

Three to five, with the actual addresses, chosen because they genuinely compare rather than because they are nearby. In a thin-comp market this is judgment supported by evidence, and you see the evidence.

03

She adjusts for what makes yours different

Square footage, lot, condition, and the local facts a spreadsheet misses. Backing onto the rail line matters. So does the county line running behind your fence.

04

You get a range, and a number

The range is honest about uncertainty. The number is what she would actually list it at, which is a different and more useful thing than an estimate.

Three numbers people confuse

Appraised, assessed and market value are not the same thing

Market value

What a willing buyer would actually pay today. It is the only one of the three that determines your sale price, and it is the one this valuation estimates.

Appraised value

A licensed appraiser's formal opinion, usually ordered by the buyer's lender to protect the loan. It can come in below the agreed price, which is a negotiation, not a disaster.

Assessed value

The county's figure for property tax purposes. It runs on its own cycle and frequently sits well below market. Do not price your house off your tax bill.

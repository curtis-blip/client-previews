PRIVACY policy.

How O’Kelley CRE handles the information you share when you browse this site or reach out about commercial real estate.

Last updated: June 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how O’Kelley CRE (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with this website. By using the site, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways. First, information you provide directly, such as when you submit a contact form, request a Broker Opinion of Value, or otherwise reach out to us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, property details, and any message you choose to send.

Second, information collected automatically as you browse, which may include:

  • Your IP address, browser type, and device information.
  • Pages you view, links you click, and the time and date of your visit.
  • The website or source that referred you to this site.

How We Use It

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, prepare and deliver the services you request, and follow up about commercial real estate opportunities relevant to you. We also use it to operate, maintain, and improve the site, and to keep our communications and records accurate.

  • To respond to messages, valuation requests, and consultation requests.
  • To provide market information and property opportunities you have asked about.
  • To analyze how the site is used so we can improve its content and performance.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and professional obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and improve your experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device. We may also use analytics tools, such as web analytics services, that collect aggregated, non-identifying information about site traffic and usage patterns.

You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Some parts of the site may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

Third-Party Services

We may rely on trusted third-party services to operate this site and our business, including website hosting, form processing, email, analytics, and the brokerage platform that supports our services. These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it consistent with their own privacy and security practices.

This site may also link to third-party websites we do not control. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Your Choices

You are in control of the information you share with us. You may choose not to submit a form or provide certain details, though doing so may limit our ability to respond. You may also:

  • Ask us to update or correct information you have provided.
  • Ask us to delete information we hold about you, subject to any legal or recordkeeping obligations.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications by letting us know.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.

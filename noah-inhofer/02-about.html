His story

From the Fighting Ring to Longmont

Noah’s road to real estate started with a broken water heater. He needed $1,500, heard a radio ad for a fighting tournament with that prize, and signed up, though he had never been in a real fight. He fought three times that night and won.

That night turned into a professional mixed martial arts career, until a severe spinal injury ended it. Noah and his wife moved to Hawaii and opened a CrossFit gym. Their daughter was born on Oahu, and their son soon followed.

To be closer to the kids’ grandparents, the family moved back to the mainland and settled in Longmont. Noah started in real estate by investing in and flipping houses, which taught him how homes are built and what they are worth, then moved to working with buyers and sellers.

“As a real estate professional, I want to take your stress and put it on my shoulders.”

Quick answers

Questions About Working With Noah

Does Noah help buyers moving from out of state?

Yes. He films every home a buyer is serious about, the good and the bad, so they fly out once for the inspection. He arranges for closing papers to be signed where the buyer lives now.

Which towns does Noah work in?

Longmont and the Northern Colorado towns around it, from Fort Collins and Windsor in the north to Arvada and Commerce City in the south. Each one has its own page on this site.

Does Noah help sellers too?

Yes. He prices a home from a detailed market analysis, markets it with professional photography across the major home sites and social media, and handles showings, negotiation and closing.

Which brokerage is Noah with?

eXp Realty. He holds Colorado real estate broker license 100088958, which anyone can look up with the Colorado Division of Real Estate.

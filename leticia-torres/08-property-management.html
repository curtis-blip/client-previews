The Process

Four Steps to a Managed Rental

From setup to long-term care, each step is explained before it happens.

  1. 01

    Onboarding

    It starts with your goals and a look at the property. The property is inspected, fees are explained clearly and a management plan is built around your rental.

  2. 02

    Preparing Your Rental

    The home is toured and its condition documented. Rent is set, the management agreement is signed, and keys and paperwork are collected.

    • Initial walk-through and property inspection
    • Condition report with photos and notes
    • Set rental price and sign the agreement
    • Collect keys and documents, and answer questions

  3. 03

    Marketing and Screening

    Your rental is photographed and listed to reach good applicants. Each applicant is checked, and fair housing laws are followed throughout.

    • Photos and descriptions for each listing
    • Listings on rental sites and social media
    • Showings and regular updates on interest
    • Credit, income, rental history and reference checks

  4. 04

    Management

    Once tenants are in place, rent collection, maintenance, inspections and reporting are handled for you. Owners get monthly statements.

    • Rent collected and deposited each month
    • Maintenance and repairs coordinated
    • Regular property inspections
    • Financial statements, renewals and extensions

Questions

Questions From Rental Owners

What types of properties does Leticia manage?

Single family homes, condos, townhouses and small apartment buildings in Oceanside and greater North County.

How is the rental price set?

By looking at current market data, including comparable rents and neighborhood trends.

How are tenants screened?

Applicants go through credit, income and background checks, plus reference and rental history checks.

How do owners receive rental income?

Rent is collected electronically and deposited into the owner's bank account, with monthly statements.

Are maintenance and repairs handled?

Yes. Leticia coordinates repairs with vendors, and the owner approves major expenses first.

How are management fees explained?

A clear fee schedule is shared during onboarding, and each charge is explained.
